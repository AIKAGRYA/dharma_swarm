# Bug Corral Index

Single front door for repo bug, slop, drift, duplicate, hygiene, and systemic
quality findings. The corral consolidates scattered finder reports into one
place so an operator can see what is actually broken, risky, inconsistent,
incomplete, or uncertain today without grepping dozens of audits.

**Role:** reference (index). Not a canonical authority surface. Live ledgers
(`docs/state/BROKEN_REGISTER.md`, `docs/governance/REPO_GOVERNANCE_AUDIT.md`,
`docs/governance/SOVEREIGN_MANIFEST.md`) remain the owners this corral points to.

**As of:** 2026-06-13
**Provenance:** every surviving finding traces to its sources in `09.md`.

## How to use this

Start here. Pick the family that matches your concern, read its findings
(sorted by severity then date), and follow the per-finding Sources line back
through `09.md` to the original reports. Open findings carry a Status of OPEN;
resolved and superseded findings are retained so they are not re-litigated.
