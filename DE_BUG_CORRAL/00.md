## Sweep receipt schema

A sweep receipt is valid when it records:

- `generated_at`
- `repo_root`
- `canonical_corral`
- `corral_state`
- `semantic_commons`
- `hit_count`
- `by_route`
- `route_deltas` or `hits`
- `next_action`

## Compost states

Every routed item should eventually be one of:

- `new`
- `accepted`
- `in_progress`
- `resolved`
- `false_positive`
- `stale_generated`

## Rule

Do not count raw hit count as progress. Progress is: routed, deduped, selected, acted, verified.
