# Nature Restoration Finance & Verified-Outcome Infrastructure — State of the Pipe, July 2026

*Research brief: the state of the pipe from capital to independently witnessed ecological outcomes, mapped against a planned "outcome graph" of causal action receipts (identity → intention → resource debit → community authority → evidence → capital → witnessed outcome → challenge/reversal → learning). Nature restoration as the first world-scale domain. Every claim carries a markdown link to a source fetched during this session; "n.a." where unconfirmable.*

**Prepared:** 11 July 2026. **Scope note:** Values are taken verbatim from fetched pages; where a page did not state a figure, the cell reads "n.a." rather than inferring.

## Executive summary

The capital→outcome pipe for nature restoration exists in fragments, each mature in isolation but poorly joined. Policy demand is codified (CBD GBF Target 19: **at least $200B/yr by 2030** ([CBD Target 19](https://www.cbd.int/gbf/targets/19))) but the resource-mobilization machinery is nascent — the Cali Fund received only its **first contribution at end-2025** ([CBD Cali Fund one-year update](https://www.cbd.int/article/2026-CaliFund)). Two credit-integrity councils (ICVCM, VCMI) and the UN's Article 6.4 PACM are simultaneously "tightening, fragmenting, and being tested at scale for the first time" ([Sustainability Atlas 2026](https://sustainableatlas.org/post/trend-watch-voluntary-carbon-market-integrity-icvcm-vcmi-in-2026-signals-winners-3305)). Digital MRV has bifurcated into (a) **registry-integrated verification engines** (Isometric Certify, Verra DMRV pilot), (b) **independent data/rating layers** (Pachama, Sylvera, BeZero, Chloris, Space Intelligence), and (c) **field-to-satellite outcome platforms** (WRI TerraMatch). Blockchain-native "proof-of-impact" rails (Regen Network, Open Forest Protocol) already implement receipt-like structures but at small scale. NVIDIA Earth-2 is a mature forecasting/digital-twin stack but **no fetched source links its forecasts to financed restoration interventions** — a clear white space.

The recurring failure is not measurement per se but **the absence of a shared, portable, per-transaction record binding identity, community authority (FPIC), capital, evidence, and reversal across these silos.** Each system re-implements a partial receipt; none interoperate. That is precisely the gap an outcome-graph / receipt layer could fill as connective tissue rather than a competitor.

## 1. CBD Global Biodiversity Framework Target 19, Cali Fund, biodiversity credits

### Target 19 — the demand mandate
Target 19 commits Parties to "mobilizing at least 200 billion United States dollars per year" by 2030 from all sources, including raising international flows to developing countries to **$20B/yr by 2025 and $30B/yr by 2030**, and explicitly names "payment for ecosystem services, green bonds, biodiversity offsets and credits, benefit-sharing mechanisms, with environmental and social safeguards" as instruments (Target 19(d)), plus enhancing "collective actions, including by indigenous peoples and local communities" (19(f)) ([CBD Target 19](https://www.cbd.int/gbf/targets/19)). The headline monitoring indicators are D.1 (international public funding) and D.2 (domestic public funding) ([CBD Target 19](https://www.cbd.int/gbf/targets/19)).

**Status 2026:** The EU's own COP17 preparation notes "an encouraging trend in mobilizing finance for biodiversity, while recognizing that there remains an important gap before reaching the target," and the revised Strategy for Resource Mobilization covers a **medium-term phase 2025–2030**, with a full review scheduled for SBI-7 / COP17 ([CBD SBI resource mobilization non-paper](https://www.cbd.int/doc/c/bae7/7a93/d669d08926d5850664e0b7c7/sbi-04-non-paper-item-04a-v2-en.pdf)). The GBF Fund (GBFF), the dedicated financing vehicle, sets portfolio targets of **36%±3% to SIDS/LDCs, 25% through IFIs, and an aspirational 20% by 2030 to IPLCs**, with programming tranches triggered each time a further **$250M** is received ([CBD GBFF page](https://www.cbd.int/financial/gbff.shtml)).

### Cali Fund (DSI benefit-sharing)
The Cali Fund — for benefit-sharing from Digital Sequence Information (DSI) — was operationalized at COP16 (Cali, Oct 2024) and **launched 25 February 2025 in Rome**; it is hosted by the UN Multi-Partner Trust Fund Office and established by UNDP, UNEP and the CBD Secretariat ([CBD Cali Fund launch 2025](https://www.cbd.int/article/cali-fund-launch-2025)). Companies benefiting commercially from DSI are asked to contribute **1% of profits or 0.1% of revenue**, potentially "tens of billions of dollars annually," with **at least 50% allocated to Indigenous peoples and local communities** ([Cali Fund — Wikipedia summary of COP16 Decision 16/2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cali_Fund); [Forward Law Review](https://forwardlawreview.com/2025/02/26/cali-fund-companies-asked-to-pay-into-biodiversity-scheme/)).

**Critical status caveat (2026):** As of the one-year anniversary, the Fund had **received only its first contribution at the end of 2025**; total contributions, number of contributing companies, and disbursement figures are **not stated** ([CBD Cali Fund one-year update, 4 Mar 2026](https://www.cbd.int/article/2026-CaliFund)). Contributions remain **voluntary** and the European Parliament flags that "financing is uncertain and insufficient, and key questions related to governance and benefit-sharing mechanisms have yet to be clarified," risking the credibility of the whole GBF ([European Parliament COP16 follow-up](https://www.europarl.europa.eu/doceo/document/O-10-2026-000017_EN.html)). A stocktaking webinar concluded the "focus has shifted decisively from negotiation to delivery," with COP17 (2026) seen as the milestone to prove the DSI system works in practice ([DSI stocktaking report, Mar 2026](https://geo-media.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Report_DSI_Stocktaking_Webinar.pdf)).

### Biodiversity credits — IAPB & Biodiversity Credit Alliance
The **International Advisory Panel on Biodiversity Credits (IAPB)** launched its Framework for high-integrity biodiversity credit markets in **October 2024 at COP16**, with **30+ pilots** being followed and pilot projects underway in nine countries (France, Gabon, Peru, Ivory Coast, Togo, Chad, India, Cameroon among them) ([IAPB Framework](https://www.iapbiocredits.org/framework); [IAPB LinkedIn Feb 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/international-advisory-panel-on-biodiversity-credits_iapb-financeincommonsummit-southafrica-activity-7301209755073544193-hIyK)). The framework highlights three buyer use cases: **contribution to global goals, insetting, or local-to-local like-for-like offsetting** ([CDC Biodiversité B4B paper](https://www.cdc-biodiversite.fr/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/B4B-CREDITS-MD.pdf)).

The **Biodiversity Credit Alliance (BCA)** is a voluntary international alliance supporting GBF Targets 19(c) and (d). It released its **2025–2026 Strategic Plan** (science-based principles, market governance, IPLC participation), issued **High-Level Principles to Guide the Biodiversity Credit Market** (May 2025), and — most relevant to a receipt/graph design — published a **2026 Issue Paper: "Foundations for a Digitally Native Biodiversity Credit Market: Integrating Digital Trust Infrastructure."** BCA and IAPB have issued a joint "Shared Vision" statement ([BCA homepage, 26 May 2026](https://www.biodiversitycreditalliance.org/); [BCA Strategic Plan](https://www.biodiversitycreditalliance.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/BCA_Strategic_Plan_25-26-v3.pdf); [BCA High-Level Principles](https://www.biodiversitycreditalliance.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/377455_High_Level_Principles_to_Guide_the_Biodiversity_Credit_Market_En_v7_May-2025.pdf)). BCA explicitly names **FPIC as recognized in UNDRIP** as a required principle and envisages a market "underpinned by a digital framework that utilizes innovative technologies" — the closest existing policy voice to an outcome-graph thesis ([BCA homepage](https://www.biodiversitycreditalliance.org/)).

**Trust model:** treaty-anchored (CBD Decisions), with credits governed by voluntary high-level principles + FPIC + third-party validation/verification. **Gap:** no fungible biodiversity metric ("there's no fungible metric for biodiversity benefit" — [The Promise and Pitfalls of Biodiversity Finance panel](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PQM944_UOJ8)); market is "in the early stages of being established" ([BCA homepage](https://www.biodiversitycreditalliance.org/)); Cali Fund contributions still trivial and voluntary.

## 2. TerraMatch / World Resources Institute (WRI)

**What it is:** TerraMatch is WRI's end-to-end platform connecting locally-led restoration projects to capital + technical assistance, then running MRV over six years. It sources projects, builds registries of proposals, finances via associated funds (grants/loans), and tracks progress ([WRI TerraMatch initiative](https://www.wri.org/initiatives/terramatch); [TerraMatch.org](https://www.terramatch.org/)).

**Scale (dated):**
- **Over 200 projects across 30 African countries**, replicating in Brazil and India (via TerraFund for AFR100) ([WRI monitoring at scale](https://www.wri.org/monitoring-locally-led-land-restoration-scale)).
- **Over 240 nonprofit organizations and enterprises** tracking impacts on the platform as of the Feb 2025 update ([WRI update Feb 2025](https://www.wri.org/update/land-degradation-project-recipe-for-restoration)).
- Partnerships: TerraFund (with One Tree Planted, Realize Impact), the Priceless Planet Coalition (Conservation International, WRI, Mastercard; **100M trees** goal), and Harit Bharat Fund (India, 20 orgs) ([TerraMatch.org](https://www.terramatch.org/)).
- **Funding moved/deployed:** total capital figure **n.a.** on fetched pages.

**MRV approach — "4 ingredients":** (1) set science-informed targets across **9 indicators** (trees, hectares, jobs, people benefited, etc.); (2) collect **polygons** (a "virtual fence") via the **Flority** mobile app (built with Wells for Zoë, Malawi); (3) gather **six-monthly field reports**; (4) verify outcomes via a three-tier model — **verify** (AI + satellite independently confirms, e.g., trees grown), **validate** (structured field visits/spot checks, e.g., jobs), **quality assure** (desk review, e.g., beneficiaries) ([WRI monitoring at scale](https://www.wri.org/monitoring-locally-led-land-restoration-scale)). WRI visits each project **three times over five years** ([same](https://www.wri.org/monitoring-locally-led-land-restoration-scale)).

**Technology/cost:** AI object-detection tree counting uses the **DINOv3** model developed with **Meta**; for ~80% of projects trees are counted from high-resolution satellite imagery, with field crews for the remainder ([WRI monitoring at scale](https://www.wri.org/monitoring-locally-led-land-restoration-scale)). Cost reduction of **up to 98%** across the portfolio; AI tree counts at **~1% of the cost** of traditional field measurement ([same](https://www.wri.org/monitoring-locally-led-land-restoration-scale)). Remote-sensing partners: **Land & Carbon Lab, Michigan State University, Maxar** ([WRI update Feb 2025](https://www.wri.org/update/land-degradation-project-recipe-for-restoration)). Project sites are uploaded to **Global Forest Watch via an API script** ([TerraFund MRV PDF](https://afr100.org/sites/default/files/TerraFund%20MRV%20Final%20February%202023%20FINAL%203.6.2023.pdf)).

**Outcome-based finance model (directly relevant to a receipt layer):** WRI is building toward "outcome payers" who **reimburse upfront financiers only for verified social/environmental outcomes** — "No impact, no payment" — against a stated **$278B** restoration finance gap ([WRI monitoring at scale](https://www.wri.org/monitoring-locally-led-land-restoration-scale)).

**Trust model:** WRI as trusted intermediary + independent AI/satellite verification + public dashboard. **API/interop:** GFW API upload exists; a documented open TerraMatch external API for third parties is **n.a.** — data is shared through the platform and a public dashboard. **Gap:** trust centralized in WRI; no per-transaction portable receipt or reversal/challenge ledger surfaced.

Senior Partner: **Clara Barby CBE** ([ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/just-climate-raises-375-million-for-natural-climate-solutions-strategy/)).

**Relevance to a receipt layer:** Just Climate is a natural early partner/anchor investor — it explicitly funds verification-tech and outcome-payment business models.

## 5. Market integrity — crisis and response

### The Verra REDD+ credibility collapse (the cautionary tale)
A January 2023 Guardian/Die Zeit/SourceMaterial investigation found that **over 90% (≈94%) of Verra rainforest REDD+ credits were likely "phantom credits"** with no genuine climate benefit ([Sustainability Atlas 2026](https://sustainableatlas.org/post/trend-watch-voluntary-carbon-market-integrity-icvcm-vcmi-in-2026-signals-winners-3305); [EcoWatch](https://www.ecowatch.com/phantom-credits-verra.html); [IDC-A](https://www.idc-a.org/news/Phantom-Carbon-Offsets-Have-No-Value)). Fallout: **Verra's CEO resigned; Shell, Gucci, EasyJet scaled back offset purchasing**; Verra phased out its legacy REDD+ methodologies, moving to a **consolidated jurisdictional baseline methodology (VM0048)** with shorter (6-year) baseline reassessments and "a serious embrace of digital MRV" ([Sustainability Atlas](https://sustainableatlas.org/post/trend-watch-voluntary-carbon-market-integrity-icvcm-vcmi-in-2026-signals-winners-3305); [Energy Monitor, Verra CEO interview](https://www.energymonitor.ai/carbon-markets/carbon-offsets-guardian-investigation-compares-apples-and-oranges-verra-ceo/)). **Root cause = self-selected, gameable baselines** — directly analogous to the "intention" and "baseline evidence" nodes of an outcome graph being unverifiable.

### ICVCM Core Carbon Principles (status 2026)
ICVCM's initial program assessment completed **late 2025**. As of the assessment-status page (last updated **11 May 2026**), **10 programs are CCP-Eligible** (ACR, ART/TREES, CAR, Equitable Earth, Global Carbon Council, Gold Standard, **Isometric**, Puro.earth, Rainbow, Verified Carbon Standard) and there are **41 CCP-Approved methodologies** ([ICVCM assessment status](https://icvcm.org/assessment-status/)). CCP-Approved nature/removal methodologies now include **VM0047 (ARR), VM0048 (project REDD+), Isometric Reforestation & Mangrove Restoration, ART TREES, and VCS JNR REDD+** — while most legacy renewable-energy and older cookstove methodologies are marked **"Does not meet"** ([ICVCM assessment status](https://icvcm.org/assessment-status/)). Roughly **35% of existing VCM supply** is estimated CCP-eligible; CCP-labeled credits carry a **$3–$8/t premium** (nature-based CCP $12–18/t vs. $4–7/t unlabeled) ([Sustainability Atlas 2026](https://sustainableatlas.org/post/trend-watch-voluntary-carbon-market-integrity-icvcm-vcmi-in-2026-signals-winners-3305)). Notably, ICVCM's own framework requires programs to consult IPs & LCs through **FPIC processes** ([CCP Book V3](https://icvcm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/CCP-Book-V3-FINAL-LowRes-10May24.pdf)).

### VCMI Claims Code of Practice
Finalized **late 2024**; establishes **Silver / Gold / Platinum** corporate-claim tiers (all requiring validated science-based targets + demonstrated direct-reduction progress first). **80+ companies** registered by early 2026 (Unilever, Nestlé, Microsoft, Salesforce) ([Sustainability Atlas 2026](https://sustainableatlas.org/post/trend-watch-voluntary-carbon-market-integrity-icvcm-vcmi-in-2026-signals-winners-3305); [Fastmarkets](https://www.fastmarkets.com/insights/vcmi-seeing-wide-support-for-claims-code-of-practice-most-companies-pursuing-platinum-status/)).

### Article 6.4 PACM status (2026)
The UN's Paris Agreement Crediting Mechanism reached key milestones: interim registry launched Q1 2025; **first methodology approved 30 Oct 2025** (A6.4-AMM-001, landfill-gas flaring/use) plus an investment-analysis (additionality) tool; **10 designated operational entities (DOEs) accredited**; ~20 more methodologies under consideration ([UNFCCC first methodology, 30 Oct 2025](https://unfccc.int/news/un-body-agrees-first-methodology-under-paris-agreement-carbon-market); [UNFCCC CMA2025/12](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/CMA2025_12.pdf)). **First-ever PACM credits issued 26 Feb 2026** — a transitioned CDM project issuing **648,783 tCO₂e** (2018–2025), with credited reductions **~40% lower** than the CDM would have issued due to conservative baselines ([UNFCCC first issuance, 26 Feb 2026](https://unfccc.int/news/un-carbon-market-approves-first-ever-issuance-of-credits-under-the-paris-agreement); [LinkedIn Article 6.4 Takes Off, Mar 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/article-64-takes-off-mexico-pushes-ets-reform-market-design-kolze)). COP30 (Dec 2025) endorsed the foundational standards (baselines, additionality, leakage, suppressed demand, non-permanence/reversals) ([Morgan Lewis COP30](https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2025/12/cop30-key-outcomes-for-carbon-markets)). **~1,500–2,462 CDM projects** are queued to transition ([LinkedIn Mar 2026](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/article-64-takes-off-mexico-pushes-ets-reform-market-design-kolze); [Article 6 Observatory](https://article6observatory.org/article-6-4/)). **Corresponding adjustments** (host-country authorization to avoid double-counting) are now a live supply constraint — Indonesia, PNG, India restricting exports; potential **30–50% VCM supply reduction by 2028** ([Sustainability Atlas 2026](https://sustainableatlas.org/post/trend-watch-voluntary-carbon-market-integrity-icvcm-vcmi-in-2026-signals-winners-3305)).

**Market context:** VCM transacted **~$1.7B in 2025** (down from $2B 2021 peak) ([Sustainability Atlas 2026](https://sustainableatlas.org/post/trend-watch-voluntary-carbon-market-integrity-icvcm-vcmi-in-2026-signals-winners-3305)).

**Relevance:** The entire integrity response is an ex-post, credit-level patch. The unresolved primitives — **verifiable baselines, corresponding-adjustment status, reversal accounting, FPIC** — are exactly the receipt fields an outcome graph would natively carry per-transaction. Rating agencies (Pachama, Sylvera, BeZero) have become "de facto quality filters" precisely because no such native record exists ([Sustainability Atlas 2026](https://sustainableatlas.org/post/trend-watch-voluntary-carbon-market-integrity-icvcm-vcmi-in-2026-signals-winners-3305)).

## 6. Indigenous / local custody and safeguards

**FPIC as codified requirement:**
- **ICVCM's CCP framework** requires programs to consult IPs & LCs and secure **Free, Prior and Informed Consent (FPIC)**, safeguard their rights/interests/values, and provide appropriate benefit-sharing ([CCP Book V3](https://icvcm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/CCP-Book-V3-FINAL-LowRes-10May24.pdf)).
- **BCA** names FPIC "as recognized in UNDRIP" as a required principle for biodiversity credits and emphasizes "meaningful participation and benefits for Indigenous Peoples and local communities" across demand, supply, measurement, and rights ([BCA homepage](https://www.biodiversitycreditalliance.org/); [BCA Strategic Plan](https://www.biodiversitycreditalliance.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/BCA_Strategic_Plan_25-26-v3.pdf)).
- **Cali Fund**: **≥50% of resources to the self-identified needs of IPLCs** — the strongest hard-wired benefit-sharing floor found ([CBD Cali Fund launch](https://www.cbd.int/article/cali-fund-launch-2025)).
- **GBFF**: aspirational **20% by 2030 to IPLC-led actions** ([CBD GBFF](https://www.cbd.int/financial/gbff.shtml)).

**Operational FPIC guidance** exists as detailed protocols: the **Accountability Framework initiative's Operational Guidance on FPIC** (requirements, best practices for companies/supply chains) and national instruments like **PNG's National REDD+ FPIC Guidelines** (protecting customary landholders) ([AFi FPIC guidance](https://accountability-framework.org/fileadmin/uploads/afi/Documents/Operational_Guidance/OG_FPIC-2020-5.pdf); [PNG REDD+ FPIC Guidelines](https://pngreddplus.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/2.-National-REDD-Free-Prior-and-Informed-Consent-Guidelines.pdf)).

**Bioregional / federated / community-led governance examples:**
- **Regen Network**: Indigenous-led credit models via **Fundación Pachamama** and **Amazon Sacred Headwaters Alliance** ("ancestral stewardship credits"), plus a **Community Staking DAO** for federated governance — an existing on-chain model of community authority as a first-class node ([Regen Network](https://www.regen.network/)).
- **Open Forest Protocol**: designed to route value directly to communities, cutting broker intermediaries who leave "local communities and stakeholders … left out of the narrative," via a distributed validator network ([OFP blog](https://www.openforestprotocol.org/blog/opening-forests-with-a-new-standard-of-mrv)).
- **TerraFund/TerraMatch**: "locally-led" model with community consultation embedded in field data collection and beneficiary QA co-defined with local orgs ([WRI monitoring at scale](https://www.wri.org/monitoring-locally-led-land-restoration-scale)).
- **DSI/Cali Fund**: praised for significance to IPLCs but IPLC disbursement mechanics still being defined toward COP17 ([DSI stocktaking report](https://geo-media.de/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Report_DSI_Stocktaking_Webinar.pdf)).

**Gap:** FPIC is required in principle everywhere but is a **document/attestation, not a verifiable, portable, revocable record** with a local-veto primitive. No fetched system implements FPIC as a challengeable/reversible receipt node. This is a differentiated design opening (community authority + challenge/reversal in one).

## 7. NVIDIA Earth-2 (state 2026)

**What it is:** Earth-2 is NVIDIA's climate/weather **digital-twin platform** (data → AI surrogate models → Omniverse visualization). Announced March 2024; a **~50-FTE, community-driven program** working with NOAA, ECMWF, NASA — explicitly **not** aiming to be a forecasting org itself but to provide deployable frameworks ([WCFC 2026 talk by Stan Posey](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a3FMjhUAlAk)).

**Model family (2026):** **CorrDiff** (generative-AI km-scale downscaling — up to **500x faster, 10,000x more energy-efficient** than CPU NWP), **FourCastNet3** (medium-range global, ~60x faster), **Nowcasting, Global Data Assimilation, cBottle** ("Climate in a Bottle," first generative-AI global km-scale climate foundation model), and the **PhysicsNeMo** training framework ([TechRadar, Jan 2026](https://www.techradar.com/pro/nvidias-next-gen-full-earth-2-digital-twin-ai-models-will-predict-and-forecast-the-next-big-storm-faster-and-more-accurately); [NVIDIA cBottle blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/earth2-generative-ai-foundation-model-global-climate-kilometer-scale-resolution/)).

**Who uses it (2026):** Taiwan Central Weather Administration, The Weather Company, **G42** (UAE National Center of Meteorology), JBA Risk Management, Spire, Israel Meteorological Service, and energy firms **TotalEnergies, Eni, GCL** (grid/PV forecasting); research: **Max-Planck-Institute for Meteorology** (km-scale ICON), **Allen Institute for AI (Ai2)**, ECMWF; geospatial: **Esri (ArcGIS), Ecopia, OroraTech, Tomorrow.io** ([TechRadar Jan 2026](https://www.techradar.com/pro/nvidias-next-gen-full-earth-2-digital-twin-ai-models-will-predict-and-forecast-the-next-big-storm-faster-and-more-accurately); [NVIDIA Earth-2 Blueprint](https://resources.nvidia.com/en-us-climate/nvidia-earth-2-climate-tech-weather-prediction); [NVIDIA cBottle blog](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/earth2-generative-ai-foundation-model-global-climate-kilometer-scale-resolution/)). Open models available on Hugging Face / GitHub ([TechRadar Jan 2026](https://www.techradar.com/pro/nvidias-next-gen-full-earth-2-digital-twin-ai-models-will-predict-and-forecast-the-next-big-storm-faster-and-more-accurately)).

**Does anyone link forecasts to financed interventions?** Adoption is concentrated in **weather agencies, insurance/risk (JBA), and energy** — climate-risk and disaster management. **No fetched source shows Earth-2 forecasts being tied to financed restoration/nature interventions or to MRV/credit systems.** The nearest adjacency is an insurance MGA using Earth-2 to "develop climate-aware products" ([LinkedIn, Aug 2025](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/sie-yin-poh_nvidia-ai-climatetech-activity-7366779461624578048-uZsm)). **This is a clear white space** — using forecast digital twins to predict reversal risk (fire, drought, flood) on financed restoration sites and feed the "challenge/reversal" node.

## 8. AI agents in conservation finance

**Rating/due-diligence layer (closest to "compressing discovery + due diligence"):**
- **Sylvera**: independent carbon-credit ratings + pre-issuance assessments; **300+ rated projects**, screenings across **~200 projects / 10 types** (REDD to biochar), neutral third-party assurance to compress diligence and disqualify weak projects early ([Sylvera Evaluate](https://www.sylvera.com/evaluate)). *(Explicit AI/ML method not stated on the fetched page.)*
- **BeZero Carbon**: carbon-credit ratings agency; adopted by buyers (Wren) and investors (**Mirova signed framework agreements with both BeZero and Sylvera, Feb 2026**) to strengthen quality diligence ([BeZero LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/bezero-carbon_wren-is-a-climate-platform-that-helps-individuals-activity-7475220107972165632-_bU2); [Mirova, Feb 2026](https://www.mirova.com/en/news/mirova-signs-framework-agreements-bezero-carbon-and-sylvera-further-strengthen-quality-US)).
- **Pachama**: AI + satellite; "conduct deep diligence in days, not months," screening hundreds of projects for baselines, leakage, social impacts, viability ([Pachama LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/pachama)) — the most explicitly "agentic" diligence-compression claim found.
- Both Sylvera and BeZero are named "essential infrastructure … functioning as de facto quality filters" using "satellite imagery, remote sensing, and machine learning" ([Sustainability Atlas 2026](https://sustainableatlas.org/post/trend-watch-voluntary-carbon-market-integrity-icvcm-vcmi-in-2026-signals-winners-3305)).

**Discovery / science layer:**
- **Restor** (Crowther Lab / ETH Zürich origin — see caveat): open-data geospatial platform connecting projects to "data, funding, and each other." **109,000 sites listed since June 2021** (11,400+ public); built on **global scientific models**; uses **Google Earth Engine** data ([Restor year-in-review, dated June 2022 content](https://about.restor.eco/blog/a-year-of-restor-ing); [Google Cloud on Restor](https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/sustainability/how-restor-is-using-google-earth-engine-data-to-tackle-ecosystem-restoration)). **Explicitly "does not validate projects," is "descriptive, not prescriptive"** — a data commons, not a verifier ([Restor About](https://restor.eco/about/)). Could be used for biodiversity credits ([TriplePundit](https://triplepundit.com/2024/restor-land-restoration/)). *(Restor's page did not itself state the Crowther Lab/ETH link; that connection is documented via Crowther Lab and third-party sources — [Crowther Lab on Restor](https://crowtherlab.com/restors-public-database-offers-new-insights-into-the-restoration-movement/).)*
- **Crowther Lab** (ETH Zürich): scientific backbone of restoration data / global forest models ([Crowther Lab restoration](https://crowtherlab.com/restoration/)).

**Gap:** AI is used heavily for **rating and monitoring** and increasingly for **diligence compression**, but no fetched source describes autonomous **agents** orchestrating the *full* discovery→diligence→monitoring→capital loop, nor agents that write to a shared receipt/outcome ledger. Rating agencies are trusted third parties, not open graphs — their outputs are proprietary scores, not portable receipts.

## (a) Where the capital→outcome pipe currently breaks

Mapped to the outcome-graph nodes (identity → intention → resource debit → community authority → evidence → capital → witnessed outcome → challenge/reversal → learning):

| Pipe stage / graph node | Current state | Where it breaks | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Identity** (who acts) | Project developers self-register on registries/platforms (TerraMatch, Regen, OFP, Verra Hub) | No shared cross-registry identity; a developer/site has different IDs in every system; no portable actor identity | [WRI](https://www.wri.org/monitoring-locally-led-land-restoration-scale); [Regen](https://www.regen.network/) |
| **Intention / baseline** | Baselines set per methodology; self-selected baselines were the REDD+ failure mode | Gameable, non-comparable baselines; VM0048/jurisdictional fixes are ex-post patches | [Sustainability Atlas](https://sustainableatlas.org/post/trend-watch-voluntary-carbon-market-integrity-icvcm-vcmi-in-2026-signals-winners-3305); [Energy Monitor](https://www.energymonitor.ai/carbon-markets/carbon-offsets-guardian-investigation-compares-apples-and-oranges-verra-ceo/) |
| **Resource debit** (capital committed) | Funds (Cali Fund, GBFF, Just Climate, TerraFund) commit capital | Cali Fund has ~1 contribution; commitments not linked to specific outcomes at transaction level | [CBD Cali Fund](https://www.cbd.int/article/2026-CaliFund); [ESG Today](https://www.esgtoday.com/just-climate-raises-375-million-for-natural-climate-solutions-strategy/) |
| **Community authority (FPIC / veto)** | FPIC required by ICVCM, BCA, Cali Fund; operational guidance exists | FPIC is a document/attestation, not a verifiable, revocable, challengeable record; no local veto primitive | [CCP Book](https://icvcm.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/CCP-Book-V3-FINAL-LowRes-10May24.pdf); [AFi FPIC](https://accountability-framework.org/fileadmin/uploads/afi/Documents/Operational_Guidance/OG_FPIC-2020-5.pdf) |
| **Evidence (MRV)** | Rich but fragmented: eDNA (NatureMetrics), biomass (Chloris), habitat maps (Space Intelligence), tree counts (WRI/DINOv3), imagery (Planet) | No common evidence schema; each provider's data is proprietary/siloed; no fungible biodiversity metric | [Space Intelligence](https://www.space-intelligence.com/); [Chloris](https://www.chloris.earth/carbonmarkets); [WRI](https://www.wri.org/monitoring-locally-led-land-restoration-scale) |
| **Capital ↔ outcome binding** | Isometric (buyer-paid, tonne-level certs) and WRI outcome-payment ("no impact, no payment") are the frontier | Still registry-specific; certificates not portable across a planetary graph; most capital pays for effort not verified outcome | [Climate Decode/Isometric](https://climate-decode.com/insights/vcm-series/vcm-2026-era-of-integrity/isometric-science-first-removals-registry); [WRI](https://www.wri.org/monitoring-locally-led-land-restoration-scale) |
| **Witnessed outcome** (independent) | VVBs, rating agencies (Sylvera/BeZero), satellite verifiers | Verification is third-party-trusted, slow, expensive, and not a shared open record; ratings are proprietary scores | [Sylvera](https://www.sylvera.com/evaluate); [Sustainability Atlas](https://sustainableatlas.org/post/trend-watch-voluntary-carbon-market-integrity-icvcm-vcmi-in-2026-signals-winners-3305) |
| **Challenge / reversal** | Buffer pools (Isometric 2% OAE; Verra 20% pilot withholding), non-permanence standards (Article 6.4) | Reversal handled as pooled insurance, not per-receipt challengeable events; forecast-driven reversal-risk (Earth-2) not linked to financed sites | [Carbon to Sea/Isometric](https://www.carbontosea.org/2025/11/25/an-independent-mrv-review-of-the-first-oae-credits/); [Verra DMRV](https://verra.org/verra-approves-first-credits-under-dmrv-pilot-for-high-frequency-issuances/); [UNFCCC](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/CMA2025_12.pdf) |
| **Learning** (feedback) | WRI publishes public dashboard + guidebook; registries publish reports | Learning is per-program, not a shared graph; failures don't propagate cross-system | [WRI](https://www.wri.org/monitoring-locally-led-land-restoration-scale) |
| **Interop / portability** | GFW API, Chloris API, Verra Project Hub | No shared receipt schema; no cross-registry identity/outcome graph; blockchain rails (Regen/OFP) are isolated islands | [TerraFund MRV](https://afr100.org/sites/default/files/TerraFund%20MRV%20Final%20February%202023%20FINAL%203.6.2023.pdf); [Regen](https://www.regen.network/); [OFP](https://commons.opencivics.co/Open-Forest-Protocol-OFP-2b006d2570f280e7abe5eaa4e5ae3875) |

**The single biggest break:** there is **no portable, per-transaction record binding identity + community authority + capital + evidence + reversal that travels across registries, funds, and MRV providers.** Every system re-implements a partial, non-interoperable receipt. Integrity councils (ICVCM/VCMI) patch this at the credit level ex-post; rating agencies arbitrage it as a paid service.

## (b) Partners vs. competitors for a receipt / outcome-graph layer

| Entity | Partner or Competitor | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Biodiversity Credit Alliance / IAPB** | **Partner (strategic)** | Already calling for a "digitally native biodiversity credit market" with "digital trust infrastructure" and FPIC/UNDRIP baked in — a policy sponsor for a receipt schema ([BCA](https://www.biodiversitycreditalliance.org/)) |
| **CBD / Cali Fund / GBFF** | **Partner (demand + legitimacy)** | Treaty-level demand ($200B Target 19) + IPLC benefit-sharing floors; a graph could serve as their missing transparency/monitoring layer ([CBD](https://www.cbd.int/gbf/targets/19)) |
| **WRI TerraMatch** | **Partner (with overlap risk)** | Best-in-class field-to-satellite MRV + outcome-payment model + public data; overlaps on "witnessed outcome" but lacks portable receipts/identity — complementary if the graph consumes its data via API ([WRI](https://www.wri.org/monitoring-locally-led-land-restoration-scale)) |

| **Regen Network** | **Partner or Competitor (coopetition)** | Already implements immutable, community-verified, protocol-based "proof-of-impact" ecocredits with a DAO — the closest existing receipt/graph; could be a rail to build on **or** a direct competitor to a new graph ([Regen](https://www.regen.network/)) |

| **Verra / Gold Standard** | **Partner (incumbent registries) + Competitor** | Their DMRV pilots and Project Hubs are moving toward digital, high-frequency, machine-readable issuance — could adopt a receipt schema or build a rival; interop essential to avoid duplication ([Verra DMRV](https://verra.org/verra-approves-first-credits-under-dmrv-pilot-for-high-frequency-issuances/)) |
| **ICVCM / VCMI** | **Partner (standards)** | Define integrity + claims rules a graph should encode; not a builder — a standards partner ([ICVCM](https://icvcm.org/assessment-status/)) |
| **Article 6.4 PACM / UNFCCC** | **Partner (compliance rail)** | Emerging UN registry with corresponding-adjustment + reversal standards; a graph interoperating here gains compliance-grade legitimacy ([UNFCCC](https://unfccc.int/news/un-carbon-market-approves-first-ever-issuance-of-credits-under-the-paris-agreement)) |
| **Pachama** | **Competitor (adjacent) or Partner** | Building a registry-integrated DMRV data platform + diligence-compression; overlaps on "witnessed outcome" and "learning"; could partner as evidence supplier or compete as the graph ([Pachama](https://pachama.com/blog/building-the-modern-carbon-market-the-planet-demands/?download_pdf=1443)) |
| **Sylvera / BeZero** | **Competitor (functionally)** | They occupy the "de facto quality filter" role a receipt graph would disintermediate; proprietary scores vs. open receipts — most direct competitors for trust capture ([Sustainability Atlas](https://sustainableatlas.org/post/trend-watch-voluntary-carbon-market-integrity-icvcm-vcmi-in-2026-signals-winners-3305)) |
| **Restor / Crowther Lab** | **Partner (discovery + science)** | Open data commons + global models for the "discovery" node; explicitly does not validate — no competitive overlap on verification/receipts ([Restor](https://restor.eco/about/)) |
| **NVIDIA Earth-2** | **Partner (reversal-risk feed)** | Digital-twin forecasts (CorrDiff/cBottle) could power the "challenge/reversal" node's predictive fire/drought/flood risk on financed sites — currently unlinked to finance = greenfield partnership ([NVIDIA](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/earth2-generative-ai-foundation-model-global-climate-kilometer-scale-resolution/)) |

**Positioning takeaway:** Build **connective tissue, not another registry.** The strongest wedge is the **portable receipt binding community authority (FPIC-as-revocable-record) + capital + evidence + reversal across existing rails.** Partner with evidence suppliers (NatureMetrics, Chloris, Space Intelligence, Planet, WRI), standards bodies (BCA/IAPB, ICVCM, PACM), and capital (Just Climate, Cali Fund/GBFF); treat rating agencies (Sylvera, BeZero) and closed registries as the incumbents to disintermediate on transparency; and treat Regen/OFP/Isometric as either rails to build on or the nearest philosophical competitors depending on chain/schema choices. NVIDIA Earth-2 is an untapped partner for the reversal node.
