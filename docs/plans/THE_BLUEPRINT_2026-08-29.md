## Part IX — The Convergence Rubric

### Ten gates for the single thing

A shared instrument so any two architects — human or AI — converge mechanically instead of rhetorically. Per the Keel: each gate is PASS / FAIL / N-A-with-written-reason, evidence required (a file:line or a runnable command), and no aggregate score — one FAIL means not yet. Reviewers score independently; a disagreement becomes a named, evidence-cited dispute the operator adjudicates. Apply it to any proposed architecture, mission, or admission.

1. **NAME** — The whole fits in one sentence a stranger understands; every component traces to a clause of it.
2. **LOOP** — Every component names the real-world loop it closes (the One Law). Can't name it → lab or archive.
3. **OWNER** — Every truth — state, decision, name, fitness — has exactly one writer. Owner count does not grow with features.
4. **REALITY** — At least one fitness source the system cannot edit. Self-manufactured signals never count as fitness.
5. **ORACLE** — Nothing grades its own work: fresh verifiers, decorrelated evaluators, human judgment un-fakeable.
6. **RECEIPT** — No receipt, it didn't happen. Claims are typed; no component can promote its own status.
7. **SOVEREIGN** — Promotion, capital, and outward publication terminate at the operator; the algedonic channel bypasses everything.
8. **SUBTRACT** — Every merge leaves the system smaller or more real. No grandfathering. The line-count ratchet only goes down.
9. **DEMO** — One canonical end-to-end run, started by the operator on demand, exercising every organ. What it doesn't exercise isn't kernel.
10. **REGROW** — Essence lives in constraints, not nouns: delete any module and the constitution forces its regrowth. Metaphor constrains behavior; it never mints a class.

### Ruling: the catalyzing mission, scored

Foundry-via-RSI as the single first mission: PASS with one amendment. It passes REALITY, ORACLE, RECEIPT, SOVEREIGN, and LOOP by design — the measured machinery already embodies them. It currently fails OWNER (two unjoined fitness authorities — the M3 weld fixes exactly this). And it fails SUBTRACT if the objective is "recruit as many modules as possible" — so the objective is amended: the mission recruits every organ, every name, and the whole constitution — never module count. The essence is the invariant (generate → gate → aggregate → promote, closed through reality, witnessed honestly), per the eigenform axiom: identity is the fixed point, not the substrate. Modules that the ring does not exercise become archive; anything truly needed regrows from the constitution (gate 10).
