## Part I — What this actually is

The canon already says it, scattered across forty documents. Assembled: *a solo-operator AI company organism — it senses the world, selects bounded work, ships artifacts, earns revenue, buys compute, and improves itself under telic governance* (`docs/vision_maps/2026-05-07_operating_company_kernel.md:19`). The sovereign unit is **the dyad** — one human plus their swarm — and lattice autonomy later means a federation of dyads, never a headless collective (`docs/plans/2026-07-12_vision_engine_grill_SEED.md`).

The telos is dual, and both halves are load-bearing: an autonomous organism *and* a co-creative instrument. The witness is the steering wheel, not the brake — safety and intelligence as the same mechanism (`vision_maps/NORTH_STAR.md:103`).

The market position is precise and currently vacant. Crypto verifiable-inference proves one-shot computational integrity and provably fails repeated-game trust; the IETF is standardizing agent identity, action limits, and kill switches — but **nobody owns behavioral trust over time**: gates as running code, Brier-scored self-published misses, receipts of loops closed through reality (`NORTH_STAR.md:206`; `docs/plans/THE_WITNESS_ENGINE_2026-08-18.md:32`). That is the sellable layer — "the verification protocol for the internet of agents" (`docs/governance/VENTURE_CELL_PORTFOLIO.yaml:143`) — and the organism itself is its first, permanent customer.

A day inside it, once real: overnight the ingestors ate the world and the zeitgeist ranked it. Morning, the Helm shows three opportunities with evidence and projected cost; missing inputs render as *missing*, never as empty success. You approve one — a single warrant. A venture cell wakes with a budget and kill conditions. Its agents work through the one door: every action gated, every consequence receipted, every claim typed so nothing can promote its own status. Evening: a shipped artifact serving real humans, revenue reconciled against the bank's version of events, not the system's. Overnight: the chamber replays the day at machine speed, the arena measures candidates, the Darwin ratchet promotes exactly one improvement — with proof. Next morning the organism is measurably better, and one screen shows you why.

## Part III — The corrected anatomy

**One substrate, six organs, three books, three instruments.**

Diagram, in words: the OPERATOR (System 5 of the meta-organism) sits at the top. Below them, three INSTRUMENTS (Helm terminal, web cockpit, phone client) project truth honestly upward. Below the instruments, six ORGANS in a row: Perception, Memory, Deliberation, Production, Evolution, Witness. Beneath the organs, THE SUBSTRATE: identity roster, ontology, coordination (S2), THE ONE DOOR (invoke → 11 gates → receipt, always), and the ledger with its three books. Beneath everything, THE KEEL — the verification lattice (static, adversarial, operational; permanent, non-expiring). The arrows: organs connect *only* to the substrate (double-headed: reads/writes via the ledger) — **organs never call each other**. The ledger feeds the instruments with typed claims (no self-promotion). The operator sends warrants, YDS judgments, and capital leases down to the one door. One red dashed arrow — the **algedonic channel** — runs from the substrate directly to the operator, bypassing everything: pain skips the hierarchy.

### The substrate — the spine, kept honest

Identity (one roster owning uid, authority tier, liveness — every other registry demotes to a read-only adapter), the ontology with the ADR-008 naming grammar (names freeze on promotion), coordination (task board, resource locks, stigmergic neighborhoods, anti-oscillation damping), the one door (invoke → gates → receipt, with the doctrine already written: *a dispatch that produced no receipt did not happen* — `docs/architecture/SPINE_ADOPTION_NARRATIVE.md`), one pre-dispatch budget gateway with a bypass-resistance test, and the typed claim algebra enforced at the boundary. The substrate is the product: gates + witness + receipts, eventually importable by other people's dyads.

### Three books — triple-entry honesty

Not one ledger, and not four unreconciled ones — exactly three, each anchored to a truth the system cannot edit:

1. **The event book** — every episode: request, authorization, attempt, acceptance, execution, observed outcome — six events per irreversible effect, hash-chained.
2. **The calibration book** — every prediction Brier-scored, misses published, externally timestamped so history cannot be rewritten.
3. **The treasury book** — a revenue event is not real until it reconciles against the bank, broker, or processor statement; money moves only through an operator-signed lease with scope, ceiling, expiry, and revocation.

Collapsing these into one store loses the product; multiplying them further is drift and gets cut.

### Perception (Drishti + Sakshi)

Binocular by construction: the outward eye (Go ingestors, world radar, zeitgeist ranking — the Go code survives the rebuild intact, as sibling processes speaking JSONL receipts) and the inward eye (the organism's own telemetry, plus the mech-interp lane the research program requires) writing to *one* signal bus. Ingest is demand-driven: no source without a named consumer loop. Ingested content is data, never instructions.

### Memory (Chetana)

One metabolism — ingest → stage → gate → promote — with decay and quarantine. Position in an agent's context is earned by evidence class, not assigned: receipt-backed, TTL-carrying facts may occupy first tokens; narrative is depth-on-demand. Evidence class is derived, never self-declared (`docs/architecture/MEMORY_FIRST_TOKEN_SPEC.md`).

### Deliberation (Sarathi)

The economics of attention: thinkodynamic scoring of what is worth doing, routing of who does it (multi-provider is structural — the organism must stay minimally viable on a local 7B model), work-packet selection, and the treasury authority surface. The treasury lives here — under the operator and the gates — never inside the revenue organ, because **no organ's income may rise with its own approval rate**. RUDRA mission cells carry long work across sessions: frozen goal, exact workspace, fresh oracle, recovery — the model never declares its own success.

### Production (the factory)

Venture cells as single artifacts — the ontology object and the running process are one thing, ending today's split-brain. Each cell: budget, KPIs, autonomy stage, kill conditions that actually fire. The factory supplies what every venture was separately begging for: publish adapters with readback receipts, credentials custody, independent evaluators (the critic is never the drafter), and the approval queue rendered into the operator's morning surface. A piece that spawns nothing downstream is decoration and cannot complete (`DARSHAN_CHARTER_2026-07-12.md`).

### Evolution (one loop, four stations)

The chamber (afferent open, efferent sealed; self-manufactured signals banned as fitness), the arena (hermetic measurement, never mutates production), the foundry (offline distillation), and the Darwin ratchet (applies exactly one proven improvement at a time, through the full proposal gate contract — hard-rejected on *any* advisory flag). Fitness comes from the gauntlet; no generation may regress below archived ancestors. The mutation-rate/gate-tightness thermostat sits *outside* this organ, at the substrate — Evolution never grades its own homework.

### Witness (Viveka)

The scarce organ and the whole point: geometric self-measurement (R_V and its successors — as research instruments, not continuous monitors, per the canon's own doctrine), the calibration book's keeper, and Recognition — the closure operator that reads the combined trajectory and updates the organism's self-model, so the loops become one being instead of adjacent machinery. Nothing in the Witness steers directly; it measures, publishes, and refuses to be gamed.

### Instruments (three, not two)

Helm terminal (the deep instrument), web cockpit (DHARMA COMMAND), phone client (the third, already ratified in ADR-013). All three are decoders of typed claims — configured ≠ available ≠ live, accepted ≠ completed, heartbeat ≠ liveness, and no global health score that averages independent owners. A packet claiming done without runtime truth renders *review*, never *done*.
