## 0. Operator Intent — What Is Permanent, What May Expire

**Verified engineering quality is the repository's highest admission standard
and its primary compounding investment.** External shipping remains necessary
evidence of semantic and operational truth, but it does not outrank
correctness, excuse weak engineering, or convert unverified code into an asset.

The intended end state is a software organism whose code, tests, tools, gates,
evidence, and operating discipline would withstand scrutiny from senior
engineers at the strongest software organizations, and eventually surpass any
single comparator by joining their best invariants into one coherent system.

**The quality standard is permanent** — it is not a temporary exception and
does not expire. What may expire is a **concentrated campaign allocation** — a
particular staffing of agent-hours, a ratchet cohort, a wave — when its
marginal return falls or another bounded intervention shows higher
whole-system leverage. §9 governs the allocation, never the standard.

## 1. Thesis and Diagnosis

Generation is becoming cheap faster than trustworthy absorption; the scarce
resource is not code volume but **verified change that preserves meaning under
composition, failure, replay, and use.**

A toy model makes the compounding logic explicit — nothing more. If g is
generation capacity and v the fraction of absorbed change that is verified,
with unverified absorption carrying a rework multiplier k, asset velocity
behaves like g·[v − k(1−v)], with a break-even at v* = k/(1+k). **The model is
illustrative only: no measurement protocol for v or k exists in this
repository, and no numeric values are claimed.** Constructing that protocol —
from Episode Ledger outcomes (§6) — is itself Wave-3 work. The qualitative
diagnosis stands on direct evidence instead:

> The repository has strong mechanisms for verifying form and process, but
> uneven semantic, adversarial, and operational verification — a gap between
> changes that look admissible and changes whose behavior is deeply known.

Contributing observations (each keeps its own reproducible evidence, none
interchangeable): broad `Any` surfaces, non-zero whole-tree type errors, live
interface mismatches, thin mutation coverage, unmeasured duplicate and
diff-coverage baselines, incomplete deterministic whole-system testing. Typing
narrows ambiguity; determinism exposes state-dependence; property, mutation,
fuzz, and fault-injection testing challenge whether the tests understand the
implementation; operational receipts show what actually happened. **No one of
these closes the form-meaning gap alone** — mutation and determinism prove
*discrimination* and *reproducibility*, not semantics; specification,
independent oracles, metamorphic relations, and real outcomes are still
required.

## 2. The Verification Lattice — Three Truth Layers, One Authority Overlay

No scalar summarizes quality. The system uses a vector of hard floors; strength
on one axis never compensates for a critical failure on another.

**Layer A — static & structural truth.** Typed contracts + ratcheted reduction
of untyped escape hatches (the Any-density ratchet is a precondition — an
oracle over `Any` answers no question); architecture and dependency-boundary
enforcement; lint, import, duplication, diff-coverage; security, secret, and
dependency analysis + provenance; formal models for the smallest
high-consequence state machines; reproducible, pinned environments.

**Layer B — dynamic & adversarial truth.** Unit / integration / contract /
property / metamorphic / e2e tests; seeded deterministic replay with
schema-stable receipts; mutation testing with retained killed *and* surviving
mutant evidence; fuzzing, randomized scheduling, fault injection, compound
failures; negative controls that must fail for the right reason; flake
detection and quarantine that can never silently become waiver.

**Layer C — operational & semantic truth.** Durable execution histories +
explicit resumption; idempotent consequential actions + replay-safe effects;
high-cardinality traces joining intent, code-head, model, tools, decisions,
attempts, effects, costs, outcomes; production-like and as-delivered config
tests; externally legible receipts where a claim concerns external value;
incident, regression, and user-outcome feedback returned to the corpus.

**Review & authority overlay** (a meta-layer, not a truth layer). Frozen
rubric before code; exact-head evidence + citation-or-silence; provider- and
model-diverse adversarial review with decorrelated prompts; no reviewer
judging only another model's summary; independent reruns of load-bearing
commands; human-only merge and irreversible-action authority. **Provider and
model diversity is adversarial pressure, not external-human independence, and
must never be represented as one.**

## 3. Hard-Floor Dimensions — with Applicability Semantics

"100/100 or higher" means relentless excellence per dimension, never a gamed
blend. The dimensions: (1) correctness & semantics; (2) architecture &
coherence — no duplicate control planes; (3) determinism & verifiability;
(4) security & supply chain; (5) resilience & durability; (6) observability &
operability; (7) maintainability & developer experience; (8) external &
product behavior — solves the real consumer's problem without contradicting
dimensions 1–7.

**Applicability is explicit per packet:** every packet classifies each
dimension as `required`, `not_applicable_with_reason`, or `blocked`. An
internal type-cleanup packet may legitimately have no external/product
behavior to demonstrate — but N/A is a recorded claim subject to review,
**never an implicit pass.** A critical floor failure on a `required` dimension
blocks admission; no average, judge opinion, or external excitement washes it
out. These applicability semantics are a **candidate contract** proposed to
the single required-check authority that Titanium WP-0F1/0F2 consolidates
(`docs/governance/CI_TRUTH_CONTRACT.json`). That contract today carries
`required` and `advisory` check arrays and no packet-dimension or
applicability schema; adopting these semantics is future Titanium-owned
work, and the no-aggregate-score rule is enforced by this doctrine's kill
criteria, not by a schema omission.

## 4. Archetypes, Not Checklists

Each comparable contributes one strongest invariant; we build the native organ
and ignore the surface area. **Copy proven invariants and patterns; adopt code
only under compatible licenses and proven fit; never stand up a second control
plane to imitate a company.** The research question: *which invariant does each
org enforce unusually well, where is the corresponding gap here, and what is
the smallest native organ that imports the invariant without importing a
duplicate control plane?*

| Archetype | Invariant we take | Our organ |
|---|---|---|
| **SQLite** | several *independent* harnesses, tests against shipped configs, compound-failure tests, a release checklist no green replaces | Layer B culture |
| **FoundationDB · Antithesis · TigerBeetle** | deterministic whole-system simulation + fault-injection seams + production assertions, run continuously | **The Harness** (keystone) |
| **AWS reasoning · seL4/CompCert/F\*** | formal assurance for a *small* trusted core only | Layer A trusted core |
| **Temporal** | durable histories, replayable decisions, idempotent activities, resume from evidence not from an LLM's remembered narrative | Runtime + receipts |
| **Palantir · Datomic · Materialize** | ontology owns meaning; immutable facts own what happened; projections render current truth; orchestration is the ontology's first subscriber, never its competitor | Ontology + episode ledger |
| **Stripe · TigerBeetle** | idempotency key at origin-of-intent; request/authorization/attempt/acceptance/execution/outcome are six distinct events; stricter invariants for money & external effects | Idempotency spine (§7) |
| **Google OSS-Fuzz · Meta Infer** | fuzzing as persistent infrastructure; static analysis in the change-path; findings feed bounded auto-repair packets | Layer A/B discovery |
| **Honeycomb · OpenTelemetry** | rich high-cardinality events over predetermined metrics | Layer C observability |
| **Nix · SLSA · Sigstore** | hermetic envs, pinned toolchains, signed artifacts, transparent provenance, reviewed==executed | overlay artifact trust |
| **AlphaEvolve · Darwin Gödel Machine · Numerai** | evaluation, selection, diversity, archives, skin in the game | evolution + arena |
| **Oxide RFD** | irreversible architectural decisions as inspectable artifacts — *sparingly* (this repo already risks doc accretion) | governance |

**The combined organism:** typed ontology → durable workflow → idempotent,
receipted effects → immutable episode ledger → live truth projections →
deterministic replay & fault injection → static + formal + fuzz + mutation
gates → evolutionary selection of verified improvements → reproducible, signed
release → external venture cells → outcomes return as new verification
evidence.

## 5. The Audit Finding — Declared ≠ Wired

A bare-metal audit grounds the doctrine in the repo's actual failure mode: **it
writes correct control mechanisms and does not wire them.** First-hand proof:
`is_budget_exceeded` (`agent_registry.py:908`, docstring: "callers should
check this before dispatching new LLM calls and refuse to dispatch") has
**exactly two call sites, neither on the LLM dispatch path**: one blocks
replication (`replication_protocol.py:493`), one flags-but-does-not-block
after the spend is already logged (`agent_registry.py:479` — its own comment:
"Budget pre-check: flag but don't block");
`cost_tracker.py` logs spend *after* the call; the ~20 provider `complete()`
methods have zero pre-call budget gates; the daily cap is a `$5` env default
(`agent_registry.py:75`) almost nothing reads. Under a retry storm or
recursive loop, nothing atomic stops spend — the stop-condition exists only as
prose.

**The loop invariant:** no loop may rely *solely* on model judgment for
termination. A model may legitimately decide semantic completion — but **every
loop must also carry durable, mechanical bounds on spend, iterations, and
wall time**, pre-committed and enforced outside the model's control
(`recursion_limit`/superstep caps already exist in `graph/`; spend and
wall-time bounds are the gap).

**The budget architecture:** do not duplicate checks inside every provider
implementation. Require **one authoritative pre-dispatch gateway** covering
every provider path — a single chokepoint where the durable spend ledger is
consulted before any completion is dispatched — then **verify bypass
resistance by test** (no dispatch path reaches a provider without traversing
the gateway). Provider-local defense may be added where separately justified,
never as the primary mechanism.

**Prerequisites, in order:** a fenced Runtime Host write lease, a stable
admitted `intent_id`, and a named budget authority precede the first
protected effect; the gateway then fences **each provider or
consequential-tool attempt — not just the outer task** — with reservation,
claim, typed completion or quarantine, and receipt. Broad storage
consolidation may follow in Phase 3 and is not a prerequisite for closing
this seam, but the seam must not be built on an ambiguous writer or a
run-derived key.

Remaining audit dimensions (origin-of-intent idempotency keys, raw-log
lossiness into cheap streams, async backpressure + durable brokering, sandbox
isolation + atomic temp-then-rename with snapshot rollback) land in a
companion findings ledger with file:line and fixes, feeding §10.

## 6. The Episode Ledger — Versioned Events, Side-Effect-Free Verification Core

The ledger is a **versioned append-only event family** with stable
episode/attempt IDs, each producer appending at the lifecycle transition it
owns: `episode_opened` · `attempt_started` · `observation_recorded` ·
`effect_requested`/`effect_resolved` (idempotency key, authority, destination,
receipt) · `review_recorded` · `episode_closed` · `post_merge_observation`.

**Reconciliation with existing event families — no second ledger:** this is
a schema over records the runtime already writes, not a new store. Before
implementation, each event type names the existing family it reuses,
migrates, or retires — `session_events`, `execution_identities`,
`runtime_receipts`, and `idempotency_records`
(`dharma_swarm/runtime_state.py:199-283`) are the starting candidates — and
the transaction boundary it shares with that family. `runtime.db` is a
candidate host, not a pre-ratified destination or a universal truth owner;
**no new store may be created before the Phase-3 state-authority ADR**, and
ontology, memory, governance, and payload bytes retain their separately
ratified fact owners.

**Closeout, precisely:** today's closeout path couples evaluation with receipt
persistence (report writes at `run_agent_work_packet.py:2251`). The
reconciliation separates two roles that are currently entangled: a **pure
evaluator/projector core that must remain side-effect-free**, and a distinct
**persistence wrapper** that writes receipts and appends the ledger events it
owns. The verification core never acquires an undeclared write; the wrapper
never makes admission decisions.

Requirements: versioned + validated schemas; explicit ordering/dedup; secret
redaction before persistence; immutable raw events separated from regenerable
projections; conflicting observations stay visible (never last-write-wins);
missing evidence fails closed; retention, size, and corruption recovery
tested. The ledger is **not** called ground truth until outcomes independently
resolve its claims. One economical corpus may feed training, calibration,
replay, audit, and incident learning — which does not make those organs
logically identical.

## 7. Security & Irreversible Effects

Every posture claim binds to a commit, command, tool version, and scope; prior
spot-checks are baseline evidence, not timeless truth. Priority questions:
interactive-session filesystem/credential authority; shared writable state and
cross-agent poisoning (`~/.dharma`); env inheritance at spawn sites;
network/tool egress; reviewed-head → executed-artifact provenance; secret
redaction across prompts, traces, ledgers, receipts; idempotency + human
authorization for consequential actions. **For any irreversible or externally
consequential effect, the six events — request, authorization, attempt,
acceptance, execution, observed outcome — are distinct; one receipt must never
stand in for all six.**

## 9. Campaign Review & the Proposed Dated Trigger

**The quality standard and every ratified floor are permanent; this section
governs only the concentrated campaign allocation.**

**Fortnightly allocation review** — publish: movement on each hard-floor
vector (never a blend); regressions and new blocker inflow; mutation,
negative-control, replay, and fault-injection evidence; gate cost, flake rate,
bypasses, waivers; verified capabilities unlocked downstream; external
semantic evidence where externally scoped claims were made; marginal return of
the next quality packet versus the best competing packet. Capacity may move
when ratchets plateau or the next intervention has lower whole-system
leverage; movement archives or narrows the campaign, never weakens ratified
floors.

**Why a dated trigger and not review alone** (retained so the reasoning cannot
be amended away without confronting it): a review the operator conducts can
always conclude that quality still has the highest leverage — the
self-protecting loop this doctrine exists to prevent. The repository's own
record motivates the concern, bound to exact artifacts with their limits: the
TAM parity receipt records $0 receipted external revenue
(`reports/governance/tam/tam_receipt.json:211` — a parity projection; it does
not claim value exists nowhere else), and the venture-comparable track was
retired 2026-07-17 while quality campaigns were admitted
(`docs/governance/ACTIVE_TRACK.yaml`, company-builder-parity closure record —
a scheduling fact, not an intent claim). A stop-condition that requires
judgment to fire shares the failure mode of §5's unwired budget gate: declared,
not wired.

**The proposed dated trigger** (honest status: this is a *proposal inside an
inert working plan*, not a wired switch — it becomes mechanical only when the
enforcement mechanism below merges into an authoritative surface):

- **Condition:** external-consumption evidence — a receipt signed by an entity
  outside fleet-and-operator (a paying reader; a Darshan issue published
  through pipeline-v0 with a named external reader, subscriber, or citation;
  an outside-witness re-derivation) — present in **at least two of the three
  review periods preceding any evaluation date, beginning 2026-10-17.** A
  single receipt does not permanently satisfy the condition; freshness is
  required. Delivery, consumption, and retention are distinct evidence
  classes; each proves only what it records.
- **On failure:** the enforcement workflow opens an **archive-proposal PR**
  that would move the concentrated allocation's freed capacity to the
  darshan lane's first next_items; **no state changes until a human merges
  it.** Ratified floors stay live gates regardless. No single receipt
  permanently disables the fortnightly review, and no absence of a witness
  voids valid internal engineering evidence — the trigger moves
  *allocation*, it never erases *evidence*.
- **Proposed enforcement design** (to be ratified and merged before the
  trigger is called wired): a scheduled CI workflow (clock: runner UTC date;
  event source: an append-only external-receipts index under
  `reports/governance/keel/` recording receipt class, date, and signer —
  built on the repo's existing receipt conventions (`stable_digest` /
  `receipt_valid`), never a new receipt primitive or parallel ledger;
  signer authentication: how an external signer's identity is verified must
  be defined and ratified before "signed" may act as a gate; transition: on
  freshness failure at an evaluation date, open the archive-proposal PR;
  owner: assigned in the Ratification Act; test: a negative control with
  injected clock and empty index must produce the archive-proposal PR in
  dry-run).
- **Amendment rule:** changes to this section require explicit operator
  ratification **and** a fresh adversarial review; **silent weakening —
  including redefining "external" elsewhere in the document — is prohibited.**
  (A working plan cannot make itself unamendable; it can and does make silent
  amendment illegitimate.)

## 10. Ratchet Baseline Protocol — Candidates Until Proven

Numbers below are *candidate observations*, not binding baselines, until each
is re-derived with a pinned tool, a scalar/structured-emitting command, a
provenance capsule (base SHA, head SHA, dirty state, tool version, config
digest, environment, exit status, output digest, derivation, timestamp,
limitations), and a **negative control proving the gate can fail.** Missing or
contradictory fields fail closed. The former "~27 live mismatches" claim is
**withdrawn** (prose-label grep against a map last X-rayed 2026-07-02); the
canonical count regenerates from the mismatch schema.

| Ratchet | Candidate | Requirement before binding |
|---|---:|---|
| Pre-dispatch budget gateway coverage | gateway absent; predicate has 2 call sites, neither pre-dispatch | single gateway on every provider path + bypass-resistance test |
| Modules over 500 lines | 207 | deterministic tracked-file command; exclude aggregate row |

**Model judgment** grades only what mechanical evidence cannot (architecture,

model's summary); verdicts retained before outcome resolution; calibrated
against later regressions, incidents, reversions, blame, and user outcomes
with uncertainty reported; **admission weight zero until a defined calibration
set and threshold are ratified.**

wave begins implementation before Titanium's Phase 0 exit gate passes

its runtime-host/identity prerequisites); pinned

diff-cover; mutation re-mint with retained controls; deterministic Harness

**Wave 3 — semantic & operational proof:** widen replayable runtime coverage;

the v/k measurement protocol (§1) from ledger outcomes; calibrated judge
study; one venture specimen with separated delivery/consumption/retention

## 13. Kill Criteria

- No weakening or renaming a gate to make a regression disappear.
- No aggregate score hiding a critical-floor failure; no implicit N/A.
- No test-count / coverage / lint / judge / receipt theater.
- No generated projection committed as doctrine; no local observation
  presented as merged-main truth; no invented quantitative precision — a
  number without a measurement protocol is presented as illustrative or not
  at all.
- No loop relying solely on model judgment for termination; no self-judging
  loop without negative controls and later outcome resolution.
- No claim of external consumption from internal publication alone.
- No second ontology, orchestrator, ledger, or control plane without proving
  the existing organ cannot own it.
- No megafile campaign PR; every implementation bounded and reversible.
- No automated or self-merge; no irreversible external action without human
  authority.
- **No silent weakening of §9** — changes require explicit operator
  ratification plus a fresh adversarial review.
