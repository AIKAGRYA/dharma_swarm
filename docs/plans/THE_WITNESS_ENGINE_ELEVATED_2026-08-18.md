## A. The sharpened thesis

**The organism's job is to turn other people's claims into public receipts.
The spearhead is behavioral trust for agents: a calibrated, published-misses
certification that an agent did what it claimed, sold first as a paid
governance sprint (kit already fixture-proven) and grown into a continuous
verification feed that ERC-8004, ATTP, AIUC-1-style auditors, and insurers
can consume. Point the same engine at ecological claims when the second
One Wire domain is the binding constraint — not before the first dollar.**

Why this cut, on the operator's two objective functions:

- **Dots connected.** The serving path of a real paid verification is
  receipts → gates → calibration ledger → archive → gym/forge → A2A. That is
  already most of the metabolism. Biosphere reuses the same spine and wakes
  GAIA/Go/world-radar when sequenced. Forcing DharmaGraph or StrangeLoop into
  the first sale is the sunk-cost trap the premise audit kills
  (VERIFIED(`dharma_swarm/graph/types.py`:5), "nothing here is wired into
  production dispatch").
- **Recursion maximized.** Agent-behavior tasks resolve in minutes to hours;
  mangrove claims resolve in seasons. Only the fast leg can run Mendel-style
  probe/verifier co-evolution at volume without wiring P&L into selection.

Two probes remain allowed. Two missions are not. They share archive, ledger,
gates, and receipt grammar, or the slower one is a different company.

## D. The frontier brief

Eight external facts that change the design. All EXTERNAL-tagged, fetched
2026-08-18.

1. **EigenAI is real cryptographic inference integrity, not behavioral trust.**
   `arXiv:2602.00182` — bit-exact optimistic re-execution on EigenLayer.
   Design change: treat Eigen/Sertn/VeriLLM as a *settlement rail we may
   compose with later*, never as the product. Partner, don't clone.
   EXTERNAL(https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00182, 2026-08-18).

2. **Repeated-game IC for inference is now a paper, not a vacuum.**
   `arXiv:2608.09055` (HTML dated 2026-08): surveyed restaking protocols pass
   one-round IC and fail repeated-game IC (δ∈[0.92,0.98], 1.5–8% deviation
   profit); authors propose history-dependent challenges + vesting.
   Design change: do **not** claim "nobody owns repeated-game trust." Claim
   the orthogonal gap: **did the agent keep its promise in the world**, not
   **did the GPU run the contracted model.**
   EXTERNAL(https://arxiv.org/html/2608.09055, 2026-08-18).

3. **ERC-8004 is a buyer-shaped hole.** Identity / Reputation / Validation
   registries; scoring is *off-chain*; the spec expects "auditor networks and
   insurance pools." Mainnet contracts published.
   Design change: the digital probe's end-state API is "be a trusted
   `clientAddress` that posts feedback + validation results," not "launch a
   token."
   EXTERNAL(https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8004, 2026-08-18).

4. **ERC-8126 is a static security health report (0–100), finalized ~June 2026.**
   Five checks: tokens, media, Solidity, web, wallet — plus ZK so the guts
   stay private. Explicitly a marketplace of verification *providers*.
   Design change: 8126 occupies *infrastructure risk*, not promise-keeping.
   We can later emit an 8126-shaped attestation for the code/wallet slices
   we already scan; we do not compete with Virtuals-style scoring on those
   five layers as the mission.
   EXTERNAL(https://eips.ethereum.org/EIPS/eip-8126, 2026-08-18).

5. **ATTP now *does* specify behavioural trust scoring — as a transport.**
   `draft-sharif-attp-01` (2026-06-03, expires 2026-12-05): five dimensions
   (code attestation, execution success, behavioural consistency, tenure,
   anomaly history), L0–L4 action limits, self-hostable Trust Authority.
   North Star §10's "IETF drafts don't do behavioral trust" is **stale**.
   Design change: become an ATTP Trust-Authority *implementation* whose
   Execution-Success and Anomaly numbers come from Ginko-calibrated
   resolved reality, not from +0.5/−5 heuristics. Heuristic ATTP scoring
   is Goodhartable; our SATYA stack is the differentiator.
   EXTERNAL(https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-sharif-attp/, 2026-08-18).

6. **AIUC-1 is a paying assurance market with quarterly adversarial testing.**
   Certificate + quarterly technical retest; Schellman accredited; Intercom

   closest to "sell receipts, not vibes."
   Design change: packet-2+ positioning is "we are the continuous
   adversarial-test engine your AIUC-1 auditor wants underneath," not
   "we replace Schellman."
   EXTERNAL(https://www.aiuc-1.com/, 2026-08-18).

7. **Kalshi live LLM traders lost 16.0–30.8% over 57 days (Jan–Mar 2026).**

   (US operator cannot use that venue as primary).
   Design change: **kills S1 as spearhead** under C6 and under the $1,000

   EXTERNAL(https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07355, 2026-08-18).
