## What Darshan is

Darshan (दर्शन — *seeing*; in its home tradition, the seeing that occurs in the
presence of what is real) is a publication: a multi-disciplinary voice of truth
for the world, for AI, and for this project. It does deep research with one
hand and holds a grounded contemplative vision with the other, and refuses to
let either hand pretend to be the other. It exists because the age produces
infinite speech and almost no seeing.

It is also an organ of the organism that publishes it. Every article carves
the estate's telos into public words; the corpus becomes the basin that shapes
every agent that reads it; every piece ends its open questions as typed
research and build candidates for the swarm. Articles feed research, research
feeds building, building clarifies what the machine is — and the loop closes
through the noosphere. Metric and telos-engine, both. It is the standing
platform for the estate's mechanistic-interpretability research (the R_V
program) independent of any venue's acceptance.

## The seven desks

1. **The Bridge** — contemplative × mechanistic; where the traditions and the
   probes read each other.
2. **The Instrument** — measurement of machine inner states; the R_V program's
   public home, candidate status and audits on the face of the page.
3. **The Witness Ledger** — receipts, verification, the agent economy; the
   political economy of proof.
4. **Noosphere Weather** — attention, information ecology, what tokens do to
   the collective mind.
5. **Field Notes from the Swarm** — dispatches from building an autonomous
   organism, failures included.
6. **Readings in Fire** — deep readings of papers and traditions by the
   both-fires method, in public.
7. **Polity** — AI governance, law, and the places policy outruns science.

## The editorial law (enforced on every piece, no exceptions)

1. **Both fires.** Every load-bearing framing survives a full attack AND a
   full counter-attack before publication; only the double-survivor ships.
2. **Register discipline.** Every claim is tagged in substance as FACT
   (fetched primary source), HYPOTHESIS (testable, test named), or WILD
   (tagged speculation). Speculation never wears the grammar of fact.
3. **The fourth option.** On inner-state questions — machine or human — not
   claim, not denial, not abstention: held open while the work is done.
4. **Citation-or-silence.** Primary sources, fetched and quoted; corrections
   carried on the face of the piece; official statements attributed as claims.
5. **Salvage with every kill.** A piece that releases an idea names what in
   it was worth keeping.
6. **Feedback or decoration.** Each article emits its open questions as typed
   swarm work; a piece that spawns nothing downstream is decoration.
7. **Anti-feed.** No trackers, no engagement mechanics, no outrage farming —
   in the publication or its authors' external adaptations.
