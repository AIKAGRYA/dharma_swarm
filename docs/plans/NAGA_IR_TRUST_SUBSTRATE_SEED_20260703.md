## Why This Exists

The operator seed argues that agentic coding is entering a new infrastructure phase: autonomous agents, Origin-like forges, xAI-scale compute, Moltbook-style agent social surfaces, compact agent languages, and formal-methods substrates are all converging. The hardened claim is narrower:

```text
NAGA-IR should become the typed epistemic substrate for agentic work.
```

That means claims, receipts, independent challenges, witnesses, scoped standing leases, canon, and compost. It does not mean a new source language first, and it does not mean NAGA-IR itself magically verifies programs.

## Research Corrections

- Cursor/xAI direction is real: public pages show autonomous cloud agents, automations, MCP, sandbox self-testing, Grok Build, and Colossus-scale compute.
- Exact acquisition terms and future platform-neutrality claims remain reported unless direct filings are attached.
- Moltbook is real and studied, but it should be treated as evidence of agent-social protocol pressure and safety gaps, not agent consciousness.
- Some compressed-protocol names in the raw seed were not verified. Keep compact-agent-language claims tied to specific sources and keep opaque encodings below auditability.
- Dharma/formal-logic bridges should be framed as disciplined modeling analogies: dependence, anti-reification, invariants, vows, and telos gates.

## Architectural Claim

NAGA-IR is a four-layer system:

1. Core IR: typed, SSA-like, effect-aware, authority-aware, source-located.
2. Spec IR: claims, invariants, pre/postconditions, authority leases, reliance scopes, linear resources.
3. VC/Witness IR: lowers obligations to Boogie, Viper, Nagini, Dafny, Verus, Kani, CBMC, MLIR SMT, CodeQL, Semgrep, runtime traces, or proof assistants.
4. Receipt Bundle: content-addressed evidence with hashes, tool versions, assumptions, solver settings, resource limits, results, counterexamples, privacy policy, and challenge state.

Key distinction:

```text
receipt = recorded evidence
witness = independently checkable evidence
```

## SAB Mapping

NAGA-IR gives SAB a typed claim language. SAB gives NAGA-IR the adversarial process:

```text
claim packet -> evidence receipt -> challenge window -> witness quorum -> standing lease -> canon/compost
```

The first SAB experiment should wrap one existing Dharma gate output as a `dharma.assurance_receipt.v1` receipt and route it through SAB shadow mode before live submission.

## Product Wedge

Do not sell "NAGA the language." Start with:

- open receipt schema and CLI;
- GitHub/CI PR-boundary agent-work receipts;
- hosted/private SAB witness mesh;
- managed challenge packs;
- domain packs for audit/compliance overlays.

This complements Origin-like causal provenance rather than competing with it.

## Kill Or Narrow Conditions

Kill or narrow this track if:

- receipts do not reduce review cost;
- privacy-preserving receipts cannot support enterprise audit;
- challenge quality becomes noisy theater;
- the schema cannot map cleanly to SLSA/in-toto/Sigstore/OpenTelemetry;
- compact agent encodings become unreviewable authority channels;
- formal claims outrun actual checkable witnesses.
