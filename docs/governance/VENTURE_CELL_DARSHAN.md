## The One Metric That Outranks Every Internal Gate

**`external_readers_who_read_and_replied`.**

Not artifacts produced. Not conductor loops fired. Not gauntlets passed. Not YDS scores. Not Chetana ingests. Every internal gate (reader / evidence / attention / transparency / anekanta / hope / substrate / sacred-name / long-now / arjuna — see the lodestone §"Season 0 Gates") is **in service of this number, or it is navel-gazing in a lab coat.**

This is the cell's defense against its own substrate. The apparatus of rigor is real and good — but it can simulate the felt-sense of shipping so fluently that the simulation satisfies the need the work would satisfy, and the work doesn't happen. The contact-gate makes "progress" *require* a stranger. See [`feedback_narration_outruns_build`](../../../.claude/projects/-Users-dhyana/memory/feedback_narration_outruns_build.md).

**Automatable form:** an artifact is not `DONE` until its bundle's `decision_delta.json` records at least one external-reader event (read + reply / inspection / decision). A bundle whose decision-delta is "use this loop to prove the loop" with `new_task_ids: []` and zero readers is `STAGED`, not `DONE`. The `dharma_swarm/venture_cell/darshan/` conductor already emits the bundle; the gate is the addition of an external-contact field that must be non-empty before the artifact counts.

## What Darshan IS (and the separation that was missing)

Darshan is a **publication**. Its paid layer, *if and when an audience exists*, is **more seeing for the same reader** — source packs, living dossiers, membership, institutional licensing of the evidence apparatus. The buyer of the free essay is the buyer of the paid dossier (the Stratechery / McKinsey-Global-Institute shape).

**Darshan is NOT Campaign X-Ray.** This is the separation that was being narrated away:

| | Darshan | Campaign X-Ray |
|---|---|---|
| instrument | publication | advisory diagnostic |
| buyer | mass reader overwhelmed by feeds | AI-native founder drowning in repos |
| deliverable | a reading arc that restores seeing | a corpus map + ranked campaign + next sprint |
| price | free → same-reader paid layer | $250–$12,000 (incoherent across docs) |
| status | ACTIVE Season 0 | **HELD** (gauntlet 28/100, $0, no sample) |

They are two businesses. Stapling them as one "receipt loop" is the error. **A Darshan article does not convert to a Campaign X-Ray sale** — and the flagship article is correctly built to refuse conversion. If the cash need is real and fast, that is Campaign X-Ray's separate problem (it needs a real buyer it does not yet have). Darshan compounds slowly, through audience. Do not measure Darshan against "first cash receipt."

## Jagat Kalyan Constraint

> Restore attention, agency, truthfulness, and wiser action — without manipulation or client capture.

Darshan earns its name through disciplined seeing, receipts, correction, humility, restraint, and service. If the work becomes vibes, the name is theft. The reader is measurably better able to want their own next thing, or the cell has failed regardless of how beautiful the artifact was.

## The Narration-Outruns-Build Guard

This cell's specific failure mode is **narrating capability as if it were shipped**. Tells: an "active track" that the governance source-of-truth doesn't list; an artifact "ready to sell" that the gauntlet held; a "repeatable loop" that ran once, manually, for zero readers. The guard is the contact-gate plus one honesty rule: **do not write a status this cell has not earned in contact with a real reader.** The governance files already model this honesty (`first_cash_receipt_status.md`: *"not allowed to claim … until a dated receipt exists"*). Extend it here.
