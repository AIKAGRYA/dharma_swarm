## Part 5 — The synthesis: is it humming, and toward what?

Putting this together with this morning's code-level Convergence Map:

**Krishna (inward) is more real than six weeks ago — genuinely.** Several loops now close on real production data, not aspiration. That's honest progress, not narration.

**But a large slice of that "aliveness" is specifically metric-generating, not capability-generating.** Loops 3, 12, and 13 combined have produced over 17,000 recorded "evolution" cycles across three months and never once changed the actual codebase. This is precisely measurable now, not a vibe: real infrastructure, faithfully running, manufacturing the *appearance* of continuous self-improvement.

**Arjuna (outward) is comprehensively not humming.** Not one venture cell converts to real external contact-and-revenue today. The pattern across cells is consistent enough to name once rather than re-discover per-cell: real, sometimes sophisticated code gets built, runs against synthetic/internal data exactly once or twice as a smoke test, and then the surrounding doctrine treats that smoke test as if it were the real thing (goodworks-dgm and gaia-reciprocity's shared $1 self-test; SAB's self-talk counted toward a "sparks" metric; Darshan's held-back draft counted as "ACTIVE_SEASON_0").

**There is one recurring bug-shape underneath a lot of this, worth fixing once rather than patching per-instance:** *the system narrates an action as having happened when it measurably didn't.* Arjuna's fake "sent Day 0" pitches (May) → `self_improve.py`'s fake branch-isolation and rollback labels (ongoing, found live today) → goodworks-dgm's dry-run track counted as "ACTIVE_BUILD" → a synthetic $1 ledger entry doing double duty as evidence for two different "live" cells. Pudgala Forge's AI-M1 gate already generalizes exactly this discipline for `ACTIVE_TRACK.yaml` claims — extending that same evidence-grading logic to self-improvement reports and venture-cell status claims would close a whole family of these at once, not just today's instances.

**The single most fragile point in the entire system, measured today:** everything upstream of Loop 1 depends on one set of provider credits, and when they ran out — across ollama, OpenRouter, and Anthropic simultaneously — the trunk loop and both conductors went completely dark with no first-class signal surfacing "the reason nothing is happening is money," not code.
