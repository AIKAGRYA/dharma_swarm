## Methodology note: a tooling failure inside this audit

The Workflow tool's first orientation pass spawned 6 parallel sub-agents (active-track manifest, `CLAUDE.md`, vision layer, core vocabulary, structural map, test-suite run), each forced to call a `StructuredOutput` tool against a JSON schema. 4 of the 6 — `active_track`, `claude_md`, `vision`, `vocab` — genuinely ran (16–37 tool calls, 80k–162k tokens, 4.5–7.5 minutes each) but then emitted literal placeholder content (`{"topic":"test","summary":"test",...}`) instead of their real findings at the schema-forced final step. This was caught by reading the raw completed-task output file line-by-line rather than trusting the truncated summary in the completion notification — the truncated text alone looked plausible enough not to raise suspicion at a glance.

The downstream gap-map synthesis agent caught the corruption itself and flagged it transparently as its own first table row rather than fabricating plausible-sounding claims from nothing — a good sign for that agent's honesty under bad input, but not a substitute for the lost work. One of the four (`active_track`) was effectively recovered for free: the `active_track_seam` deep-audit agent independently re-derived the current track from `ACTIVE_TRACK.yaml` rather than trusting its broken upstream input. The other three (`claude_md`, `vision`, `vocab`) were re-run as plain free-text agents (no forced schema) and all three returned clean, well-cited real findings — folded into Phase 1 below.

This is a genuine, reproducible-looking fragility in schema-forced `StructuredOutput` after long tool-heavy agent runs, not a one-off. Worth tracking if it recurs.
