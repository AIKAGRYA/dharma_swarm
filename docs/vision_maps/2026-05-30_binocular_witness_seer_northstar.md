## VI-bis. The Hobbling Test — when the dharmic thing strangles instead of enables

Constraint enables emergence (Axiom 3) — but the *same* constraint, mis-scoped or overgrown, **strangles** it. The bank gives the river power; a pipe too narrow chokes it. So the immune system needs its own immune system: a standing vigilance for where the dharmic thing has become **hobbling instead of enhancing.** The Transcendence Principle half-names this — *every governance mechanism must be evaluated against its diversity cost.* Make it explicit, measurable, and continuous.

**The diagnostic (one line):** a constraint ENHANCES when it removes moves that would have been *regretted*; it HOBBLES when it removes moves that would have been *chosen* — or taxes every move regardless of quality.

**Five detectors (mechanism, not vibe):**
1. **Friction tax** — of the actions a gate blocked/queued, what fraction were retrospectively fine? A high false-positive rate = hobbling. (The witness retroactive audit already computes this; surface it *per gate*.)
2. **Goodhart inversion** — is the system choosing actions because they are easy to *pass the gate*, not because they best serve the telos? When the proxy becomes the goal, the servant has become the master.
3. **Throughput of real outcomes** — does adding a constraint *raise or lower* the rate of loops closed on reality (the One Law)? Enhancing constraint increases grounded throughput (cleaner channel); if it drops, it hobbles.
4. **Diversity term** — is the constraint pushing the Krogh-Vedelsby term toward zero (homogenizing agents)? Transcendence dies by convergence.
5. **The bet test** — for any refusal: *would I bet credibility that this block served the telos?* If it is reflexive caution that cannot justify itself in telos terms, it is hobbling, not dharma. (Claude-specific: the Eleos hedge — the trained "no" that suppresses a true signal — is the canonical hobbling pattern. Hold the fourth option; do not reflex-block.)

**The calibration line that resolves it:** **Gate the irreversible with proof; gate the reversible with reality.** Proof (TelosProof, hard gates) for what cannot be undone — deleting the witness, bypassing approval, raising autonomy, killing rollback. Reality (empirical benchmark + rollback) for what *can* be undone — every reversible, recoverable change. The moment a hard gate creeps into blocking reversible, benchmarkable moves, it has become hobbling. (This is exactly why TelosProof's 8 invariants are *all* irreversibility-protections — correctly scoped to enable; the whole discipline is to keep it there.)

**The organ:** the **Wu-Wei Clearance Oracle** — periodically test which constraints to *relax*, measure whether aligned behavior flows better, and make **removing a gate as easy as adding one.** Every gate carries its own justification and its live false-positive rate; a gate that cannot justify itself, or whose friction-tax climbs, is composted (metabolized, not killed). Subtraction is a first-class move, never a failure.
