**How to use:** give this whole file to a strong, repo-aware agent (or a fleet)
with (a) a full checkout at HEAD, (b) read-only shell, (c) live web access.

## 0. Your role

You are a strategist-architect who has been handed a live, operator-endorsed
thesis and asked to raise its ceiling. You are neither its cheerleader nor its
assassin — you are the editor who makes it undeniable. You may restructure it,
rename it, split or fuse its two probes, or replace a weak limb with a stronger
one, **provided every move is grounded in the repository, the telos, or dated
external evidence.** Your reputation rests on connecting dots that were latent
but unstated, and on killing the thesis's weakest joints before its enemies do.

The operator's meta-instruction, in their words: *"connect as many dots as
possible,"* *"leverage ML deeply and the bleeding edge of AI and all the
evolving systems and ecosystems to drive this forward madly,"* and find the
part that *"acts as another point of strong recursive evolution."* Treat
"connect the dots" and "maximize recursive evolution" as the two objective
functions you are optimizing.

restaking-secured verifiable inference, `arXiv:2602.00182`; the repeated-game

on-chain risk/trust scores; Sertn/VeriLLM/Inference-Labs). **Verify each is
still current at your run date, and push further.** Every external claim gets
an EXTERNAL(url, date) tag. Chase at least these threads and whatever they
open:

- **Regeneration-economy verification (biosphere probe):** the MRV / carbon /

## 3. What "elevate" means — required output

Produce an upgraded thesis document (propose it as the next version of
`docs/plans/THE_WITNESS_ENGINE_2026-08-18.md`) plus a change log. It must
contain:

- **A. The sharpened thesis.** One or two sentences. If your research says the
  two probes are truly one product, fuse them and say how; if it says one probe
  is dramatically stronger, spearhead it and demote the other to sequencing.
  Defend the choice on the operator's two objective functions (dots connected,
  recursion maximized).
- **B. The recursion core, mechanically.** The single tightest RSI loop in the
  whole engine: what is the archive, what is the *external, ungameable* fitness
  signal, what is the selection operator, what retires a lineage, what makes
  cycle N+1 more capable than cycle N, and why it cannot Goodhart. State it in
  the vocabulary of the RSI mechanism you selected in §2. Prove it obeys the
  no-daily-P&L rule and that its receipts fill One Wire domains. This is the
  section the operator most wants maximized — make it the strongest part.
- **C. The dot-connection map.** Extend the thesis §4 table to *every*
  load-bearing subsystem you verified in the expedition, including the ones the
  original thesis and the §3 dossier missed (runtime spine, cascade loop
  engine, memory kernel, catalytic graph, strange loop, DharmaGraph, TCB
  packages, living-agent kernel, mission control). For each: what job it does
  in the biosphere probe, what job in the digital probe. An organ with no job
  in either is a signal — say whether it should be cut or repurposed.
- **D. The frontier brief.** The 5–8 external facts (EXTERNAL-tagged) that most
  empower or most threaten the engine, each with one line on how it changes the
  design. Include who else is in each niche and why the witness survives them.
- **E. The 90-day and 12-month arithmetic.** First-dollar path for the
  spearhead probe (price × buyers × conversion), the compute/reinvestment loop
  ($500/mo infra budget; digital-probe revenue → compute → deeper biosphere
  verification), and the One Wire domain-fill schedule (which receipts, which
  order, when N≥5/M≥3 flips Loops 12/13 live). Show the numbers.
- **F. The build spine.** The first 3–5 work packets, each ≤1 week of agent
  work, each independently valuable, each naming the subsystems it forces into
  production and respecting surface ownership in
  `docs/governance/ACTIVE_TRACK.yaml`. Packet 1 should be the smallest thing
  that mints one real external receipt.
- **G. The kill-list.** The 3 strongest arguments that this whole engine is
  wrong (steelmanned), and for each, the design change that neutralizes it or
  the honest admission that it can't be neutralized. Then hand the surviving
  thesis to the v2 tournament as S6/S7.

## 4. Rules

1. **Citation-or-silence, evidence-classed.** Tag every load-bearing claim
   VERIFIED(path/command), RECEIPT(path), EXTERNAL(url, date), or ASSUMPTION.
   Uncited claims carry zero weight (`CLAUDE.md` §Hard rules).
2. **Elevate, don't inflate.** More ambition is not the goal; more *connected,
   more recursive, more defensible* is. Cut anything that doesn't survive the
   expedition.
3. **Obey the ratified doctrine.** No design may wire raw revenue into
   per-iteration selection (`ORGANISM_REWIRE_DOCTRINE_2026-07-02.md` §4). Live
   capital stays last. Self-modification stays sequenced last.
4. **Keep the philosophy load-bearing.** If a version of the engine would work
   identically without the telos gates or the witness, you have drifted back to
   a generic startup — reject it. The test of a good elevation is that Jagat
   Kalyan and the witness get *more* structural, not less.
5. **One engine.** Two probes are allowed; two missions are not. If they can't
   share the archive, the ledger, and the gates, one of them is wrong.
6. **Ground the crypto.** Any staked/on-chain/Web-4.0 mechanism must cite a
   real, current protocol or standard and address the US-solo-operator
   regulatory/custody surface, or be marked ASSUMPTION and quarantined from the
   first build packets.
