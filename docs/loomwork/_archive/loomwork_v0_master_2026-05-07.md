## TELOS GATES (publication firewall)

Every revelation passes 7 gates before publishing:
1. **Vulnerable-person** — no exposure of refugees, defenders, undercover workers
2. **Libel** — every claim against an entity traces to verified source
3. **Citation-retrievability** — every cited source is publicly retrievable
4. **Disinformation** — upstream-source manipulation flag check
5. **Pramana** — every atom tagged proxy/behavioral/geometric/documentary
6. **Confidence-floor** — minimum corroboration threshold
7. **Staleness** — alerts older than X days don't auto-promote

The contemplative spine that was navel-gazing yesterday is **the immune system today**. Witness mode = adversarial robustness. Viveka = discrimination on what becomes a revelation. Pramana = provenance. Same skills, world-pointed.
