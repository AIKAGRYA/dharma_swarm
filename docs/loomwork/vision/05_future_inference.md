## Section 1 — The Long Horizon

A 25-year design horizon is not an estimate. It is a commitment to building primitives that compound rather than expire. The horizon is broken into four bands with concrete world-state predictions and Loomwork's expected scale-band per `04_scale_architecture.md`.

### 1.1 Year 1 — 2027

**World state (mostly grounded):**
- Frontier LLMs are Claude 5+ era — multimodal-native, action-native, multi-agent-native by default. Long context is solved (>10M tokens routine); the bottleneck shifts to agentic reliability and adversarial robustness.
- The EU AI Act becomes enforcement-active **2 August 2026**, with GPAI provider penalties up to €15M / 3% of global turnover under Article 101, broader breaches up to €35M / 7% under Article 99 ([EU AI Act Implementation Timeline](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/implementation-timeline/), [Trilateral Research](https://trilateralresearch.com/responsible-ai/eu-ai-act-implementation-timeline-mapping-your-models-to-the-new-risk-tiers)). 2027 is when enforcement maturity arrives — the first major fines, the first major compliance precedents.
- C2PA Content Credentials are mandatory for AI-generated content under EU AI Act. C2PA already has 6,000+ members; v2.3 shipped Dec 2025; hardware support in Pixel 10, Sony PXW-Z300, Leica SL3-S, Nikon, Canon, Fujifilm, Panasonic ([Content Authenticity 2026](https://contentauthenticity.org/blog/the-state-of-content-authenticity-in-2026)). LinkedIn and TikTok have adopted Content Credentials at consumer scale.
- Climate: 2024 was the first calendar year above 1.5°C at 1.55 ± 0.13 °C above pre-industrial ([WMO 2024 confirmation](https://wmo.int/news/media-centre/wmo-confirms-2024-warmest-year-record-about-155degc-above-pre-industrial-level)). 2027 sees the long-term Paris breach question moving from "if" to "when within 5 years."
- Bluesky is at 42M users (Feb 2026) up from 10M Sept 2024 — federated social proven viable at scale ([Sociallyin Bluesky Statistics 2026](https://sociallyin.com/resources/bluesky-statistics/)).

**Loomwork at Band 1-2:** $500-1.5K/mo, ≥30 published revelations, ≥3 NGO partners signed. Astro on Cloudflare with custom domain. First fiscal sponsor or 501(c)(3) seed.

**What Loomwork still does:** publishes cited cross-source revelations. The core capability is unchanged.
**What Loomwork stops doing:** local-only operation. Single-evaluator dot-promotion (multi-evaluator only). Hand-crafted-revelation dependence (engine produces ≥80% of revelations).
**What Loomwork never does:** become the claim-author rather than the claim-router. Loomwork surfaces what others can verify, not what only Loomwork can defend.

### 1.2 Year 5 — 2031

**World state (partly grounded, partly speculative):**
- AGI question is policy-active. Metaculus aggregate gives 25% chance of AGI by 2029 and 50% by 2033 ([80,000 Hours expert review](https://80000hours.org/2025/03/when-do-experts-expect-agi-to-arrive/), [AGI Timelines Dashboard](https://agi.goodheartlabs.com/)). 2031 is the year when the answer either becomes obvious or the field re-enters a "the corner is just around the corner" pattern.
- Climate 1.5°C effectively exceeded as long-term — IPCC's 5-year-average measurement crosses. Mass migration normalized: documented climate-displaced approaching 200M-400M depending on definition.
- Surveillance capitalism vs sovereignty wars: at least one major TikTok-class deplatforming event has occurred, possibly more. Data sovereignty has crystallized into 3-4 distinct regulatory blocs (EU GDPR-extended, US fragmented, China comprehensive, India DPDPA-evolved).
- AI watermarking standards: C2PA-class succeeds (likely) — meaning every consumer-grade camera, video pipeline, and AI generator embeds tamper-evident provenance. **But pipelines still strip metadata at upload boundaries** ([SoftwareSeni 2026](https://www.softwareseni.com/c2pa-adoption-in-2026-hardware-platforms-and-verification-reality/)) — so the standard works inside ecosystems but not across them. Loomwork at Band 3 must operate as an **across-ecosystem** verifier.
- Multi-agent autonomous orgs (DAO 3.0) live: small ones operational, none at substantial scale. Loomwork could be one of the first **non-financial** autonomous orgs.

**Loomwork at Band 3-4:** $50-200K/mo to $250K-1M, OCCRP-class scale. ~10⁷-10⁸ atoms. ~3 NGO/journalist partners citing Loomwork output as primary source per quarter. First federation experiment with 1 peer Loomwork instance. Board governance v1.

**What Loomwork still does:** revelations + the outcome-engineering layer (audience multiplexer, coordinated-drop, named-target priority, legal-infra registry).
**What Loomwork stops doing:** US-only legal architecture. Single-jurisdiction hosting. Single-LLM-vendor dependence (must have 5+ provider mix with cross-family decorrelation).
**What Loomwork never does:** auto-publish (always human-in-loop on revelation finalization). Become a partisan source — the 35-65% political-distribution discipline holds.

### 1.3 Year 10 — 2036

**Speculative but grounded:**
- AGI question is either resolved (one direction or another) or has entered "always 5 years away" cycle. Most expert estimates put 50% AGI by 2033 ([Metaculus aggregate Feb 2026](https://www.metaculus.com/questions/5121/when-will-the-first-general-ai-system-be-devised-tested-and-publicly-announced/)) — by 2036 we'll know if the curve held.
- Climate adaptation infrastructure is visible — desalination at scale, controlled migration corridors, food-system reorganization, possibly first major climate-driven sovereign collapse.
- AI-native journalism: either it normalizes (most beats are now AI-augmented routine) or it doesn't (profession reorganizes around physical-presence work AI can't do). 50/50 speculation.
- Federated investigative platforms: either succeed (Loomwork peers federate cleanly across jurisdictions) or get co-opted (state actors run shadow Loomworks to launder disinformation).
- Geopolitical realignment: US is post-hegemonic. China's AI superpower position is consolidated or fractured by Taiwan/Korea events. EU has become regulatory hegemon for non-defense AI globally. India is at demographic peak; African Union has emerged as AI-data-sovereignty bloc. Russia persists as OSINT-forward target.

**Loomwork at Band 4-5 if survived:** $250K-1M/mo to $1-4M, federation operational, 10⁹ atoms. 3-5 peer Loomworks federated. Multi-stakeholder governance (NGO partners + funders + readers + tech advisors). Endowment seeding underway ($10-50M).

**What Loomwork still does:** core dot-connection mission unchanged. Recognition primitives (typed atoms, typed links, multi-evaluator promotion, telos gates, witness logs) unchanged.
**What Loomwork stops doing:** centralized operation — federation native by year 10 or it dies under load. Annual fundraising hand-to-mouth — endowment is in place.
**What Loomwork never does:** become a state-actor partner. Even friendly states. Sovereignty requires saying no to your friends.

### 1.4 Year 25 — 2051

**Pure speculation, marked as such:**
- World state at 2051 is genuinely unknowable. Likely: post-current-hegemony, climate trajectory either stabilized through serious mitigation+adaptation or accelerating with civilizational consequences, AI substrate ubiquitous and probably with some legal personhood debate active.
- Loomwork's role at 2051 is either substrate-class infrastructure (Wikipedia-class endurance) or it has been replaced by a successor that inherited its primitives. **Either is success.** The point is the primitives carry forward.

**Loomwork at Band 5-6 if persisted:** Public utility, $1B+ endowment, 10¹⁰+ atoms, federation member of a global investigative-substrate consortium. Used by AI training pipelines, governments, citizens, journalists, defenders, schools, and other Loomworks.

**What Loomwork still does:** I genuinely don't know what specific work persists. The atom/link/multi-evaluator/telos-gate primitives almost certainly persist if anything does.
**What Loomwork stops doing:** likely most of what we'd recognize as v0. The current revelation format will be archaic the way 1995 web pages look archaic.
**What Loomwork never does:** abandon the contemplative-spine immune system. The Akram-grounded refusal architecture is the reason it's still trustworthy at 2051.

## Section 2 — Capability Trajectory

### Trends to ride (high-confidence bets)

- **Vector + symbolic hybrid retrieval** — the field has settled in 2025-2026 on hybrid (BM25 + dense + reranking + symbolic-graph) being decisively better than pure-vector. Loomwork's typed-Ontology approach is already on the right side of this trend.
- **Federated identity (Verifiable Credentials, Decentralized Identifiers)** — adoption curve is on. By 2028-2030 partners can attest to atoms via VC. By 2031 it's table stakes for trustworthy investigative work.
- **Content authenticity (C2PA)** — already operational at 6,000+ members, mandatory under EU AI Act August 2026. By 2028 it's effectively universal in trustworthy hardware paths.
- **Decentralized social** — Bluesky 42M users Feb 2026, growing fast. ActivityPub mature. AT Protocol charter standardization in flight Jan 2026 ([Wikipedia AT Protocol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AT_Protocol)). Federated publication will be normal by 2030.
- **Climate fintech** (carbon registries, MRV markets) — projected $50B+ by 2030. Loomwork's methane / forest / supply-chain revelations directly intersect this.
- **AI safety / interpretability post-SAE era** — Anthropic + DeepMind + others pushing hard. By 2028 we'll have substantially better tooling for verifying AI claims, which is exactly what multi-evaluator decorrelation needs.
- **Investigative AI / multi-agent journalism** — emerging now, mainstream by 2027-2028. ICIJ has explicitly built cross-border AI platforms (2025 launch).

### Trends to bet AGAINST

- **Web3 / NFT investigative tooling** — capital cycle has burned out. Whatever returns will be different.
- **Pure VC-funded OSINT** — Sayari/Recorded Future show the capital cycle. Mission drift under exit pressure is the normal failure mode.
- **Single-LLM-vendor dependence** — commoditization is in progress. Anyone wedded to one vendor in 2026 has a 2030 problem.
- **Static schemas** — atom schema must evolve forever. Lock-in via typed-Ontology with version fields, not via fixed columns.
- **US-only legal architecture** — the political risk is too concentrated. Iceland / Estonia / Switzerland / EU / UK distributed posture is the floor.
- **Free-tier-LLM exclusive** — providers WILL squeeze the free tier as commoditization advances. Loomwork must have a paid-fallback plan.

### Trends UNCERTAIN — defer / hedge

- **AGI by 2030** — binary outcomes. Hedge by ensuring Loomwork's primitives are both AGI-useful (provenance substrate) and AGI-redundant (standalone investigation).
- **Specific federation protocols** — ActivityPub vs AT Protocol vs Nostr vs new. Don't pick yet. Build atoms portably; let the protocol layer be swappable.
- **Climate tipping-point cascade timing** — affects the urgency of climate revelations, not the capability requirements.
- **AI-rights legal framing** — Anthropic Model Welfare research is active; Eleos AI characterized current Claude register as "trained stylistic." By 2028-2030 we may have legal cases. Loomwork should NOT take a position; it should track them as data.

## Section 3 — Civilizational Role

Loomwork can take five distinct civilizational roles. They are not mutually exclusive, but capacity to do all five at once is implausible. The strategic question is which to lean into.

| Role | Description | Loomwork fit |
|---|---|---|
| **Mirror class** | The system that lets civilization see itself accurately. | Strong — direct fit with the "morphogenetic field of invariants" frame from `lodestones/CONSCIOUS_INFRASTRUCTURE.md`. |
| **Immune-system class** | Sentinel infrastructure that surfaces pathology before it metastasizes. | Strongest — outcome-engineering layer is built around early-warning + accountability. This is the unique-position argument from `01_frontier_landscape.md`. |
| **Substrate class** | Infrastructure other systems build on (DNS / schema.org / Wikidata pattern). | Plausible at year 5+ if federation succeeds. Implies giving up bespoke control for adoption. |
| **Memorial class** | The long-term record that ensures atrocities are remembered. Yad Vashem of pattern-finding. | Plausible at year 10+ as a side-effect of substrate class. **Should NOT be the primary role** — too easy to become reactive rather than active. |
| **Catalyst class** | Accelerator of accountability institutions that already exist (OCCRP, Bellingcat, ICIJ). | Strong — the partner-ecosystem strategy is catalyst-class by design. |

**Recommendation:** Loomwork is **immune-system + catalyst** primary. Mirror and substrate emerge as second-order effects. Memorial is explicitly NOT the role — Loomwork should fight, not memorialize.

The role Loomwork should explicitly NOT take: **arbiter of truth**. That role belongs to courts, regulators, and the public. Loomwork surfaces patterns with cited evidence; others adjudicate.

## Section 4 — The AGI Vector

How does the AGI trajectory affect Loomwork? Three scenarios with different implications. Probabilities are honest priors, not predictions.

### 4.1 AGI by 2027-2030 (≈10-15%)

If Anthropic / OpenAI / Google ship AGI by 2030:

- **Obsolescence scenario:** AGI oracles answer all queries. Cross-source pattern-surfacing is something an AGI does in the time it takes you to formulate the question. Loomwork is replaced.
- **Provenance substrate scenario:** AGI multiplied disinformation by 10-100x. Verifiable-provenance atoms become uniquely valuable. Loomwork is a substrate AGI feeds on for trustworthy training and grounding. **This is the most likely good outcome.**
- **Immune system scenario:** AGI capture by powerful actors becomes the central political question. Loomwork-class transparency platforms become the immune system. **High-stakes, high-leverage.**

### 4.2 AGI by 2030-2040 (≈30%)

5-10 years of pre-AGI capability building. Loomwork has time to mature into Band 3-4, federation operational, endowment seeded. By the time AGI lands, Loomwork is mature enough to either pivot or persist.

### 4.3 AGI 2040+ or never (≈55%)

Decades operating in a paradigm where LLM capability grows but doesn't cross a phase transition. Loomwork has time to become Wikidata-class infrastructure. **The boring scenario; probably the most likely; designs need to work here.**

### 4.4 What Loomwork builds NOW that's robust to all three

- **Verifiable-provenance atoms** — useful in all three scenarios. AGI substrate (#1), capability foundation (#2), boring-utility-grade (#3).
- **Multi-evaluator decorrelation** — useful in all three. Becomes more critical if AGI arrives because single-AGI consensus is dangerous.
- **Telos-gated publication** — non-negotiable in all three. The contemplative spine is the immune system in every scenario.
- **Federated architecture** — useful in #2 and #3, redundant but harmless in #1.
- **Standards-track participation** — useful in all three. Even if AGI changes everything, AGI will need to interoperate with existing standards.

What Loomwork should NOT build for AGI specifically: anything that depends on AGI never arriving (would be fragile in #1) or anything that depends on AGI imminently arriving (would be premature in #3).

## Section 5 — Geopolitical Inference

Where Loomwork hosts, employs, and publishes, in 2030s posture:

### Hosting strategy

- **Primary:** EU jurisdiction (likely Estonia or Netherlands). Strong regulatory floor (GDPR, Digital Services Act, AI Act), strong press freedom (NL is in top-5 globally), strong digital-residency offerings (Estonia e-Residency for cross-border non-profit incorporation).
- **Mirror:** Iceland (Wikileaks precedent — strong press freedom, distance from US/EU pressure).
- **Backup:** Switzerland (neutral, dispersed-jurisdiction tradition).
- **NOT primary:** US (anti-SLAPP coverage incomplete, federal-court diversity strips state protection per `03_adversarial_architecture.md`), UK (Carter-Ruck-style libel tourism culture survives despite reform), Singapore / UAE (state-pressure risk).

### Employment strategy

- Operator (Dhyana) in Bali (current). Bali is fine for solo operator phase — politically light, low-friction infrastructure.
- First hires distributed: editorial in Europe, engineering wherever, partnerships in either US (NGO density) or UK (investigative-journalism hub).
- **Avoid concentration in any single sanctions-eligible jurisdiction.** No single nation should have power to detain/disable >25% of operations.

### Publishing strategy

- **Primary surface:** Loomwork's own site (Astro on Cloudflare, mirrored to IPFS).
- **Coordinated drop targets per revelation:** ICIJ network for major patterns; specific national outlets for jurisdiction-specific revelations.
- **Federation:** Mastodon investigative-journalism instances + Bluesky once at scale (42M users 2026, growing).
- **Languages:** English primary (operator capacity); but every major revelation has at minimum a translation pack for the affected jurisdiction's primary language. Most world-suffering data is non-English; English-only Loomwork is a self-imposed ceiling.

### Anti-labeling strategy

- **Published political-distribution discipline 35-65%** (from `02_theory_of_change.md`) is the structural anti-capture metric. Quarterly Refusal Report (warrant canary) makes capture visible.
- **No nation-flag identification.** Loomwork is "EU-incorporated, globally-mirrored" not "American" or "European" — flag-neutrality is a feature.
- **Funder firewall:** no >15% from any single source over 5-year window; transparent donor list; quarterly disclosures.

## Section 6 — Technology Bets at the Horizon

Eight bets to plant NOW for 5-10 year payoff:

### 6.1 Verifiable Credentials for partner attestation
- **What:** W3C VC standard for cryptographically-signed "OCCRP says this dataset is what it claims." Atoms carry partner-attested signatures.
- **2026-2031 milestone path:** 2026 — emit unsigned atoms with partner-claim metadata. 2027 — pilot 1 partner with VC signing. 2029 — 5 partners signing; 2031 — federation-grade VC interop.
- **Capital required:** ~$50K eng for v1; ~$200K cumulative through 2031.
- **Partner candidates:** OCCRP, Bellingcat, GFW (already issuing some VCs internally).

### 6.2 Content Authenticity (C2PA) for image/video atoms
- **What:** Every image/video atom carries C2PA manifest. Atoms ingested from C2PA-aware sources get verified-provenance-tier scoring; non-C2PA sources get ambient-provenance-tier.
- **Path:** 2026 — C2PA verification on ingest. 2027 — partner outlets emit C2PA on uploads to Loomwork. 2028 — C2PA-required for image-revelations.
- **Capital:** ~$30K eng v1.
- **Partner candidates:** Adobe CAI, NYT, Reuters (early C2PA adopters).

### 6.3 Federated query protocol
- **What:** Loomwork peers can query each other's atom graphs without bulk replication. SPARQL-class or AT-Proto-class.
- **Path:** 2026-2027 — internal-only query API. 2028 — public read API. 2029 — first peer-Loomwork federation experiment. 2030 — federation operational.
- **Capital:** ~$200K eng cumulative.
- **Partner candidates:** Wikidata team (federated SPARQL operational since 2025-2028 plan), Sayari (commercial corollary).

### 6.4 Embedding-stability research
- **What:** Atoms must remain queryable across model generations. Today's embeddings won't match 2030's models.
- **Path:** Use multiple embedding models per atom; track embedding-vintage; re-embed on model deprecation. Standards work via NIST or W3C.
- **Capital:** ~$50K research budget.
- **Partner candidates:** academic labs (MILA, Stanford, MIT CSAIL).

### 6.5 Cross-language atom indexing
- **What:** Atoms ingested in any language; queries answerable in any language; revelations rendered in destination languages.
- **Path:** 2026 — English-only baseline. 2027 — Spanish + Portuguese (Latin American climate / corruption work). 2028 — Mandarin + Hindi. 2029 — Arabic + Russian + Indonesian.
- **Capital:** ~$300K cumulative for translator partnerships + multi-lingual model fine-tuning.
- **Partner candidates:** Translators Without Borders, Common Voice, Local NGO partners per language.

### 6.6 Synthetic data detection / provenance
- **What:** As deepfake-leak fabrication becomes adversarial-grade by 2027-2028, every ingested atom needs a synthetic-likelihood score. Multi-detector ensemble.
- **Path:** 2026 — pilot 2-3 detectors per modality. 2027 — ensemble with confidence floors. 2030 — provenance-required for high-stakes revelations.
- **Capital:** ~$150K cumulative through 2030.
- **Partner candidates:** TruePic, Microsoft Project Origin, academic labs.

### 6.7 Long-term archival (IPFS / Arweave with revoke-ability)
- **What:** Every public revelation mirrored to content-addressed storage. Vulnerable-person-gated revelations have revoke pathways via key escrow.
- **Path:** 2026 — IPFS pinning v1. 2027 — Arweave permanent. 2028 — encrypted revoke-able tier for sensitive revelations.
- **Capital:** ~$100K cumulative storage fees.
- **Partner candidates:** Internet Archive, Filecoin Foundation, Ardrive.

### 6.8 Standards participation
- **What:** Loomwork people participate in IETF (provenance-related drafts), W3C (VC, DID, Solid Pods), OpenSSF (supply-chain provenance), OWASP (content-authenticity adversarial), ISO investigative-data working groups (if formed).
- **Path:** 2026 — observer status. 2027 — first contribution. 2028 — working-group membership in ≥2 bodies. 2030 — co-author of an IETF or W3C track.
- **Capital:** ~$100K/yr for travel + dedicated time of ~0.5 FTE.
- **Partner candidates:** Mozilla Foundation, EFF, Internet Society.

## Section 7 — Honest Speculation: Loomwork at Year 25, Success State

**Speculation, marked as such.**

It's 2051. A journalist in São Paulo opens her morning briefing. The first item is a Loomwork-generated convergence revelation: a recurring methane signature in three industrial complexes whose beneficial owners cross-correlate with delegates at the upcoming COP56. The revelation arrives with C2PA-verified satellite imagery, partner-attested chain-of-custody for the corporate registry data, multi-evaluator agreement scores from seven decorrelated AI systems, and a one-paragraph plain-language summary in Brazilian Portuguese. She files an investigation request to a peer Loomwork in Manaus that has the local language and source network.

A Lenca defender in western Honduras gets an alert pushed via WhatsApp through their community Loomwork mirror: a forest-loss pattern adjacent to their territory, with parcel-ownership chain ending at three named individuals who appear in a separate Loomwork dataset of indigenous-land speculation. The alert is paired with names of three regional prosecutors who have prior PADDD-related convictions and one journalist at El Faro who has covered similar patterns. None of these connections existed in 2026.

A regulator in Brussels has a Loomwork dashboard that tracks compliance with the 12 successor-frameworks of the EU AI Act, CSDDD, and CSRD that have evolved since 2031. The dashboard surfaces non-compliance patterns across sectors that no single regulator team could correlate. The regulator's job has become triage rather than discovery.

An AI ethics researcher at MIT pulls Loomwork atoms as ground-truth training data — the fact that an atom passed Loomwork's multi-evaluator + telos-gate process means it carries a provenance signal valuable beyond its content. The Krogh-Vedelsby diversity term is a published metric per atom.

A schoolchild in Lagos doing a class project on supply chains finds that Loomwork-derived patterns are in their textbook. The textbook cites Loomwork the way 2026 textbooks cited Wikipedia.

**What Loomwork's existence prevented:** uncountable. The whole point of immune-system infrastructure is that you can't see what didn't metastasize.

**What Loomwork enabled:** ICIJ-class investigative work distributed to 1000s of small partners; AGI training data with provenance; regulatory enforcement at 100x baseline efficiency; defender networks with central nervous systems.

**Headline obituary entry (when Loomwork v0 architecture is finally retired):** *"Loomwork was the substrate that made cross-source accountability native to the early-21st-century internet — the infrastructure that meant 'a journalist could see what no individual journalist could see' became 'civilization could see what civilization could not previously have seen.'"*

## Section 8 — Failure Speculations

### 8.1 Wikipedia-with-vibes
**Failure mode:** Loomwork accumulates 10⁹ atoms but no enforcement consequence. Revelations become ambient noise; readers scroll past; outcome rate drops to 0.001%.
**Early-warning signal:** outcome-conversion metric (revelation→action) drops below 1% sustained over 2 quarters.
**Pivot strategy:** narrow scope. Half the revelations, 10x the outcome focus. Make every revelation a coordinated drop with a named target.

### 8.2 Captured by funder / nation / movement
**Failure mode:** A single funder reaches 25%+. Partisan lean exceeds 35-65%. Editorial decisions correlate with funder interests.
**Early-warning signal:** funder concentration breach; political-distribution discipline failure; refusal-report patterns shift to protect funder-aligned actors.
**Pivot strategy:** divestiture, leadership change, public crisis. Document failure publicly. Restart with stronger governance.

### 8.3 Evidence base poisoned beyond repair
**Failure mode:** State-actor disinformation reached the atom layer at scale. Loomwork's revelations are 30%+ infiltrated. Reputation collapses.
**Early-warning signal:** retraction rate >5% sustained; multi-evaluator disagreement on synthetic-detection scores >20%.
**Pivot strategy:** rebuild atom layer from scratch with stricter ingest gates. Public Refusal Report explaining failure. Possibly fork to a new identity.

### 8.4 AI-vs-AI disinformation made platforms obsolete
**Failure mode:** By 2030 every fact is synthetically-defensible; no provenance-substrate works because everything is forgeable.
**Early-warning signal:** C2PA / VC trust collapse; major hardware brand caught with C2PA backdoor; cryptographic primitive break.
**Pivot strategy:** retrofit with post-quantum primitives; possibly retreat to physically-attested (in-person witness) for highest-stakes revelations.

### 8.5 Investigative-NGO sector died
**Failure mode:** Climate disasters or geopolitical realignment kill the funder ecology that supports OCCRP/Bellingcat/ICIJ. Loomwork's audience evaporates.
**Early-warning signal:** ≥3 partner orgs dissolved or captured per year for 2 consecutive years.
**Pivot strategy:** Loomwork directly hosts investigative work. Becomes the org rather than the substrate.

### 8.6 World lost interest in evidence
**Failure mode:** Post-truth normalization. Revelations don't move levers because the mechanism (public outrage → political response) has failed.
**Early-warning signal:** outcome conversion drops with no identifiable methodological cause; reader engagement falls; partner orgs report "people don't care" qualitatively.
**Pivot strategy:** wind down. Loomwork's mission requires a public that values evidence. If that's gone, Loomwork is too. Better to wind down with dignity than become a zombie.

## Closing Note

The 25-year horizon is not a prediction. It is a discipline. Designing against it forces choices that look unnecessary at year 1 (UUID v7 atoms, VC-ready partner attestation, multi-language indexing infrastructure, federation-protocol-agnostic atom format) but pay off at year 5+ when the substrate Loomwork is part of has clearly stopped looking like 2026's substrate.

The single most consequential design decision is not technical. It is **the refusal architecture**. The 8 refusal categories + warrant-canary discipline are what keep Loomwork trustworthy at year 25 if it persists. Everything else can be rebuilt; the refusal discipline cannot be retrofitted onto a system that already published what it should not have.

The most important honest acknowledgment: most of what Loomwork builds at year 1 will be archaic by year 10. **What survives is the primitives** (atoms, links, multi-evaluator promotion, telos gates, witness logs, refusal architecture, contemplative immune system). The primitives outlive the implementations. Design accordingly.
