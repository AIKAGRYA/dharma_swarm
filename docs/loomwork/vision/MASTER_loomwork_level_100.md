## 1. Executive Statement

Loomwork is the public, telos-gated, multi-evaluator dot-connection substrate that Palantir would have been if its customer were civil society instead of intelligence agencies. It ingests world-data atoms — deforestation alerts, methane plumes, leaked filings, court records, sanctions, dark-vessel tracks, refugee profiles, regulatory dockets, AI-harm reports — through a typed Ontology, cross-pollinates them across heterogeneous sources via decorrelated multi-evaluator inference, and publishes cited revelations engineered for outcome-conversion (named target × pre-existing legal infrastructure × audience-specific artifact × coordinated drop), governed by a contemplative refusal architecture that explicitly declines to publish technically-true patterns when publication would harm vulnerable persons, expose sources, fuel ethnic profiling, or compromise active investigations.

At level 15 (today) it is a 14-day local v0 with five hand-crafted launch revelations. At level 100 it is a federated multi-decade public-utility substrate with $1B+ endowment, used by journalists in São Paulo, defenders in Honduras, regulators in Brussels, AI training pipelines, schools in Lagos, and peer Loomwork instances across at least three jurisdictions. The unit of meaning at level 100 is not atoms but recognized patterns and the institutional memory those patterns constitute.

The platform's structural claim to existence: no other organization spans the union of investigative consortia × OSINT shops × climate accountability × wildlife/ocean × conflict tracking × modern slavery × civil society defense × refugee mobility × public health × indigenous rights × commercial intelligence platforms. Each existing org has a slice; Loomwork's product is the cross-pollination layer the slices cannot build for themselves. The contemplative spine — Akram-grounded refusal architecture — is what prevents the cross-pollination layer from becoming weaponizable. That spine is operationally distinctive: it does not exist anywhere else.

## 2. The Six Findings That Reshape The Design

**01 — Frontier landscape.** No public, open-source, typed-Ontology platform exists for civil-society pattern surfacing. The architectural pattern (Palantir Foundry/Gotham) is correct; the customer base is the inversion. The window is open 12–24 months before Big Tech commodification; closing risks include trust-capture events, state-actor disinformation breakthroughs, and EU AI Act compliance overhead favoring incumbents. Naming: lock **Loomwork** pending domain verification; **Plumbline** as backup; **Constellate** hard-killed (collision with Constellate.ai + Constella.ai). The moat is partner-org trust + open-source substrate + telos-gate doctrine + contemplative immune system + outcome-engineering layer — none of which is replicable by capital alone.

**02 — Theory of change.** The primary engineering problem is not increasing the rate of atom→revelation. It is increasing the rate of revelation→outcome. Pandora Papers had MORE atoms than Panama Papers but produced **18× LESS outcome** ($76.4M vs $1.36B in tax recoveries) — *more atoms produce less outcome unless the publication moment is engineered.* Loomwork must architect for the whole 11-stage causal chain (raw signal → world-state change), not just the publish event. Eight audiences need eight artifact shapes per revelation. Eight inference pattern classes (hypocrisy / ownership / timing / convergence / contradiction / geographic / network / temporal) — convergence patterns are Loomwork's unique contribution. Seven honesty mechanisms compose; eight refusal categories must hold; quarterly Refusal Report with warrant-canary structure is the trust signal.

**03 — Adversarial architecture.** The single highest-leverage attack vector is the **Wikileaks 2010 banking blockade** (Visa/MC/PayPal/BoA/Western Union destroyed 95% of revenue Dec 7, 2010, restored only after 2012 Iceland court order against Valitor; Visa+Mastercard control 97% of European card payments). **Bitcoin financial sovereignty** is what saved Wikileaks and is therefore non-negotiable for Loomwork. **No federal anti-SLAPP** exists in the US — Fifth/Seventh/Tenth/Eleventh/DC Circuits strip state protection in federal diversity jurisdiction. C2PA at 6,000+ members provides provenance but does NOT detect deepfakes. NIST FIPS 203/204/205 PQC migration deadline is end of 2030 (EU). 146 environmental defenders killed in 2024 globally — source-protection failure is lethal. **BR-014 in `dharma_swarm/docs/state/BROKEN_REGISTER.md` (BHED_GNAN hard-pass at `telos_gates.py:512-513`) must be wired before Loomwork ships.**

**04 — Scale architecture.** Seven scale bands (B0 single-operator local at $0–200/mo through B6 public utility at $1B+ endowment). Forward-compatibility decisions for B0 that survive to B6: UUID v7 atom IDs, atom versioning from day one, content-hash field reserved, evaluator-stack provenance per atom, no rendered HTML in atom content. Migration hardpoints: schema migration across 10⁹ atoms, single-region → multi-region without downtime, evaluator-pool diversity collapse, founder succession, single-jurisdiction → federation, single-team → multi-team. **Inference, not storage, is the dominant cost driver at every band.** Funding rule: no funder >15% over any 5-year window.

**05 — Future inference.** Four-horizon forecast (2027/2031/2036/2051). Loomwork's primary civilizational role is **immune-system + catalyst** (mirror and substrate emerge as second-order; memorial explicitly NOT primary; arbiter-of-truth explicitly forbidden). Three AGI scenarios: ≈10–15% AGI by 2027–2030 (Loomwork as provenance substrate is the most likely good outcome), ≈30% by 2030–2040, ≈55% AGI 2040+ or never. **EU-primary hosting (Estonia or Netherlands), Iceland and Switzerland mirrors, US explicitly NOT primary.** The single most consequential design decision is **the refusal architecture** — everything else can be rebuilt; refusal cannot be retrofitted.

**06 — Partner, governance, funding.** **OCCRP cautionary tale anchored:** $22.1M budget 2023, ~50% USAID, lost $5M+ (29%) in 2025 USAID freeze, 20% staff layoffs. ICIJ contrast: only 6.2%/8.6% State Dept dependency, survived. The 15% rule is not theoretical; it is the precondition for surviving the next funding-source revocation. 38 named partner orgs across 11 domains. **Code for Science & Society** as primary fiscal sponsor; **Open Collective Foundation** as backup. Apache 2.0 (engine) + AGPL-3.0 (public render) + CC-BY 4.0 (revelations) + CC0 (raw atoms). MacArthur March 2026 $100M democracy commitment is the strongest fresh funder signal. Anthropic Fellows Program 2026 (Kyle Fish lineage) is the natural seat for Loomwork's contemplative-spine framing. TBB activates Y1 *post*-Revelation 3 (warm intro from sight, not pitch).

## 3. Architectural Synthesis — The Ten-Layer Composition

The design composes ten layers that interlock at v0 and remain coherent through B6. Each layer cites the vision doc that supports it.

**A. Constitutional layer (irreducible v0).** Boot-time `DharmaKernel.verify()` enforced at module load; Foundations Corpus injected via `context.read_foundations()` before every LLM call; witness JSONL at `~/.dharma/witness/loomwork/<YYYY-MM-DD>.jsonl`; pramana provenance tagging; telos gates registered as `GateProposal` instances for S5 approval. This layer is non-negotiable from day 1. Source: spec ([`2026-05-07-loomwork-design.md`](../2026-05-07-loomwork-design.md)) + [03 §2.12](./03_adversarial_architecture.md) on BHED_GNAN hardening.

**B. Outcome-engineering layer (LOCKED v0).** Audience-shape multiplexer (≥3 artifact shapes per revelation: journalist brief / NGO advocacy pack / regulator complaint draft / prosecutorial evidence pack / political brief / activist card / market-signal feed / citizen card); coordinated-drop infrastructure (embargo timestamp + partner notification list, Panama Papers model); pre-existing-legal-infrastructure registry as a first-class data source; named-target priority scoring; convergence-pattern bias. **This is the difference between Pandora and Panama outcomes.** Source: [02 §1, §4, §5](./02_theory_of_change.md).

**C. Honesty architecture (7 mechanisms).** Claim-vs-evidence pramana separation; confidence floor; multi-evaluator decorrelation with Krogh-Vedelsby diversity > 0; adversarial review queue; algedonic feedback (reader-flagged → auto-retract); source diversity ≥2 independent; published political-distribution discipline (35–65% bounds — explicit anti-capture metric). Source: [02 §6](./02_theory_of_change.md), [03 §1.7](./03_adversarial_architecture.md).

**D. Refusal architecture (8 categories + warrant canary).** Hard-refuse: vulnerable-person · active-investigation interference · state-actor target lists · intimate-relationship details · ethnic/racial profiling · medical-condition exposure · religious surveillance · civilian collateral. Quarterly Refusal Report with silent-absence-as-capture-warning. **Per [05 §closing](./05_future_inference.md), this is the single most consequential design decision — everything else can be rebuilt; refusal cannot be retrofitted onto a system that already published what it should not have.** Source: [02 §7](./02_theory_of_change.md), [05 closing](./05_future_inference.md).

**E. Inference layer.** Free-models-first (Ollama Cloud GLM-5 baseline; Haiku for telos gates; Sonnet only on edge cases); ≥3 decorrelated evaluators required for `dot → revelation` promotion; 8 inference pattern classes with explicit specialization; confidence-floor publication gate. At B3+, automated decorrelation scoring matrix prevents evaluator-pool collapse. At B5+, in-house foundation model capped at 30% of evaluator pool weight. Source: [02 §5](./02_theory_of_change.md), [04 §3](./04_scale_architecture.md).

**F. Substrate layer (Ontology — already exists).** Atoms = `OntologyObj` instances of typed kinds (WorldEvent, Entity, Pattern, Hypothesis, Revelation, Actor, DataSource, Claim, OpenQuestion, **LegalInfrastructureSlot**); typed `Link` instances for connections; lifecycle through `ActionExec` records. **Forward-compat from B0:** UUID v7 IDs, atom versioning, content-hash, evaluator-stack provenance. Source: [04 §2](./04_scale_architecture.md), [`dharma_swarm/dharma_swarm/ontology.py`].

**G. Adversarial layer (level-100 from day 1).** **Bitcoin financial sovereignty** (Wikileaks 2010 lesson — Visa+MC blockade survived only on BTC); C2PA content authenticity for image/video atoms (provides provenance, NOT deepfake detection — pair with manipulation-detection pipeline); Sigstore + SLSA Level 4 for build provenance; multi-region mirroring readiness (deploy deferred to v1); cryptographic provenance chain per revelation; DID infrastructure for partner attestation (forward-compat); NIST FIPS 203/204/205 PQC migration plan with end-of-2028 hybrid target; mirror network at ≥10 partner-hosted instances by v1. Source: [03 §2, §4, §3.3](./03_adversarial_architecture.md).

**H. Render layer.** v0: local Astro on `localhost:4321`. v1: Astro on Cloudflare Pages with custom domain (post-research). v3 (B4): federated mirror network. JSON-LD typed atoms via the published ontology vocabulary. RSS / JSON Feed as legacy distribution. ActivityPub-class messaging with structured-data overlay (NOT raw ActivityPub — known scaling pathologies). Source: [04 §1, §7](./04_scale_architecture.md), [05 §6.3](./05_future_inference.md).

**I. Governance layer.** v0: solo operator + advisory partners. v1: 501(c)(3) or fiscal sponsor (**Code for Science & Society** primary, Open Collective Foundation backup). v3: federation governance v0. Funding rules: no funder >15% over 5-year window; no funding from investigation targets (transitively — funder's parent companies count); annual transparency report; recusal protocol when investigations touch funder's parent. **EU-primary hosting (Estonia/Netherlands) + Iceland/Switzerland mirrors; US explicitly not primary** per anti-SLAPP federal-diversity gap. Source: [06 §3, §4, §5](./06_partner_governance_funding.md), [03 §1.2, §2.4](./03_adversarial_architecture.md), [05 §5](./05_future_inference.md).

**J. Strange-loop / recognition seam.** Loomwork outputs feed back into `~/.dharma/meta/recognition_seed.md`; algedonic feedback updates evaluator weights; Loomwork's revelation history is itself an ingested data source (Ouroboros). At B5, Loomwork-trained foundation model becomes one evaluator among many — never the dominant evaluator (governance constraint, not technical). Source: spec + [04 §3.5](./04_scale_architecture.md).

**The interlock.** A through D are non-negotiable from v0. E and F are operational from v0. G escalates band-by-band. H scales with reach. I matures with scale. J runs continuously as the substrate's self-recognition. **The refusal architecture (D) is the seventh-pillar moat that prevents A–J from being replicable by Big Tech capital alone.**

## 5. The 25-Year Trajectory (B0 → B6)

**B0 — level 15 — single-operator local (this v0).** ~10⁴ atoms, M5 + DuckDB + Astro localhost, $0–200/mo, Dhyana decides everything. Ships in 14 days. The schema decisions made here either compound or constrain every later band. Internal adversary only: scope creep, atom-schema lock-in, evaluator monoculture. What survives to B6: UUID v7, content-hash, provenance-as-first-class, telos-gate metadata, multilingual encoding.

**B1 — level 25 — single-operator hosted.** ~10⁵ atoms, Postgres + pgvector + Astro on Cloudflare Pages, $500–1.5K/mo, Dhyana + advisory board (3–5 names), ~10 named partners with API access. First adversaries arrive: SEO spam, cease-and-desist letters from shell-company targets. Migration trigger from B0: DuckDB row count or first journalist asks "can I query programmatically?" What changes: documented retraction policy, public methodology page, written editorial-firewall policy.

**B2 — level 40 — small team.** ~10⁶ atoms, Postgres + Iceberg + ClickHouse + NATS/Redpanda + pgvector, $5–20K/mo, 5-director board, fiscal sponsor, ~100 partners, peer review on all revelations. Real adversaries: coordinated harassment, state-actor probing, provider-level pressure. Migration trigger: peer review becomes the bottleneck. What changes: editorial committee separate from board; conflict-of-interest declarations published; whistleblower hotline; multi-CDN posture; encrypted backups in 2+ jurisdictions.

**B3 — level 60 — NGO-tier (OCCRP-class).** ~10⁷–10⁸ atoms, sharded Postgres + Vespa + Iceberg + multi-region K8s, $50–200K/mo, independent 501(c)(3), 15–40 staff, ~1000 partner orgs, multi-region for sovereignty. State-actor adversaries directly; corporate adversaries with seven-figure legal budgets. Migration trigger: ≥1 nation-state attempt at disruption + federation surface area becomes force-multiplier. What changes: SOC 2 Type II; defense-in-depth across 7 telos gates + cryptographic source provenance; in-house fine-tuned classifiers; automated decorrelation scoring matrix.

**B4 — level 80 — federation.** ~10⁹ atoms across federation, peer Loomwork instances run by partner orgs, federation council with peer-org governance, $250K–1M/instance. Federation creates new attack surfaces (compromised peer = injection vector); multi-jurisdictional spread is structural defense. Migration trigger: federation math beats expansion math. What changes: per-instance signing keys; cross-instance integrity audits; constitutional document defining schema-evolution voting + atom-revocation + dispute resolution.

**B5 — level 95 — substrate (Wikidata-class).** ~10¹⁰+ atoms, federated SPARQL + GraphQL Federation, foundation-model-class trained on Loomwork corpus (capped at 30% of evaluator pool), $1–4M/instance, $100M+ endowment, multi-stakeholder governance (NGO partners + funders capped + readers elected + tech advisors + mission-trustees with veto). Capture risks become structural: AI vendor capture, foundation capture, legitimacy attacks. What changes: published audited evaluator-stack composition; periodic external audits by independent journalism academics; legal-protected mission constitution.

**B6 — level 100 — public utility.** Multi-decade durability. Atom count continues; meaningful unit is recognized patterns + institutional memory. Original founder gone. Standards-track. Self-sustaining. Resilient to individual nation-state pressure. ~$1B endowment. The interfaces (atom schema, federation protocol, telos-gate composability, source-provenance, evaluator-decorrelation governance) survive multiple stack rewrites. What changes about Loomwork at this point: **most of it.** What survives: the primitives.

## 6. The Critical Risks (Top 7, Honestly Named)

1. **Pandora-class evaporation** — Loomwork ships, publishes, and fades. Outcome-engineering layer prevents this only if executed end-to-end. Mitigation: ≥1 coordinated-drop drill before launch; first 10 revelations all paired with named targets and pre-existing-legal-infra slots. ([02 §3.3](./02_theory_of_change.md))

2. **Banking blockade / payment-processor cutoff** — the single highest-leverage attack against an investigative platform. Mitigation: Bitcoin treasury from day 1; multi-jurisdiction banking; donation rails on ≥3 channels; never let one financial provider exceed 40% of donation volume. ([03 §1.6](./03_adversarial_architecture.md))

3. **Funder capture via 15% rule violation** — OCCRP's USAID dependency is the cautionary tale. Mitigation: hard 15% rule enforced quarterly; funder-conflict audit by board; recusal protocol; transparent donor disclosure (Wikipedia model). ([06 opening + §4](./06_partner_governance_funding.md))

4. **Vulnerable-person false-pass through telos gates** — single failure = catastrophic. Source-protection failures are lethal: 146 environmental defenders killed in 2024 globally. Mitigation: external red-team review before any v0 launch; vulnerable-person gate as multi-evaluator ≥3-of-3 unanimity, not 2-of-3 majority; quarterly Refusal Report with vulnerable-person count category visible. ([03 §1.3](./03_adversarial_architecture.md))

5. **Evaluator pool diversity collapse at B3+** — Krogh-Vedelsby diversity term drops to zero when evaluators converge in failure modes. Mitigation: automated decorrelation scoring matrix; redundant-evaluator rotation; diversity audit annually; deliberate diversity injection (smaller open-source labs, non-Western training corpora, alternative architectures); foundation-model evaluator capped at 30% of pool weight. ([04 §3, Migration 3](./04_scale_architecture.md))

6. **Trust capture event** — a Loomwork-class platform publishes a false revelation with reputational consequence; entire category gets discredited. Mitigation: telos gates + multi-evaluator + conservative confidence floor + 35-65% political distribution discipline + algedonic auto-retract + quarterly Refusal Report with warrant-canary + adversarial red-team review. ([01 §5](./01_frontier_landscape.md))

7. **Operator burnout / capture (existential, internal).** Solo operator with 24-year contemplative grounding has more resilience than most, but burnout is the modal failure mode. Mitigation: succession plan documented by month 6 of v0; named contingency operators (≥2) by Y1; deputy structure with 6-month operational handoff; quarterly mission audit; sustainability protocol (working hours, sabbatical, rotation). ([03 §6.1](./03_adversarial_architecture.md), [06 §6](./06_partner_governance_funding.md))

## 7. The Build Path — How v0 Maps to Level 100

The 14-day v0 build (per `~/.claude/plans/floating-launching-summit.md`) ships level 15 with the irreducible level-100 primitives baked in. The mapping:

**Days 1–2 — Constitutional layer.** Boot-time `DharmaKernel.verify()` + Foundations Corpus injection + witness JSONL + telos `GateProposal` registration. **Layer A in place.** This layer is identical at B0 and B6; the implementations underneath change but the contracts don't.

**Days 3–5 — Substrate layer.** 10 atom kinds as `OntologyObj` subtypes; typed `Link` instances; lifecycle through `ActionExec`. UUID v7 + version + content-hash + evaluator-stack provenance fields populated from day 1. **Layer F in place; B0→B1 migration ready by construction.**

**Days 6–8 — Inference layer.** 3 scout adapters (GFW DIST-ALERT + Carbon Mapper + OCCRP Aleph as the first three sources, per [01 §1.1, §1.3](./01_frontier_landscape.md)); atomizer; linker; multi-evaluator gate (GLM-5 + Haiku + Sonnet, three distinct model families); 8 pattern classes wired. **Layer E in place; Krogh-Vedelsby > 0 enforced from day 1.**

**Days 9–11 — Outcome-engineering layer (LOCKED v0 per user clarification 2026-05-07).** Audience multiplexer producing ≥3 artifact shapes per revelation; coordinated-drop infrastructure with embargo timestamp + partner notification list; named-target scorer; legal-infra registry seeded with 5 frameworks (UK Modern Slavery Act, EU CSDDD, EU AI Act, USA FREEDOM Act, AMLD5). **Layer B in place — this is what makes v0 different from a Pandora-class evaporation.**

**Days 12–13 — Render + adversarial primitives.** Astro local site at `localhost:4321`; 5 hand-crafted revelations × 3 audience shapes each = 15 artifacts; C2PA verification on ingest; sigstore-signed manifest per revelation; Bitcoin donation address visible (even if no donations yet — primitive in place); refusal-report scaffold. **Layers H + G partial; G escalates at v1.**

**Day 14 — Engine autonomous revelation + ship.** EvolutionRoom proposes a revelation candidate; it passes 7 telos gates; renders to public site without human authoring. Verification battery runs (12 tests per `~/.claude/plans/floating-launching-summit.md` §Verification). **Day-14 hard gate met or build extends 7 days without scope creep.**

**What's irreducible v0 (cannot ship without):** constitutional layer (A); substrate layer (F) with all forward-compat fields; inference layer (E) with multi-evaluator from day 1; outcome-engineering layer (B); refusal architecture (D); witness logs; Bitcoin donation primitive; BHED_GNAN gate fix (BR-014); pramana provenance tagging.

**What's v1 deferred:** public domain registration; Cloudflare deploy; RSS / JSON Feed / email digest; subscribe form; 501(c)(3) or fiscal sponsor incorporation; mirror network deployment; partner outreach to TBB / OCCRP / GFW (warm-brief AFTER v0 ships, per the [06 TBB activation note](./06_partner_governance_funding.md)); Refusal Report public publication; trademark filing; insurance.

**What's v3+ deferred (Bands 2–4):** federation protocol design; in-house fine-tuned classifiers; multi-region replication; SOC 2 Type II; DID infrastructure for partner attestation; ActivityPub-overlay federation; endowment fundraising.

**Decision-gate at Day 14:** if v0 succeeds (autonomous revelation passes all 7 gates and renders to local site), proceed to v1 planning with 2-week retrospective. If v0 fails, extend by 7 days but do NOT add features — diagnose the gate failure and ship narrower. If a second 7-day extension is needed, escalate to user for scope reset.

The path from v0 to level 100 is not a roadmap; it is a sequence of phase transitions, each of which kills the previous architecture if not anticipated. The Band 0 decisions made in the next 14 days either compound or constrain every later band. **Make them with the 25-year horizon in mind.**
