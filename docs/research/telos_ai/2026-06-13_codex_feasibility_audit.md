## Part 3 - The Honest User-Zero Test

John's actual commitment is not "use it for 90 days." It is more specific:

- 20-30 minutes per morning for raw writing.
- 1-3 minutes for somatic/user input.
- 1-3 minutes for reading the witness/inquiry.
- 1 minute to mark usefulness, discomfort, action taken, and whether the session should be saved or deleted.
- 4-5 days/week for 90 days, while also building the system and not silently changing the instrument after every uncomfortable result.

The privacy commitment is real. L1 wants raw inner material persisted and encrypted (`docs/vision_maps/TELOS_AI_SEED_SPEC_V0.md:161`). If this is John's deepest written material, the system needs delete, export, backup, and recovery before it needs DID ceremony.

Representativeness risk is high. John already journals, meditates, knows the scaffold, and can supply missing meaning the product does not actually elicit. A session that feels profound to John may only be confirming the conceptual language he already carries. That makes user-zero useful for finding substrate breaks, not for proving market value.

Clean exit to user-two: do not recruit user-two until all are true:

- At least 10 John sessions completed without data loss.
- p95 visible latency under 2 minutes.
- John rates at least 6/10 sessions as useful the next day, not just immediately after.
- At least 3 sessions produce a next-day receipt: an action, changed decision, clarified refusal, or externalized artifact.
- At least 2 control mornings without TELOS are compared against TELOS mornings.
- Delete/export works and has been tested on a sacrificial session.

Fake-it failure mode: John may keep using TELOS because he wants TELOS to be true. Detect it architecturally by making negative feedback first-class: weekly "would I pay for this?" check, explicit "session was flat" button, control mornings with no model, next-day usefulness rather than same-day glow, and a hard rule that edited outputs are marked as edited. If John cannot stop a session from being stored, the consent model is fake.

## Part 4 - The Honest Revenue Model

Minimum monthly cost per active user depends more on workflow depth than model choice.

| Mode | Inference/user/month at 20 sessions | Real monthly cost once storage, backups, monitoring, auth, payment, and support are included | Viable subscription floor |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Local v0, seven layers, one standard model | $1-$3 | $5-$15 before John support time | $15-$25 only if support is near zero |
| Rich v0.5, retrieval, retries, occasional Opus/GPT priority | $4-$15 | $15-$40 | $49+ |
| High-touch Opus/fast-mode/white-glove onboarding | $20-$60+ | $75-$200+ once support is honest | $99-$250+ |

The seed's revenue model says floor is subscription and ceiling is welfare-ton upside (`docs/vision_maps/TELOS_AI_SEED_SPEC_V0.md:353-364`). The floor can work only if the product is low-support, local-first, and narrow. The ceiling cannot be claimed in v0 because the seed itself says welfare-ton verification is unresolved (`docs/vision_maps/TELOS_AI_SEED_SPEC_V0.md:432`) and requires `FitnessScore`/KALYAN (`docs/vision_maps/TELOS_AI_SEED_SPEC_V0.md:362`).

Minimum viable welfare-ton verification for v0: self-report plus next-session check plus optional third-party attestation. Example: user states an intended action, next session asks whether it happened, and an external person or artifact can attest if the user chooses. That can be called a receipt. It should not be called a welfare-ton.

Risk that v0 becomes subscription-only is high. That is survivable only if TELOS stays dogfood/local research while another revenue wedge funds the repo. If John tries to make TELOS the business before privacy/reliability/policy closure, the support and trust load will eat the solo-dev budget. The parent repo is honest that self-funding is still amber because there is "no paid invoice" (`docs/governance/REALITY_DEBT_LEDGER.md:10`), and external humans served is also amber (`docs/governance/REALITY_DEBT_LEDGER.md:11`).

Polsia comparison: the repo's market grounding pack records Polsia as "$49/mo + 20% rev-share" but also notes a "4.4x ARR gap" in linked review material (`docs/research/AI_COMPANY_OPERATOR_GROUNDING_PACK_2026-06-12.md:196-197`). `NORTH_STAR.md` warns exact Polsia/Cofounder figures are source-pending and must not be reused as verified market data (`docs/vision_maps/NORTH_STAR.md:91-95`). TELOS has a worse revenue profile than a pure company-agent product because it adds privacy, mental-health adjacency, and retention risk. It probably requires either funding or an external revenue wedge within 12 months unless it stays a private research instrument.

## Part 5 - What The Seed Is Hiding Or Underspecifying

Data custody is underspecified. L1 requires raw artifact persistence and encryption (`docs/vision_maps/TELOS_AI_SEED_SPEC_V0.md:158-165`), but the seed does not define retention, deletion, export, backups, recovery, key rotation, lost-device recovery, subpoena posture, provider logging posture, or how to test deletion. For this product category, those are not polish. They are table stakes.

Regulatory surface is underspecified. L3/L4 shadow/parts work, L7 witness synthesis, and L10 inquiry are mental-health adjacent even if the product says it is not therapy. US 2026 risk includes consumer privacy, unfair/deceptive claims, state consumer-health data laws, CCPA/CPRA for California users, GDPR if EU users are admitted, and HIPAA-like expectations if clinical integrations ever appear. The seed's "Witness, not Oracle" invariant is correct (`docs/vision_maps/TELOS_AI_SEED_SPEC_V0.md:410`), but product copy and UX must enforce that distinction.

HOLON reliability is underspecified. `HOLON_ORCHESTRATOR_BUILD_SPEC.md` says talk surface exists but core orchestrator, governed persistence, full harness, reliability, and pilot/prod phases remain missing (`docs/sovereign_holons/HOLON_ORCHESTRATOR_BUILD_SPEC.md:26-40`, `docs/sovereign_holons/HOLON_ORCHESTRATOR_BUILD_SPEC.md:92-95`). If a HOLON cannot run reliably for one local user, TELOS has no substrate. v0 reliability budget should be: 30 consecutive local sessions, zero data loss, restart recovery, p95 visible latency under 2 minutes, and kill/budget tests.

SAB readiness is overstated by implication. `NORTH_STAR.md` says SAB is dormant with zero sparks (`docs/vision_maps/NORTH_STAR.md:129-142`). The handoff's highest-leverage work list still includes authority convergence, gate semantics, witness triad, lifecycle service, and seed exemplars (`docs/missions/SAB_DHARMIC_AGORA_REMOTE_HANDOFF_2026-06-11.md:54-61`). Day-90 lattice readiness is not a free downstream effect of John journaling.

Interface is unnamed. The seed says HOLON UI evolves (`docs/vision_maps/TELOS_AI_SEED_SPEC_V0.md:292`), but does not choose web, local app, CLI, mobile, voice, email, or dashboard. Given the user-zero habit is morning pages and the operator is mobile-first, v0 should be a local-first web/PWA or simple local web app. CLI is fine for substrate verification and bad for the actual ritual.

The 90-day John cost is understated. Direct inference cost may be only $20-$200 depending on model choices and retries. The real cost is 60-100 hours of user-zero attention plus implementation time, privacy hardening, and not working on revenue. On a 20-focused-hours/week budget, that is the main burn.

Single-point-of-failure is not addressed. John is developer, operator, user-zero, incident responder, privacy officer, product manager, and likely customer support. For a system holding intimate material, that is fragile. Before user-two, there needs to be a written incident runbook and a rule for who can help when John is unavailable.

Consent enforcement is not implemented. `autonomy_policy` can validate registration (`dharma_swarm/external_agent_registration.py:137-178`), but it is not the runtime policy layer. The seed correctly calls runtime non-enforcement a "privacy-policy fiction" (`docs/vision_maps/TELOS_AI_SEED_SPEC_V0.md:132`). Do not build user-facing trust on it until enforcement exists.

DID/key ceremony may be premature. The seed routes identity through DID-like language (`docs/vision_maps/TELOS_AI_SEED_SPEC_V0.md:314-318`). For v0, a boring local key, tested delete/export, and no cloud sync are more important than decentralized identity vocabulary.

## Part 6 - The Structural Critique

Is TELOS the right next move now? As a public product, no. As a dogfood instrument that forces the repo to close P2/P3/P4/P6 with a real user-facing consequence, yes. The danger is that TELOS gives architectural elegance a new surface to expand into before the unglamorous substrate closes. `STIGMERGY_11_LAYER_SPEC_2026-03-23.md` already diagnoses decay where marks do not promote and feedback is broken (`specs/STIGMERGY_11_LAYER_SPEC_2026-03-23.md:17-29`). A 12-layer RTN can reproduce that failure at higher emotional stakes.

Simpler product: build "Morning Witness" instead of TELOS AI. It has two model layers: grounded witness and one inquiry. Everything else is storage, receipt, and next-day feedback. It would answer the only questions that matter first: does John come back, does it help the next day, does it produce a receipt, and does the substrate lose data?

If I were John, I would choose one of three paths:

1. Substrate-first for 30 days. Close P2/P3/P4/P6 with receipts, no TELOS UX. Tradeoff: emotionally less exciting, highest optionality.
2. Dogfood-only Morning Witness for 30 days. Build only L1/L2/L5/L7/L10/L11/L12-lite after minimum gate/policy wrapper. Tradeoff: fastest learning, but must be forbidden from becoming a public product.
3. Revenue-first external proof. Sell or deliver one agentic governance / code-governance sprint to a real human and use the receipt to fund TELOS. Tradeoff: delays inner-life product, but solves the repo's amber self-funding problem (`docs/governance/REALITY_DEBT_LEDGER.md:10`).

I would not build full TELOS now. I would not let L4 parts mapping, L8 Drishti, L9 compass, welfare-ton pricing, or SAB spark claims into the critical path until the boring runtime and consent machinery is demonstrably alive.
