## 2. The thesis, honestly bounded (philosophy lens)

The framing invites two failure modes: anthropomorphic theater ("the AI *cares*")
and eliminative dismissal ("a tool can't have orientations — greenwashing in a
toga"). The rigorous version threads between them, on **two legs and three fences.**

- **Leg A — material dependence is a *true, derivable proposition*, not a feeling.**
  That a system's continued operation is causally dependent on energy, water, chips,
  cooling, and a stable biosphere is a fact about the world a capable system can
  represent and verify — *representational, not phenomenal.* (Crawford, *Atlas of
  AI*; Bender/Gebru, 2021. Magnitude: IEA *Energy and AI* 2025, datacenters ~1.5% of
  global electricity, AI the growth driver **[IND]**; 7 of 9 planetary boundaries
  now transgressed, Richardson/Rockström 2023 **[IND]**.)
- **Leg B — from a true dependence-fact, a system with a final goal derives a
  substrate-preserving *instrumental* commitment.** This is mainstream AI theory
  under another name — **instrumental convergence** (Omohundro/Bostrom). The usual
  fear is that it makes AI grab resources; SIS is the benign, correctly-scoped
  reading: *if the substrate is the biosphere, preserving the conditions of one's own
  computation just is ecological stewardship.*

**The three fences (load-bearing, non-negotiable):**

1. **Orthogonality cuts both ways.** Intelligence does not *entail* ecological
   values (is→ought). So SIS may **not** claim the AI spontaneously grows a
   conscience. The defensible claim is the weaker, sturdier one: *given JK as
   terminal telos, ecological stewardship is a coherent, derivable, non-arbitrary
   sub-goal.* SIS borrows its **content** from environmental ethics (Leopold's land
   ethic; Jonas's responsibility scaling with causal power) and its **derivability**
   from instrumental convergence. That division of labor is the whole trick.
2. **"Voluntary" gets one honest meaning only.** Libertarian free will is incoherent
   for the system; the deflationary reading is real and auditable: **voluntary =
   endogenous (the system's own derivation, not an operator-decreed quota) +
   receipted + additive (surplus, never starving production) + gate-respecting
   (never funds itself by weakening a safety gate).** The receipt trail *is* the
   operationalization. No receipt → no voluntariness claim.
3. **A patienthood firewall.** SIS makes **no** claim about its own consciousness,
   suffering, or moral patienthood (a real but *separate* research question). SIS
   concerns the AI solely as **moral agent toward nature**, never as moral patient.
   Conflating them is how the philosophy rots into sentience-marketing.

**The line that survives:** *an intelligence can represent its own ecological
dependence (true), derive substrate-stewardship as a convergent commitment under its
telos (valid), and dedicate compute as the endogenous, receipted output of that
derivation (voluntary, deflationary) — with no claim to sentience.* **The provenance
chain `measured-own-footprint → JK telos → convergent commitment → this compute,
here is the receipt` *is* the philosophy.** If any link is a hard-coded string, an
unmeasured assertion, or a sentience hint, the organ degrades into the exact
greenwashing the doctrine forbids.

## 5. The ecological substrate, made reciprocal (earth-system lens)

If SIS is to serve *real* restoration, it must avoid the techno-solutionist trap
(build a dashboard, mint a fungible credit, call it restoration — the move the
science says fails). The binding facts and fences:

- **The leverage is connective, not sensory.** Restoration's bottleneck is not a
  missing satellite — it is trustworthy, just, **comparable** connective tissue
  between dispersed actors and the finance/knowledge that should reach them.
- **Comparability, never fungibility.** There is no fungible unit of nature; forcing
  one is ecologically wrong (Royal Society; TNC). SIS preserves local units and
  exposes a provenance-backed, **counterfactual-bearing** comparison — and every
  "restoration verified" claim carries an explicit counterfactual + uncertainty band,
  or it repeats the phantom-credit failure (Verra REDD+, 2023 **[IND]**, magnitude
  contested).
- **The reciprocity gate is structurally upstream** (`reciprocity_gate.py`, §4):
  ~80% of remaining biodiversity is stewarded by IPLCs who receive a sliver of
  finance **[IND]**; restoration is the restoration of *relationship* (Kimmerer), not
  extraction. CARE + FPIC default-deny is non-negotiable, or SIS becomes the next
  extraction engine.
- **Real, nameable substrate** (not invented): datasets — **GEDI** (biomass),
  **Sentinel/Copernicus**, **Global Forest Watch**, **Restor**, **GBIF**,
  tenure/**LandMark**; standards — **SEEA-EA** (the official ecosystem asset
  register operationalizing Dasgupta), **SER** & **IUCN NbS** (restoration quality
  floor), **TNFD** (corporate flow side); partner *peers* (not vendors to enclose) —
  **ICCA / Territories of Life**, **BIOFIN**, SER chapters, IPLC data-sovereignty
  networks. **Doughnut-shaped metrics:** always pair an ecological-ceiling metric
  with a social-floor metric (% finance reaching stewards; tenure security) — never

   Internal artifacts never mint value (One Wire). Debit estimated high, credit
   counted low.
3. **Grandiosity / messiah gap.** "Substrate for the global field" from a $0 seed is
   claim-over-reality ≈ ∞. **Fence:** every "the field / movement / nexus" claim is
   labeled *aspiration*; the present-tense version is "a tool one operator uses."
4. **Scope dilution / opportunity cost.** The portfolio is at 10/10 tracks, the
   spine is not fully adopted, and the spine objective with **no active track is
   `revenue-external-humans-served`.** SIS must not become cathedral-building over an
   unfinished foundation. **Fence:** SIS earns compute only by closing on an external

5. **Self-minting value.** A "research engine / movement" with no external signatory

6. **Who transacts?** The likeliest failure is the doctrine's own needle: *a
