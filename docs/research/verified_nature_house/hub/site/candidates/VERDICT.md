# Design verdict — the panel proved the thesis

**Method:** three **decorrelated** judges (a design director · an adversarial skeptic ·
a data-journalist/editor), each scoring A/B/C independently from source against the six
fixed criteria. *No* single judge decided — because judging this project's own designs
with one correlated voice would be the exact failure the project critiques.

## The finding: no consensus winner — and that IS the result

| | Designer (craft) | Skeptic (trust) | Journalist (lands/spreads) | sum |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| **A** Living Instrument | 24 | 24 | 25 | 73 |
| **B** Long Read | 24 | 27 | 24 | 75 |
| **C** Console | 28 | 22 | 27 | 77 |
| **each judge's #1** | **C** | **B** | **A** | — |

Three judges, **three different winners.** The raw sum nudges C ahead, but that's noise:
the honest reading is that **each candidate dominates a different axis** (C = craft &
distinctiveness; B = trust & accessibility; A = lands & spreads), and the right move is
not selection but **aggregation — a graft.** *This is Krogh–Vedelsby running on our own
front end: diverse competent judges, decorrelated errors, and the aggregate beats any
one of them. The design contest became the product demo.*

## What decorrelation bought (each judge caught what the others missed)

- **Skeptic (the headline catch):** Candidate A narrates its `Math.random()` constellation
  as *"Each point is a real, sourced actor."* That is the brand **fabricating the data it
  calls real** — the single worst integrity failure in the set, and **neither other judge
  saw it.** One correlated judge would have shipped it. (Fixed below.)
- **Skeptic:** C's named-org grid (esp. the **Sylvera** "rated high while peers low" claim
  with no in-row citation) is defamation-adjacent and the most screenshot-pokeable artifact
  across all three; and C's `telemetry / diagnosing field…` idiom *performs* the live
  authority the brand must not fake.
- **Journalist:** A is the only candidate that travels as a single social card — but its
  hero needs a one-line teaching label so the "aha" lands without reading the lede.
- **Designer:** C wins on pure craft/distinctiveness; A's green accents drift toward the
  eco-cliché the brief forbids; A's "Weave" section writes a check the static page can't cash.

## The verdict: ship **The Graft**, not a candidate

All three judges converged on essentially the same aggregate:

- **HERO → A's animated empty-center constellation.** The single best idea in the contest;
  the thesis *is* the moving image. (All three keep it.)
- **READER → B's editorial column + the giant diagnosis line + hedge-in-the-claim honesty.**
  The argument *felt*, not just shown; the most trustworthy chassis. (All three keep it.)
- **PROOF EXHIBIT → C's `UNOCCUPIED` actor-grid row.** The hole as an empty seat in a real
  ledger — "no actor holds this position; that is the finding." (All three keep it.)
- **HOUSE HONESTY SYSTEM → the `[IND]` / `band` / `as-of` tagging**, kept everywhere — but
  *never* inside a fake-"telemetry"/live frame.

> A makes you **feel** it · B makes you **finish** it · C makes you **believe** it.

## Binding fixes before the graft ships (from the panel)

1. **[done] Kill the lie.** A's "real, sourced actor" → honest "illustrative field, not the
   live roster." (Applied this commit.)
2. **De-fake the precision.** The Seam Index renders as a **band / qualitative** (high · ~0 ·
   0 → "≈ 0 lift"), never invented 2-decimal "measurements" of our own construct.
3. **Drop the live-instrument cosplay.** No "telemetry / diagnosing field…" framing; keep
   honest tags + an explicit `as-of` and "our construct."
4. **The actor grid ships post-`--redact`, bands not decimals, with in-row citations** — the
   Sylvera-class claims get a source on the row or get softened.
5. **Teach the aha on the visual.** One line on the hero: *"the dark middle = the verification
   layer no one occupies."*
6. **Demote A's green accents** away from eco-cliché; make the "Weave" a clearly-labelled
   *coming* preview (or cut until built).

## Honest limitation

Judged from **source + intent, not rendered pixels** (no browser). A human visual pass is
still owed on: A's hero-text contrast over the live canvas; C's `--dim` on dark for AA; B's
drop-cap at narrow widths. The graft must be built and *seen*, then re-judged.
