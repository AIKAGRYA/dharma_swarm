The Seam Index · our construct, illustrative

A hundred geniuses, no smarter than the best single one.

What the constellation shows

The field, made visible — orbiting an empty center.

Each point stands for an actor in the field — an illustrative arrangement here, not yet the live ~105-actor roster. The colors are the six clusters that don't connect to each other. The dark middle is the seam: the decorrelated, receipted, footprint-metered verification layer no one occupies. The map's whole argument is the hole in it.

In the full Atlas, every node opens its card — what it does, the structural blind-spot we read, its sources and live metrics — and the Weave lets you connect any two (or ten, or twenty) and see what emerges through the center.

The offer

A free, decorrelated second opinion on one public restoration or carbon-removal claim — surfacing the counterfactual and the disagreement single-vendor judges miss, with our own estimated footprint printed on the output. No cost, no strings. If it helps, it helps; if it's wrong, we show our work. Earned, not decreed.
