A hundred brilliant people, each holding one true piece — and nothing that connects them.

AI has stopped being abstract. It now runs on real electricity, real water, real mined silicon — and it is making content, claims, and analysis infinite and nearly free. When anything can be generated, the scarce asset becomes the one thing that cannot be faked at scale: verified truth. That is the macro-thesis under everything here.

Nowhere is the hunger for it sharper than where money tries to heal nature. A "credit" is supposed to mean a tonne kept out of the sky or a hectare brought back to life. But investigations found something like 90% of one major program's rainforest credits were likely phantom — and the tell is that buyers now pay a premium of 10–100× for the credits they can actually believe. The market is already paying, a lot, for proof it cannot reliably get.

The shape of the field

So we mapped roughly a hundred of the most serious actors working on this — the AI-energy people, the carbon market, the nature-tech labs, the restoration movement, the standards bodies, and the thinkers. They are not failures; each is expert at the slice it holds. The problem is the shape they make together.

The diagnosis, in one line

There is a proven theorem — Condorcet (1785), Krogh–Vedelsby (1995) — that a group of judges beats any single judge, but only when three conditions hold. The field has the first in abundance and neither of the others:

Diverse competence~105 expert actors
Decorrelated errorsshared blind spots
Quality aggregationnothing combines them
competence × decorrelation × aggregation = collective lift ≈ 0

A hundred competent judges, errors that move together, and no one tallying them — so the whole system gets no lift over its best single rater. The missing organ is not another sensor, rater, fund, or model. It is the decorrelated, receipted, footprint-metered verification seam that links the cost of intelligence to the repair of the living world — and that no one is standing in.

The offer

A free, decorrelated second opinion on one public restoration or carbon-removal claim — surfacing the counterfactual and the disagreement single-vendor judges miss, with our own footprint printed on the output. No cost, no strings. Earned, not decreed.
