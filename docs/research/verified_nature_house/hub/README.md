## What this is

In a world where AI makes claims infinite and nearly free, the scarce asset becomes
**verified truth**. The field that should supply it — AI-energy accounting, carbon
markets, nature-tech/MRV, ecological restoration, nature finance, and the thinkers
behind them — is a hundred brilliant actors who **don't connect to each other.** This
hub maps them honestly and stands, deliberately, in the one place no one occupies: the
**decorrelated, receipted, footprint-metered, sovereignty-respecting verification seam**
between the *cost of intelligence* and the *repair of the living world.*

It is publish-ready content (Markdown), drafted for a public surface (a site, a
newsletter, wherever the operator chooses to publish). Nothing here is live
infrastructure or a transaction.

## The honesty fences (binding on everything in this hub)

- **$0 revenue. Seed-stage.** Nothing is shipped, sold, or piloted. No aspiration is
  presented as fact.
- **Independent analysis of public actors.** The "seams" are our reading, not the
  actors' words; the actors' competence is real and credited; no bad faith is alleged.
  Named actors have not endorsed this and are not affiliated with it.
- **Every figure is sourced**, and per-unit energy/carbon/water numbers are estimates
  with ~1–2 orders-of-magnitude uncertainty, flagged on the spot.
- **The "welfare-ton" and "the Circle" are our constructs**, not field consensus.
- **We meter our own cost.** Any verification this hub eventually produces will print its
  own estimated energy/carbon footprint. An AI advocating for ecological honesty must
  first be honest about its own bill.
- **The seam is earned, not decreed.** No "everything must pass through us." Trust is
  earned one honest, useful receipt at a time — and the offer (a free decorrelated second
  opinion) is exactly that: useful first.
- **Reciprocity, not extraction.** Data sovereignty (CARE / FPIC) is a hard line; nothing
  here ingests or re-publishes community data without consent.

## The offer

A free, decorrelated **second opinion** on one public restoration or carbon-removal
claim — ensembling independent methods to surface the counterfactual and the
disagreement single-vendor judges miss, with our own footprint printed on the output.
No cost, no strings. If it helps, it helps; if it's wrong, we show our work.
