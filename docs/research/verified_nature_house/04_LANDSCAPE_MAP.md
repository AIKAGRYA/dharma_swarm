# The Field — Landscape Map (~90 actors)

**Status:** SEED. World-class landscape scan, 2026-06-27, from a 7-cluster parallel
research fan-out. Every actor below was verified with sources during research;
self-reported/vendor figures are flagged in the source lanes. **Coverage note:**
the AI-for-nature, earth-data, standards, integrity, and MRV/market clusters are
covered at depth; the *authors/economics* and *NGO/coalition* and *biodiversity-
market-operator* clusters are covered at medium depth here and flagged for a
deeper follow-up pass (those lanes were still running at synthesis time).

**Update 2026-06-27:** all seven lanes completed; the "pending" caveats below are
resolved and the author / MRV-company / finance / NGO / market rosters are folded
in. ~90 actors total. The map's purpose is not a directory — it is to locate the
**recurring hole** every cluster circles. See `05_INVARIANTS_AND_BRIDGE.md` for the
twelve cross-cluster invariants and the bridge.

## Cluster 1 — Standards, disclosure & methodology bodies

The deference stack: **Capitals Coalition** (Natural Capital Protocol — the
valuation *method*) → **TNFD** (disclosure *framework*; LEAP; proposed a "Nature
Data Public Facility" — an open admission no shared nature-data layer exists) →
**SBTN** (science-based *targets*; AR3T; only freshwater+land methods mature) →
**GRI 101** (impact-materiality *reporting*) → **ISSB/BEES** (financial-materiality
*accounting standard*, the only one capable of becoming law; exposure draft ~Oct
2026). Each defers data quality to the next; **none enforces; all admit pervasive
upstream/supply-chain data scarcity.**

## Cluster 2 — Integrity / quality-benchmark layer

- **ICVCM** (Core Carbon Principles) — rates *programs*, not credits; delegates
  field verification to the programs; **its Board approved 3 REDD+ methodologies
  over its own Expert Panel's objections — two scientists (Oeko-Institut, Infras)
  resigned.** The watchdog's own integrity is contested.
- **VCMI** (Claims Code) — demand-side; from Jan 2026 requires CCP/Article-6.4
  credits, so its claims *rest on* ICVCM's contested label.
- **Carbon-credit raters: Sylvera, BeZero, Calyx Global** — independent ratings
  that **openly disagree** (a "rating the raters" study found low cross-rater
  correlation; one Amazon project rated high by Sylvera, low by the other two).
  Single-vendor proprietary judges, not a decorrelated ensemble.

## Cluster 3 — MRV / nature-tech (21 companies verified)

- **Independent raters (cleanest, but single judges that disagree):** **Sylvera,
  BeZero, Calyx Global, Renoster.** Never sell credits; but additionality &
  permanence are *modeled, not measured*, and they assign inconsistent ratings to
  the same project.
- **Pure measurement / dMRV:** **Chloris Geospatial** (above-ground biomass only),
  **Space Intelligence** (SAR+LiDAR+optical), **CarbonSpace** (CO₂ flux; ISO
  14064-3 audited), **Albo Climate** (satellite soil-carbon *inference*).
- **eDNA / biodiversity:** **NatureMetrics** (600+ clients, $25M Series B led by
  Just Climate) — presence not abundance; ~half of TNFD metrics.
- **Restoration + self-monitoring (conflicted by design):** **Dendra Systems**,
  **Treeconomy**, **Pachama** (acquired by Carbon Direct Nov 2025), **CreditNature**.
- **Web3 / decentralized:** **Open Forest Protocol** (30+ independent validators —
  the strongest structural decorrelation in the cluster), **GainForest**,
  **Single.Earth** (token dormant).
- **Data infra / marketplaces (assess nothing):** **Cecil** (aggregates 40+
  geospatial datasets — exists *because* nature data is fragmented), **NCX** (1-yr
  carbon model rejected by Verra on permanence), **Restor**, **Vibrant Planet**.
- **Crediting programs the integrity layer rates:** **Verra** (VCS/SD VISta/Nature
  Framework; ~90% of REDD+ credits branded "phantom," 2023; CEO resigned; VM0048
  cuts ~40% of credits), **Gold Standard** (GS4GG; SDG-plus), **ART/TREES**
  (jurisdictional; HFLD carve-out left ~58M credits CCP-ineligible), **Plan Vivo**
  (60% benefit-share to communities; ex-ante crediting risk).
- **The cluster's own verdict:** *"every company is a single expert with correlated
  blind spots… no mechanism aggregating them so errors cancel — precisely the
  transcendence-condition failure."*

**Pollination** + **Climate Asset Management** (HSBC JV; $2bn ambition cut to

## Cluster 5 — NGOs, science & coalitions

**WWF** (Living Planet Index; human-rights scandal), **TNC** (Resilient Land
Mapping; offset-additionality critique), **Conservation International**, **Natural
Capital Project** (InVEST; GEP accounting), **WRI / Global Forest Watch** (best-
documented IPLC record; "loss ≠ deforestation"), **WEF** (1t.org, TFA — 100% of
2020 deforestation pledgers failed), **WBCSD**, **CISL**, **IUCN** (Red List, WDPA —
taxonomic bias, can't enforce), **UNEP-WCMC** ("paper parks"), **Crowther Lab /
Restor** (trillion-trees correction + ETH probe), **Half-Earth Project**, **Campaign
for Nature** (30×30), **Nia Tero** & **ICCA Consortium** (Indigenous-governed — the
strongest rights record *and* the data-sovereignty constraint any "nature ledger"
must honor), **Stockholm Resilience Centre** (Planetary Boundaries). Cluster verdict:
data fragmented across silos with no shared ledger; monitoring strong but
verification/causation/enforcement weak; voluntary pledges have no teeth; **everyone
wants a shared "nature ledger," no one owns one — and the closest thing (ICCA
Registry) is built on communities' right to *withhold* their data.**

## Cluster 6 — AI-for-nature & earth-data infrastructure (covered exhaustively)

**Detection/data abundance, verification absence — every one defers the decision
to a human and disclaims decision-grade output:**

- **Earth data:** NASA/ESA Landsat+Sentinel (open, warranty-free), **Microsoft
  Planetary Computer** (open data + paid Pro; access control, not provenance),
  **Planet Labs** (proprietary; generative SuperRes blurs observed vs inferred —
  itself a trust concern), **Google Earth Engine** (compute; disclaims fitness).
- **Detection platforms:** **Climate TRACE** (Gore's emissions coalition; peer-
  reviewed critique: ~50% under-estimate on non-AI plants, ~70% on urban
  vehicles; positions as *complement* to official inventories), **Global Forest
  Watch** ("tree-cover loss ≠ deforestation"; alerts are triage, not legal proof;
  ~65% of one year's alerts unconfirmed), **OpenET** ("rebuttable estimates," not
  for legal/regulatory enforcement; ~35–50% error off-cropland).
- **Foundation models:** **Google/DeepMind Perch 2.0** (bioacoustics, ~15k
  species; uncalibrated, human builds classifiers), **BioCLIP 2** (vision, 200M
  images), **SatCLIP** (location prior), **DeepMind GraphCast/GenCast** (weather;
  explicit "in no way replace official alerts").
- **Sensing/field ops:** **EarthRanger** (Ai2; situational awareness, doesn't
  verify inputs), **Skylight/Shippy** (Ai2; *the one* treating provenance/
  reproducibility as first-class — every answer links to source records — but
  still stops at the legal-judgment boundary), **Conservation X Labs Sentinel**
  (edge AI; flags → human dispatches), **Wild Me / Wildbook** ("assists you in
  photo ID but **never makes a decision for you**"), **Rainforest Connection**
  (bioacoustic alerts; false positives by design, human validation required).
- **Data commons:** **GBIF** (~3.1B records; "validity cannot be assured, use at
  own risk"), **iNaturalist** (Research Grade = community 2/3 vote, not audited),
  **Restor** (Crowther Lab; steward-drawn polygons; outcome-verification
  outsourced to a separate certifier).

## Cluster 7 — Biodiversity-credit & nature-market operators

**This is two markets wearing one name.** A real, *regulation-forced* market — **UK
Biodiversity Net Gain** (~£135–274M/yr; Environment Bank et al.) and the **EU Nature
Restoration Law** (National Plans due Sept 2026) — and a near-empty *voluntary*
market (~$8–10M cumulative sales *ever*). Voluntary operators: **Terrasos** (~$1.8M
sold — among the largest), **Wilderlands** (>100k units; Lendlease, Monash),
**Savimbo** (Indigenous; "inherently additional" — a 73-credit/$365 deal),
**rePLANET / Wallacea Trust** (the most rigorous %-uplift method; underpins Plan
Vivo Nature), **CreditNature** (grant-funded, "hasn't seen much demand"),
**Single.Earth** (dormant token), **South Pole** (Kariba scandal — 57% excess
credits; CEO resigned). Standards: **Verra Nature Framework** (Qha; "must NOT be
used as offsets"), **Gold Standard biodiversity** (slipped), **Cercarbono**. The
cluster's verdict on **why trust fails at the point of sale:** unit
incomparability + no trusted independent verifier (8 of 11 suppliers used none) +
additionality opacity + permanence + double-counting + reputational contagion +
adverse selection — **and no single party guarantees any of them.** Their own
"what a credible bridge needs" list (commensurability-not-fungibility, conflict-free
verification, RS+eDNA+ground fusion, shared anti-double-count registry, claims-
integrity rules, IPLC participation) is, almost verbatim, the substrate's spec.
