## The strict ordering (why arcs, not phases)

The ONE LAW forbids claiming status before closing a real loop. So the roadmap is three
**arcs**, strictly ordered: each must close through the world before the next is
licensed. This is the same discipline `06_THE_CIRCLE.md:152-160` and
`07_SIS_MATERIAL_LEDGER.md:152-160` already state — the CAR expresses it once as a
receipt.

### Arc n=1 — the swarm meters its own compute (the only currently-licensed move)

The recursive first proof: a SIS projector over *this repo's own* `EvidenceReceipt`s
(zero new stores; one new projector + one seed-labeled model→energy table), prices the
debit in welfare-tons, routes it to one verified restoration outcome, obtains an external
countersignature, and emits the result as the **first full Causal Action Receipt** — all
nine nodes populated once, node 4 (community authority) honestly `None`
(`01_CAUSAL_ACTION_RECEIPT.md` § "Arc n=1 worked example").

- **Why first:** it needs no new authority, no external partner to *start*, and it is the
  strange loop showing up as accounting (`07_SIS_MATERIAL_LEDGER.md:154-160`). The system
  that prices compute prices its own compute.
- **What it proves:** the torus at n=1 (`06_THE_CIRCLE.md:95-99`) — that the debit→
  throat→credit→propagation loop closes once, verifiably.
- **What it does NOT prove:** federation, community authority, or commercial demand.
  Those are Arcs 2 and 3, and claiming them off Arc n=1 is the founder delusion the fence
  guards against.

### Arc commercial — "verifiable compute offsets" (licensed only after n=1 closes)

The half a buyer actually pays for (`07_SIS_MATERIAL_LEDGER.md:98-113`). The ready-made
first engagement, already drafted: **tag ~10,000 real API calls with per-inference
carbon, offset via welfare-ton credits from one pilot restoration, measure the
welfare-ton output** → a publishable case study and the first external *acted* receipt.
The product is a tamper-evident statement — "this inference workload's estimated SIS
debit was offset by N welfare-tons of restoration verified by an independent decorrelated
quorum, receipt `sha…`" — a credible "beyond carbon neutral" claim that survives the
scrutiny the bulk-REC market is dying under. Demand is on the record: Microsoft
contracted 45 Mt of removal ([CarbonCredits.com](https://carboncredits.com/microsoft-more-than-double-carbon-removal-deals-to-45-million-tonnes-in-2025/),
`research/footprint_and_visions.md:45`); Frontier's AMC reached $1.8B with Anthropic
joining ([Frontier](https://frontierclimate.com/writing/growth-amc), `:46`).

### Arc domain — the nature-restoration outcome graph (world-scale; furthest out)

The world-scale domain: a portable CAR binding identity + community authority + capital +
evidence + reversal across existing registries, funds, and MRV providers — the "single
biggest break" the nature-finance field names in its own words
(`research/nature_finance_mrv.md:178`; `00_FIELD_MAP.md` §2). This is where node 4
(FPIC-as-revocable-record) and node 8 (challenge/reversal) finally get real owners.
Strictly downstream of Arcs n=1 and commercial; listed to show direction, not to claim.

## Move 5 — the unoccupied quadrant is the whole strategic bet

PIC aims at the empty corner of the governance terrain (`00_FIELD_MAP.md` §3;
`research/footprint_and_visions.md:144`): **legitimacy + teeth.** CCAI/vTaiwan have
legitimacy without teeth (deliberation that doesn't bind — vTaiwan "stalled" precisely
because consultation wasn't binding); per-MW fee bills have teeth without legitimacy
(coercive, no process); ReFi had neither at scale. PIC's bid: SAB-grade witnessed
deliberation (legitimacy) binding SIS debits and GAIA outcome-gated release (teeth),
sitting deliberately **between coercion and charity** (`:158`) — voluntary but verifiable.

## Move 7 — the objections ledger (all five, answered honestly)

The five hardest objections (`research/footprint_and_visions.md:150-160`). None is fully
defeated; each gets an honest posture, not a rebuttal-by-assertion.

1. **"AI is already planet-positive — why debit it?" (the NVIDIA/Huang objection,
   `:152`).** Efficiency gains (Blackwell, Earth-2) let incumbents argue the debit is
   pre-paid by progress ([Bloomberg on Huang](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-03/nvidia-ceo-says-ai-build-out-will-eventually-lower-energy-costs)).
   *Posture:* Jevons rebound + the modeling≠restoring distinction. This is a hard
   narrative fight against the dominant vendor; PIC does not pretend to win it by
   argument — it wins by producing a receipt the "pre-paid by progress" claim cannot,
   i.e. a *specific verified restoration outcome* tied to a *specific workload*.

2. **The ReFi graveyard (`:154`).** Tokenized ecological coordination already failed at
   scale (REGEN ≈ −94%; Gitcoin regen rounds crowdfund in the low thousands,
   [Coinbase REGEN](https://www.coinbase.com/price/regen-network)). *Posture:* PIC is
   **not** ReFi 2.0 — it reuses ReFi's MRV/verification learnings and **explicitly
   rejects a native speculative token**; funding comes from compute-indexed obligation,
   not token appreciation (`footprint_and_visions.md:101`). The CAR has no token
   (`01_CAUSAL_ACTION_RECEIPT.md` §7).

3. **Deliberation decays into theater without binding power (the vTaiwan objection,
   `:156`).** *Posture:* this is precisely why PIC couples legitimacy to teeth — the
   `communityAuthorityGrant` node is wired to a binding, automatically-enforced debit and
   a challenge/reversal state machine (`01_CAUSAL_ACTION_RECEIPT.md` §5), not to advisory
   consultation. If PIC can't *enforce* its debit, it collapses into the same
   legitimacy-washing — a real risk the fence keeps visible.

4. **Voluntary reciprocity is out-competed by mandatory fees — and vice versa (`:158`).**
   If states pass per-MW fees, a voluntary layer looks redundant; if they don't, operators
   have no reason to volunteer. *Posture:* PIC's niche is **standards-setter + verification
   rail**, not a competing fee. A per-MW fee still needs a way to prove the money bought a
   *verified* outcome — that verification is PIC's product regardless of whether the debit
   is voluntary or legislated. It rides either outcome.

5. **Measurement is contested at the base layer (`:160`).** Per-query energy varies
   4–20×; Scope 2 is mid-revision; water/embodied footprints barely standardized. *Posture:*
   the hardest technical dependency PIC does not control. SIS answers it not by claiming
   precision but by shipping every number inside an explicit p05/p95 band, labeled
   rebuttable and decision-useful, never legal-grade (`07_SIS_MATERIAL_LEDGER.md:64-77`).
   A banded, honest debit is defensible where a false-precise one is not.

## Federated and bioregional

The throat must not become a chokepoint-by-fiat. The federation design (referenced from
the vision braid Move 4) holds a deliberately thin center and thick edges:

- **The federated layer holds only:** identity/interoperability, the constitutional
  invariants (anti-double-write, debit≠credit, rebuttable numbers, decorrelated quorum),
  challenge rights, and receipt/IR schemas. Nothing else.
- **Local, bioregional communities retain:** custody of their data, the right to define
  what counts as legitimate benefit, and the power to **veto or revoke** via the
  `communityAuthorityGrant` + `reversalEvent` nodes. FPIC becomes a verifiable,
  revocable record instead of a static attestation — the gap the field states in its own
  words (`research/nature_finance_mrv.md:129`).
- **Existing models to compose, not replace:** Regen's Indigenous-led credit models +
  Community Staking DAO, Open Forest Protocol's community-routing, TerraFund's locally-led
  QA (`nature_finance_mrv.md:123-127`). PIC is connective tissue over these, not a
  replacement registry.

This is the Circle federated (`06_THE_CIRCLE.md`): the n=1 torus's single-org membrane
becomes a thin federated membrane, and the throat moves from one org to a shared layer —
but **who holds custody stays local.**

## What it would cost to start (the honest price of Arc n=1)

The portfolio is at **WIP 10/10** (`docs/governance/ACTIVE_TRACK.yaml`; the model is
1..N co-equal WIP-limited tracks, `CLAUDE.md`). PIC claims **no track**. Therefore
building even Arc n=1 has a real, stated cost:

1. **Compost a track first.** A new project is a new track (`CLAUDE.md` build-session
   rule), and the WIP limit is already at max. Arc n=1 cannot open until an existing
   track is closed/composted to free a slot — this is a governance fact, not a
   formality.
2. **The one genuinely new artifact** is the SIS `model→energy` table, seeded from public
   estimates and hard-labeled SEED until its source (the un-metabolized Mac file at
   `/Users/dhyana/jagat_kalyan/CARBON_ATTRIBUTION_FEASIBILITY.md`) reaches main per the
   canon-metabolism rule (`07_SIS_MATERIAL_LEDGER.md:53-69`).
3. **One new projector module** (proposed `dharma_swarm/sis/`), projection-only, touching
   no owned surface — no edits to `spine/**`, `providers.py`, `orchestrator.py`,
   `agent_runner.py` (`07_SIS_MATERIAL_LEDGER.md:127-138`).
4. **One external countersignature** on one restoration outcome — the part that cannot be
   faked and cannot be produced internally.

Until a track is composted and a slot opens, PIC stays a **braid**: read, cited, fenced,
and unbuilt. That is the correct state at 5/100.
