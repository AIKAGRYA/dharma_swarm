# Field Report: Web Evolution — What Happened to Every Prior Next-Web, and What Web 4.0/5.0 Could Credibly Mean

**Date:** 2026-07-11 | **Researcher:** Fable 5 deep-research subagent | **For:** Planetary Intelligence Commons synthesis (operator master plan, custody grade BRAINSTORM)
**Method:** 15 web searches + 8 primary-source fetches; load-bearing claims double-sourced where possible. Volatile facts carry dates. UNVERIFIED flags where confirmation failed.

## 1. Executive Summary

Every branded "next web" of the last 15 years either died, plateaued into a niche, or survived only by decomposing into primitives that solved someone's concrete, paid pain. The pattern is brutally consistent: **manifesto-first webs die; primitive-first infrastructure survives when an economically motivated actor adopts it for a non-ideological reason.** Meanwhile, the actual next web arrived unannounced in 2024–2026 — the agentic web (MCP, A2A, AP2, agent registries) — and it has a real, publicly acknowledged hole exactly where the operator's Causal Action Receipt sits: authority, burden, evidence, outcome, challenge. That hole is being filled *right now*, vertically and corporately (Visa, Cloudflare, Google, Experian), but only for commerce. Nobody owns the horizontal, constitutional, outcome-accountability layer. The window is real and it is short.

## 2. The Graveyard and the Survivors — Fate of Each Prior Next-Web (as of mid-2026)

### 2.1 TBD / Block "Web5" — DEAD (November 2024); primitives donated

- Jack Dorsey's Block announced in its Q3 2024 shareholder letter that it was "winding down TBD," the unit building "Web5," to fund bitcoin mining and the Bitkey wallet; most TBD employees were laid off. ([CNBC, 2024-11-08](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/11/08/jack-dorsey-dramatically-shutters-blocks-tbd-crypto-unit.html); [TechCrunch, 2024-11-07](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/07/block-scales-back-tidal-investment-and-shutters-tbd-in-favor-of-bitcoin-mining/); [CoinDesk, 2024-11-07](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2024/11/07/jack-dorseys-square-to-invest-more-in-bitcoin-mining-and-shut-decentralized-web5-web-venture))
- Web5 (announced June 2022) = decentralized identity + personal data stores (Decentralized Web Nodes) + verifiable data exchange, anchored to Bitcoin. It had earlier embarrassed itself by trying to trademark "Web5," reversing within hours. ([Decrypt, 2022](https://decrypt.co/116018/hours-after-trademarking-web5-jack-dorseys-bitcoin-project-tbd-reverts); [Bitcoin Magazine, 2022](https://bitcoinmagazine.com/business/jack-dorseys-tbd-presents-bitcoin-based-decentralized-web5))
- What survived: Block contributed the Web5 identity components — the `did:dht` DID method, Verifiable Credentials implementations, and Decentralized Web Nodes — to the Decentralized Identity Foundation (DIF). ([Block official, late 2024](https://block.xyz/inside/block-contributes-digital-identity-components-to-the-decentralized-identity-foundation); [DIF blog](https://blog.identity.foundation/block-contributes-to-dif/))
- **Lesson:** a corporate-sponsored next-web has a single point of failure — the sponsor's strategy. When Block pivoted, "Web5" evaporated in one earnings letter. Its only durable residue is what was handed to a neutral standards body. Also: the term "Web5" is now a *burned brand* — it names a dead Dorsey project.

### 2.2 Solid / Inrupt / Tim Berners-Lee — ALIVE BUT MARGINAL; governance moved to a nonprofit, product pivoted to "wallets"

- October 2024: Inrupt formally transferred stewardship of Solid to the Open Data Institute (ODI, co-founded by Berners-Lee). ([ODI announcement](https://theodi.org/news-and-events/news/odi-and-solid-come-together-to-give-individuals-greater-control-over-personal-data/); [Fast Company, 2024](https://www.fastcompany.com/91231379/tim-berners-lee-solid-inrupt-pod-digital-wallet))
- Inrupt's commercial pivot: from "pods" to a white-label **data wallet** product — deliberately converging with the digital-identity-wallet wave rather than fighting it. ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91231379/tim-berners-lee-solid-inrupt-pod-digital-wallet); [Inrupt](https://www.inrupt.com/solid))
- Community continues at low intensity: ODI ran "Solid World" events through Feb 2025, Jun 2025, Sep 2025, and Feb 2026. ([ODI events](https://theodi.org/news-and-events/events/solid-world-feb-2026/))
- Berners-Lee's public position going into 2025: hope for "a wave of standardisation… and data rights, human rights, and digital sovereignty." ([Euronews, 2024-12-26](https://www.euronews.com/next/2024/12/26/world-wide-web-creator-sir-tim-berners-lees-hopes-for-2025-data-rights-and-a-social-media-))
- After ~8 years, Solid has no mass adoption; even sympathetic coverage concedes it "hasn't transformed the world" because it requires buy-in from parties incentivized against it. ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91231379/tim-berners-lee-solid-inrupt-pod-digital-wallet); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid_(web_decentralization_project)))
- **Lesson:** the world's most credible web founder + a correct critique + real standards was still not enough, because Solid was architecture-first with no killer app and no distribution. Its ideas are winning anyway — through *regulation* (eIDAS 2.0 EUDI wallets) rather than through the Solid stack itself. Being right is not a go-to-market.

### 2.3 DSNP / Frequency / Project Liberty — FUNDED, INSTITUTIONALLY BACKED, STILL ADOPTION-STARVED; tried to buy distribution

- DSNP (Decentralized Social Networking Protocol) is Frank McCourt's Project Liberty protocol, running at scale on Frequency, a Polkadot parachain. MeWe adopted it in 2023. ([DSNP/MeWe use case](https://dsnp.org/about/MeWe-use-case.html); [Cointelegraph, 2023](https://cointelegraph.com/news/mewe-social-network-with-20m-users-to-integrate-with-polkadot-parachain))
- Migration progress: ~1M+ on-chain users celebrated Aug 2024; ~1.4M+ claimed by early 2025 (UNVERIFIED beyond ecosystem posts — MeWe's "20M users" headline number is a legacy registered-accounts figure, not migrated actives). ([Parachains.info](https://parachains.info/details/frequency); [PRNewswire, 2023](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/frequency-blockchain-to-deliver-decentralized-social-experience-to-mewes-20-million-users-301807957.html))
- Frequency led the Polkadot ecosystem in Q1 2025 transactions: 26.8M txns, 19.5% of the ecosystem total. ([Messari State of Polkadot Q1 2025](https://messari.io/report/state-of-polkadot-q1-2025))
- The revealing move: Project Liberty — backed by ~$500M of McCourt money, an institute partnered with Georgetown/Stanford/Sciences Po and 90+ civic organizations — concluded it needed to **buy TikTok US** ("The People's Bid," formal offer Jan 2025, ~$20B, still "on standby" as of May 2025) to get distribution for its people-own-their-data internet. ([Project Liberty](https://www.projectliberty.io/peoples-bid-for-tiktok/); [CNBC, 2025-01-09](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/01/09/billionaire-frank-mccourts-project-liberty-bids-for-tiktok.html); [The Record interview](https://therecord.media/frank-mccourt-tiktok-project-liberty-interview))
- **Lesson:** this is the closest existing analog to the operator's "peoples governance" web — and even with half a billion dollars and elite institutional legitimacy, protocol-first adoption was so slow that buying a 170M-user platform looked cheaper than organic growth. A solo operator cannot out-spend this problem; they must route around it.

### 2.4 ATProto / Bluesky — THE PARTIAL SUCCESS; growth plateauing, standards path opening, governance risk live

- Scale (2026): ~43M registered users by March 2026 (up from 41.4M end-2025); DAU ~3.5M (~9% of registered); 1.41B posts in 2025 = 61% of all posts ever on the platform. Post-election surge (13M→30M in ~4 months, Oct 2024–Jan 2025) has flattened: 30M→40M took ~7 months. ([Bluesky guide/stats roundups, 2026](https://www.poster.ly/guides/bluesky-guide); [Sprout Social](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/bluesky-statistics/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluesky))
- Money & leadership: $100M Series B closed April 2025, **led by Bain Capital Crypto**, disclosed 2026-03-19; Jay Graber stepped down as CEO 2026-03-09 (→ chief innovation officer), interim CEO venture capitalist Toni Schneider. ([TechCrunch, 2026-03-19](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/19/bluesky-announces-100m-series-b-after-ceo-transition/); [Bluesky blog](https://bsky.social/about/blog/03-19-2026-series-b))
- Governance/standards (fresh, volatile): after IETF 124 (Montreal), an **IETF ATP working group was formally approved in late March 2026**; an independent **PLC Organization** is forming to take `did:plc` identity infrastructure out of Bluesky's sole hands; IETF 125 Vienna is July 2026. ([AT Protocol Spring 2026 Roadmap](https://atproto.com/blog/2026-spring-roadmap))
- Ecosystem: 1,000+ apps used weekly on ATProto; 400k+ SDK downloads/month; Flipboard's Surf, Skylight, Flashes. ([Bluesky blog](https://bsky.social/about/blog/03-19-2026-series-b))
- The decentralization critique (Christine Lemmer-Webber, ActivityPub co-author): ATProto is "not meaningfully decentralized, and not federated" — it offers "credible exit," not exit. Relay economics: ~1TB storage July 2024 → ~5TB Nov 2024 (~$512/month just for block storage), i.e., independent full-network operators must host "all of Twitter"; `did:plc` keys were custodially controlled by Bluesky; the "shared heap" architecture concentrates cost. ([dustycloud.org](https://dustycloud.org/blog/how-decentralized-is-bluesky/); Bluesky's reply: [bnewbold](https://whtwnd.com/bnewbold.net/3lbvbtqrg5t2t); [MIT Tech Review, 2025-01-17](https://www.technologyreview.com/2025/01/17/1110063/we-need-to-protect-the-protocol-that-runs-bluesky/))
- **Lesson:** the only next-web with mainstream traction got there **product-first, protocol-second, standards-third** (IETF only after 40M+ users). Its unresolved contradiction — VC ownership of a protocol whose pitch is "the company is a future adversary" — is precisely the gap a commons-constituted alternative would exist to close. Also: content addressing went mainstream here (see §5).

### 2.5 ActivityPub / Fediverse — DURABLE, STAGNANT, CHEAP; the only pure commons that survived

- Scale (early 2026): >12M registered users, ~2M monthly actives (FediDB); Mastodon largest single platform at ~10.5M registered, ~750K–1M MAU (May 2026). Growth is wave-driven (each Twitter/Reddit disruption brings a wave; retention improving slightly per wave). Measurement is inherently fuzzy — no central registry, self-reported instance metrics. ([Fediview, 2026-03-10](https://fediview.com/articles/fediverse-in-numbers-mastodon-stats-2026/); [TheWorldData](https://theworlddata.com/mastodon-statistics/); [elest.io blog](https://blog.elest.io/the-fediverse-is-growing-why-decentralized-social-media-matters-in-2026/))
- Threads, Flipboard, and WordPress now federate over ActivityPub to varying degrees — the W3C standard (2018) quietly became the interop lingua franca for big players hedging. ([Fediview](https://fediview.com/articles/fediverse-in-numbers-mastodon-stats-2026/); [dasroot.net](https://dasroot.net/posts/2026/04/activitypub-fediverse-decentralized-social-networking/))
- Cost structure is the survival secret: a GotoSocial instance runs on a Raspberry Pi — thousands of small servers can persist indefinitely with no business model because the per-node cost is trivial. ([dustycloud.org](https://dustycloud.org/blog/how-decentralized-is-bluesky/))
- **Lesson:** a no-economic-loop gift-economy web **survives but cannot grow past its ideological base**. Its persistence trait — radically cheap local nodes, thin global standard — is exactly the "federated/bioregional" architecture in the operator's Web5 definition. Its stagnation trait — no revenue organ at all — is the failure to avoid copying.

### 2.6 Nostr — LOYAL NICHE, FLATLINED

- Measured usage is tiny and measurement-dependent: one Oct 2025 analysis counted ~21k total profiled users, ~3,675 DAU, ~14.8k MAU (69.4% monthly participation — very loyal); other metrics (daily "trusted pubkey events" >228k, early 2025) count differently. Treat all Nostr numbers as low-confidence; direction (plateau/decline in posting through late 2025) is consistent across sources. ([glukhov.org, Oct 2025](https://www.glukhov.org/post/2025/10/nostr-overview-and-statistics/); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nostr))
- Distinctives that DID work: dead-simple protocol (signed JSON events + dumb relays), strong Lightning-payments integration (82.5% growth in profiles with LN addresses), genuine censorship resistance, zero corporate owner. ([glukhov.org](https://www.glukhov.org/post/2025/10/nostr-overview-and-statistics/); [nostr.org](https://nostr.org/))
- **Lesson:** maximal protocol simplicity buys implementability and ideological loyalty but not growth. HOWEVER — Nostr's design (self-sovereign keys, signed events, interchangeable relays, one-page NIP specs) is the cheapest **witness-network pattern in production**, and the most directly reusable blueprint for a receipt-relay mesh.

### 2.7 IPFS / Filecoin — SUPPLY WITHOUT DEMAND; 2026 = the admission year

- Filecoin's own 2026 strategy states: "This year marks a shift in ecosystem focus, **away from growing supply and towards scaling demand**" — after building "exbibytes of capacity," priorities are now paid on-chain deals, network profitability as token vesting ends, and flagship clients. That is a tacit admission that token incentives built the world's largest decentralized storage network largely empty. ([Filecoin 2026 Network Strategy](https://filecoin.io/blog/posts/the-2026-filecoin-network-strategy/); [Filecoin Foundation mirror](https://fil.org/blog/the-2026-filecoin-network-strategy))
- Real, credible anchor clients exist at the archival edge: Internet Archive, Smithsonian, MIT Open Learning, Starling Lab, Flickr Foundation. ([Filecoin 2025 year in review](https://filecoin.io/blog/posts/filecoin-in-2025-year-in-review/))
- IPFS node reality (Q1 2025): ~210,000 publicly reachable nodes, but 83% churn within weeks; only ~4,900 nodes "sticky" ≥6 months. ([Medium/FileMarket overview](https://medium.com/filemarket-xyz/the-evolution-of-filecoin-and-ipfs-an-overview-of-challenges-and-future-opportunities-795ce237c4b6) — secondary source, UNVERIFIED against primary measurement study)
- **Lesson:** the content-addressing *primitive* triumphed (see §5) while the incentive-token *network* around it struggled. Any SIS/material-debit token design that rewards supplying verification capacity rather than consuming it will replay this failure.

### 2.8 The Semantic Web (the original "Web 3.0") — DEAD AS A PROGRAM, ALIVE AS EXHAUST

- The inference-driven Semantic Web died of incentive failure: annotation was costly, no economic model paid for it, and logic engines choked on the web's noise. ([arXiv unified narrative, 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.10644); [bobdc](https://www.bobdc.com/blog/stopsemanticweb/))
- What survived: schema.org structured data (>31% of web pages by 2016 and growing), JSON-LD, Open Graph, Wikidata/DBpedia feeding Google's Knowledge Graph — i.e., the annotation layer survived **because search engines paid for it with ranking**. ([ACM Queue, Schema.org history](https://queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=2857276); [CACM](https://cacm.acm.org/practice/schema-org/))
- **Lesson:** the purest case of the pattern: identical technology, dead where unpaid, ubiquitous where a giant's business model rewarded adoption.

### 2.9 The Metaverse (the thing "Web 4.0" was supposed to be) — ABANDONED BY ITS OWN CHAMPION

- February 2026: Meta announced Horizon Worlds goes "almost exclusively mobile," explicitly decoupled from Quest VR — after ~$80B cumulative Reality Labs losses since 2020, ~1,500 Reality Labs layoffs (Jan 2026), and VR studio shutdowns. Zuckerberg's bet is now AI glasses, not virtual worlds. ([TechCrunch, 2026-02-20](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/20/meta-metaverse-leaves-vr-horizon-worlds-mobile/))
- **Lesson:** $80B of the most aggressive capital in tech could not make an era happen on schedule. Era-declaration is not a strategy.

## 3. How Institutions Already Define "Web 4.0" — the EU Strategy

- On **11 July 2023** the European Commission adopted its "strategy on Web 4.0 and virtual worlds" (COM(2023) 442), deliberately dropping the word "metaverse." Its Web 4.0: the fourth generation "allow[ing] an integration between digital and real objects and environments, and enhanced interactions between humans and machines." ([Commission press release IP/23/3718](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_23_3718); [digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/towards-next-technological-transition-commission-presents-eu-strategy-lead-web-40-and-virtual); [COM(2023) 442 PDF](https://www.kowi.de/Portaldata/2/Resources/fp/2023-COM-Web4.0-Virtual-Worlds.pdf))
- Four pillars: (1) people & skills; (2) business/industrial ecosystem; (3) public services/societal progress; (4) **global standards for open, interoperable virtual worlds "not dominated by a few big players"** — with infrastructure handled by separate programs. Market projection cited: €27B (2022) → >€800B (2030) for virtual worlds. ([Taylor Wessing analysis](https://www.taylorwessing.com/en/insights-and-events/insights/2023/09/the-eus-strategy-to-lead-on-web-4); [Bird & Bird](https://www.twobirds.com/en/insights/2023/global/european-commission-presents-eu-vision-on-web-40-and-virtual-worlds))
- Implementation since has been quiet and criticized as under-promoted and thin ([metaversepolicy.eu scoring](https://www.metaversepolicy.eu/p/20-scoring-the-eus-strategy); [cep policy brief](https://www.cep.eu/eu-topics/details/eu-metaverse-strategy-web-40-virtual-worlds-ceppolicybrief.html)) — and the metaverse premise underneath it has since been abandoned by Meta itself (§2.9).
- **Strategic fact:** "Web 4.0" is *institutionally occupied* — the EU defined it as physical-digital convergence / virtual worlds. That framing is now half-orphaned because the actual transition that arrived in 2024–2026 was **agentic**, not immersive. This leaves a legible institutional slot: whatever comes *after* the EU's Web 4.0 must answer the question its own AI Act poses — who is accountable for what machines do.

### The term "Web 5.0" as of 2026

Two incompatible prior meanings, both weak: (a) academic/futurist "symbiotic/emotional web" (affective computing-flavored, no implementation) ([flatworldbusiness overview](https://flatworldbusiness.wordpress.com/flat-education/previously/web-1-0-vs-web-2-0-vs-web-3-0-a-bird-eye-on-the-definition/); [101blockchains](https://101blockchains.com/web-5-0/)); (b) Dorsey's Web5 — now a shuttered corporate project (§2.1). **The term is unclaimed but carries baggage from both.**

## 4. The Agentic Web (2024–2026): the next web that actually arrived

- **MCP** (Anthropic, Nov 2024): LLM↔tools/data standard, de facto universal. ([protocol landscape](https://getstream.io/blog/ai-agent-protocols/))
- **A2A** (Google, Apr 2025 → Linux Foundation): agent↔agent discovery/task delegation; by 2026-04-09, 150+ production organizations, SDKs in 5 languages. ([Rapid Claw A2A guide](https://rapidclaw.dev/blog/a2a-protocol-complete-guide-2026); [state of agentic standards](https://amdatalakehouse.substack.com/p/the-state-of-agentic-ai-standards))
- **AP2** (Google + 60 partners incl. Mastercard, PayPal, AmEx, Coinbase, announced 2025-09-16): agent payments via three signed **Mandates — Intent, Cart, Payment — carried as W3C Verifiable Credentials**. ([eco.com AP2 explainer](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14845479-ap2-agent-payments-protocol-explained); [AP2 2026 explainer](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15192002-ap2-protocol-explained-google-s-agentic-commerce-standard-2026))
- **Agent identity/authority (late 2025–2026, moving fast):** Visa's **Trusted Agent Protocol** (open standard, with Cloudflare/Adyen/Stripe) lets agents prove identity+authorization to merchants ([Visa GitHub](https://github.com/visa/trusted-agent-protocol); [Visa Developer specs](https://developer.visa.com/capabilities/trusted-agent-protocol/trusted-agent-protocol-specifications)); Cloudflare **Web Bot Auth** + an open **agent registry format** (Feb 2026, with Amazon Bedrock AgentCore) for cryptographic agent recognition ([Cloudflare signed agents](https://blog.cloudflare.com/signed-agents/); [Cloudflare agent registry](https://blog.cloudflare.com/agent-registry/)); Experian **"Know Your Agent"** framework tying agent transactions to verified consumer identity ([Experian, 2026](https://www.experianplc.com/newsroom/press-releases/2026/experian-announces-agent-trust-to-power-trusted-ai-driven-commer)).
- **The acknowledged accountability gap:** legal analysis in 2026 states liability for agent actions "lands on the company that deployed the agent or on no one at all" ([Clifford Chance, Feb 2026](https://www.cliffordchance.com/insights/resources/blogs/talking-tech/en/articles/2026/02/agentic-ai-and-the-liability-gap-your-contracts-may-not-cover.html)); multi-agent systems are outpacing single-agent liability frameworks ([Berkeley Tech Law Journal, Jun 2026](https://btlj.org/2026/06/multi-agent-ai-is-outpacing-the-liability-frameworks-built-for-single-agent-systems/)); California **AB 316** (effective 2026-01-01) bars the "the AI did it autonomously" defense ([myLawCLE summary](https://mylawcle.com/products/whos-liable-when-ai-agents-misbehave-a-2026-guide-to-deployer-responsibility-and-compliance-by-design/)); the **EU AI Act becomes fully applicable 2026-08-02**, with GPAI **enforcement powers (fines, evaluations, document demands) entering into application 2026-08-02** and GPAI obligations already live since 2025-08-02 ([EU AI Act implementation timeline](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/implementation-timeline/); [Chapter V enforcement](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/enforcement-of-chapter-v-under-the-eu-ai-act/); [Commission GPAI guidelines](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/guidelines-gpai-providers)); the revised EU Product Liability Directive extends strict liability to AI products from Dec 2026 ([Clifford Chance](https://www.cliffordchance.com/insights/resources/blogs/talking-tech/en/articles/2026/02/agentic-ai-and-the-liability-gap-your-contracts-may-not-cover.html)).

**Reading:** the mission-context claim — "A2A/MCP let agents communicate; FIDO/AP2 handle identity/payments; NOTHING answers under whose authority, what burden, which telos, what evidence, who can challenge" — is **~80% verified, with a caveat**. Authority-to-transact and agent identity are now actively being solved *for commerce* (AP2 mandates, Visa TAP, Cloudflare registry, Experian KYA). What remains genuinely unclaimed as of 2026-07: **material/ecological burden accounting, telos declaration, independently witnessed outcomes, challenge/reversal rights, and cross-domain learning from outcomes** — i.e., the back half of the Causal Action Receipt.

## 6. The Lessons — Why Each Next-Web Stalled

1. **No economic loop → death or stagnation.** Semantic Web annotation died unpaid, thrived when Google paid ranking for it. Fediverse persists but can't grow without one. Filecoin built supply the market didn't demand. ([ACM Queue](https://queue.acm.org/detail.cfm?id=2857276); [Filecoin strategy](https://filecoin.io/blog/posts/the-2026-filecoin-network-strategy/))
2. **Ideology-first repels the mainstream.** Web3 asked users "to learn what plumbing is before using the toilet" (seed phrases, gas); Nostr and early Mastodon onboarding filtered for believers. ([Giles Crouch](https://gilescrouch.medium.com/what-happened-to-web3-anyway-2e9e1156c935); [dev.to Web3 UX](https://dev.to/resourcefulmind/why-web3-keeps-losing-users-and-how-we-actually-fix-it-in-2025-12g))
3. **Architecture-first without a killer app doesn't compound.** Solid had standards and a founder-saint; no app anyone needed. ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/91231379/tim-berners-lee-solid-inrupt-pod-digital-wallet))
4. **Corporate sponsorship is a single point of failure.** Web5 died in a shareholder letter. Bluesky's VC structure is a live version of the same risk ("the organization is a future adversary" — their own phrase). ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/11/08/jack-dorsey-dramatically-shutters-blocks-tbd-crypto-unit.html); [dustycloud](https://dustycloud.org/blog/how-decentralized-is-bluesky/))
5. **Decentralization theater re-centralizes under load.** Web3's access layer lived on AWS (Oct 2025 outage took exchanges down); Bluesky's relays cost enough that only Bluesky runs the full network; IPFS nodes churn 83%. ([cryptoslate](https://cryptoslate.com/this-single-point-of-failure-can-kill-web3s-dream-of-an-open-decentralized-internet/); [dustycloud](https://dustycloud.org/blog/how-decentralized-is-bluesky/))
6. **Even massive capital can't force an era.** Meta: ~$80B → metaverse abandoned. Project Liberty: $500M → had to bid for TikTok to find users. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/20/meta-metaverse-leaves-vr-horizon-worlds-mobile/); [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/01/09/billionaire-frank-mccourts-project-liberty-bids-for-tiktok.html))
7. **What actually wins:** an invisible primitive + a motivated payer + a forcing function. Passkeys (platforms shipped them; phishing was the pain). C2PA (newsroom trust + EU AI Act Art. 50). VCs/DIDs (eIDAS mandate; AP2 needed signed mandates for payment liability). Structured data (SEO). ([FIDO](https://fidoalliance.org/fido-alliance-reports-accelerating-global-passkey-adoption-on-world-passkey-day-2026/); [eyesift](https://www.eyesift.com/faq/c2pa-content-credentials-2026-cryptographic-provenance-adoption/))
8. **Standards bodies are where survivors go to become permanent** — after traction, not before: ATProto→IETF WG (2026), A2A→Linux Foundation, Web5's remains→DIF, ActivityPub→W3C. ([ATProto roadmap](https://atproto.com/blog/2026-spring-roadmap); [Rapid Claw](https://rapidclaw.dev/blog/a2a-protocol-complete-guide-2026))

## 7. What a Credible "Web 5.0" Claim Would Need

**Working definition under test:** *the web becomes capable of coordinating and evolving under witnessed purpose, material accountability and reciprocal obligation.*

### Technically (compose survivors; invent nothing)
- **Receipts as W3C Verifiable Credentials 2.0** — AP2 already proved regulators-and-payment-giants will accept VC-encoded mandate chains (Intent→Cart→Payment). The Causal Action Receipt is that chain extended upstream (delegated authority, telos) and downstream (material debit, witnessed outcome, challenge/reversal). Same envelope, longer spine. ([eco.com AP2](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/14845479-ap2-agent-payments-protocol-explained); [GS1 VC landscape](https://ref.gs1.org/docs/2025/VCs-and-DIDs-tech-landscape))
- **Content-addressed, merkle-chained, signed** — ATProto's repo design (dag-cbor nodes, CIDs, signed commits, proof chains) is the production-proven pattern for non-backdateable personal data at tens-of-millions scale. ([ATProto repository spec](https://atproto.com/specs/repository))
- **Nostr-grade relay simplicity for the witness mesh** — one-page schemas, dumb interchangeable relays, self-sovereign keys — NOT ATProto-grade relays whose costs re-centralize the network. ([nostr.org](https://nostr.org/); [dustycloud relay economics](https://dustycloud.org/blog/how-decentralized-is-bluesky/))
- **Human authority anchored in passkeys** (5B deployed — the delegation root every user already has) and **agent identity via the emerging registry/Web Bot Auth patterns**. ([FIDO](https://fidoalliance.org/fido-alliance-reports-accelerating-global-passkey-adoption-on-world-passkey-day-2026/); [Cloudflare registry](https://blog.cloudflare.com/agent-registry/))
- **C2PA-style manifest binding for evidence**, learning from its failure mode: manifests must survive replication/transcoding, so receipts must live in signed logs, not strippable metadata. ([arXiv C2PA critique](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.24890v1))
- **No supply-side token.** Filecoin is the controlled experiment. Charge real money for real verification pain. ([Filecoin 2026 strategy](https://filecoin.io/blog/posts/the-2026-filecoin-network-strategy/))

### Narratively (the harder half)
- **Claim the layer, not the era.** Every era-claim (Web3, Web5, metaverse, EU Web 4.0) either died or drifted. The survivors named a function (passkeys, content credentials, agent payments). The credible move: "the **accountability layer of the agentic web**" / "causal receipts" — and let historians call it Web 5.0. The term itself is doubly burned (Dorsey's dead project + fringe "emotional web" academia). ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/11/08/jack-dorsey-dramatically-shutters-blocks-tbd-crypto-unit.html); [101blockchains](https://101blockchains.com/web-5-0/))
- **If era-positioning is ever useful, position against the EU's own sequence:** the EU officially defined Web 4.0 (2023) as physical-digital convergence, then shipped an AI Act whose enforcement (from 2026-08-02) *presupposes evidence about what AI systems did* that no current web layer produces. "Web 5.0 = the layer that makes Web 4.0 governable" is legible to the one institution that already uses this numbering. ([EU strategy](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/towards-next-technological-transition-commission-presents-eu-strategy-lead-web-40-and-virtual); [AI Act timeline](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/implementation-timeline/))
- **Arrive with a working artifact and a paying pain, not a manifesto.** The three narratives that get a hearing in 2026: compliance evidence for the AI Act; dispute/chargeback evidence for agentic commerce (the exact gap Visa TAP/AP2 leave once money moves); liability evidence for the deployer-holds-the-bag regime (California AB 316, PLD). ([Clifford Chance](https://www.cliffordchance.com/insights/resources/blogs/talking-tech/en/articles/2026/02/agentic-ai-and-the-liability-gap-your-contracts-may-not-cover.html); [myLawCLE](https://mylawcle.com/products/whos-liable-when-ai-agents-misbehave-a-2026-guide-to-deployer-responsibility-and-compliance-by-design/))
- **Show the federated-custody math up front.** The critique that killed credibility for Bluesky's decentralization was *cost accounting* (who can afford to run the thing). A bioregional commons must publish its node economics on day one and keep them Raspberry-Pi-cheap. ([dustycloud](https://dustycloud.org/blog/how-decentralized-is-bluesky/))

## 8. What This Means for the Commons — Strategic Read

**1. The history is unambiguous: the manifesto is the kiss of death; the primitive is the path.** Solid, Web3, Web5, the metaverse, and the EU's own Web 4.0 all led with a worldview and asked adoption to follow. Passkeys, VCs, CIDs, C2PA, AP2 mandates led with a boring object that solved a paid pain, and the worldview rode in silently. The Planetary Intelligence Commons should be *published as a receipt schema and a working verifier*, not as a constitution. The constitution comes out of the drawer after the third paying user. This independently confirms the 2026-07-11 upstream sweep's 7/10 convergence on "SIGN THE RECEIPT SPINE" (canon: `~/.claude/projects/-Users-dhyana/memory/MEMORY.md`, §20 "Upstream compounding sweep").

**2. The gap named in the prior synthesis is real — but it is being eaten from the commerce side right now.** As of late 2025–2026, Visa TAP answers "is this agent authorized to buy," Cloudflare/Amazon answer "which agent is this," Experian answers "which human is behind it," AP2 answers "what did the human intend to spend." That's the front third of the Causal Action Receipt, built by incumbents, for commerce, in ~9 months. What no one owns: **material/compute burden, telos, independently witnessed outcomes, challenge/reversal, and the learning graph over outcomes**. Estimate: 12–24 months before incumbents crawl upward from payments into general authority-delegation. The Commons' claim must be staked in the unowned back half, and it must interoperate with (not compete against) AP2/TAP — their mandate-VCs are the on-ramp.

**3. Solo-operator math forbids the protocol-adoption war.** Project Liberty spent $500M and partnered with Stanford and still concluded it had to buy TikTok for users. Bluesky needed $100M+ of VC. The only adoption paths open to a solo operator with zero revenue are the Nostr path (a spec so simple others implement it in a weekend) and the schema.org path (a vocabulary an economically motivated giant adopts). Concretely: the receipt schema must be a one-page NIP-style document + a reference verifier + dharma_swarm itself as the first production emitter — the operator's own agent estate is the demo corpus no competitor has.

**4. The EU AI Act date is the forcing function — use it as the wedge, not the identity.** GPAI enforcement powers activate 2026-08-02; Art. 50 machine-readable disclosure enforcement lands August 2026; PLD strict liability in December 2026; California AB 316 already bars the "autonomous AI" defense. Every deployer of agents in/into Europe suddenly needs non-backdateable evidence of what its agents did under whose authority. That is a *purchasable* pain the receipt spine answers this year — the "AI Reciprocity/SIS as first wedge" vote survives contact with the evidence, but its 2026 form is compliance-evidence, with ecological debit as the differentiating extension rather than the door-opener.

**5. Architecture verdict: the operator's federated/bioregional instinct is validated by the only two survivors.** The fediverse survives because nodes cost nothing and the global layer is just a W3C spec; Bluesky is credible only where it is content-addressed and standards-bound, and weakest where its relays centralize cost. The Commons global layer should hold exactly what the mission context says (schemas, invariants, challenge rights, identity interop) and *nothing that costs money to run*. Custody, benefit-definition, and veto stay local. This is not idealism; it is the empirically cheapest survival configuration.

**6. Do not mint a token; do not say "Web 5.0" in the first sentence.** Filecoin is the controlled experiment for supply-side token incentives (exbibytes built, demand pivot forced in 2026). And "Web5" already names a corpse; "Web 5.0" names vaporware academia. Internally, "our own Web 5.0" is a fine telos. Externally, the credible sentence is: *"Receipts for the agentic web: who authorized it, what it cost the world, what actually happened, and who can challenge it."* If the numbering war ever matters, the EU's own Web 4.0 framing provides the ladder to climb — Web 5.0 as "the layer that makes the agentic/physical web governable" is the only version of the claim that arrives with an institutional referent instead of a vibe.

**7. The unique unclaimed asset is the challenge/reversal right.** Nothing in AP2, TAP, KYA, or the registries lets an affected third party contest an agent action after the fact — yet every liability regime now being enacted presupposes exactly that capability. Witnessed outcome + standing to challenge + reversal path is simultaneously (a) technically buildable from surviving primitives, (b) legally demanded from Aug 2026, (c) owned by no incumbent, and (d) constitutionally native to the SAB/Dharmic Agora design. That intersection is the Commons' deepest defensible ground.
