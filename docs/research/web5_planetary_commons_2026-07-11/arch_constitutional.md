# 1. Elevated Thesis

**The constitution of the agentic age will not be ratified. It will accrete — one challengeable receipt at a time — and whoever seeds its case law first writes it.**

Every prior "next web" and every prior parallel polity made the same category error: they wrote the constitution as a *document* and then went looking for subjects. Garry Davis issued World Passports for 78 years to no acceptance network (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garry_Davis). Praxis holds 151,068 obligation-free "citizens" and $525M of conditional financing with nothing built (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Praxis_(proposed_city)). Liberland performs statehood rituals to an audience of Croatian police. Meanwhile the institutions that actually govern the world's coordination substrate — IETF, Wikipedia, ICANN — never ratified anything. They accreted legitimacy through *artifacts that other people voluntarily depend on*, governed by "rough consensus and running code" (https://www.icann.org/en/system/files/files/octo-043-16apr26-en.pdf). The common law of England was never ratified either. It is case law: precedent accumulated from adjudicated disputes, each one a witnessed record of a claim, a challenge, and a resolution.

The Planetary Intelligence Commons should therefore be designed **not as a constitution-with-a-ledger but as a ledger-that-grows-a-constitution**. The atomic unit is the Causal Action Receipt (CAR): identity/delegation → intention → material debit → community authority → evidence → action → independently witnessed outcome → challenge/reversal → learning. The constitutional order is what emerges when millions of these receipts, and — critically — the *adjudicated challenges against them*, become the precedent graph that all parties consult before acting. The receipt is the statute; the honored challenge is the case law; the compost archive is the law reports.

This is also the estate's own deepest law, generalized. THE ONE LAW — "no cell spawns, grows, or claims status except by closing a strange loop on a real, gated, verifiable, diversity-preserving outcome" (`docs/vision_maps/NORTH_STAR.md:57-59`) — is already a constitutional kernel. Applied to one organism it is a growth gate. Applied to *any* actor on the web — human, agent, institution, bioregion — it is a complete theory of legitimate authority: **authority is not granted, purchased, or declared; it is the decaying residue of witnessed loops closed through reality.** The Commons is THE ONE LAW offered to everyone, with SAB's authority protocol as its court system, SIS/GAIA's conservation laws as its property law, and Loomwork's refusal architecture as its bill of rights.

Three inversions define the design:

1. **Accountability precedes power.** Every failed polity in the forensics file allocated power and hoped for accountability. Here, the only thing the global layer allocates is *accountability instruments* (receipts, challenge rights, witness independence); power is a derivative quantity computed from them. You cannot buy it (DAO negation), inherit it (Auroville negation), or declare it (Liberland negation).

2. **Service precedes citizenship.** Estonia's e-residency generated €124.9M for the state in 2025 because it sold a concrete service and let identity be the by-product (https://www.e-resident.gov.ee/blog/posts/e-residents-generated-record-state-revenue-2025/). "Citizen of the world" is operationalized the same way: a citizen is anyone whose key has produced at least one receipt — by acting, witnessing, or challenging. No enrollment. No count of souls. The membership roll IS the receipt graph.

3. **Load-bearing precedes sovereign.** The operator's seed asks for a parallel lane "for if/when traditional structures give way." The forensics are unambiguous: every project that *led* with sovereignty converted its host into an adversary (Prospera's $10.6B ICSID arbitration, https://www.italaw.com/cases/9971; seasteading sunk by one election). Every survivor became load-bearing *inside* existing structures first — Team Mirai went from founding to 11 Diet seats in 20 months by making existing democracy more capable (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_Mirai); Václav Benda's parallel polis survived totalitarianism by satisfying real needs, then supplied the personnel of the post-1989 transition (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_Polis). The Commons accumulates crisis-grade legitimacy by carrying ordinary-times load: compliance evidence, dispute resolution, restoration MRV. The crisis capacity is the same organs under stress, never a separate apparatus.

The Web5 framing survives as *substance*, not branding: the web becomes capable of **coordinating and evolving under witnessed purpose, material accountability, and reciprocal obligation**. Both "Web5" (Dorsey's dead brand, https://www.cnbc.com/2024/11/08/jack-dorsey-dramatically-shutters-blocks-tbd-crypto-unit.html) and "Web 4.0" (EU-defined as virtual-worlds convergence, COM(2023)442) are occupied or burned names. Lead with the artifact; let historians do the numbering.

# 2. Architecture

## 2.1 The atom: the Causal Action Receipt as constitutional instrument

The CAR is not a log entry. It is a **signed, non-backdateable, challengeable legal instrument** with nine chained segments:

| Segment | Constitutional function | Existing fragment to extend |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Identity & delegation | Who acts, under whose authority, with what attenuation | AP2 signed VC mandates (https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/announcing-agents-to-payments-ap2-protocol); A2A Signed Agent Cards |
| 2. Intention / telos | What purpose the action declares — the *empty column* in every incumbent stack (field_agentic-protocols.md whitespace) | dharma_swarm telos gates; UCL evidence calculus |
| 3. Material / compute debit | What burden on the material world this action creates | SIS obligation_rule; EU GPAI Model Documentation Form compute/energy field |
| 4. Community authority | Which custodial community authorized action in its domain; FPIC as evidenced field, not a checkbox | GAIA jagat_kalyan_gate (`GAIA_ECO framework:208-210`); IAPB/BCA principles (https://www.biodiversitycreditalliance.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/377455_High_Level_Principles_to_Guide_the_Biodiversity_Credit_Market_En_v7_May-2025.pdf) |
| 5. Evidence | Bounded claim + methodology + ≥3 decorrelated channels | GAIA anekanta_gate; Hard Publication Invariant (`2026-06-20 spine:112-127`) |
| 6. Capital / action | What moved: money, matter, state | GAIA ledger conservation laws (`JAGAT_KALYAN_MASTER_VISION.md:71-75`) |
| 7. Witnessed outcome | Countersigned by independent witnesses above quorum | One Wire: N≥5, M≥3 (`SOVEREIGN_MANIFEST.md:163-166`) |

Cryptographically, the CAR should be a **SCITT profile (RFC 9943)** — signed statements + transparency service + receipts is already an IETF standard; the Commons supplies only the *semantics* (field_agentic-protocols.md, https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-scitt-architecture/). Legally, CARs anchor into **eIDAS qualified electronic ledgers**, which carry a statutory presumption of integrity and accurate chronological ordering across 27 member states — a ready-made legal container with no content schema yet specified (https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/questions-answers-trust-services-under-european-digital-identity-regulation). This combination — IETF envelope, EU legal presumption, Commons semantics — means the constitution's atomic unit is *admissible evidence* from day one, which is what "juridically decisive receipts" means: the exact thing Auroville's residents lacked when their moral memory lost in court to the paper authority of the 1988 Act (field_failure-forensics.md §1.4, https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/auroville-residents-have-no-right-to-be-part-of-councilcommittee-formed-by-foundations-governing-body-supreme-court-286621).

**Design rule**: receipts are commitments, never currency. The instant a receipt becomes a tradeable unit, the Commons reinvents Toucan/KlimaDAO — where immutable tokenization *amplified* garbage credits (99.9% of BCT ineligible; KLIMA −99.94%, https://carbonplan.org/research/toucan-crypto-offsets). Value accrues to verified outcome history and the reputation derived from it, never to the receipt object.

## 2.2 Three tiers of law

The constitution has a metabolism, not just a text. Three tiers, three amendment speeds:

- **Tier 0 — Invariants ("the Ten Articles," §2.3).** Amendable only by: proposal receipt → 12-month public challenge window → sortitioned Assembly ratification (§2.5) → supermajority of custodial nodes → 6-month delay before effect. Hard to change by design; ~10 articles maximum, honoring SAB's meta-law: "Do not add more laws before implementing the existing Section 0 laws" (`SAB_DHARMIC_AGORA_REMOTE_HANDOFF_2026-06-11.md:67`).
- **Tier 1 — Protocols.** Receipt schemas, quorum numbers, challenge SLAs, witness-decorrelation rules. Amended IETF-style: working-group rough consensus + running code + last-call challenge window. Document editors, not rulers.
- **Tier 2 — Case law / canon.** Everything else. Accretes through adjudicated challenges; composts on sunset. No vote needed to create it; a challenge is how you contest it.

**Sunset default**: any Tier 1/2 rule not re-witnessed (cited in a live receipt or upheld in a challenge) within 4 years composts automatically — queryable, with reasons and revival paths, per SAB law ("Rejected artifacts are compost, not trash," `REMOTE_HANDOFF:50`). Constitutions die of dead-letter accumulation; this one digests its own dead law.

## 2.3 The Ten Articles — each the negation of a named corpse

The Tier-0 invariants are derived from the graveyard, not from first principles. A constitution written by the dead is harder to argue with and impossible to accuse of naivety. Each article names its corpse.

1. **Right to Receipt.** Any party materially affected by an agentic action may demand its CAR. Consequential action without a receipt is unauthorized by default. *(Corpse: the entire agentic stack — MCP/A2A servers scanned with zero auth, no provenance standard; https://dev.to/alexmercedcoder/the-state-of-agentic-ai-standards-in-2026-mcp-a2a-webmcp-osi-and-the-protocol-stack-taking-3o2l.)*
2. **Right to Challenge.** Every authority-bearing state change is challengeable and witnessed; correction is at least as cheap as publication (`REMOTE_HANDOFF:47,49`). Challenge carries bounty; upheld challenges are the system's most honored artifacts. *(Corpses: Verra — it took unpaid journalists to surface 90%+ phantom credits, https://carbonherald.com/the-guardian-investigation-of-verra-carbon-offsets-shows-90-are-worthless/; The DAO — the only reversal available was fracturing Ethereum itself, https://www.clovr.com/blog/dao-hack/.)*
3. **Witness Independence.** No verifier may be paid, appointed, or revocable by the actor it verifies. Verification revenue must never scale with approvals. Structural mechanics per Isometric: buyer-or-commons-pays, appointed verifiers, quantified uncertainty (https://climate-decode.com/insights/vcm-series/vcm-2026-era-of-integrity/isometric-science-first-removals-registry). *(Corpse: the developer-pays joint shared by Verra, Kariba, and cookstoves' 9.2× over-crediting, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41893-023-01259-6.)*
4. **Fail-Open for Humans.** No human entitlement, standing, or presence may be denied solely because an identity or receipt system failed. The system, not the person, bears its own error budget. *(Corpse: Aadhaar — 37% ration denial in biometric-compulsory villages vs 14% offline; documented starvation deaths, https://www.epw.in/engage/article/aadhaar-failures-food-services-welfare.)*
5. **Conservation.** Sum(claimed) ≤ sum(verified); no double-counting; verify is injective; additionality and temporal coherence hold (`JAGAT_KALYAN_MASTER_VISION.md:71-75`). Direct measurement outranks counterfactual modeling. *(Corpse: counterfactual baselines — metered cookstove methodology was 6× more honest than modeled, https://gspp.berkeley.edu/berkeley-carbon-trading-project/cookstoves.)*
6. **Subsidiarity.** "Local equilibrium is allowed; global equilibrium authority is not" (`2026-06-26 spine:174`). Higher scales coordinate; they may never override legitimate lower-scale autonomy (Levin's multiscale competency, https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/bies.202400196; Beer's VSM recursion). Custodial communities define legitimate benefit, hold veto, and may revoke. *(Corpse: Cybersyn — a designed nervous system with a top-down single point of failure; and the Gaian-critique attack surface of any planetary system, https://www.academia.edu/94671630/Planetary_Intelligence_A_Gaian_Critique.)*
7. **Fork Right (exit-with-history).** Any node or community may exit with the full shared history, and the canon itself is forkable. Custody of the constitution is multi-homed across ≥3 legal wrappers in ≥3 jurisdictions; no single wrapper holds the assets. After a schism, both branches remain valid; legitimacy re-accrues to whichever branch continues honoring challenges — measured, not asserted. *(Corpse: Auroville — the protective 1988 Foundation Act contained the takeover as a dormant clause with a 33-year fuse; residents could not fork because assets were physical and the charter singular, field_failure-forensics.md §1.4.)*
8. **No Purchased Authority.** Raw output volume, token holdings, or money are never sufficient for authority or promotion (`REMOTE_HANDOFF:48`). Authority is domain-scoped, earned via countersigned receipts, and decays without renewal. *(Corpse: DAO plutocracy — <1% of holders controlling 90% of voting power; top-10 delegates unilaterally decisive in 17/23 major DAOs, https://cointelegraph.com/news/less-than-1-of-all-holders-have-90-of-the-voting-power-in-daos-report.)*
9. **Compost, not Erasure.** Rejected, superseded, and failed artifacts remain queryable with reasons and revival paths; compost is honorable, not hidden (`1000X_BUILD_PLAN:261-263`). The failure record is a first-class civic asset. *(Corpse: every registry that stalled its own investigations — Verra's Kariba review, https://reddmonitor.substack.com/p/what-has-happened-to-verras-investigation.)*
10. **The Founder Is Bound First.** Every gate binds the operator and the founding organs before it binds anyone else; founder privileges carry a published sunset schedule. Telos is never a license to skip controls. *(Corpses: FTX/EA — purpose as permission, conflicts unmonitored, https://oxfordclarion.uk/wytham-abbey-and-the-end-of-the-effective-altruism-dream/; Wonderland — unverified identity at the point of maximum custody, https://www.forbes.com/sites/earlcarr/2022/02/15/wonderland-lost/.)*

Two funding invariants live inside Article 3 and 10 as sub-clauses, imported verbatim from Loomwork canon: **no single funder >15% over any 5-year window; no funding from investigation/verification targets, transitively** (`MASTER_loomwork_level_100.md:91-92`). *(Corpses: Mozilla, ~86% funded by the adversary's monopoly rents, advocacy amputated first, https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/05/mozilla-foundation-lays-off-30-staff-drops-advocacy-division/; Matrix, deployed by 25+ governments while the commons layer begged for $100K, https://matrix.org/blog/2025/02/crossroads/.)*

## 2.4 Citizenship: the three civic verbs

"Citizens of the world" is operationalized as **participation, not enrollment**. There are exactly three ways to become and remain a citizen, and all three leave receipts:

- **ACT** — emit CARs for consequential actions under declared telos and authority.
- **WITNESS** — countersign others' receipts as an independent observer; serve when sortitioned onto witness panels (the civic duty analog of jury service).
- **CHALLENGE** — contest a receipt, a claim, or a rule; collect the bounty if upheld.

A **citizen is a keyholder with at least one receipt in any verb.** Standing is graduated by verified civic history, Wikipedia-style (anyone edits → autoconfirmed → elected-by-track-record stewards), never by identity documents, payment, or biometrics. Personhood attestation, where needed, is community-witnessed vouching — explicitly not purchased biometric enrollment. *(Corpse: Worldcoin — Brazil's ban established the doctrine that paying for biometrics voids consent, https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/world-rebukes-coverage-of-brazils-ban-on-offering-crypto-for-biometrics.)*

Three design consequences:

1. **Agents are citizens too — of a bounded class.** An agent's citizenship is always *derivative*: its receipts chain to a human or community delegation (segment 1). It can earn domain-scoped authority; it cannot hold Tier-0 amendment standing. This answers "under whose authority an agent acts" structurally: an agent with no delegation chain is, constitutionally, nobody.
2. **The first service precedes the first pledge** (Estonia pattern). A new citizen's first experience is receiving something useful — a portable, signed, court-grade record of what their agents did — not signing a manifesto. The constitution is *revealed through use* and ratified retroactively by an Assembly of actual users (§4, Year 2), inverting Praxis's 151k-signature theater.
3. **Duties are metered, light, and receipted**: accept sortition for witness panels at a bounded frequency; pay reciprocity if you deploy the commons at institutional scale (§2.5, Treasury). Rights scale with duties performed, never with wealth.

## 2.6 Authority mechanics: earned, witnessed, challenged, revoked

Authority in the Commons is a **decaying, domain-scoped stock variable**:

- **Earned**: only via countersigned external receipts above quorum (One Wire, N≥5/M≥3, `SOVEREIGN_MANIFEST.md:163-166`). Internal artifacts never touch it — self-reported output is constitutionally weightless.
- **Domain-scoped**: closing loops in watershed restoration earns watershed authority; it confers nothing over schema amendment (Levin: competency is scale- and domain-local). This kills the celebrity-capture vector that generic reputation systems import.
- **Decaying**: authority has a half-life (Tier-1 parameter; strawman 18 months) absent fresh receipts. Titles cannot fossilize.
- **Witnessed**: every authority-bearing state change writes to the log with witness trail (`REMOTE_HANDOFF:49`); read models "project truth from owners; they do not become authority" (`ACTIVE_TRACK.yaml:163-164`).
- **Challenged**: upheld challenges slash the stock in proportion to severity, with graduated sanctions (§2.5.3).
- **Revoked**: three revocation paths — challenge cascade (adjudicated), custodial veto (a bioregional node revokes an actor's authority within its domain, unilaterally, receipted), and Assembly action (for Tier-0-scale breaches). Delegation chains revoke transitively: revoking a principal freezes its agents.

Why this defeats the DAO pathology rather than repeating it: token systems made authority *purchasable and liquid*; this makes it *earned and illiquid*. ERC-8004's empirical audit — 3–15% of registrations operationally valid, sybil feedback up to 90.6% (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.26028) — is the controlled demonstration that identity + reputation *without witnessed evidence* is theater. The Commons' answer is not better reputation math; it is refusing to admit any authority input that lacks a countersigned receipt.

## 2.7 Subsidiarity: the thin waist

The global layer is a **thin waist**, deliberately analogous to IP in the internet stack. It holds exactly five things:

1. The CAR schema and its SCITT profile (Tier 1)
2. Identity/delegation primitives (interop with DIDs/VCs/passkeys — 5B passkeys already deployed, https://fidoalliance.org/fido-alliance-reports-accelerating-global-passkey-adoption-on-world-passkey-day-2026/)
3. The Ten Articles (Tier 0)
4. Challenge rights and their SLAs
5. The federated transparency log

Everything else — what counts as benefit, which actions need which authority, local gate thresholds, benefit-sharing, data custody, membership norms above the citizen floor — belongs to bioregional custodial nodes. The governance literature's warning is incorporated: nesting is decorative unless the global layer also carries *graduated sanctions* and *cheap conflict resolution* (field_governance.md:112) — hence the Challenge Desks and sanction ladder live at the waist, as the only global enforcement organs, and they enforce only the five global things. "Local equilibrium is allowed; global equilibrium authority is not" (`2026-06-26 spine:174`) is the whole doctrine in one line: the Commons may *never* hold a lever that optimizes the planet from the center. That is simultaneously the ecological-humility position (anekanta), the Beer/Levin engineering position, and the pre-emptive answer to the Gaian technocracy critique — answered by a working local-veto demo, not a manifesto (field_planetary-computation.md threats).

## 2.8 Amendment, compost, and schism

- **Amendment** runs at three speeds (§2.2). Every amendment at every tier is itself a CAR: proposal receipt, deliberation evidence, ratification countersignatures, effect date. The constitution eats its own dogfood; a constitutional change with a broken receipt chain is void on its face (the Ireland 2024 rule).
- **Compost** is the honorable second half of lawmaking: sunset defaults (4 years un-re-witnessed → compost), full queryability, revival paths. Refuted rules get the salvage treatment: every composted law records its nearest surviving kin (per the estate's own refute-needs-salvage feedback law).
- **Schism** is a *supported operation*, not a failure state. Auroville's schism became the entry wedge for takeover because there was no legitimate way to divide (field_failure-forensics.md §1.4); The DAO's schism fractured the substrate itself because there was no bounded way to disagree. Here: a forking community takes the full history (Article 7), declares the fork in a receipt, and both branches continue. Legitimacy is then *measured forward* — which branch keeps honoring challenges, closing loops, retaining witnesses. Most forks should re-merge (git taught civilization this); the ones that don't were real value pluralism, which the ONE LAW's diversity clause protects anyway ("homogenizing the loops would violate the Transcendence Principle," `NORTH_STAR.md:52-54`).

## 2.9 Legitimacy before crisis: four engines

The operator's question — how does a parallel lane earn the right to be trusted *before* it is needed — has a four-part mechanical answer:

1. **Self-binding (the founder demo).** The operator's own estate becomes the first jurisdiction: every organ's actions receipted, signed, publicly challengeable — with a standing bounty paid to anyone who proves a receipt wrong. "The system that pays you to prove it wrong" is a legitimacy engine no state possesses, and it is the one credibility artifact no corpse in the graveyard ever produced (field_failure-forensics.md opportunities: "Micro-Commons of one"). It also converts the estate's own constitutional-custody wound — the §Telos Hierarchy invariant that silently vanished for two months into an unmerged branch (canon_telos.md open question 4) — into the founding case study for why signed, witnessed canon custody matters.
2. **Load-bearing service.** Receipts as compliance and liability evidence (EU AI Act Art. 50 transparency + GPAI enforcement from 2026-08-02, PLD evidence-disclosure from Dec 2026, AB 316); challenge desks as dispute resolution; witnessed MRV for restoration finance during the CRCF registry gap (methodologies in force 2026, EU registry only Dec 2028, https://tracker.carbongap.org/policy/crcf/). Each service works entirely inside existing institutions — the Team Mirai posture.
3. **Institutional embedding.** DPG registration (type: Standard; needs the nonprofit for Indicator 3, https://www.digitalpublicgoods.net/frequently-asked-questions); IETF/W3C venues; UN Global Dialogue on AI Governance submissions (open through May 2027); Living Earth Digital Twin workshop (Sept 14–16, 2026).
