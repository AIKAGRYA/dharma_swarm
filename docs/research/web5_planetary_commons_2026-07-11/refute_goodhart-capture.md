## 0. The shape of the attack

The five essays share one immune-system story: Verra died of developer-pays verification, so the Commons wins by (a) revenue never scaling with approvals, (b) non-transferable value, (c) independent lottery witnesses, (d) a challenge organ incumbents "structurally cannot" build, (e) an outcome graph that is a read model and never authority, and (f) a self-notarized founder whose signed spine is a credibility asset "no consortium can purchase."

My attack: **each of these six defenses is aimed at the last war's capture vector, and each opens a new one.** Goodhart does not enter through the fee schedule alone; it enters through customer churn, forum shopping, coverage selection, sybil-cheap citizenship, bounty farming, precedent seeding, and graph curation. And the "incumbents can't do this" premise is contradicted by running systems (chargebacks, UDRP, the Oversight Board) plus already-shipped incumbent products (Azure Confidential Ledger sells Merkle-receipt tamper evidence as a managed service today; Visa announced dispute-resolution modernization in 2026).

## 1. CLAIM: "Incumbents/consortia structurally cannot own witnessed challenge-and-reversal" — **REFUTED**

**Where claimed:** arch_constitutional.md:244-246 ("Visa-owned dispute layer adjudicating Visa's agents is Verra adjudicating Verra's credits... a solo operator can beat every incumbent to the constitutional ground floor"); arch_dharmic.md:41 ("consortia cannot confess... a payments network cannot make reversal ordinary, because its economics run on settlement finality"); arch_dharmic.md:191-193; arch_protocol.md:161 ("the dispute organ nobody in the payment-protocol war owns"); arch_civilizational-strategist.md:108 ("the graveyard's universally missing organ").

**The refutation.** The claim is stated as a structural impossibility. Counterexamples are running at civilizational scale:

1. **Card-network chargebacks are exactly a bounded, adjudicated, reversal-as-ordinary-operation challenge layer, owned and operated by the actor-bound incumbents.** Visa alone processed ~106 million disputes in 2025; global chargeback losses ran ~$33.8B across 261M disputed transactions (https://investor.visa.com/news/news-details/2026/Visa-Unveils-New-Services-to-Modernize-Dispute-Resolution-Process/default.aspx; https://www.chargeflow.io/blog/chargeback-statistics-trends-costs-solutions; https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/Insights/2025/2025-global-chargebacks-outlook.html). The dharmic essay's specific mechanism claim — "payment networks cannot make reversal ordinary because their economics run on settlement finality" — is factually wrong about the incumbent it names: reversal-at-scale is a *load-bearing trust feature* of card economics and has been for decades. Visa is, in July 2026, actively productizing "modernized dispute resolution" — the incumbent is already moving into the exact organ the essays declare unownable by incumbents.
2. **ICANN UDRP** (1999–): domain-name challenge-and-reversal adjudicated by WIPO-class panels under a policy written by the registry ecosystem itself; tens of thousands of adjudicated transfers. An industry consortium built a challenge court binding its own members. (Training knowledge; canonical source wipo.int/amc/en/domains — re-verify before external citation.)
3. **Meta's Oversight Board** (2020–): an incumbent under legitimacy pressure chartered a nominally independent challenge court, funded via an irrevocable trust, whose case decisions bind Meta and have reversed Meta's own removals. Whatever its weaknesses, it is a direct existence proof that a trillion-dollar actor CAN construct external challenge-and-reversal over its own actions when regulators and markets demand it. (Training knowledge; oversightboard.com — re-verify.)
4. The essays' own C2PA playbook cuts against them: when regulation demanded provenance, incumbents did not fail to build it — they built the coalition and now own the steering committee (Adobe, Amazon, BBC, Google, Intel, Meta, Microsoft, OpenAI, Publicis, Sony, Truepic: https://c2pa.org/membership/). If liability regimes demand a challenge layer, the base rate says incumbents charter one — an "Agent Dispute Coalition" with FIDO/LF branding — and regulators name *it* by example.

**What the impossibility claim confuses:** incumbents cannot *credibly adjudicate claims against the house itself* (Visa's own fee disputes go to antitrust courts, not chargebacks; the Oversight Board cannot audit Meta's ad economics). That is a real, narrower gap. But "cannot own witnessed challenge-and-reversal over their networks' actors" is refuted by decades of running systems, and the strategic conclusion built on it — that the constitutional ground floor is safe from incumbent capacity — does not follow. The 12-24-month window estimate should be treated as *optimistic*, since the dispute organ is the one layer incumbents have the most operational experience running.

**nearby_survivor:** The defensible claim is scope, not impossibility: incumbent challenge layers are (a) scoped to their own commercial perimeter (payment disputes, content takedowns, domain strings), (b) never cross-platform, and (c) never binding on the platform's own conduct, material burden, or ecological/welfare externalities. A commons can own **cross-boundary challenge, challenge-against-the-platform, and non-commercial-consequence challenge** (telos, FPIC, material debit) — the columns no incumbent's dispute desk covers because no revenue attaches. "Consortia externalize confession rather than perform it" also survives as a *marketing* asymmetry for a founder-bound micro-commons — an asset at n=1, not a structural moat at n=planet.

## 2. CLAIM: "Revenue-invariance (income never scales with approvals) + buyer-pays witnessing prevents receipt-Goodhart" — **WOUNDED**

**Where claimed:** arch_commons-economist.md:21 ("no organ's revenue may ever correlate with approval rate"), :57 (Organ 1: "the party who needs to trust pays — never the actor"), :75 (rule 2, "the master rule"); arch_protocol.md:197, 226; arch_civilizational-strategist.md:104; arch_constitutional.md:71 (Article 3); arch_dharmic.md:201.

**The attack.**

1. **The wedge violates its own master rule on page one.** In the first-wedge loop (arch_commons-economist.md:163-167), "customer pays obligation + assurance fee" — and the customer is the AI company whose compute is metered, i.e., **the actor whose claims are being verified pays the assurance fee.** That is actor-pays with a lottery intermediary, not Isometric buyer-pays (where the credit *buyer*, not the project developer, pays). The design's flagship anti-Verra invariant is contradicted by its own flagship revenue loop. Per-verdict fee flatness does not fix this, because:
2. **Goodhart enters at the portfolio level through churn, not the invoice line.** A subscription customer whose receipts get REFUTED cancels. The Commons' aggregate revenue therefore *does* scale with keeping paying emitters happy, which is approval correlation with one indirection — the exact mutation the audit industry demonstrated (auditors nominally serve relying parties; engagement economics served management; Enron/Andersen). Credit-rating agencies show the conflict mutates rather than dies when you flip the payer. Publishing the treasury's receipts shows fee *structure*, not churn *selection pressure*.
3. **Per-examination witness pay creates volume Goodhart.** A witness pool paid per event (arch_commons-economist.md:57, 98: node viability needs 10-20 witnessed receipts/month) has a collective interest in maximal receipt throughput and minimal friction — soft-pass drift with no individual bribe anywhere. Rotation caps limit pairwise collusion, not pool-level culture.
4. **Forum shopping across federated nodes recreates the registry race-to-the-bottom.** Verra vs. Gold Standard standard-shopping is reproduced one level up: bioregional nodes set their own conversion rates and run their own witness pools (arch_commons-economist.md:46-51); emitters choose where to route extinguishment. Nodes that want throughput revenue converge on lenient rates and agreeable witnesses. The "fungibility firewall" bans *netting across* bioregions, not *choosing* the cheapest one. Nothing in any essay pins routing destination to the burden's provenance.
5. **The accreditation pipeline is the capture point the essays skip.** Who admits witnesses to the pool? The Witness College admits "by track record of countersignatures that survived challenges" (arch_constitutional.md:103) — but early challenges are adjudicated by desks whose case law the founder seeds (arch_constitutional.md:206). The bootstrap is circular, and whoever controls accreditation controls the oracle set regardless of fee rules. Verra's deepest failure was not the fee flow; it was that the methodology/verifier-accreditation pipeline answered to growth.

**nearby_survivor:** Revenue-invariance survives as **necessary but not sufficient**, and the design already contains its own best patch, under-weighted: Goodhart telemetry (rule 7 — Brier-scoring every witness/channel against later durability re-witness, arch_commons-economist.md:80, with `detect_goodhart_drift()` on disk). Salvaged mechanism: (a) fix the wedge to true relying-party-pays (the disclosure *consumer* — insurer, funder, regulator-facing buyer — pays the fee) or declare the actor-pays wedge honestly as such; (b) publish churn-vs-verdict correlation as a constitutional treasury metric; (c) assign extinguishment bioregion by burden provenance or lottery, never emitter choice; (d) random cross-node re-audit with node slashing. With those four, the anti-Goodhart architecture is credible defense-in-depth rather than a slogan.

## 3. CLAIM: "Junk offsets were a fungibility problem, not a fraud problem; global evidence + bioregionally non-fungible value ends the junk-offset era structurally" — **WOUNDED**

**Where claimed:** arch_commons-economist.md:13-19 (thesis), :175 (boldest claim: "every historical failure mode... excluded by construction rather than policed by audit"; "integrity failure an order of magnitude below credit markets").

**The attack.**

1. **Over-crediting flourishes with zero fungibility.** The Green Climate Fund's first REDD+ results-based payment — $96.5M to Brazil for 2014-15 results — was a non-tradeable, non-fungible, sovereign results payment, and it drew exactly the junk-offset critique: self-submitted reference levels, "national cherry-picking," avoided emissions that "in part exist only on paper" (https://www.wrm.org.uy/bulletin-articles/misguided-funding-green-climate-fund-support-for-redd; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0264837715002689; https://www.greenclimate.fund/project/fp100). No secondary market, no KlimaDAO, no commodity — the inflation lived entirely in the baseline and the measurement oracle. The root cause of junk credits is **beneficiary-shaped counterfactuals plus captured measurement**, which the economist essay itself concedes in rule 1 (cookstove over-crediting is methodology-level: 9.2x counterfactual vs 1.5x metered — and note 1.5x means metered STILL over-credits 50%). Fungibility was an *amplifier* (Toucan proved that), not the cause. A thesis that misidentifies the root cause will declare victory after removing the amplifier.
2. **"Excluded by construction" is unfalsifiable armor.** Every registry claimed its failure modes were excluded by construction; the failures arrived through the constructs (Verra's buffer pools, ICVCM's Core Carbon Principles). The order-of-magnitude integrity claim has zero receipts behind it — it is a design estimate presented in the vocabulary of a measured result, which is precisely the aspirational-as-fact pathology every canon digest flags (arch_constitutional.md:263 names it as risk 12).
3. **Bioregional rate-setting relocates the inflation, it doesn't remove it.** Locally-ratified conversion rates + local witness pools + emitter-chosen routing = Delaware effect (see §2.4). The essay's own elite-capture check (arch_commons-economist.md:81) concedes "bioregional custody without global contestability just relocates Verra to the village scale" — and then rests the entire counterweight on a challenge right whose practical accessibility to villagers is unmodeled (see §7).

**nearby_survivor:** Two components genuinely survive: (a) **non-transferability + demurrage** (see §8 — kills the financialization amplifier outright); (b) **dropping equivalence/neutrality claims** (arch_commons-economist.md:42) really does exit the counterfactual-baseline kill zone *for the reciprocity product specifically*, because a published burden-vs-flow gap statement doesn't need a baseline to be honest. The salvaged thesis: "non-fungible reciprocity accounting removes the two strongest amplifiers of the junk-offset dynamic (financialization and neutrality claims); the measurement-oracle and baseline problems remain live and must be policed by telemetry + challenge, not declared solved by construction."

## 4. CLAIM: "Within 5 years, no consequential autonomous action will be insurable, procurable, or legally defensible without a signed, registered, challengeable receipt" — **WOUNDED**

**Where claimed:** arch_protocol.md:221 (boldest claim); supporting: arch_protocol.md:12, arch_civilizational-strategist.md:89.

**The attack.**

1. **Liability regimes demand evidence, not this evidence.** PLD evidence-disclosure duties compel defendants to disclose what they have; AI Act Art. 12 mandates logging, not transparency-log registration; AB 316 removes a defense, it doesn't specify an evidentiary format. Courts have accepted ordinary business records, WORM storage, and testimony for a century; insurers underwrite on questionnaires and audits today. The inference "regimes presuppose evidence → regimes will require SCITT-registered challengeable receipts" skips the step where incumbent-controlled logging satisfies the regulator.
2. **The "embrace" half of EEE has already shipped.** Azure Confidential Ledger is a GA managed service selling append-only, Merkle-proof, cryptographically verifiable tamper-evident audit trails — receipts included — "no one, not even Microsoft, above the ledger" (https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/confidential-ledger/overview; https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/products/azure-confidential-ledger). When GPAI deployers need "non-backdateable evidence," their cloud vendor sells it as a checkbox in the console, integrated with the agent platform, satisfying Art. 12-shaped duties without any commons, any witness quorum, or any challenge organ. The essays' 12-24-month enclosure window (arch_protocol.md:227) is honest but understated: for the *evidence* layer (vs. the challenge layer), the enclosure product predates the commons.
3. **The C2PA path the essays celebrate is the extinguish scenario.** "A code of practice names CARs by example, as Art. 50 guidance names C2PA" (arch_protocol.md:207; arch_dharmic.md:172) — but the schema regulators named in the C2PA case is authored and steered by Adobe/Google/Microsoft/OpenAI/Amazon/Meta (https://c2pa.org/membership/). Regulator-naming selects for the consortium with the compliance-lobby budget. And standards venues are not neutral ground for a solo operator: Google, Apple, and Mozilla nearly killed W3C DID 1.0 with last-minute formal objections after two years of silence (https://github.com/w3c/2021-didwg-formal-objection-faq; https://www.w3.org/2022/06/DIDRecommendationDecision.html). Rough consensus favors whoever staffs the room.
4. **Insurability runs on actuarial data, not receipts.** Insurers priced cyber risk for a decade on questionnaires; they moved to telemetry only where loss data forced it. Five years is not enough loss history for agent actions to force a cryptographic-receipt underwriting standard economy-wide.

**nearby_survivor:** The narrower claims survive and are strong: (a) no *current* protocol produces **cross-organization, third-party-verifiable, contestable** action evidence (arch_protocol.md:12's citation of arXiv 2603.24775 stands); (b) per-vendor tamper-evident logs (Azure's) are precisely NOT court-durable against the vendor's own interest and NOT interoperable across boundaries — that gap is real whitespace; (c) in *specific* regulated verticals (restoration MRV in the CRCF gap, high-stakes procurement), receipt-grade evidence plausibly becomes table stakes within 5 years. Salvage: rescope the boldest claim from "no consequential action anywhere" to "no cross-boundary consequential action in liability-exposed verticals," and treat Azure Confidential Ledger as the named competitor in every pitch.

## 5. CLAIM: "Self-notarization is the credibility asset no consortium can purchase — 'the first organism that audits itself in public,' a receipt trail begun before there was anything to gain by faking it" — **WOUNDED**

**Where claimed:** arch_protocol.md:217, 221; arch_constitutional.md:148, 240; arch_civilizational-strategist.md:177; arch_commons-economist.md:121; arch_dharmic.md:183.

**The attack.**

1. **Coverage is the new leakage.** A transparency log proves what was submitted was not altered or backdated. It proves nothing about what was *never submitted*. The emitter decides which actions emit receipts; a self-notarized estate can run its embarrassing actions off-log and its flattering ones on-log, immutably. This is the direct structural analog of unmeasured leakage in carbon accounting — and no essay proposes a completeness/emission-boundary invariant. "Audits itself in public" is, as specified, "publishes a tamper-evident diary of its chosen entries in public."
2. **"Before there was anything to gain by faking it" is false on its own timeline.** The signing ceremony is being scheduled *as the centerpiece of grant applications due in 11 days* (Longview 07-22/24) and a five-essay civilizational strategy. There is enormous, documented, immediate gain — funding, standards positioning, the founding narrative. The trail begins at the moment of maximum incentive to curate it.
3. **The estate's own base rate cuts against self-attestation.** Operator memory: "EVERY prior self-report in the chain had ≥1 measurable inaccuracy" (memory `[[sakshi-ecosystem-reverification-2026-07-01]]`); receipts written for failed calls (`[[grand-orchestration-ground-truth-2026-07-02]]`); the 88/100 claim rejected to 54/100 (arch_protocol.md:175). Signing this pipeline forward makes the *log* trustworthy, not the *claims* — a signed spine full of self-reported outcomes is junk-offset-grade content in an audit-grade envelope until the witness half (the expensive, human, unbuilt half — arch_commons-economist.md:189 admits "witness-pool theater" risk) exists. Crypto-washing is the precise term for what the marketing line risks.

**nearby_survivor:** Forward-signing with a genesis confession (Receipt Zero as a signed adverse finding, arch_commons-economist.md:121) survives — it is cheap, honest, and creates real tamper-evidence plus a falsifiable public record; the standing self-challenge bounty (arch_constitutional.md:199) is the strongest single credibility mechanism in all five essays because it pays outsiders to attack the coverage problem. Salvage: add a **declared emission boundary** ("all actions of class X emit receipts; here is the process attestation") as a Ring-0 invariant, and market the artifact as "tamper-evident, challenge-open" — never "audits itself" — until at least one independent witness countersigns coverage.

## 6. CLAIM: "The outcome graph learns which interventions produce durable welfare; 'read models project truth, they never become authority' keeps it safe; it is capturable by no one" — **WOUNDED**

**Where claimed:** arch_protocol.md:14, 139; arch_commons-economist.md:23 ("the one asset that compounds and cannot be captured"), :80; arch_civilizational-strategist.md:118, 166; arch_dharmic.md:114-116.

**The attack.**

1. **Welfare is the hardest metric in existence, and the essays never operationalize it.** What the graph can score is artifact persistence (trees alive at t+1y), payment flows, and challenge outcomes. "Durable welfare" — for a village, a watershed, a species, an agent-touched labor market — is contested, multi-horizon, and value-plural. Every scored proxy invites the classic substitutions: paying on tree survival gets fast-growing monoculture plantations; paying on witnessed-outcome density gets legible short-horizon interventions over illegible long-horizon ones (the McNamara fallacy with Merkle proofs). Anekanta as doctrine does not survive contact with a Brier score: the moment channels are publicly down-weighted against "durable reality" (rule 7), someone chose the reality metric, and that choice IS the planetary value function the thin waist swears never to hold.
2. **Sybil poisoning at the input layer.** CAR-0 is self-signed and "one afternoon of integration" (arch_protocol.md:124). A graph over millions of CARs is a graph mostly over unwitnessed self-attestations — ERC-8004's 90.6% sybil pathology (the essays' own favorite citation) reproduced one level up, unless the graph filters to CAR-2+, in which case the "millions of receipts" scale claim collapses to the tiny witnessed subset.
3. **The read-model firewall fails at adoption.** "Anyone can compute one; nobody owns the canonical one" (arch_protocol.md:139) ignores network effects: the first graph cited by an insurer or the GBFF becomes canonical de facto, its curator becomes an unaccountable rating agency (S&P was also "just publishing opinions"), and the essays' own canon says read models everyone consults shape behavior — the estate's doctrine that "text IS thinkodynamic intervention" applies to dashboards too. De facto authority with de jure irresponsibility is the *worst* quadrant, and "never becomes authority" as a compiled invariant is unenforceable against adoption dynamics outside the protocol.

**nearby_survivor:** Domain-scoped graphs over CAR-2+/CAR-3 receipts with physical oracles survive: the **annual durability table for restoration** (arch_commons-economist.md:145) is measurable, falsifiable, and genuinely absent from every registry — it is the honest core of the outcome-graph vision. Salvage: (a) rename the deliverable from "durable welfare" to "durability of witnessed outcomes" until a welfare construct earns its receipts; (b) require any published graph to carry its own CAR (methodology, data cut, curator identity) so graph curation enters the challenge system; (c) pre-commit that graph outputs are inadmissible as sole authority in any Commons organ decision — compiled, not doctrinal.

## 7. CLAIM: "Citizenship-by-receipt, sortitioned witness panels, and paid challenge bounties are capture-resistant civic mechanics" — **WOUNDED**

**Where claimed:** arch_constitutional.md:84-96 (three civic verbs; "citizen is a keyholder with at least one receipt"), :103 (Witness College), :104 + arch_commons-economist.md:60 (bonds/bounties), :106 (Assembly sortition from receipt-holders), :13 ("whoever seeds its case law first writes it").

**The attack.**

1. **Citizenship is sybil-cheap.** A citizen = keyholder with ≥1 receipt in any verb; CAR-0 receipts are self-signed and free. One actor can mint a million keyholders with one receipt each. Everything downstream that draws on the citizen roll — Assembly sortition "from active citizens, stratified by verb" (arch_constitutional.md:106), witness-panel sortition, post-fork legitimacy measurement ("which branch keeps honoring challenges") — inherits a poisoned electorate. The essays demolish Worldcoin's purchased biometrics and Praxis's enrollment theater, then rebuild enrollment theater at zero marginal cost. Stratification and "graduated standing, Wikipedia-style" are gestured at but the floor is explicitly one receipt.
2. **Challenge bounties industrialize into lawfare.** Paid challenges + bounty-on-upheld + authority-slashing-on-upheld is an invitation to professional challenge farms — the patent-troll/DMCA-abuse/ADA-drive-by pattern: high-volume challenges against small emitters who settle rather than defend, while deep-pocketed actors treat bonds as petty cash to harass rivals and *accrue authority* (upheld challenges are "the system's most honored artifacts," arch_constitutional.md:70). Meanwhile bonds and challenger stakes price out exactly the affected-but-poor humans the FPIC/veto story depends on: a Kariba-class village member does not post a bond on a SCITT log. The one organ built to protect the powerless is, as priced, most usable by the powerful.
3. **Case-law seeding is a capture surface, by the design's own thesis.** "Whoever seeds its case law first writes it" (arch_constitutional.md:13) is offered as the operator's opportunity; it is equally the incumbents'. The founding precedent graph is seeded by the founder plus "3-5 named skeptics" the founder invites and pays (arch_constitutional.md:206); the Witness College bootstraps on challenge outcomes adjudicated under that seeded precedent. A well-funded actor entering in month 7 with fifty staffed challenges can buy a disproportionate share of the early precedent graph — and precedent, unlike authority stock, is designed NOT to decay.

**nearby_survivor:** The three civic verbs survive as an *ontology* (ACT/WITNESS/CHALLENGE is a genuinely better citizenship primitive than enrollment); what fails is the threshold. Salvage: (a) civic standing accrues only from **CAR-2+ (independently witnessed) receipts**, making sybil citizenship cost a real witness quorum; (b) per-key and per-delegation-chain caps on sortition weight; (c) a **subsidized challenge intermediary / ombudsman organ** (Commons-funded standing counsel for affected communities) so the challenge right is exercisable below the bond line — this also converts the fail-open-for-humans clause from adjective to institution; (d) adversarial red-team of bond/bounty calibration as a pre-launch gate, since bounty economics is where every prior adversarial-market design (bug bounties included) needed years of tuning.

## 8. CLAIM: "Material Debit Entries are non-transferable, demurrage-bearing, extinguish-only — no secondary market because there is no security; this structurally kills Toucan/KlimaDAO financialization" — **SURVIVES**

**Where claimed:** arch_commons-economist.md:36-37, :74-81 (rules 1, 5); arch_constitutional.md:53; arch_protocol.md:230.

**Why it survives my attack surface.** I tried three routes and none breaks the core mechanism:
1. *Synthetic transferability* — third parties could write derivatives referencing MDE extinguishment events; but with no bearer instrument, no registry transfer function, and a constitutional ban on index/derivative products referencing receipts (rule 5), a synthetic market has no settlement hook inside the system; it becomes an ordinary off-system bet, which cannot inflate the ledger the way BCT tokenization inflated Verra issuance.
2. *Obligation banking* — killed by demurrage by construction.
3. *Laundering via routing* — real, but it attacks the conversion-rate layer (see §§2-3), not transferability itself.

The remaining exposures are honest and already flagged in the essay: demand viability (nobody voluntarily holds a growing liability without regulatory/reputational pressure — a business-model risk, outside this attack vector) and routing arbitrage (patched by provenance-pinned or lottery-assigned routing per §2 salvage). Within the Goodhart/capture surface, this is the single strongest design decision in the five essays: it removes the entire class of failure that requires a tradeable unit as substrate.

**nearby_survivor:** n/a — claim survives as stated; adopt the routing-assignment patch to close its one adjacent leak.

## 9. Cross-cutting findings

### 9.1 Internal contradiction registry (for the operator)
- **Anti-Verra invariant vs. first wedge:** Organ 1 "never the actor pays" (arch_commons-economist.md:57) vs. wedge step 2 "customer [the metered actor] pays obligation + assurance fee" (:164). One of these must yield.
- **"Millions of CARs form the outcome graph" vs. CAR-0 self-signed sybil cost ≈ 0:** scale and integrity claims currently trade off silently.
- **"Fungibility was the root cause" vs. rule 1's own evidence** that counterfactual-baseline methodology (not trading) drove 9.2x cookstove over-crediting.
- **C2PA as adoption model vs. C2PA as enclosure precedent:** the same citation is used as hope; its governance roster is the threat model.

### 9.2 What the attack could NOT break (beyond §8)
- **SCITT-profile non-backdateability as scoped** (arch_protocol.md:101-110): pinning a receipt inside [T, T'] via mutually distrusting parties is real, standardized, zero-new-crypto engineering. The overclaim is only in what the pin *means* (see §5).

- **Fail-open-for-humans as a principle** — untested but unattacked; my §7 finding is that it lacks an institution, not that it is wrong.

### 9.5 The one-paragraph synthesis

The design's prohibitions are excellent; its positive mechanisms are under-adversarialized.
