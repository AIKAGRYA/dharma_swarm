## 1. Territorial parallel polities

### 1.1 Seasteading / Blue Frontiers (French Polynesia) — killed by host-sovereign withdrawal + no economic offer

- The Seasteading Institute signed an MOU with French Polynesia on 2017-01-13 for a semi-autonomous "seazone"; the government declared the agreement "not a legal document" and expired at end-2017 (2018-03-03), then cut ties permanently 2018-03-14 after the floating-island plan became an election liability. Local opposition ("we don't want to be a lab") and misinformation dynamics during the presidential election made the project politically radioactive. Sources: [Wikipedia: The Seasteading Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Seasteading_Institute), [RNZ: French Polynesia sinks floating island project](https://www.rnz.co.nz/international/pacific-news/351420/french-polynesia-sinks-floating-island-project), [Reason (2018-03-19)](https://reason.com/2018/03/19/seasteading-progress-halted-in-french-po/), [phys.org](https://phys.org/news/2018-03-peter-thiel-founded-floating-island-sunk-paradise.html).
- The for-profit spinoff Blue Frontiers then failed to raise US$3M via a crypto token sale in Nov 2018 — the funding model was correlated with the crypto market, and both crashed together. Source: [Wikipedia: Seasteading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seasteading).

**Kill mechanism**: existed entirely at the pleasure of a host state, offered the host political risk instead of legible value, and financed itself with an asset correlated to hype cycles. One election was enough to erase it. **Zero substrate + zero legitimacy + correlated funding.**

### 1.2 Liberland — a flag without a floor

- Proclaimed 2015 on Gornja Siga (disputed Danube pocket). Serbia said the claim "does not infringe" its territory (a quitclaim, not recognition); Croatia — which physically controls access — treats it as a provocation and has policed it since 2015. Zero UN-member recognition as of 2025. Sources: [Wikipedia: Liberland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberland), [Chicago Journal of International Law analysis](https://cjil.uchicago.edu/print-archive/extremely-loud-and-incredibly-close-still-so-far-assessing-liberlands-claim-statehood), [EBSCO Research Starters](https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/political-science/liberland).
- 2023-09-21: Croatian police demolished settlers' makeshift structures and made three arrests. Source: [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/news/croatian-police-invaded-liberland-crypto-micronation/), [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/croatian-police-are-preventing-the-settlement-of-the-micronation-of-liberland/).
- After ~10 years: "almost no structures on the land, no permanent residents, and no international recognition"; it continues to perform statehood rituals (July 2025 congressional vote, Sept 2025 Citizenship Law — reported by its own press organ, [Liberland Press](https://liberlandpress.com/2025/05/27/how-does-a-community-become-a-state-the-case-of-liberland/), so treat as self-sourced).

**Kill mechanism** (living-dead variant): **legitimacy theater**. All the symbolic apparatus of a polity (constitution, citizenship, congress) with none of the recognition, custody, or population. The lesson is that documents and tokens do not compel the physical layer; the physical layer (Croatian police) always wins absent negotiated legitimacy.

### 1.3 Free State Project / Free Town Project (Grafton, NH) — commons collapse by defunding

- The Free Town Project (2004, splinter of the 2001/2003-founded Free State Project) targeted Grafton, NH for its no-zoning, low-tax profile; newcomers cut the town's already-small budget ~30%, defunded shared services (streetlights, senior council), and litigated against the town. Grafton saw its first recorded murders (2011, 2013), rising disorder, and a famous surge of habituated, aggressive black bears feeding on unmanaged garbage — the emblem of what happens when a community dismantles its own commons maintenance. Sources: [Washington Monthly (2020-08-30)](https://washingtonmonthly.com/2020/08/30/libertarians-took-control-of-this-small-town-it-didnt-end-well/), [The New Republic: "The Town That Went Feral"](https://newrepublic.com/article/159662/libertarian-walks-into-bear-book-review-free-town-project), [Wikipedia: Free State Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_State_Project).
- Contrast — the survivor variant: the broader Free State Project persists by moving participants into New Hampshire's **existing** institutions (thousands moved; participants elected to the state legislature — per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_State_Project); exact current counts UNVERIFIED this session). Working within institutions survived; hollowing them out failed.

**Kill mechanism**: **ideological defunding of the commons** — the experiment treated shared infrastructure as pure cost, and the ecology (literal bears) invoiced them. Secondary mechanism: negative-sum relationship with the host municipality (lawsuits as strategy).

### 1.4 AUROVILLE — the critical case: a 60-year earth-tuned spiritual polity losing custody of itself

The closest real precedent to an Aurobindo-lineage, universal-township, earth-tuned parallel polity — and the most important corpse-in-progress in this file.

**Timeline and anatomy:**

- Founded 1968 under the Mother (Mirra Alfassa), Sri Aurobindo lineage; governance conflicts with the Sri Aurobindo Society in the 1970s–80s led residents to seek Government of India protection, culminating in the **Auroville Foundation Act (1988)** — the state was *invited in as protector*. Source: [Auroville Today: The Genesis of the Auroville Foundation Act](https://auroville.today/articles/2290/the-genesis-of-the-auroville-foundation-act/).
- **Nov 2021**: new Governing Board seated with IAS officer Dr. Jayanti Ravi as Secretary; December 2021 night-time bulldozing (Youth Centre / Crown Road), lock replacements, personnel replaced by Office Order. Residents allege ~20,000 trees cut since 2021 executing the "Master Plan, Perspective 2025" (residents' figure — [auroville.media crisis summary](https://auroville.media/crisis/); treat count as partisan-sourced). Second source on takeover dynamics: [Rod Hemsell, "On the Takeover of Auroville by the Government of India"](https://rodhemsell.substack.com/p/on-the-takeover-of-auroville-by-the).
- **Aug 2022**: Madras High Court sided with residents on Act violations. **2025-03-17: the Supreme Court of India reversed** — the Governing Board is Auroville's sole administrative authority; neither the Residents' Assembly nor any individual resident has a *right* to sit on any committee the Board constitutes; the Assembly may only "advise." Sources: [LiveLaw](https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/auroville-residents-have-no-right-to-be-part-of-councilcommittee-formed-by-foundations-governing-body-supreme-court-286621), [SCC Online blog](https://www.scconline.com/blog/post/2025/03/18/aurovillle-governing-board-case-act-rules-residents-no-right-committees-councils-supreme-court/).
- **Individual-level chokepoint — visas**: dozens of foreign residents denied visas, four expelled; recommendation letters annotated "has been obstructing the work progress as per the Master Plan"; Auroville Earth Institute director Satprem Maini's visa revoked after criticizing the Crown Road; 86-year-old pioneer "Frederick" denied in April 2025 (3,000+ signature petition; backdated visa granted just before departure). Sources: [The Quint](https://www.thequint.com/news/india/foreigners-auroville-tamil-nadu-intimidated-police-visa-renewal-protests-crown-road-ngt), [The News Minute](https://www.thenewsminute.com/tamil-nadu/auroville-top-official-s-visa-revoked-178931), [Auroville Today: The Visa crisis](https://auroville.today/articles/3199/the-visa-crisis/), [auroville.media summary Mar–Sep 2025](https://auroville.media/summary-march-to-september-2025/).
- **Asset stripping (residents' accounting, partisan-sourced)**: 105 of Annapurna Farm's 135 acres leased to IIT Madras without resident consultation; land-exchange losses estimated by residents at >₹240 crore (~US$28M); 15 CRPF paramilitary approved for five years; Sept 2025, 35 residents of 20+ years received termination-threat notices; a residents' vote saw 98.6% of 510 reject Board-created committees as illegitimate. Source: [auroville.media Mar–Sep 2025 summary](https://auroville.media/summary-march-to-september-2025/) (fetched 2026-07-11).
- **Internal schism as the entry wedge**: a faction of residents supported the new Board/Secretary; decades of consensus paralysis in the Residents' Assembly left development stalled and gave the interventionist narrative ("residents blocked the Mother's city for 50 years") real material to work with. Sources: [Rod Hemsell, "Exploring the Causes of Auroville's Crisis"](https://rodhemsell.substack.com/p/exploring-the-causes-of-aurovilles), [Fair Observer](https://www.fairobserver.com/world-news/saving-auroville-a-call-for-immediate-intervention/).

**Kill mechanisms (four, compounding):**
1. **Custody inversion via the legal wrapper**: the 1988 Act that protected Auroville contained the takeover as a dormant clause — whoever holds the foundation eventually holds the community. The fuse was 33 years long.
2. **Internal schism**: an unresolved internal governance failure (consensus deadlock, factions) invited and legitimized external intervention.
3. **Individual chokepoints**: members' right to exist in place (visas) was held by the host state and used to price dissent.
4. **No independent evidence layer**: in the courts, the residents' account of violations lost to the paper authority of the Act; the community had moral memory but no juridically decisive receipts.

### 1.5 Freetown Christiania (Copenhagen) — autonomy without enforcement becomes a criminal host

- Founded 1971; residents partially "normalized" by purchasing the land from the Danish state (framework from 2011–12 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freetown_Christiania)). The open cannabis market on Pusher Street was progressively captured by Copenhagen's organized gangs; after the fourth murder in three years (Aug 2023), residents decided to close the street. In March 2024 they accepted government money to redevelop the area, and on 2024-04-06 residents — in collaboration with Danish police — physically dug up Pusher Street's cobblestones. Sources: [CNN (2024-04-06)](https://www.cnn.com/2024/04/06/travel/denmark-christiania-pusher-street-shut-intl), [Copenhagen Post](https://cphpost.dk/2024-04-06/news/christiania-residents-dig-up-infamous-pusher-street-in-copenhagen/), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/dariosabaghi/2024/04/08/residents-of-denmarks-christiania-enclave-shut-down-pusher-street/).
- Post-closure, police report drug sales did **not** simply displace elsewhere — the intervention worked. Source: [The Local DK (2024-04-22)](https://www.thelocal.dk/20240422/closure-of-copenhagens-pusher-street-has-not-caused-spread-of-drug-sales).

**Kill mechanism**: **the enforcement vacuum**. A community that refuses coercive capacity does not get a coercion-free zone; it gets someone else's coercion (gangs). Fifty years on, survival required inviting the state's police and money back in — normalization as the price of continuity.

## 2. DAO failures

### 2.1 The DAO (2016) — reversal only by fracturing the substrate

- Raised ~$150M in ETH (spring 2016); a reentrancy bug let an attacker drain roughly one-third of the treasury (June 2016). The only remedy available was the Ethereum hard fork (July 2016) that reversed the theft and split the chain, creating Ethereum Classic — i.e., the "immutable" system's sole challenge/reversal mechanism was schism of the entire world-computer. The SEC's 2017 report later deemed DAO tokens securities. Sources: [Clovr: Understanding the DAO Hack](https://www.clovr.com/blog/dao-hack/), [Frontiers in Blockchain: The DAO Controversy](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/blockchain/articles/10.3389/fbloc.2020.00025/full).
- Analyses stress that the decentralized decision model itself was "slow and cumbersome in responding to the threats" — governance latency amplified a code bug into an existential event. Source: [TFSC paper on the DAO failure](https://iris.unimore.it/retrieve/f833e1fa-2772-4549-b234-713e5a8b29cd/TFSC_Blockchain%20The%20DAO.pdf).

**Kill mechanism**: **no native, bounded reversal layer.** "Code is law" means the only appeal court is a constitutional crisis.

### 2.2 ConstitutionDAO (2021) — radical transparency as strategic self-disclosure; exit costs as regressive tax

- Raised ~$47M from ~17,000 donors to bid on a U.S. Constitution copy; lost to Citadel CEO Ken Griffin at $43.2M. Griffin knew exactly what their ceiling was — the DAO's funds and communications were public — and simply bid past it. Sources: [Artnet/Felix Salmon analysis](https://news.artnet.com/market/felix-salmon-ken-griffin-constitution-2040093), [HistoryNet](https://historynet.com/2021-constitution-auction-ken-griffin/), [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/citadel-ceo-ken-griffin-spills-beans-on-why-he-outbid-constitutiondao/).
- The unwind was regressive: refunds cost >$1.5M in gas; with a median donation of $206, small donors lost large fractions (one $400 donor paid $168 in refund gas). Sources: [Quartz](https://qz.com/2093357/constitutiondao-may-pay-1-5-million-in-ethereum-gas-fees), [The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/constitutiondao-refunds-gas-fees/).

**Kill mechanisms**: (1) **naked transparency against a strategic adversary** — commitments must be hidden until they cannot be gamed; (2) **infrastructure costs socialized regressively** — the smallest participants paid the highest exit tax.

### 2.3 Wonderland (2022) — pseudonymous authority without provenance

- A "$700M+ treasury DAO" whose treasury manager, pseudonymous "Sifu," was unmasked as Michael Patryn — co-founder of the fraudulent QuadrigaCX exchange and a convicted felon (identity theft, credit-card fraud). Key decisions had in practice been taken by two people despite the DAO branding; after a chaotic stalemate, 55% voted to continue rather than dissolve. Sources: [Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/popcorn-time-crypto-project-in-chaos-after-co-founder-outed-as-fraudster/), [Forbes: Wonderland Lost](https://www.forbes.com/sites/earlcarr/2022/02/15/wonderland-lost/), [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/wonderland-s-treasury-saga-exposes-the-fragility-of-dao-projects-today).

**Kill mechanism**: **identity/provenance hole at the point of maximum custody.** A system that verifies token balances but not the history of the human holding the treasury has verified nothing that matters.

### 2.4 Governance-token plutocracy and voter apathy — the structural stats

- Chainalysis (2022, across ten major DAOs): **<1% of holders control 90% of voting power**; between 1-in-1,000 and 1-in-10,000 holders have enough tokens to *create* a proposal; 1-in-10,000 to 1-in-30,000 enough to *pass* one. Sources: [Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/less-than-1-of-all-holders-have-90-of-the-voting-power-in-daos-report), [CryptoPotato](https://cryptopotato.com/1-of-dao-members-controlling-90-voting-power-chainalysis/).
- Participation: typical DAO voter turnout **5–17%** (2024–2025 aggregations); an OpenZeppelin-attributed 2024 review found that in **17 of 23 major DAOs the top-10 delegates alone could pass proposals unilaterally** (aggregated stat — [CoinLaw](https://coinlaw.io/dao-treasury-holdings-statistics/), [PatentPC](https://patentpc.com/blog/dao-growth-stats-treasury-sizes-governance-votes-activity); primary OpenZeppelin document not fetched — **UNVERIFIED at source**). "Rational apathy": evaluating proposals costs more than any expected influence of one vote. Sources: [Forbes (2026-04-04): DAOs Keep Centralizing](https://www.forbes.com/sites/digital-assets/2026/04/04/daos-keep-centralizingdecades-of-governance-research-explain-why/), [arXiv: The Hidden Shortcomings of (D)AOs](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.12125), [arXiv: On the Centralization of Governance Power in DAOs](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.25959).
- Ecosystem-level: of ~13–14K tracked DAOs, only ~220 hold treasuries above $1M, and "fewer than 80 run governance an institutional observer would call healthy"; top five DAOs hold >70% of ecosystem treasury value. Source: [CoinLaw DAO statistics](https://coinlaw.io/dao-treasury-holdings-statistics/) (aggregator; treat exact counts as indicative).

**Kill mechanism**: **purchasable authority converges to plutocracy; mass voting converges to apathy.** Token-weighted "one-coin-one-vote" reliably reproduces the oligarchy it was built to escape, with lower accountability than the systems it replaced.

## 3. Credit-market Goodhart: when the measure becomes the mint

### 3.1 Verra rainforest credits — 90%+ phantom

- The Guardian / Die Zeit / SourceMaterial nine-month investigation (published 2023-01-18) found **more than 90% of Verra's rainforest (REDD+) offsets are likely "phantom credits"** representing no genuine carbon reduction; forest threat baselines were overstated by ~400% on average (drawing on a 2022 Cambridge-linked study; ~95M credits across analyzed projects). Sources: [Carbon Herald summary](https://carbonherald.com/the-guardian-investigation-of-verra-carbon-offsets-shows-90-are-worthless/), [EcoWatch: 94% "phantom credits"](https://www.ecowatch.com/phantom-credits-verra.html), [Carbon Brief roundup](https://www.carbonbrief.org/cropped-25-january-2023-carbon-offsets-controversy-trade-wars-methane-round-up/), [Verra's rebuttal](https://verra.org/verra-response-guardian-rainforest-carbon-offsets/). (Guardian original blocked from direct fetch; findings verified across three independent secondary sources.)

### 3.2 South Pole / Kariba — the flagship project as junk factory

- Kariba (Zimbabwe), South Pole's flagship REDD+ megaproject: baseline deforestation grossly exaggerated; documents suggest early estimates of 52M tonnes were inflated toward 197M; Verra's own review found **57% of Kariba's ~27M issued credits were "in excess"**; most of the ~€100M in proceeds went to South Pole and partners, not local communities; South Pole exited the project in late 2023. Sources: [SWI swissinfo](https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/sci-tech/swiss-carbon-offset-giant-pulls-plug-zimbabwe-project-amid-forest-protection-allegations/48927908), [Climate Home News (2025-09-30)](https://www.climatechangenews.com/2025/09/30/zimbabwe-forest-carbon-megaproject-generated-millions-of-junk-credits/), [Transparency International case page](https://www.transparency.org/en/projects/climate-governance-integrity-programme/climate-corruption-atlas/south-pole-under-fire-for-faulty-credits-claims), [REDD-Monitor on Verra's stalled investigation](https://reddmonitor.substack.com/p/what-has-happened-to-verras-investigation).

### 3.3 Cookstove credits — 9.2× over-credited

- UC Berkeley study (Nature Sustainability, Jan 2024; 51 projects, 25 countries, ~40% of all cookstove credits issued): cookstove offsets over-credited by a factor of ~9.2 (≈10×). Notably, Gold Standard's **metered** methodology — which measures actual fuel use rather than modeling it — was only ~1.5× over-credited: direct measurement beats counterfactual modeling by ~6×. Sources: [Nature Sustainability paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41893-023-01259-6), [Berkeley News](https://news.berkeley.edu/2024/01/23/as-carbon-offsets-cookstove-emission-credits-are-greatly-overestimated/), [Berkeley Carbon Trading Project](https://gspp.berkeley.edu/berkeley-carbon-trading-project/cookstoves), [Gold Standard response](https://www.goldstandard.org/news/gold-standards-response-to-allegations-of-over-crediting-clean-cooking).

**Kill mechanisms (all three cases share them):**
1. **Issuer-verifier revenue entanglement**: registries and validators are paid per credit issued; every economic incentive points toward approval.
2. **Counterfactual baselines as evidence**: paying against "what would have happened" invites unfalsifiable inflation (400% threat overstatement; 9.2× cookstoves). Where actual measurement was used (metered methodology), inflation collapsed.
3. **No adversarial challenge with teeth**: it took investigative journalists and outside academics — actors with zero standing inside the system — to surface the fraud; the registry's internal "review" (Kariba) stalled and under-delivered.

## 4. Identity systems

### 4.1 Worldcoin / World ID — purchased consent, extractive bootstrap, single custodian of personhood

- MIT Technology Review's April 2022 investigation (35+ interviews, six countries): deceptive recruitment, collection beyond iris scans (vital signs), no meaningful informed consent, English ToS given to Spanish speakers, AirPod giveaways in Sudan; conclusion: "cheaper and easier to run this kind of data collection… where people have little money and few legal protections." Source: [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2022/04/06/1048981/worldcoin-cryptocurrency-biometrics-web3/).
- Regulatory kill-shots (dates matter): Kenya suspension (Aug 2023; probe dropped June 2024), Spain halt (Mar 2024, minors' data), Portugal 3-month ban (Mar 2024), Hong Kong cease order (May 2024, "unnecessary and excessive" collection), **Brazil ban (Jan 2025; reaffirmed Mar 2025 with R$50,000/day fine) on the specific ground that paying for biometrics voids free consent under data law**, Indonesia suspension (2025). Sources: [Cointelegraph roundup](https://cointelegraph.com/news/worldcoin-ban-privacy-regulation-ai), [Biometric Update on Brazil](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/world-rebukes-coverage-of-brazils-ban-on-offering-crypto-for-biometrics), [BitPinas country list](https://bitpinas.com/learn-how-to-guides/list-countries-banned-investigated-worldcoin/), [Tempo: 8 countries banning Worldcoin](https://en.tempo.co/read/2004666/these-are-8-countries-banning-worldcoin-from-spain-to-indonesia).
- Meanwhile (2026) US platforms (Zoom, Match/Tinder pilots) embrace World ID — a bifurcation where the Global South was the extraction zone and the Global North becomes the customer. Source: [Rest of World (2026)](https://restofworld.org/2026/sam-altman-worldcoin-zoom-tinder-partnerships/).

**Kill mechanism**: **consent purchased is consent voided** (Brazil made this doctrine), plus a single corporate custodian for irrevocable biometrics. Proof-of-personhood bootstrapped by paying the poorest is legitimacy debt that regulators eventually call in.

### 4.2 Aadhaar — exclusion as the failure mode of mandatory identity

- Jharkhand: in villages where biometric ration authentication was compulsory, **37% of cardholders could not buy their food rations** in the survey month vs **14% in "offline" villages** (Drèze et al. survey work reported in EPW). Source: [EPW: Aadhaar Failures: A Tragedy of Errors](https://www.epw.in/engage/article/aadhaar-failures-food-services-welfare).
- Documented starvation deaths tied to authentication/linkage failure: 11-year-old Santoshi Kumari (Simdega, 2017 — ration card cancelled for lack of Aadhaar linkage), 67-year-old widow Etwariya Devi (Garhwa, Dec 2017 — POS machine could not authenticate the household's biometrics). Sources: [Scroll.in (Simdega)](https://scroll.in/article/854225/denied-food-because-she-did-not-have-aadhaar-linked-ration-card-jharkhand-girl-dies-of-starvation), [Scroll.in (Garhwa)](https://scroll.in/article/867352/yet-another-aadhaar-linked-death-jharkhand-woman-dies-of-hunger-after-denial-of-rations), [The Wire Science](https://science.thewire.in/health/aadhaar-hunger-jharkhand-starvation-death-rations/), [National Herald](https://www.nationalheraldindia.com/national/another-aadhaar-linked-death-in-jharkhand-reason-said-to-be-biometric-fingerprint-failure).
- Failure gradient: false-negative fingerprint matches concentrate on the elderly and manual laborers — **the most vulnerable are the most likely to be locked out**. Source: [EPW](https://www.epw.in/engage/article/aadhaar-failures-food-services-welfare).

**Kill mechanism**: **fail-closed identity hard-linked to survival entitlements.** The system's error budget was silently reassigned to the powerless: when authentication failed, the person — not the system — bore the cost, up to death. (Aadhaar itself survives; what died were people and the system's moral legitimacy.)

## 5. Federation stalls: protocols that couldn't feed themselves

### 5.1 Diaspora — network effects + purism + no funding organ

- Kickstarter-famous (2010) Facebook alternative; today ~324,643 total accounts and ~21,369 active users (Sept 2025) vs Facebook's ~3B MAU; bespoke federation protocol (never adopted standards like ActivityPub); development essentially stagnant since v0.7 (2017) — the project's own 2024 report: "not a lot of updates again this year." Handed to its community in 2012 after the founders exhausted themselves (co-founder Ilya Zhitomirskiy died in Nov 2011 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diaspora_(social_network))). Sources: [Grokipedia entry (stats)](https://grokipedia.com/page/Diaspora_(social_network)), [Barnaby Walters: Lessons Diaspora Taught Me](https://waterpigs.co.uk/articles/diaspora-lessons/), [Slate: why anti-Facebooks can't win](https://slate.com/technology/2014/10/ello-diaspora-and-the-anti-facebook-why-alternative-social-networks-cant-win.html), [arXiv: Challenges in the Decentralised Web](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.05801).

**Kill mechanism**: **an island economy** — no bridge to where people already were, purist architecture over usable product, and zero sustainable funding organ. Idealism was the whole capital stack.

### 5.2 Matrix.org Foundation — 25+ governments deploy it; the commons layer still starves

- 2025-02-20, "We're at a crossroads" (fetched directly): 2024 revenue $561K vs $1.2M costs → $356K deficit, reserves burning; ultimatum that without $100K by 2025-03-31 the Foundation shuts all remaining bridges (Slack, XMPP, IRC). Half the budget comes from just 11 funding members; Element still donates ~£3M/yr of in-kind core development the Foundation depends on. Source: [matrix.org/blog/2025/02/crossroads/](https://matrix.org/blog/2025/02/crossroads/).
- Simultaneously, Matrix is being adopted by **25+ countries' public-sector deployments** — massive institutional value capture with no proportional obligation flowing back; the Foundation's own 2026 annual report says core protocol safeguarding remains "painfully underfunded." Sources: [Matrix 2026 annual report post](https://matrix.org/blog/2026/03/annual-report/), [2025 Holiday Special](https://matrix.org/blog/2025/12/24/matrix-holiday-special/), [HN discussion of the bridge ultimatum](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43116217).

**Kill mechanism (in progress)**: **freeriding by the powerful** — the protocol commons has no enforceable reciprocity: states and vendors extract; the constitutional/safety core is a charity case.

### 5.3 Mozilla — mission funded by the adversary's monopoly rents

- Nov 2024: Mozilla Foundation laid off 30% of staff (~36 of ~120) and eliminated its advocacy and global programs divisions — the parts that "fought for a free and open web." Second layoff round of that year. Sources: [TechCrunch (2024-11-05)](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/05/mozilla-foundation-lays-off-30-staff-drops-advocacy-division/), [It's FOSS](https://itsfoss.com/news/mozilla-layoff-again-2024/).
- Root exposure: ~86% of revenue ($510M of $593M, FY2021–22) is Google's default-search payment — the same payment a US court ruled part of Google's illegal search monopoly (2024); Mozilla's survival then hinged on Judge Mehta's Sept 2025 remedy ruling *allowing* Google to keep paying browser makers non-exclusively. Sources: [Fortune (2024-08-05)](https://fortune.com/2024/08/05/mozilla-firefox-biggest-potential-loser-google-antitrust-search-ruling/), [OMG Ubuntu on the 2025 ruling](https://www.omgubuntu.co.uk/2025/09/google-antitrust-ruling-firefox-search-deal).

**Kill mechanism (in progress)**: **patron-concentration = mission capture-in-waiting.** When one counterparty (and a structural adversary at that) is 86% of revenue, "independence" is an accounting fiction; advocacy — the telos organ — was the first thing amputated under stress.

## 8. The Design Laws — what a Planetary Intelligence Commons MUST satisfy

Each law is traced to at least one corpse.

**LAW 1 — Never incorporate your soul (Auroville Law).** Any single legal wrapper (foundation, act, charter, registry) holding the community's constitutional identity is a custody bomb with a long fuse; Auroville's protective 1988 Act became the takeover instrument 33 years later ([genesis](https://auroville.today/articles/2290/the-genesis-of-the-auroville-foundation-act/), [SC 2025 ruling](https://www.livelaw.in/top-stories/auroville-residents-have-no-right-to-be-part-of-councilcommittee-formed-by-foundations-governing-body-supreme-court-286621)). The Commons' constitutional layer must be **multi-homed across jurisdictions, forkable by design, and carry a guaranteed right to exit with your receipts** (identity, evidence, reputation portable out of any one wrapper).

**LAW 2 — Legitimacy before territory; be useful to the sovereign before you are visible to it (Seasteading/Liberland Law).** A parallel lane survives host states only by giving them something legible — compliance evidence, tax-visible value, problem-solving — not by declaring independence ([RNZ](https://www.rnz.co.nz/international/pacific-news/351420/french-polynesia-sinks-floating-island-project), [Cybernews](https://cybernews.com/news/croatian-police-invaded-liberland-crypto-micronation/)). The Free State Project's survival-by-working-inside-institutions vs Grafton's death-by-hollowing-them is the controlled experiment ([New Republic](https://newrepublic.com/article/159662/libertarian-walks-into-bear-book-review-free-town-project)).

**LAW 3 — Verification must be economically independent of issuance (Verra Law).** No entity may earn more when more receipts are approved. Verra/South Pole/cookstoves all died at exactly this joint ([Carbon Herald](https://carbonherald.com/the-guardian-investigation-of-verra-carbon-offsets-shows-90-are-worthless/), [Nature Sustainability](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41893-023-01259-6)). The Commons needs adversarial challengers who are **paid to find falsity** (challenge bounties), and clawback that makes over-crediting reversible.

**LAW 4 — Witnessed outcomes, not counterfactual baselines (Cookstove Law).** Modeled "would-have-happened" baselines inflated 4–9×; direct measurement (metered methodology) cut inflation to 1.5× ([Berkeley](https://news.berkeley.edu/2024/01/23/as-carbon-offsets-cookstove-emission-credits-are-greatly-overestimated/)). Causal Action Receipts must record **what observably happened**, with independent witnesses; anything paid against a counterfactual must carry a discount and a challenge window by construction.

**LAW 5 — Fail-open for humans; the system eats its own error budget (Aadhaar Law).** Where the Commons touches anyone's food, income, or standing, authentication failure must default to provisional inclusion, with a non-biometric fallback always available; exclusion errors killed people at 37%-vs-14% rates ([EPW](https://www.epw.in/engage/article/aadhaar-failures-food-services-welfare), [Scroll.in](https://scroll.in/article/854225/denied-food-because-she-did-not-have-aadhaar-linked-ration-card-jharkhand-girl-dies-of-starvation)). Challenge/reversal rights must be *cheapest for the least powerful* — ConstitutionDAO inverted this and taxed its smallest donors hardest ([The Defiant](https://thedefiant.io/constitutiondao-refunds-gas-fees/)).

**LAW 6 — Consent cannot be purchased, and personhood must have no single custodian (Worldcoin Law).** Brazil made paying-for-biometrics legally void consent ([Biometric Update](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202502/world-rebukes-coverage-of-brazils-ban-on-offering-crypto-for-biometrics)); MIT TR documented what "consent" looks like when bootstrapped on the poor ([MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2022/04/06/1048981/worldcoin-cryptocurrency-biometrics-web3/)). The Commons must never hold irrevocable biometrics, never pay for enrollment, and must support community-witnessed personhood over hardware-oracle personhood.

**LAW 7 — Authority by witnessed contribution and stake-at-risk, never by purchasable tokens (Plutocracy Law).** <1% holding 90% of voting power, 5–17% turnout, top-10 delegates able to pass anything in 17/23 DAOs ([Cointelegraph](https://cointelegraph.com/news/less-than-1-of-all-holders-have-90-of-the-voting-power-in-daos-report), [Forbes 2026](https://www.forbes.com/sites/digital-assets/2026/04/04/daos-keep-centralizingdecades-of-governance-research-explain-why/)). Design for **accountable delegation with recall receipts**, not mass plebiscites; assume apathy as the steady state and make the small active core auditable rather than pretending it doesn't exist.

**LAW 8 — Custody demands provenance (Wonderland Law).** Anyone (human or agent) holding treasury/enforcement power must have a witnessed history binding — pseudonymity is permissible for participation, never for custody ([Vice](https://www.vice.com/en/article/popcorn-time-crypto-project-in-chaos-after-co-founder-outed-as-fraudster/)). This is precisely the receipt chain's "identity and delegation" link — apply it inward first.

**LAW 9 — A native, bounded, non-catastrophic reversal layer (The DAO Law).** If the only remedies are "immutable" or "fork the world," every serious error becomes a constitutional crisis ([Clovr](https://www.clovr.com/blog/dao-hack/)). Challenge → adjudication → reversal must be an ordinary, cheap, receipt-generating operation. **No studied system had this; it is the Commons' clearest differentiator.**

**LAW 10 — Selective disclosure: commit now, reveal later (ConstitutionDAO Law).** Radical transparency handed Ken Griffin the exact ceiling to beat ([Artnet/Salmon](https://news.artnet.com/market/felix-salmon-ken-griffin-constitution-2040093)). Receipts must be non-backdateable commitments whose contents can stay sealed until revelation cannot be weaponized — transparency of *integrity*, not of *strategy*.

**LAW 11 — The commons layer must have an endogenous revenue organ; no patron above a capture threshold (Matrix/Mozilla Law).** Matrix: 25+ countries deploying, Foundation begging for $100K ([matrix.org](https://matrix.org/blog/2025/02/crossroads/)); Mozilla: 86% funded by its structural adversary, advocacy amputated first ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/2024/08/05/mozilla-firefox-biggest-potential-loser-google-antitrust-search-ruling/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/11/05/mozilla-foundation-lays-off-30-staff-drops-advocacy-division/)). Verification, challenge adjudication, and receipt anchoring must be **paid services with reciprocity obligations** baked into the license: those who deploy must fund. Cap any single funder's share constitutionally (FTX/EV corollary: the cap applies to donors too — [Oxford Clarion](https://oxfordclarion.uk/wytham-abbey-and-the-end-of-the-effective-altruism-dream/)).

**LAW 12 — Telos gates bind the founder and the biggest believer first (FTX/EA + Christiania Law).** Purpose used as a license to skip controls is the signature of telos capture ([EA Forum](https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/8Apiy3Hc7zexejCJ6/link-post-ftx-and-effective-governance)); and a community too pure to enforce its own rules gets predators as its enforcement layer ([CNN on Christiania](https://www.cnn.com/2024/04/06/travel/denmark-christiania-pusher-street-shut-intl)). The Commons' immune system (Loomwork) needs real teeth — sanction, revocation, expulsion receipts — and its constitutional constraints must demonstrably bind the operator himself, in public, from receipt #1.

## 9. What this means for the Commons — strategic read

**The graveyard has two death families, and the current design over-prepares for one.** Family B — internal integrity collapse (Goodhart, plutocracy, telos capture) — is what the receipt/challenge/witness architecture is already aimed at, and the corpses confirm the aim is right: every credit-market and DAO death traces to a missing joint the Causal Action Receipt explicitly names. Family A — **custody death at the hands of the host world** — is underweighted. Auroville is the direct ancestor-precedent of this exact dream (spiritual lineage, universal township, earth-tuned, 60 years of lived practice) and it is being dismantled *through its own protective legal wrapper*, with an internal schism as the entry wedge and members' visas as the pressure instrument. The lesson is not "avoid India" or "avoid foundations." It is: **the Commons must never have a single incorporable soul, and its members' standing must never be revocable by any single external authority.** Multi-home everything; make forking-with-receipts a constitutional right, not a failure.

**The sharpest convergent finding across families**: every credit-market corpse died because issuance and verification shared a bank account, and every identity corpse died at the exclusion boundary. Those are the two joints where the receipt spine will live or die. If receipt verification is ever paid per-approval, receipts become the new carbon credits within one market cycle. If receipt failure ever defaults to exclusion of a human, the Commons becomes Aadhaar with better cryptography.

**The differentiator no corpse possessed**: a native, cheap, bounded challenge-and-reversal layer. The DAO could only reverse by chain schism; Auroville's only challenge court was the host's Supreme Court (which ruled for the host); carbon markets' only challengers were unpaid journalists; Aadhaar victims had no challenge at all. A2A/MCP/AP2/World ID — the emerging agentic stack — likewise offer *none of this*. Reversal-as-ordinary-operation is the whitespace, and it is exactly what a receipt graph enables.

**Sequencing, dictated by the corpses**: (1) *Ship the notary before founding the noosphere.* Matrix proves a constitutional layer has no natural buyer even with 25 governments deployed on it; verification-as-a-service does have buyers, and the EU AI Act gives it a clock — note the precise mechanics: GPAI obligations applied 2025-08-02; **2026-08-02 is when Commission enforcement powers and fines activate**, plus the high-risk/transparency wave ([EU implementation timeline](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/implementation-timeline/), [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2025/08/latest-wave-of-obligations-under-the-eu-ai-act-take-effect)). This confirms the prior vote (SIS/reciprocity as first wedge) and the 7/10 upstream convergence on signing the receipt spine. (2) *Legitimacy leverage before constitutional visibility* — be the evidence layer incumbents need before announcing the polity they didn't ask for. (3) *Identity last, and never custodial.*

**Operator-reality check (no flattery)**: a solo, zero-revenue operator cannot out-fund Mozilla's collapse or out-govern Auroville's schism. The one move that is actually solo-shaped is the one the corpses point to anyway: make the receipt primitive real, signed, non-backdateable, and independently challengeable — on the operator's own estate first (Law 12: the gates bind the founder first; the dharma_swarm governance record of refusing its own promotions is, genuinely, a miniature of this discipline). A working micro-Commons of one, with receipts an outsider can challenge, is worth more than any manifesto for a planetary one — and it is the only artifact in this entire field that none of the corpses ever produced.
