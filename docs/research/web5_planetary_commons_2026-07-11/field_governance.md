## 0. Executive Summary

The mid-2026 field splits cleanly into three lineages, and the evidence about which ones acquire durable legitimacy is remarkably consistent:

1. **Exit-flavored parallel states** (Praxis, Próspera, Network School) are well-capitalized and legally embattled. They generate host-state antibodies in proportion to their sovereignty claims. Próspera is now a multi-billion-dollar arbitration case; Network School is a viral national-security controversy in Malaysia; Praxis has $525M in *conditional* financing and 151k self-reported "citizens" with no obligations.
2. **Service-flavored parallel institutions** (Estonia e-residency, IETF, Wikipedia, citizens' assemblies, Edge City, Team Mirai) grow quietly and durably. Estonia's e-residency returned €124.9M to the state in 2025; Team Mirai went from founding (May 2025) to 11 Diet seats (2026) on deliberation tooling; Edge City turned Zuzalu into a self-sustaining network with open-source infra.
3. **Mechanism-flavored commons governance** (Optimism two-house, Gitcoin QF/COCM, retro funding, sortition, Pol.is, Habermas Machine) has converged, after brutal post-mortems, on one lesson: **voting mechanisms don't create legitimacy; verified evidence of impact does.** Optimism moved retro funding to continuous metrics-based evaluation; Gitcoin moved to domain allocators with multiple mechanisms; DAOs that stayed pure-token-vote rotted (<2% turnout, 1% of holders holding 90% of power).

Meanwhile the agentic internet is standardizing fast (A2A at Linux Foundation, 150+ production orgs; AP2 payment mandates) and the academic literature **explicitly confirms the gap the Commons targets**: as of early 2026, *no implemented protocol combines verifiable delegation of authority, chained policy, and provenance-aware completion records* across MCP/A2A ([arXiv:2603.24775](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.24775)). That is the Causal Action Receipt's slot, stated in the field's own words.

**Core answer to the core question:** parallel institutions earn legitimacy by (a) delivering a concrete service the incumbent can't or won't, (b) maintaining an explicit, non-hostile legal interface with incumbents, and (c) producing artifacts whose adoption is voluntary and whose value is verifiable. They become toys when they lead with identity, flags, constitutions, or exit rhetoric before service. Design rules distilled in §14; strategic read in §15.

## 1. The Network-State Movement: Praxis, Próspera, Network School

### Praxis
- Praxis raised **$525M in conditional financing** (announced Oct 2024): $500M from GEM Digital, $25M from Arch Lending, released in stages against milestones ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/321268/network-state-project-praxis-secures-525-million-to-build-crypto-friendly-city); [Praxis announcement](https://www.praxisnation.com/news/financing-announcement-10-24); [ChainCatcher](https://www.chaincatcher.com/en/article/2147629)). This is *not* $525M in the bank — a staged facility of this type is milestone-gated.
- As of January 2026 Praxis self-reports **151,068 "citizens" from 80 countries** and claims member-founded companies with aggregate valuation over $1.1T ([Wikipedia: Praxis (proposed city)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Praxis_(proposed_city))). These are self-reported sign-ups with no residency, taxation, or obligation — a mailing list wearing a flag.
- Location has churned: June 2025 Vanity Fair reporting placed "Atlas" near Vandenberg Space Force Base, California; by Dec 2025 it was rebranded a "defence-focused spaceport city," with talk of a separate non-US city in 2026 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Praxis_(proposed_city))). Nothing built as of this writing (2026-07-11).
- Critique lineage: the network-state concept is increasingly read as an anti-democratic exit ideology by mainstream analysts ([TechPolicy.Press on privatized sovereignty](https://www.techpolicy.press/trumps-gaza-fantasy-and-the-network-state-the-tech-fueled-future-of-privatized-sovereignty/); [The Unpopulist](https://www.theunpopulist.net/p/how-silicon-valleys-corrupted-libertarianism)). The Collective Intelligence Project's counter-frame — "**network societies, not network states**": interoperate with democracies rather than exit them — is the most useful positioning document in this space ([CIP](https://www.cip.org/blog/network-societies)).

### Próspera (Honduras ZEDE)
- ICSID Case No. ARB/23/2 (Honduras Próspera Inc. et al. v. Republic of Honduras) is live. Feb 26, 2025: tribunal **rejected Honduras's preliminary objections** (exhaustion of local remedies), letting the case proceed ([Jus Mundi decision](https://jusmundi.com/en/document/decision/en-honduras-prospera-inc-st-john-s-bay-development-company-llc-and-prospera-arbitration-center-llc-v-republic-of-honduras-decision-on-the-respondent-s-preliminary-objections-pursuant-to-cafta-dr-article-10-20-5-wednesday-26th-february-2025); [Kluwer Arbitration Blog](https://legalblogs.wolterskluwer.com/arbitration-blog/a-local-remedies-pitfall-avoided-for-now-key-takeaways-from-honduras-prospera-inc-v-honduras/)).
- Damages claims escalated: by Oct 2025, Próspera's experts valued its 30-year business plan at **$10.6B average, up to $26.4B** ([italaw case page](https://www.italaw.com/cases/9971); [Dark Atlas summary](https://thedarkatlas.com/posts/prospera-private-city-honduras) — the widely-cited figure "suing Honduras for ~$11B" refers to this). Honduras's GDP context makes this claim politically explosive ([Public Seminar](https://publicseminar.org/2025/03/honduras-techno-economy/)).
- Honduras **withdrew from ICSID in August 2024** (does not affect pending cases) ([Impakter](https://impakter.com/honduras-withdraws-from-icsid-amid-investment-dispute-what-it-means-for-future-arbitration/)). Procedural orders continued into 2026 (bifurcation Mar 19, 2026; amicus interventions May 6, 2026 — [UNCTAD case tracker](https://investmentpolicy.unctad.org/investment-dispute-settlement/cases/1292/pr-spera-and-others-v-honduras)). Próspera remains operational; no final ruling near-term.
- **Lesson:** a charter city that bet its existence on a host-state legal carve-out saw the carve-out repealed at the first change of government, and its survival now runs through investor-state arbitration — the least legitimate venue imaginable in the eyes of the host population.

### Network School (Balaji Srinivasan)
- Launched Sept 2024 in Forest City, Johor, Malaysia; grew from ~150 to **400+ members from 70+ nationalities** by mid-2025 ([Deeper Insights profile](https://deeperinsights.com/features/the-network-school-in-forest-city-balaji-srinivasans/); [Wikipedia: Balaji Srinivasan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balaji_Srinivasan)).
- Exploratory talks with Malaysia's Digital Ministry occurred, but by mid-2026 the project went viral domestically as a **sovereignty/national-security controversy** (unconfirmed allegations about entrants using second passports; a minister's supportive post deleted) ([Malay Mail, May 2026](https://www.malaymail.com/news/money/2026/05/14/network-school-project-in-johor-by-ex-coinbase-tech-chief-draws-govt-interest-and-scrutiny/219910); [KakiForex, July 2026](http://www.kakiforex.com/2026/07/network-school-goes-viral-netizens.html)). The allegation itself is UNVERIFIED; the backlash is the datapoint.
- **Lesson:** even a modest 400-person campus triggers host-state immune response the moment it is framed as a proto-state rather than a school.

## 2. Zuzalu → Edge City: The Pop-Up Village Lineage (the healthy branch)

- Zuzalu (2023, Montenegro, ~200 people, Vitalik Buterin-initiated) spawned **20+ derivative pop-up villages within 18 months**; Zupass (zero-knowledge identity/ticketing) went prototype→production ([Gitcoin case study](https://gitcoin.co/case-studies/zuzalu-and-pop-up-cities-temporary-coordination-experiments); [Bankless retrospective](https://www.bankless.com/read/zuzalu-one-year-later)).
- Edge City is now a self-sustaining organization running multiple annual events: **Edge Esmeralda 2025 drew 1,300+ visitors, 300+ full-time residents from 60+ countries** over a month in Healdsburg, CA; Edge Esmeralda 2026 runs May 30–June 27 co-organized with the Esmeralda Institute (a *permanent* village project); Edge City India (Goa) runs Oct 11–Nov 1, 2026 ahead of Devcon Mumbai ([Edge City about](https://www.edgecity.live/about); [Edge Esmeralda 2026](https://www.edgeesmeralda.com/about); [Edge City roadmap](https://www.edgecity.live/roadmap)).
- Edge City is building **EdgeOS**, an open-source resident portal (applications, payments, housing, coordination) — infrastructure any village can adopt ([Edge City about](https://www.edgecity.live/about)).
- **Lesson:** Zuzalu succeeded precisely where Praxis struggles because it made *zero sovereignty claims*: it was forkable culture plus shared open-source infrastructure plus time-boxed physical co-presence. Its legitimacy is generativity — measured in forks, not citizens.

## 3. DAO Governance Post-Mortems: What Died, What Survived

### The pathology (well-documented by 2025)
- **Under 2% of token holders vote** on most DAO proposals; Chainalysis found **1% of holders control 90% of voting power** across 10 major DAOs; a single ApeCoin wallet with 4% of supply vetoed a $1M grant; Uniswap routine-proposal turnout below 3% ([pen-caforr DAO governance 2026 review](https://pen-caforr.org/2026/04/15/dao-governance-2026-hybrid-models-legal-wrappers-and-the-end-of-token-voting/); [Chainscore Labs](https://chainscorelabs.com/blog/defi-renaissance-yields-rwas-and-institutional-flows/defi-risk-management-frameworks/why-dao-governance-fails-at-crisis-management)).
- Delegation itself breeds apathy: holders delegate to whales/VCs who lack context for emergency decisions ([Chainscore Labs](https://chainscorelabs.com/blog/defi-renaissance-yields-rwas-and-institutional-flows/defi-risk-management-frameworks/why-dao-governance-fails-at-crisis-management)).
- Field direction: pure token-voting is being replaced by **hybrid models** — off-chain deliberation + expert committees + legal wrappers + optimistic (veto-based) approvals ([pen-caforr](https://pen-caforr.org/2026/04/15/dao-governance-2026-hybrid-models-legal-wrappers-and-the-end-of-token-voting/)). Even so, 10% turnout is considered "good" and whale influence persists under quadratic voting.

### What actually works now: Optimism's two-house → four-stakeholder system
- **Season 8** (announced June 12, 2025; protocol-upgrade modules effective Aug 1, 2025): governance restructured around **four stakeholder groups** — tokenholders (Token House, token-weighted) plus a Citizens' House subdivided into **end-users (up to 1,000, proof-of-personhood, activity thresholds), apps (min 100, by gas usage), chains (min 15, by revenue)** — each 1-member-1-vote; **optimistic approval** (proposals auto-pass unless vetoed) to cut governance burden; protocol upgrades delegated to a technical **Developer Advisory Board** ([Optimism blog: Governance in Season 8](https://www.optimism.io/blog/governance-in-season-8-the-next-phase); [Yellow.com coverage](https://yellow.com/news/optimism-launches-season-8-governance-overhaul-with-4-stakeholder-groups); [Optimism citizenship docs](https://community.optimism.io/citizens-house/faq-citizenship)). Stated rationale: "reduce platform risk for the Collective's stakeholders" — governance as insurance, not as participation theater.
- **Retro Funding evolved from annual rounds to a continuous, metrics-driven reward mechanism** in 2025 — impact measured on a regular cadence against pre-declared metrics, rolling applications, badgeholders tune the evaluation rather than re-vote every project ([Optimism: Retro Funding 2025](https://www.optimism.io/blog/retro-funding-2025); [governance forum: Evolution of Retro Funding in 2025](https://gov.optimism.io/t/the-evolution-of-retro-funding-in-2025/9414); two-year lessons: [forum post](https://gov.optimism.io/t/lessons-learned-from-two-years-of-retroactive-public-goods-funding/9239); social-choice analysis: [arXiv:2508.16285](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.16285)).
- **Read:** the most sophisticated on-chain polity converged on (a) separating money-votes from person-votes, (b) verifiable, on-chain-checkable citizenship criteria, (c) evidence-metrics replacing repeated subjective voting, (d) default-approve with challenge rights. Items (b), (c), (d) are structurally *receipt logic*.

### Gitcoin quadratic funding
- GG23 (2025): ~$1.3M matching + $95K crowdfunded; sybil/collusion resistance via **COCM (connection-oriented cluster matching) + Passport model-based detection** — mitigates but does not eliminate collusion ([GG23 results](https://www.gitcoin.co/blog/gg23-results-recap); [Gitcoin QF mechanism page](https://gitcoin.co/mechanisms/quadratic-funding)).
- GG24 (Oct–Nov 2025): **$1.8M+ across six domains under a Domain Allocator model** — community-appointed operators independently run rounds; **six mechanisms deployed simultaneously** (QF, Deep Funding, MACI private voting, conviction voting, retro funding, peer-reviewed Hypercerts) ([GG24 case study](https://gitcoin.co/case-studies/gg24-first-funding-round-of-gitcoin-3-0)).
- **Read:** Gitcoin gave up on one-mechanism universalism and became a *pluralistic mechanism marketplace with delegated local judgment* — polycentricity applied to funding. Note Hypercerts: peer-reviewed impact certificates are a cousin of the Causal Action Receipt, but without authority/delegation/material-debit semantics.

## 4. Liquid Democracy: The Cautionary Classic

- The canonical dataset remains Germany's Pirate Party LiquidFeedback deployment (2010–2013): **super-voters accumulated enormous theoretical power via transitive delegation**, though in practice they mostly voted with majorities (a stabilizing but legitimacy-eroding pattern); sustained informed participation declined after initial surges; an internal crisis over delegative democracy's role began as early as 2011 ([Kling et al., ICWSM 2015, arXiv:1503.07723](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.07723); [AAAI version](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/ICWSM/article/view/14618)).
- Delegate voting rates as low as 30–40% in some systems; engagement "superficial rather than substantive" ([Grokipedia synthesis — secondary, use with care](https://grokipedia.com/page/Liquid_democracy); theory: [Fluid Mechanics of Liquid Democracy, arXiv:1808.01906](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.01906)).
- No major polity has adopted liquid democracy at scale since. **Lesson:** delegation without accountability receipts re-creates oligarchy with extra steps. Any delegation layer needs visible, revocable, *scoped* delegation with an audit trail — precisely what the receipt's identity→delegation chain encodes.

## 5. vTaiwan, Pol.is, Plurality — and the Team Mirai Breakout

- vTaiwan's track record: of ~26 national tech-policy issues deliberated, **~80% led to government action** (self-reported by the project; treat as advocacy figure) ([Crowdlaw case study](https://congress.crowd.law/case-vtaiwan.html); [Pol.is Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pol.is)).
- Audrey Tang: Digital Minister Aug 2022–May 2024, now Taiwan's Cyber Ambassador-at-large; **2025 Right Livelihood Award laureate** ([Right Livelihood](https://rightlivelihood.org/the-change-makers/find-a-laureate/audrey-tang/); [HBS BiGS profile](https://www.hbs.edu/bigs/taiwans-digital-revolution-audrey-tang)).
- The Plurality movement's declared 2030 targets: 1,000 core advocates, 1M book copies, 1B people sympathetic ([Time interview](https://time.com/6979012/audrey-tang-interview-plurality-democracy/)).
- **The 2025–26 breakout is Japan.** Team Mirai, founded 8 May 2025 by AI engineer Takahiro Anno out of the Digital Democracy 2030 project, won its **first Diet seat in the July 2025 upper-house election (2.6% proportional)**, then **11 seats in the 2026 lower-house election (~3.97M votes, 6.9%)** — campaigning on its open-source "Idobata" deliberation system (citizens discuss policy with chatbots feeding a party feedback loop) ([Wikipedia: Team Mirai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Team_Mirai); [Asia Times, Mar 2026](https://asiatimes.com/2026/03/team-mirai-pushing-to-bring-digital-democracy-to-japan/); [TechPolicy.Press](https://www.techpolicy.press/japans-team-mirai-uses-tech-to-bolster-democracy-not-undermine-it/); Tang's collaboration with Digital Democracy 2030 noted by [Right Livelihood](https://rightlivelihood.org/the-change-makers/find-a-laureate/audrey-tang/)).
- **Read:** this is the single strongest 2025–26 evidence that deliberation tooling can win *inside* traditional structures. The Taiwan→Japan transfer worked because the tooling was packaged as *better representation*, not as a parallel state. People joined before any crisis because it made their existing citizenship more effective.

## 6. AI-Assisted Deliberation: Habermas Machine and After

- DeepMind's Habermas Machine (Science, Oct 2024): 5,700+ UK participants; AI-mediated group statements **preferred over human mediators' (>56%)**, incorporating dissent rather than majority-flattening; deliberation measurably reduced group division ([DeepMind follow-up paper, Knight Institute, Apr 2025 — arXiv:2601.05904](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05904v1); [Reboot Democracy analysis](https://rebootdemocracy.ai/blog/habermas-machine/)).
- 2025 critique literature: consensus-optimization may conflict with deliberation-as-communicative-autonomy; risk of subtle minority marginalization; the "agreement" produced may not be the kind that legitimates decisions ([Volpe, Ethics & Information Technology 2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10676-025-09854-1); [AAAI/AIES paper on the embedded theory of deliberation](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AIES/article/view/36687)).
- The DeepMind team's own 2025 extension paper proposes **hierarchical aggregation for mass-scale deliberation** and integration with expert testimony/fact-checking, while flagging fairness, oversight-at-scale, and algorithmic aversion as open problems ([arXiv:2601.05904](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05904v1)).
- **Read:** AI mediation is now a proven *component* (statement drafting, common-ground finding) and a disproven *authority*. Nobody serious proposes the model as the legitimator. Legitimacy still requires human ratification with challenge rights — the Commons' "witnessed authority + challenge/reversal" framing is aligned with where the critique literature landed.

## 7. Citizens' Assemblies & Sortition: From Events to Institutions

- The movement has crossed from one-offs to **permanent institutions**: standing assemblies exist in Paris, Brussels, Ostbelgien, Bogotá, and elsewhere ([IWM report](https://www.iwm.at/europes-futures/publication/citizens-assemblies-democratic-responses-to-authoritarian-challenges)); 30+ UK assembly initiatives in five years; EU-level assemblies on food waste and mobility; CitiDem is running local assemblies in 8 EU countries plus transnational assemblies through 2025–26 ([Citizens Take Over Europe](https://citizenstakeover.eu/citidem/)).
- OECD-standard defining features: sufficient deliberation time, sortition-based representativeness, and a **formal link to public decision-making** ([IWM](https://www.iwm.at/europes-futures/publication/citizens-assemblies-democratic-responses-to-authoritarian-challenges)).
- **Ireland is the canonical natural experiment in both directions.** Assembly→referendum successes: marriage equality (2015) and abortion (2018 — assembly 64% for liberalization, public 66% yes). Failure: the **March 2024 family/care referendums lost 67%/73%** — post-mortems attribute failure to government putting forward wording that *diverged from the assembly's recommendation*, bundling unclear issues without a concrete policy delta ([Involve on the abortion assembly](https://www.involve.org.uk/news-opinion/opinion/citizens-assembly-behind-irish-abortion-referendum); [2024 referendums, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Irish_constitutional_referendums); [I-CONnect analysis](https://www.iconnectblog.com/the-future-for-citizens-assemblies-in-ireland/)).
- Public support for sortition-based assemblies is broad across 15 countries but shallow — support is higher among those who feel unrepresented ([EJPR study](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/european-journal-of-political-research/article/public-support-for-deliberative-citizens-assemblies-selected-through-sortition-evidence-from-15-countries/304435B08BDFAB26CE17CDE39F1F4CEA)). A live technical literature is professionalizing selection (dropout-robust sortition, temporal panels: [arXiv:2511.16897](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.16897), [arXiv:2602.16194](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.16194)).
- **Read:** the deliberation→decision *linkage* is the legitimacy organ. When the chain from deliberation to implemented text is unbroken and auditable, assemblies move constitutions. When an intermediary silently edits the output, the whole apparatus's credibility burns. This is a receipts problem: Ireland 2024 failed for lack of what the Commons would call an unbroken causal action receipt from deliberation to ballot text.

## 8. Ostrom & Polycentric Governance Applied to Digital Commons

- Ostrom's design principles (bounded membership, congruence with local conditions, collective-choice participation, monitoring, graduated sanctions, cheap conflict resolution, recognized right to self-organize, **nested tiers**) remain the reference frame; active programs continue at the [Ostrom Workshop](https://ostromworkshop.indiana.edu/research/commons-governance/index.html).
- Application to blockchain/digital governance is now a mature literature: polycentric, bottom-up nested tiers with embedded participation/enforcement/dispute rules are recommended for protocol ecosystems ([Blockchain as Commons](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/365997822_Blockchain_as_Commons_Applying_Ostrom's_Polycentric_Approach_to_Blockchain_Governance); [UCL CBT essay](https://medium.com/uclcbt/a-case-for-ostroms-polycentricity-integrating-commons-management-in-blockchain-governance-20b76a42e150)); 2025 work extends commons theory to AI-augmented resource management ([Ostrom in the Digital Era](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/396375265_Ostrom_in_the_Digital_Era_Applying_Commons_Theory_to_AI-_Augmented_Resource_Management_Frameworks)); a revised-principles synthesis for online commons exists ([Life With Alacrity](https://www.lifewithalacrity.com/article/a-revised-ostroms-design-principles-for-collective-governance-of-the-commons/)).
- Knowledge-commons nuance: digital resources are non-subtractable; the governance risk shifts from overuse to **enclosure and entry burden** ([ResearchGate: Nudging the Ostroms' Vision](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/377134745_Nudging_the_Ostroms'_Vision_of_the_Commons_on_Polycentric_Governance_into_the_Digital_Environment)).
- **Read:** the federated-bioregional architecture in the master plan (global layer = identity/invariants/challenge-rights/schemas; local custody and veto) is a direct instantiation of Ostrom's nested-tier principle. The literature would add: the global layer must also carry *graduated sanctions* and *cheap conflict resolution* or the nesting is decorative.

## 9. ICANN / IETF / Wikipedia: The Living Global Commons — Under Strain

- **ICANN**: the 2016 IANA transition remains the flagship proof that a global resource can be governed multistakeholder-ly without a state owner ([Internet Society policy brief, 2025](https://www.internetsociety.org/resources/policybriefs/2025/internet-governance/)). But measured legitimacy beliefs are only moderate and decline away from the regime's insider core ([Global Governance 27(2) 2021](https://brill.com/view/journals/gg/27/2/article-p298_7.xml?language=en)). A sharper critique — that the model "turned attendance into mandate" (participation by whoever shows up ≠ representation) — circulates ([heng.lu essay](https://heng.lu/the-multi-stakeholder-mirage-how-the-multi-stakeholder-model-turned-attendance-into-mandate/) — UNVERIFIED: page returned 403 on fetch; characterization from search snippet only).
- The **WSIS+20 review (2025)** put the whole multistakeholder settlement in play, with some UN member states pushing multilateral (state-controlled) replacement of the IGF ([Internet Society WSIS brief](https://www.internetsociety.org/resources/doc/2025/internet-governance-and-wsis/); [ICANN in-brief, Oct 2025](https://www.icann.org/en/system/files/files/in-brief-multistakeholder-internet-governance-28oct25-en.pdf)).
- **IETF**: legitimacy from *voluntary adoption of working artifacts* — "rough consensus and running code"; anyone may participate; standards bind only because operators choose them ([ICANN OCTO-043 on ICANN and the IETF, Apr 2026](https://www.icann.org/en/system/files/files/octo-043-16apr26-en.pdf)).
- **Wikipedia at 25** faces its named existential threat: generative AI reduces visits → fewer new editors → slower error correction, while AI-generated text floods inbound ([Scientific American, Jan 2026](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/at-25-wikipedia-now-faces-its-most-existential-threat-generative-a-i/)). Its governance response is instructive: three years of failed comprehensive policy attempts ended in **successful minimalism** — criterion G15 speedy-deletion for unreviewed LLM pages (adopted Aug 4, 2025), an LLM writing guideline (Nov 24, 2025), and a March 2026 prohibition on LLM-generated article content with narrow exceptions ([Springer AI & Society analysis](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00146-026-03046-1); [secondary summary](https://www.wikibusines.net/blog/wikipedia-ai-content-ban-2026) — the March 2026 prohibition detail rests on secondary sources; treat scope as approximately-right, UNVERIFIED in exact wording). WMF's 2025–26 plan names declining trust and fragmenting consensus-on-truth as the operating environment ([WMF Annual Plan 2025-2026](https://meta.wikimedia.org/wiki/Wikimedia_Foundation_Annual_Plan/2025-2026)).
- **Read:** the three living global commons all derive legitimacy from *output* (the DNS resolves; the protocols interoperate; the encyclopedia is useful) plus open process, and all three are being stress-tested simultaneously by state re-assertion and AI slop. The epistemic commons is losing its provenance layer — which is exactly the layer receipts provide.

## 10. Historical World-Citizen Movements: The Toy Benchmark

- **Garry Davis** renounced US citizenship in Paris, 1948; popularized "world citizen"; founded the World Service Authority (1953), which still issues the **World Passport** — grounded in UDHR Art. 13(2), described by Wikipedia flatly as "a fantasy travel document," occasionally accepted ad hoc, never systematically ([Wikipedia: Garry Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garry_Davis); [WSA history](https://garrydavis.org/history.html); [Wikinews interview with WSA president David Gallup](https://en.wikinews.org/wiki/Wikinews_interviews_the_President_of_WSA:_today's_World_Passports_and_World_Citizen_Government)).
- The **World Federalist Movement** (post-1945 wave) persists as an advocacy network (WSA is an associate member) with real historical wins adjacent to its program (e.g., ICC advocacy) but no progress toward federation itself ([Citizens for Global Solutions on world citizenship's history](https://globalsolutions.org/updates/mondial-journal/world-citizenship-a-recent-phenomenon-with-ancient-roots/)).
- **Read:** 78 years of world-citizen identity documents demonstrate the hard limit of *identity-first* parallel institutions: a passport is only as real as the network of parties that accept it. Davis had moral clarity, global press, and millions of sympathizers at peak — and it never compounded, because holding the document changed nothing in the holder's daily life. The acceptance network, not the document, is the institution. (Direct design constraint for any "citizens-of-the-world" membership artifact.)

## 11. Estonia E-Residency: The Service-First Counterexample

- 2025 results: **€124.9M state revenue** (labour taxes €54.5M, dividend-related income tax €66M, fees €4.3M) — 87% YoY growth; cumulative impact ~€400M; **>€12 returned per €1 invested** ([official e-Residency blog, Feb 2026](https://www.e-resident.gov.ee/blog/posts/e-residents-generated-record-state-revenue-2025/); [ERR News](https://news.err.ee/1609934015/estonian-e-residency-program-brought-in-125-million-in-2025)).
- Scale: **135,000+ e-residents from 185 countries; 39,000+ companies founded; 5,556 companies in 2025 (+15%); one in five new Estonian companies is e-resident-founded; 13,828 new e-residents in 2025 (+20%, best in six years)** ([e-Residency blog](https://www.e-resident.gov.ee/blog/posts/e-residents-generated-record-state-revenue-2025/); [Fintech Baltic](https://fintechbaltic.com/11268/fintechestonia/estonias-e-residency-generated-eur-125m-in-state-revenue-2025-bringing-total-impact-to-eur-400m/)).
- Roadmap: card-free mobile e-residency projected to lift company formation ≥20% ([e-Residency blog](https://www.e-resident.gov.ee/blog/posts/e-residents-generated-record-state-revenue-2025/)).
- **Read:** the most successful "digital citizenship" scheme on Earth is (a) backed by a real state's legal system, (b) sold as a *service* (company formation, banking, signatures), (c) revenue-positive for the host, (d) identity-verified. It is the mutual-aid pattern in state clothing: join for the concrete benefit; the identity is a by-product. Any Web5 citizenship layer should ask: *what is our equivalent of "start an EU company in a day"?*

## 13. The Parallel-Institution Canon: Parallel Polis & Mutual Aid

- **Parallel polis** (Václav Benda, samizdat essay, May 1978): rather than confront the regime, build parallel structures — underground universities, churches, presses, theater — that satisfy real needs the official system cannot; the parallel polis made "universal claims" (moral legitimacy beyond the group) ([Wikipedia: Parallel Polis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_Polis); [Law & Liberty, Flagg Taylor](https://lawliberty.org/building-the-parallel-polis/); [Gariwo on the parallel polis in Eastern Europe](https://en.gariwo.net/righteous/dissent-in-eastern-europe/the-power-of-the-powerless-7759.html)). It worked: it preserved civil society through totalitarianism and supplied the personnel and legitimacy of the post-1989 transition.
- **Mutual aid / fraternal societies**: at peak (~1910), roughly **one-third of adult American males** belonged to lodges; fraternal analogues existed for *every* major welfare-state service (orphanages, hospitals, job exchanges, elder homes) ([Beito, *From Mutual Aid to the Welfare State*; Heritage summary](https://www.heritage.org/political-process/report/mutual-aid-welfare-state-how-fraternal-societies-fought-poverty-and-taught); [UNC Press](https://uncpress.org/9780807848418/from-mutual-aid-to-the-welfare-state/)). Decline by the 1930s: welfare-state substitution, restrictive insurance regulation, commercial-insurer competition — and with the decline, the loss of "voluntary reciprocity and autonomy" networks ([Heritage/Beito](https://www.heritage.org/political-process/report/mutual-aid-welfare-state-how-fraternal-societies-fought-poverty-and-taught)).
- **Read:** the two deepest historical proofs both say: parallel institutions live exactly as long as they deliver a *daily-life service* under a *universalizable moral claim*, and they die when (a) the incumbent absorbs their function, or (b) regulation prices them out. Neither died from lack of ideology. Both were joined *before* any crisis, for concrete benefit; the constitutional meaning was carried silently inside the service.

## 14. CORE QUESTION — What Gives a Parallel Institution Legitimacy and Durability; What Makes It a Toy

Synthesizing across all thirteen streams:

### Sources of legitimacy (observed, not theorized)
1. **Output legitimacy first**: the DNS resolves (ICANN), companies get formed (Estonia), the sick get paid (mutual aid), the banned book circulates (parallel polis), the statement finds common ground (Pol.is/HM). Every durable case delivered a service the incumbent couldn't or wouldn't. ([ISOC](https://www.internetsociety.org/resources/policybriefs/2025/internet-governance/); [e-Residency](https://www.e-resident.gov.ee/blog/posts/e-residents-generated-record-state-revenue-2025/); [Beito](https://www.heritage.org/political-process/report/mutual-aid-welfare-state-how-fraternal-societies-fought-poverty-and-taught))
2. **A non-hostile legal interface with incumbents**: Estonia *is* a state; assemblies have a formal decision link (OECD criterion); IETF standards are voluntary; Edge City signs leases. The counterexamples (Próspera's repealed carve-out, Network School's visa controversy) show sovereignty claims converting host states into adversaries. ([UNCTAD](https://investmentpolicy.unctad.org/investment-dispute-settlement/cases/1292/pr-spera-and-others-v-honduras); [Malay Mail](https://www.malaymail.com/news/money/2026/05/14/network-school-project-in-johor-by-ex-coinbase-tech-chief-draws-govt-interest-and-scrutiny/219910))
3. **Universalizable moral claims** (Benda) — the parallel polis claimed values *for everyone*, not privileges for members. Contrast: network-state exit is read, accurately, as secession-for-the-rich, and inherits that brand. ([Law & Liberty](https://lawliberty.org/building-the-parallel-polis/); [CIP](https://www.cip.org/blog/network-societies))
4. **Verifiable membership criteria and verifiable impact**: Optimism's on-chain-checkable citizenship + metrics-based retro funding; Gitcoin's COCM; OECD assembly standards. The 2020s discovery is that *evidence infrastructure is upstream of voting infrastructure*. ([Optimism S8](https://www.optimism.io/blog/governance-in-season-8-the-next-phase); [Retro Funding 2025](https://www.optimism.io/blog/retro-funding-2025))
5. **An unbroken, auditable chain from deliberation to decision** — Ireland 2018 vs Ireland 2024 is the controlled experiment: same country, same instrument; the one where the government silently edited the assembly's output failed 67/73. ([I-CONnect](https://www.iconnectblog.com/the-future-for-citizens-assemblies-in-ireland/))
6. **Forkability and federation instead of central ownership**: Zuzalu's 20+ forks via shared open infra; IETF's anyone-can-implement; Ostrom's nested tiers. Durability correlates with how cheaply a local node can adopt the pattern under local custody. ([Gitcoin case study](https://gitcoin.co/case-studies/zuzalu-and-pop-up-cities-temporary-coordination-experiments); [Ostrom Workshop](https://ostromworkshop.indiana.edu/research/commons-governance/index.html))

### What makes a toy
- **Identity before acceptance network** (World Passport, 78 years; Praxis's 151k obligation-free "citizens"). ([Wikipedia: Garry Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garry_Davis); [Wikipedia: Praxis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Praxis_(proposed_city)))
- **Constitution before service** — grand founding documents with no daily-life function.
- **Governance without material stake** — DAO voting collapsed into apathy exactly because votes were costless and consequence-free for most holders. ([pen-caforr](https://pen-caforr.org/2026/04/15/dao-governance-2026-hybrid-models-legal-wrappers-and-the-end-of-token-voting/))
- **Deliberation without decision linkage** — the assembly whose output evaporates.
- **Exit rhetoric** — instantly converts every host state into an immune system. ([TechPolicy.Press](https://www.techpolicy.press/trumps-gaza-fantasy-and-the-network-state-the-tech-fueled-future-of-privatized-sovereignty/))
- **Mechanism monoculture** — one clever voting rule asked to carry all legitimacy (pure QF, pure liquid democracy). The survivors are mechanism-plural. ([GG24](https://gitcoin.co/case-studies/gg24-first-funding-round-of-gitcoin-3-0))

### Design rules for a citizens-of-the-world noosphere people join BEFORE a crisis
1. **Lead with a service, reveal the constitution through use.** The join reason must be a same-week concrete benefit (Estonia: company-in-a-day; mutual aid: sickness insurance). For the Commons: *a signed, portable, non-backdateable receipt for what your agent did and on whose authority* — useful the day the EU AI Act's enforcement powers activate (Aug 2, 2026 — see §15).
2. **Be an interface, not an exit.** Frame as "network society" infrastructure that makes existing institutions *more* capable (Team Mirai's move), never as their replacement-in-waiting. The parallel lane earns its role by being load-bearing during normal times.
3. **Verify persons and stakes separately; never let money vote alone.** Optimism's two-house/four-stakeholder separation with proof-of-personhood is the current best practice. ([Optimism docs](https://community.optimism.io/citizens-house/faq-citizenship))
4. **Make evidence the base layer and voting the exception**: default-approve + challenge rights (optimistic governance) with graduated sanctions and cheap dispute resolution (Ostrom principles 5–6).
5. **Keep the global layer thin** (identity, schemas, invariants, challenge rights) and everything else locally custodied and vetoable — Ostrom's nesting, the plan's existing federated-bioregional stance, confirmed by every survivor in this survey.
6. **Preserve the deliberation→decision receipt chain end-to-end.** Ireland 2024 is what a broken chain costs.
7. **Design for forks**: publish the schema and reference implementation the way IETF publishes RFCs; measure success in independent adoptions, not member counts.
8. **Moral universalism in the constitution's voice** — claims for everyone (Benda), including host states; explicitly renounce sovereignty claims.

## 15. What This Means for the Commons (strategic read)

**1. The thesis is confirmed by the field's own literature — the window is real but already visibly closing.** The A2A/MCP/AP2 stack matured dramatically (A2A: donated to Linux Foundation, 150+ production orgs by Apr 2026; AP2's signed Intent/Cart/Payment mandates already form "a non-repudiable audit trail of who authorized what") ([state of agentic standards](https://amdatalakehouse.substack.com/p/the-state-of-agentic-ai-standards); [AP2 overview](https://www.emergentmind.com/topics/agent-payments-protocol-ap2)). But the academic gap statement is explicit: *"as of early 2026, no single implemented protocol combines offline attenuable delegation, expressive chained policy, provenance-aware completion records, and transport bindings across MCP, A2A, and HTTP"* ([AIP, arXiv:2603.24775](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.24775)). That is the Causal Action Receipt described in a standards paper's vocabulary — *minus* the Commons' unique additions: material/compute debit (SIS), community authority and challenge/reversal (Agora), and outcome-graph learning. The additions are the moat; the core delegation-receipt layer will be standardized by others within ~18 months. **Consequence: interoperate with AP2's mandate chain and A2A cards — extend them with telos/debit/witness fields — do not compete with them.** The 7/10 convergence on "sign the receipt spine" from the 2026-07-11 upstream sweep points the same direction. Nuance on the forcing function: GPAI obligations have applied since Aug 2, 2025; what activates **Aug 2, 2026 is the Commission's enforcement powers (fines, model recalls)** — while the Digital Omnibus (provisional, May 7, 2026) defers Annex III high-risk obligations to Dec 2, 2027 ([EU AI Act timeline](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/timeline/timeline-implementation-eu-ai-act); [Gibson Dunn on the omnibus](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/)). So the regulatory tailwind is real but softer than the estate's shorthand implies — sell receipts on *provenance and audit-readiness*, not on a single compliance cliff.

**2. Do not found a noosphere; grow an acceptance network.** Garry Davis is the controlled experiment for "citizens-of-the-world identity, announced first": 78 years, zero compounding. Praxis is running the modern replication with $525M of conditional money and is producing lawsuits-in-waiting and press cycles, not institutions. The survivors' pattern (Estonia, mutual aid, IETF, Team Mirai) says the noosphere's first form should be *boring and immediately useful to institutions that already exist*: a receipt schema + verifier that a bioregional fund (BioFi/Salmon Returns — values-aligned, verification-starved), a retro-funding round (Optimism/Gitcoin Hypercerts — already buying impact evidence), or an AI-audit client can adopt this quarter. Constitutional identity accrues silently to whoever operates the evidence layer everyone else trusts — exactly how ICANN and IETF became constitutional organs without ever being founded as ones.

**3. The field has already voted for receipt-logic without naming it.** Optimism: metrics-based continuous retro funding + on-chain-verifiable citizenship + optimistic approval with veto = evidence, verified membership, challenge rights. Gitcoin GG24: peer-reviewed Hypercerts. Ireland's failure mode: broken deliberation→decision chain. Wikipedia's crisis: provenance collapse under AI slop. Every live governance system in this survey is independently converging on "witnessed evidence + challenge rights" as the scarce input. The Commons is not proposing an exotic layer; it is proposing the *common factor* — which means the pitch to each community should be in *their* pathology's vocabulary (sybil resistance, impact verification, MRV, audit trail), not in Web5 vocabulary.

**4. Honest solo-operator assessment.** This field's funded actors: Praxis ($525M conditional), Optimism Foundation, Gitcoin, Metagov (grant-funded, 21-team interop cohort, Mina-backed; the reported $1M Scotland commitment is UNVERIFIED — it appears in a Metagov fundraising post summary but not in the IDT project update I fetched ([Metagov substack](https://metagov.substack.com/p/interoperable-deliberative-tools); [fundraising post](https://metagov.substack.com/p/advance-digital-self-governance-by))), DeepMind, Linux Foundation. A solo operator with ~zero revenue cannot out-build any of them and should not try. The asymmetric moves are: (a) **ship the smallest signed-receipt artifact** (schema + CLI verifier + one real adopter's receipts) and get it cited into Metagov's interop ontology / a Gitcoin round / a BioFi pilot — all three are open networks that absorb outside contributions; (b) **write the field-defining essay** mapping the authority-gap (this report's §15.1) — the "network societies not network states" essay slot for the agentic era is *unclaimed*; (c) let the dharma_swarm estate be the reference implementation, not the product. The estate's existing receipts spine plus this field's demonstrated hunger for evidence infrastructure is a genuine fit — but only the shipped, signed, third-party-verifiable artifact converts it from doctrine to institution. Everything in this survey says: the acceptance network is the institution. Start with one acceptor.
