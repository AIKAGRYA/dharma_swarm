**Refuter lens:** Adversarial — legitimacy/cold-start. Attack vector: *Nobody joins parallel governance before a crisis, and by crisis it is too late to have built trust. Citizens-of-the-world has 70+ years of noble failure.*
**Date:** 2026-07-11
**Target:** the combined elevated design (5 architect essays, `arch_*.md` in this directory), read in full; `field_failure-forensics.md` read in full; web verification performed 2026-07-11.
**Discipline:** canon/target claims cited as `path:lines`; web claims as URLs. "Cannot verify now" = UNRESOLVED, never REFUTED. Every REFUTED/WOUNDED verdict carries a `nearby_survivor` (operator standing feedback: refuters must salvage).

## 0. Attack thesis

The five architects have written the most self-aware parallel-institution design in the genre's history: Garry Davis, Praxis, Liberland, Auroville, and the DAOs are all named, autopsied, and negated article-by-article. **But the design refutes membership *theater* while leaving membership *dependency* intact.** Strip the vocabulary ("citizenship-by-receipt," "participation not enrollment," "agents as first constituency") and the sequencing still requires, on a named calendar: witnesses who accept accreditation, challengers who post stakes, skeptics who show up to adjudicated bounties, three independent log operators, a sortitionable population of receipt-holders, bioregional communities that form authority registries, municipalities that sign MOUs with a one-person nonprofit, and paying customers who choose a solo commons over Visa, the Big 4, and their own cloud vendor's audit trail. Each of these is "somebody shows up." The design's own kill-lists concede the current show-up count: **zero external countersigned receipts, operator outreach HOLD since 05-27** (arch_commons-economist.md:184; arch_dharmic.md:202; arch_civilizational-strategist.md:196; arch_protocol.md:232). The adoption story's first step has already been failing for six weeks in its easiest form — the founder himself contacting anyone.

Below: the historical base rate (§1), the steelman of where the design genuinely dodges the attack (§2), the hidden membership dependency table (§3), eight verdicts on the most load-bearing adoption claims (§4), the three-sided cold-start circularity (§5), and the salvaged adoption story that survives the attack (§6).

## 1. The historical base rate: what 78 years of "citizens of the world" actually shows

The attack vector's premise is verifiable and the record is worse than the essays let on — in an instructive direction.

**1.1 World Federalism peaked *during* a crisis, not before one — and still died.**
The World Federalist Movement reached ~150,000 members across 19–22 countries by 1948–1950; the American UWF peaked at 47,000 in 1949 (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Federalism; https://wfm-igp.org/about-us/history-members/). That peak was manufactured by the largest legitimacy shock in modern history — Hiroshima plus the fresh memory of world war — i.e., it is a *crisis-adoption* data point, not a pre-crisis one. The movement then declined through the Korean War and Cold War "due to a lack of funding and successors" (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Federalism). **Both halves of the attack are confirmed at once: (a) pre-crisis voluntary enrollment in parallel world-governance ≈ zero, and (b) even crisis-driven enrollment evaporates when the crisis normalizes.** Trust built *in* the crisis did not persist *past* it. Garry Davis (78 years, no acceptance network — cited by the essays themselves, arch_constitutional.md:15) and Praxis (151,068 obligation-free "citizens," nothing built — arch_constitutional.md:15) are the individual-scale and startup-scale replications of the same result.

**1.2 The design's own evidence base contains almost no pre-crisis adoption of parallel *governance*.**
Audit of every adoption precedent cited across the five essays:

| Precedent (as cited) | When adoption happened | Pre-crisis governance adoption? |
|---|---|---|
| Dáil courts (arch_civilizational:31) | 1919–1921, *while the British petty-sessions system was collapsing* (magistrates resigning, RIC withdrawing — their own text) | **No — crisis-concurrent** |
| Solidarity (arch_civilizational:35) | Born of the 1976/1980 strike crises; Round Table required state collapse | **No — crisis-born** |
| COVID mutual aid (arch_civilizational:43) | Activated in weeks *during* the pandemic; "most dissolved within 18 months" (their own text) | **No — crisis-activated, then evaporated** |
| M-Pesa (arch_civilizational:39) | Pre-crisis, yes — but it is a *service* (send money home cheaper), not governance | Partially — proves service adoption, not polity adoption |
| ARES/RACES ham radio (arch_civilizational:47) | Pre-crisis, yes — but atop a pre-existing intrinsically-motivated hobbyist population and an FCC charter (47 CFR 97.407) | Partially — needs a hobbyist base the Commons lacks |
| Team Mirai (arch_constitutional:27) | Won 11 Diet seats *inside* electoral democracy — a party, not a parallel institution | Not a parallel-lane data point at all |
| Estonia e-residency (arch_constitutional:25) | State-backed; see V6 — 100k in 9 years vs its own 10M projection | State service, not parallel governance |
| Benda's parallel polis (arch_constitutional:27) | Under totalitarianism — the crisis was ambient and permanent | **No — crisis-ambient** |

**Conclusion of §1:** within the design's own citations, the claim "a parallel lane can accumulate governance-grade trust before crisis" has exactly zero clean instances. What has pre-crisis adoption: *services with immediate selfish value* and *hobbyist communities*. This does not kill the design — it forces a reclassification (§6): everything that adopts pre-crisis in this record is a vendor or a hobby, not a polity.

## 2. Steelman: where the design genuinely dodges the attack

Honesty first — three moves in the combined design are real answers to "nobody joins," and the attack must go around them, not through them:

1. **"No enrollment counts ever" / citizenship-by-receipt** (arch_constitutional.md:25,84-96) removes the vanity-metric failure mode (Praxis's 151k, Davis's passports). You cannot fail at membership theater if there is no membership to perform.
2. **Dual-use doctrine — no crisis-only organs** (arch_constitutional.md:151; arch_civilizational.md:74-80) means nothing is asked to be "joined before crisis"; institutions buy boring services and drills. This is the strongest single move in the genre and it has genuine precedent (ARES/RACES, EMAC, Estonia's data embassy).
3. **Grammar-not-place** (arch_protocol.md:14) means adoption can be incumbent-carried: grammars (JSON, Markdown, RSS, SQLite, C2PA) get adopted without anyone joining anything. A SCITT profile needs implementers, not citizens.

The attack therefore concentrates where these moves do NOT reach: the demand side (who pays, against which incumbents), the labor side (who witnesses/challenges/adjudicates), and the constitutional superstructure (assemblies, nodes, federation) whose calendar assumes a population that nothing in the sequencing creates.

## 4. Verdicts

### V1 — REFUTED: "Incumbents structurally cannot own the witnessed challenge/reversal layer; the challenge layer can only be owned by a commons"

**Claim as stated:** arch_constitutional.md:244-246 ("FIDO, Google, Cloudflare, and the card networks... cannot own witnessed challenge and reversal, because each is an actor that would be bound by it: a Visa-owned dispute layer adjudicating Visa's agents is Verra adjudicating Verra's credits"); echoed as "consortia cannot confess" (arch_dharmic.md:41) and "no incumbent builds" the challenge organ (arch_civilizational.md:108).

**Attack.** The claim's central counterexample has been running at planetary scale for fifty years: the card networks' own chargeback/dispute systems. Visa Resolve Online is the mandatory dispute platform for all Visa issuers and acquirers (https://www.chargeflow.io/blog/visa-chargeback-dispute-rules-fees-time-limit); Mastercard's Dispute Resolution Management team acts as "final judge" at arbitration (https://www.chargebackgurus.com/blog/understanding-the-mastercard-dispute-resolution-initiative); the network adjudicates disputes on its own rails, among parties whose fees fund it, across 233B+ transactions/year (https://corporate.visa.com/en/solutions/acceptance/chargebacks.html) — an actor-owned, structurally conflicted challenge-and-reversal layer that is nonetheless the *trusted default* for billions of humans. PayPal/eBay buyer protection, Amazon A-to-z claims, and ICANN UDRP are further live instances. Worse for the claim: **Visa is already extending this to agents.** The Trusted Agent Protocol (Oct 2025, open framework, GitHub public) plus Visa's extension of its zero-liability guarantee to agent-initiated transactions, with dispute rule changes rolling through 2026 (https://investor.visa.com/news/news-details/2025/Visa-Introduces-Trusted-Agent-Protocol-An-Ecosystem-Led-Framework-for-AI-Commerce/default.aspx; https://github.com/visa/trusted-agent-protocol; https://rivero.tech/blog/agentic-commerce-disputes-liability). The empirical law is the inverse of the essay's: **conflicted dispute layers with distribution beat neutral dispute layers without it, every time it has been tried.** "Structurally conflicted" has never once prevented ownership; it only caps credibility at the level users tolerate — and users tolerate a lot when reversal actually works. The Verra analogy fails on its own terms: Verra died because its verification revenue scaled with approvals AND nobody could reverse anything; chargebacks thrive because reversal is the product, conflict notwithstanding.

**Why REFUTED, not WOUNDED:** the claim is stated as a structural impossibility ("structurally cannot," "can only be owned by a commons"). One standing counterexample refutes an impossibility claim; there are at least four.

**nearby_survivor:** The defensible narrow claim: *incumbents cannot credibly adjudicate first-party disputes — challenges against themselves — nor cross-network disputes between rival stacks.* Visa's DRM will never uphold a challenge that indicts Visa's own protocol design, and no Visa desk adjudicates an AP2-vs-x402 dispute. So the commons-shaped niches are real but smaller: (a) disputes *across* incumbent stacks (the "Switzerland" positioning already in field_positioning-funding, cited at arch_protocol.md:161); (b) *auditing the adjudicators* — who checks Visa's agent-dispute statistics? — which is Loomwork-shaped; (c) disputes where the respondent is the platform itself. Rewrite the boldest claim as "incumbents will own second-party dispute resolution and cannot own first-party accountability" and it survives.

### V2 — WOUNDED: "AI agents are a constituency with no incumbent government — the first jurisdiction for the stateless — so there is no cold-start competition for constituency"

**Claim as stated:** arch_civilizational-strategist.md:16-20,187 (boldest claim: "the first durable parallel polity of the digital era will be built for a constituency that has no incumbent government: AI agents... no incumbent to displace").

**Attack.** Agents are not a constituency; they are property. They do not choose jurisdictions, feel legitimacy, pay dues, or show up — their *deployers* do all of those, and deployers are the single most densely governed population on earth: corporate compliance regimes, cloud-provider ToS, card-network rules (now explicitly extended to agents — V1), insurer requirements, sectoral regulators, and now Big-4 AI assurance practices (PwC/EY/Deloitte/KPMG racing to launch AI audit offerings; KPMG committing $2B to AI services — https://thefinancestory.com/big-4-to-launch-ai-assurance-services; https://www.consultancy.uk/news/40424/big-four-weighing-up-new-ai-audit-offerings; https://chatfin.ai/blog/big-4-ai-agents-ey-kpmg-deloitte-pwc-finance-teams-2026/). The rhetorical move dissolves the *political rival* problem (real win: no state is provoked) but silently replaces "no incumbent" with "every enterprise-governance incumbent simultaneously, competing for the same deployer budget." The "legal vacuum" is a vacuum of *protocol semantics*, not of *governance supply* — and protocol-semantics vacuums are filled by whoever owns the deployer relationship (Visa TAP, MCP audit-trail roadmap — arch_protocol.md:150 concedes MCP's roadmap "already wants audit trails"). "Born into the Commons' jurisdiction" requires deployers to configure their agents to emit CARs; the essay offers no reason a deployer does this except compliance value, which routes to V3 and its incumbents.

**nearby_survivor:** Two pieces survive cleanly. (a) The *posture* value: agents-first framing genuinely avoids the host-state immune response that killed every territorial project in the forensics file — as strategy-of-speech it is sound. (b) The narrow factual claim from the standards literature stands: no implemented protocol today combines attenuable delegation + provenance-aware completion + challenge across MCP/A2A/HTTP (https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.24775, cited at arch_protocol.md:12). A real gap — but a gap is an absence of supply, not a presence of demand, and their own field report says so: "unclaimed is a thesis, not a green light" (quoted at arch_civilizational.md:208).

### V3 — WOUNDED: "The 2026–2028 liability wave presupposes CAR-shaped evidence → within five years no consequential autonomous action will be insurable, procurable, or legally defensible without a signed, registered, challengeable receipt"

**Claim as stated:** arch_protocol.md:221 (boldest claim); load-bearing for every essay's wedge (the 08-02 timing, the $500–5,000 attestation, the PLD sales calendar).

**Attack.** Verified against the regulation texts' summaries: **Article 50 requires disclosure and marking, not witnessed receipts.** Its obligations are: inform natural persons they are interacting with AI; machine-readable marking of synthetic content; deepfake disclosure; emotion-recognition notification (https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/article/50/; https://datamatters.sidley.com/2026/06/24/eu-ai-act-transparency-obligations-preparing-for-compliance-by-2-august-2026/). Every one of these is satisfiable by provider-side tooling (C2PA watermarks, UI banners) with zero third parties, zero witnesses, zero challenge windows. GPAI documentation obligations run provider→regulator, privately. PLD evidence-disclosure duties compel disclosure of *whatever logs exist* — they do not privilege signed public logs over internal ones in any way the essays demonstrate. **No regulation in the cited wave requires the challenge/reversal half — which is precisely the 40% of the CAR that differentiates it.** Meanwhile the compliance budget the wave creates is being captured by incumbents with liability-grade brands: the Big 4 are launching AI assurance lines now (URLs above), and a GC choosing between "PwC attestation" and "solo operator's transparency log" for court-grade defense chooses PwC every time not because of quality but because *choosing PwC is itself the defensible act* (nobody gets fired for hiring the auditor of record). The five-year insurability prediction is unfalsifiable today and is presented with a hedge ("argued, not proven") in one essay but sold as the demand engine in all five.

**nearby_survivor:** The boutique evidentiary wedge survives: *non-backdateable signed logs anchored where eIDAS grants a statutory presumption of integrity* (https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/questions-answers-trust-services-under-european-digital-identity-regulation, cited throughout the essays) are cheaper to produce than to dispute, and a $500–5K attestation for GPAI-touched small deployers — the segment the Big 4 won't price for — is a real micro-market that requires **zero citizens, zero witnesses beyond the operator, zero assemblies.** Also surviving: the essays' own date discipline (never sell 08-02 as a cliff — arch_protocol.md:234) is correct and should be extended to never selling "the regulation demands receipts" — it demands evidence, of which receipts are one candidate form.

### V4 — WOUNDED: "Self-binding manufactures adoption-grade credibility ('credibility is manufactured by exactly one process: being bound first, challenged publicly, and honoring the challenges')"

**Claim as stated:** arch_constitutional.md:246; the "first organism that audits itself in public" motif in all five essays; "a receipt trail that begins before there was anything to gain by faking it" (arch_protocol.md:221); "being small enough to confess is the decisive competitive advantage" (arch_dharmic.md:191).

**Attack.** As an *adoption engine*, the mechanism has a fatal dependency the essays never supply: **an audience.** A standing bounty on an unknown solo operator's receipts attracts no challengers, because challenge incentive scales with *reliance* on the challenged artifact, and nobody relies on it yet. "Challenged publicly and honoring the challenges" therefore never fires; "unchallenged at bounty X" is evidentially indistinguishable from "unwatched at bounty X" — a fact the design's own quality-metric tuple (bond, window, witness diversity, challenges survived — arch_commons-economist.md:60) cannot express, since all four values are trivially satisfied in an empty room. Historical check: no case in the essays' 13-file evidence base — nor any I could locate — where a solo actor's public self-audit generated institutional adoption. Their model precedent, C2PA, was named by regulators because Adobe, Microsoft, BBC, and Intel built and shipped it into distribution — *capital and distribution manufactured that credibility*, exactly the process the boldest claim says cannot work in this layer. The estate's own memory supplies the auto-counterexample: every prior self-report chain contained ≥1 measurable inaccuracy (cited by the constitutional essay itself at arch_constitutional.md:263) — self-audit by the same mind that acts has a documented error floor, which a hostile future customer can weaponize regardless of signatures.

**nearby_survivor:** Self-binding survives as (a) **disqualifier-removal** — table stakes for selling audits at all (you cannot sell notarization while unnotarized), (b) a **grant-channel differentiator** — Longview/LTFF/SFF reviewers do read for integrity artifacts, and "the applicant's estate audits itself in public, adverse findings included" is a genuinely rare sentence in an application, and (c) **rhetorical insurance** — it removes the hypocrisy attack surface every corpse died with. Necessary hygiene and a funding edge; not an adoption mechanism. The Receipt-Zero-as-confession move (arch_commons-economist.md:121) is the best version of it and should be kept.

**Claim as stated:** arch_constitutional.md:25,84-96 ("a citizen is a keyholder with at least one receipt... The membership roll IS the receipt graph"; "citizenship-by-receipt definition makes enrollment structurally impossible to fake — there is nothing to sign up for," arch_constitutional.md:258).

**Attack.** The redefinition deletes the metric, not the dependency. §3's table shows ten distinct show-up requirements on the design's own calendar, none with a recruitment mechanism stronger than "invite," "pay from future fees," or an unnamed university. The historical base rate (§1) is unambiguous: voluntary pre-crisis civic labor for parallel governance has a 78-year record of approximately zero, and even the one 150,000-member spike was crisis-driven and evaporated (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Federalism). The design's civic verbs make the problem *harder*, not easier, than Davis's: Davis needed passive signature-holders; the Commons needs active skilled labor (witnessing to decorrelation standards, bonded challenging, panel service). And the design is already generating its own falsifying data point: with the world's most motivated possible first citizen (the founder), the first external receipt has been blocked for six weeks by the operator's own outreach HOLD — all five essays list this as a top kill condition (arch_commons-economist.md:184: "this design is a beautiful corpse" without one external counterparty). ### V6 — WOUNDED: "The Estonia e-residency pattern proves service-first membership works (service precedes citizenship)"

**Claim as stated:** arch_constitutional.md:25 (inversion 2, with the €124.9M revenue figure); arch_civilizational.md:111 ("Membership is a by-product of service"); arch_dharmic.md:151 ("the constitution is revealed through use — Estonia pattern").

**Attack.** The analogy transfers the conclusion while omitting the engine. Estonia's service value was **sovereign monopoly value** — legal EU company formation, a thing only a state can sell — delivered by a state-owned foundation (Enterprise Estonia) with ministry leadership and national-brand marketing (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-Residency_of_Estonia). Even with all that, adoption ran ~100,000 e-residents in nine years against the founders' own 10-million-by-2025 projection — a ~100× shortfall (same URL). Three corrections follow: (a) the pattern's engine (sovereign-grade service) is precisely what a stateless commons cannot replicate; (b) even the best case of service-first identity is 100× slower than its architects projected — a base rate the Commons' Year-2 federation and Year-3 node counts should be deflated by; (c) e-residency is not parallel governance at all — it is a state's export product — so it cannot evidence *parallel-lane* adoption whatsoever.

**nearby_survivor:** The negative lesson survives untouched and is the analogy's real content: *never count members; sell something useful and let standing be a by-product.* That is a lesson about what to avoid (Praxis), not evidence about what will happen. It remains good design hygiene. The positive engine must instead be imported from V3's surviving wedge (a service with standalone value to the buyer, independent of any commons narrative) — which pins the entire adoption story on the attestation product finding real buyers, and honestly so.

### V7 — WOUNDED: "Assembly Zero / sortitioned panels / the constitutional superstructure can convene on the stated calendar (panels months 7–12; Assembly Zero Year 2)"

**Claim as stated:** arch_constitutional.md:215 ("first sortitioned witness panel (even 7 people, properly drawn, is a working precedent)"), arch_constitutional.md:221 ("Assembly Zero: a sortitioned assembly of actual receipt-holding citizens ratifies the Ten Articles"), Year-2 row of the sequencing in all five essays.

**Attack.** Sortition's legitimacy properties (OECD assembly standards, the cited Irish lesson — arch_constitutional.md:106) depend entirely on drawing from a population large and diverse enough that the draw means something. On the design's own sequencing, the Month-12 receipt-holder population is plausibly: the founder, one-to-three paid contractors, 3–5 invited skeptics, and one restoration partner — overwhelmingly people selected or paid by the founder. A "sortitioned" panel of 7 drawn from ~12 founder-adjacent keyholders is not a working precedent; it is the *appearance* of one, and by the design's own Irish rule (broken deliberation chains destroyed 2024 referenda at 67%/73% — arch_constitutional.md:106) a legitimacy ritual performed without its preconditions is worse than none: it hands any future critic a founding-theater artifact, which is exactly the Liberland pathology ("legitimacy theater" — field_failure-forensics.md:25) the design buries elsewhere. The same page-count problem afflicts Year-2 Assembly Zero ratifying the Ten Articles "after use, by users": with fewer than ~100 independent keyholders, ratification-by-assembly is ratification-by-the-founder's-Rolodex wearing a blindfold.

**nearby_survivor:** The *principle* — ratify after use, never before; receipt the deliberation chain — is the best constitutional idea in the design and survives fully. Until then, use named-adversary adjudication (the invited-skeptics model) and *say so* — a panel of named skeptics is honest at n=12; a "sortitioned assembly" is not.

## 5. The three-sided cold-start circularity, and the single point it actually rests on

The adoption story, traced end to end, is a three-sided marketplace:

- **Emitters** (deployers) come for trust value → which exists only if receipts are *witnessed and challenge-tested*;
- **Witnesses** are paid from fee revenue → which exists only if emitters pay;
- **Challengers** are paid from bounties funded by bonds and issuance fees → which exist only if emitters post them → which emitters do only if surviving challenges confers trust → which requires challengers.

Three-sided cold starts are strictly harder than the two-sided ones that killed Diaspora and ONDC (their own forensics, field_failure-forensics.md:139; arch_civilizational.md:56). The standard escape is to subsidize one side. The design's chosen subsidy source, when you strip the language, is: **1–3 philanthropic grants applied for in a 13-day window (Longview by 07-22/24, SFF, LTFF), by an operator whose outreach HOLD has been standing since 05-27.** Every essay's Year 1 quietly routes through this needle. That is not a refutation — it is a concentration-of-risk finding: the "constitution of the agentic age" currently has the adoption physics of a single grant cycle. The civilizational strategist alone states it at full strength: "The gap being real does not make it fillable by default... Everything in Sections 3–7 is contingent on Phase 0" (arch_civilizational.md:208).

**One further asymmetry the essays under-price:** the crisis-legitimacy trade cuts both ways. §1 shows crisis *creates* joiners (World Federalism's 150k, mutual aid's 4,000 groups) and normalcy dissolves them. The design assumes trust must precede crisis; history suggests capacity must precede crisis but *membership arrives with it and leaves with it*. A design fully aligned with its own evidence would optimize for **dormancy-cheap capacity + crisis-time absorptive surface** (how fast can strangers plug in when they suddenly want to?) rather than pre-crisis constituency in any form. The auxiliary doctrine (arch_civilizational.md:93-99) is 80% of the way there; its missing piece is an explicit "surge onboarding" design — the thing mutual aid had (WhatsApp + spreadsheet in a weekend) and formal systems never do.

## 6. What survives: the salvaged adoption story

Rigorous destruction owes a reconstruction. Ranked by how much attack each piece survived:

1. **The grammar strategy (arch_protocol) survives best.** Grammars are the one artifact class in the record that gets adopted without anyone joining anything — JSON, Markdown, RSS, SQLite, and SCITT itself were authored by tiny actors and carried by incumbents. A SCITT profile + verifier CLI + failing-backdate test vector requires implementers, not citizens, and its adoption channel (IETF/W3C) is the one venue where a solo with running code has ever out-competed capital. **The protocol essay should be the whole public face for 24 months.**
2. **The dual-use/auxiliary doctrine survives as posture** (V7 of the attack surface): no crisis-only organs, boring drills, chartered service. Add the surge-onboarding surface from §5.
3. **The boutique attestation wedge survives as the only demand-side bet that needs nobody to show up but a buyer** (V3 salvage) — sold in the segment beneath Big-4 pricing, on eIDAS-presumption evidentiary value, never on "the regulation requires receipts."
4. **Self-binding survives as hygiene and grant-differentiator** (V4 salvage), with Receipt-Zero-as-confession as its best form.
5. **The first-party accountability niche survives as the moat claim** (V1 salvage): incumbents will own second-party disputes; they cannot audit themselves. Position against Visa TAP not as rival dispute layer but as *the audit of the adjudicators*.
6. **The constitutional superstructure (citizenship, assemblies, nodes, Ten Articles) survives only as sealed cargo**: correctly designed, prematurely scheduled. Gate every civic organ on population preconditions (V7 salvage), rename all pre-threshold versions honestly (named skeptics, paid contractors, vendor relationships), and let "citizen" be a word the system earns the right to use — which, fittingly, is the design's own ONE LAW applied to its own vocabulary.

**The single sentence version:** the design survives as *a solo-authored evidence grammar plus a boutique attestation vendor with unusually honest books, positioned to be absorbed by incumbents on its own terms* — and it dies anywhere it is asked to be a polity before roughly Year 3, because on its own evidence, polities do not acquire members before crises, and the members crises deliver do not stay.
