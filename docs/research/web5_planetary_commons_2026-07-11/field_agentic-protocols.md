## 1. Executive Summary

The agentic web's plumbing consolidated with startling speed between mid-2025 and mid-2026. Communication (MCP, A2A), identity-of-the-caller (Web Bot Auth, ERC-8004, OIDC-A), delegated payments (AP2, x402, ACP, Visa TAP, Mastercard Agent Pay), and content provenance (C2PA) each now have credible owners, foundations, and production deployments. Every one of these answers *"can this agent connect, prove a key, and move money?"*

**None of them answers: under whose living authority did this action occur, what material burden did it create, which purpose did it serve, what independent evidence proves the outcome, and who can challenge or reverse it.** The closest artifacts — Mastercard/Google's "Verifiable Intent" at FIDO, IETF SCITT's freshly-published receipt architecture (RFC 9943), and two 2026 academic papers on agent-action receipts — each hold one or two fragments. Nobody binds the full causal chain into one receipt, and nobody at all touches material/ecological debit or community-defined telos.

The receipt *envelope* is now a solved, standardized problem (SCITT: COSE signed statements + transparency log + receipts). The unclaimed layer is the receipt *semantics* — the Causal Action Receipt's ontology. That is exactly the whitespace, and the window is 12–24 months before FIDO's payments-scoped intent-logs or hyperscaler "audit trail" features metastasize into a de facto answer.

The EU AI Act's 2026-08-02 date is real but narrower than commonly stated: it is the start of Commission *enforcement* against GPAI providers plus Article 50 transparency duties — while the big receipt-shaped wave (high-risk record-keeping and logging) was just postponed to Dec 2027/Aug 2028 by the Digital Omnibus. The forcing function exists; it must be cited precisely or it will discredit the pitch.

## 2. Layer-by-Layer State of the Stack (mid-2026)

### 2.1 Communication & Orchestration

**MCP (Model Context Protocol)** — Anthropic donated MCP to the new **Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF)** under the Linux Foundation on 2025-12-09, co-founded with OpenAI (AGENTS.md) and Block (goose), supported by Google, Microsoft, AWS, Cloudflare, Bloomberg ([Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-the-model-context-protocol-and-establishing-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation); [Linux Foundation press](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-the-formation-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/12/09/openai-anthropic-and-block-join-new-linux-foundation-effort-to-standardize-the-ai-agent-era/)). Scale (secondary sources, treat as order-of-magnitude): ~97M monthly SDK downloads by March 2026 ([Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/mcp-97-million-downloads-model-context-protocol-mainstream)); official registry ~9,650 server records as of 2026-05-24, third-party registries 16k–20k ([MCP Manager stats](https://mcpmanager.ai/blog/mcp-adoption-statistics/)); 41% of surveyed software orgs in limited-or-broad production per Stacklok ([same](https://mcpmanager.ai/blog/mcp-adoption-statistics/)). The [2026 MCP roadmap](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-mcp-roadmap/) (2026-03-09) prioritizes Tasks, `.well-known` server discovery, **enterprise audit trails**, SSO auth, DPoP (SEP-1932) and Workload Identity Federation (SEP-1933); a next-spec release candidate is slated for late July 2026 ([MCP blog](https://blog.modelcontextprotocol.io/posts/2026-07-28-release-candidate/)) with a stateless core, Extensions framework, and authorization hardening. Security reckoning: 30+ CVEs filed Jan–Feb 2026; only 12.9% of servers score "high trust" in one census ([ChatForest ecosystem review](https://chatforest.com/guides/mcp-ecosystem-2026-state-of-the-standard/) — secondary).

**A2A (Agent2Agent)** — Google donated A2A to the Linux Foundation in June 2025 ([LF press](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-launches-the-agent2agent-protocol-project-to-enable-secure-intelligent-communication-between-ai-agents); [Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/google-cloud-donates-a2a-to-linux-foundation/)). **v1.0 shipped 2026-04-09**: 150+ supporting organizations, 22k+ GitHub stars, SDKs in 5 languages, integrated into Azure AI Foundry/Copilot Studio and Amazon Bedrock AgentCore, production use in supply chain, financial services, insurance, IT ops ([LF one-year press release](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/a2a-protocol-surpasses-150-organizations-lands-in-major-cloud-platforms-and-sees-enterprise-production-use-in-first-year)). Relevant to us: v1.0 added **Signed Agent Cards** (cryptographic identity verification of the agent's self-description) and treats AP2 as a payments extension (same source). Signed Agent Cards authenticate a *capability advertisement* — not authority, burden, or outcome.

**OASF / AGNTCY** — the Open Agentic Schema Framework (Cisco Outshift-originated, now under Linux Foundation's AGNTCY project) is an OCSF-derived schema taxonomy for describing agents, feeding a content-addressed, DHT-federated **Agent Directory Service** ([OASF docs](https://docs.agntcy.org/oasf/open-agentic-schema-framework/); [GitHub](https://github.com/agntcy/oasf); [Directory overview](https://dir.agntcy.org/latest/dir/dir-overview/); [arXiv 2509.18787](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.18787)). This is agent *description and discovery* metadata — the closest thing to a schema-governance precedent for a receipt taxonomy, and a plausible home or rival for one.

**W3C** — the [AI Agent Protocol Community Group](https://www.w3.org/groups/cg/agentprotocol/) (proposed 2025-05-08) meets biweekly, has a draft white paper and spec targeting agent discovery/identity/collaboration for an "Agentic Web," with specs expected 2026–2027 ([CG site](https://www.w3.org/community/agentprotocol/); [white paper](https://w3c-cg.github.io/ai-agent-protocol/)). A separate **Agent Identity Registry Protocol CG** opened with a call for participation 2026-04-24 ([W3C](https://www.w3.org/community/agent-identity/2026/04/24/call-for-participation-in-agent-identity-registry-protocol-community-group/)). Google and Microsoft shipped an early Chrome Canary preview of a W3C-developed structured agent-website interaction protocol (WebMCP lineage) in Feb 2026 ([DEV community roundup](https://dev.to/alexmercedcoder/the-state-of-agentic-ai-standards-in-2026-mcp-a2a-webmcp-osi-and-the-protocol-stack-taking-3o2l) — secondary). **Community Groups are free and open to individuals without W3C membership** — the lowest-friction institutional on-ramp that exists for a solo actor.

**MIT NANDA** — "Internet of AI Agents": federated **registry quilt** (gossip/CRDT/cross-signing across Web2+Web3 registries), Verified **AgentFacts**, agent "passports"; v0.3 work-in-progress, NANDA Summit held at MIT April 9–11, 2026 ([MIT Media Lab](https://www.media.mit.edu/groups/nanda/overview/); [GitHub](https://github.com/aidecentralized/NANDA-Quilt-of-Registries-and-Verified-AgentFacts); [arXiv 2507.14263](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.14263); [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/how-mits-project-nanda-aims-to-decentralize-ai-agents/)). Philosophically closest to the Commons' federated/bioregional instinct, but scoped to discovery + identity, not accountability.

### 2.2 Identity, Delegation & Trust

**Cloudflare Web Bot Auth / Signed Agents** — cryptographic bot authentication via HTTP Message Signatures (RFC 9421), Ed25519 key per agent, `Signature-Agent` header + JWKS directory ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/web-bot-auth/); [docs](https://developers.cloudflare.com/bots/reference/bot-verification/web-bot-auth/)). The **Signed Agents** program (2025-08-28) extends this to user-directed agents (ChatGPT agent, Block's goose, Browserbase, Anchor Browser signed at launch) ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/signed-agents/)). Now an **IETF Working Group (WEBBOTAUTH)**, chairs Schinazi & Shekh-Yusef; milestones: standards-track auth specs to IESG by 2026-04-30, key-management BCP by 2026-08-31 ([IETF datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/group/webbotauth/about/); [architecture draft](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/draft-meunier-web-bot-auth-architecture)). Verifies *who signed the request* — explicitly not user delegation, intent, or outcomes (per the program's own scope; [Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/signed-agents/)).

**ERC-8004 "Trustless Agents"** — proposed 2025-08-13 by contributors from MetaMask, Ethereum Foundation, Google, Coinbase; three on-chain registries: Identity (ERC-721), Reputation (feedback signals), Validation (validator-contract results) ([Ethereum Magicians](https://ethereum-magicians.org/t/erc-8004-trustless-agents/25098); [contracts](https://github.com/erc-8004/erc-8004-contracts)). Deployed on Ethereum mainnet + Base/Linea/Hedera testnets, adopted on Avalanche C-Chain (Feb 2026) and BNB Chain ([QuickNode guide](https://blog.quicknode.com/erc-8004-a-developers-guide-to-trustless-ai-agent-identity/); [Medium/Avalanche](https://medium.com/@gwrx2005/erc-8004-a-trustless-agent-standard-for-on-chain-ai-in-avalanche-c-chain-4dc1bdad509a)). **The most important datum in this whole report:** the first empirical study (data through 2026-05-13; [arXiv 2606.26028](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.26028), submitted 2026-06-24) found only **3–15% of registrations are operationally valid** agents (3% Ethereum, 4% BSC, 15% Base); reputation values "not commensurable, feedback records rarely grounded in verifiable interactions"; **sybil/coordinated-inauthentic reviewer behavior at 73.5% (Ethereum), 59.2% (BSC), 90.6% (Base)**. Trust registries without witnessed, grounded evidence demonstrably fail — this is peer-reviewed-grade ammunition for the receipt thesis.

**Enterprise/OAuth lane** — OpenID Foundation's "Identity Management for Agentic AI" (Oct 2025) concluded OAuth2/OIDC suffice inside bounded systems but named gaps: agents switching between autonomous and on-behalf-of modes can't be tracked; OBO flows acknowledge an "impersonation accountability gap" ([OpenID Foundation AuthZEN news](https://openid.net/openid-foundation-advances-authorization-for-the-agent-era-with-new-authzen-working-group-drafts/); [CSA research note](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CSA_research_note_okta_ai_agent_iam_framework_enterprise_gap_20260318-csa-styled.pdf)). AuthZEN WG drafts now cover access-request/approval and MCP tool authorization (COAZ). Academic proposals: **OIDC-A 1.0** for delegation chains and agent attestation ([arXiv 2509.25974](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25974)); **AIP: Agent Identity Protocol** for verifiable delegation across MCP and A2A ([arXiv 2603.24775](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.24775)). A common enterprise failure mode: agents reusing user tokens, "erasing audit separation and shifting liability onto individuals who never approved the action" ([Resilient Cyber](https://www.resilientcyber.io/p/identity-is-the-agentic-ai-problem)).

**Know-Your-Agent / agent passports** — a vendor category now, not a standard: Trulioo, Vouched, AstraSync, RNWY et al. sell "Digital Agent Passport" identity/KYC-style verification for agents ([Trulioo whitepaper](https://www.trulioo.com/resources/white-papers/know-your-agent-an-identity-framework-for-trusted-agentic-commerce); [Vouched](https://www.vouched.id/learn/blog/know-your-agent-guide); [RNWY](https://rnwy.com/learn/ai-agent-passport)). WEF, Gartner, Forrester all published agent-identity reports Oct 2025–Jan 2026 (vendor-reported — UNVERIFIED individually). The intellectual root is governance research: **Chan et al., "IDs for AI Systems"** ([arXiv 2406.12137](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.12137)) — instance-level IDs carrying system cards/certifications so incidents can be attributed and systems shut down; **"Visibility into AI Agents"** ([arXiv 2401.13138](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.13138)) — agent IDs + real-time monitoring + **activity logs** as the three visibility levers; **"Infrastructure for AI Agents"** ([arXiv 2501.10114](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2501.10114)) — proposes attribution, interaction, and **response** (incl. rollback) infrastructure categories. Chan's trio is the academic blueprint of the layer the Commons wants to build; none of it is deployed as open infrastructure.

**LOKA Protocol** ([arXiv 2504.10915](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.10915), April 2025) — the only protocol proposal found that names *ethics as a layer*: Universal Agent Identity Layer + intent-centric communication + a "Decentralized Ethical Consensus Protocol." Academic only, no deployment found (UNVERIFIED beyond paper). Significant as prior art for telos-binding — and as proof the idea has no owner.

### 2.3 Payments & Commerce

**AP2 (Agent Payments Protocol)** — announced by Google 2025-09-16 with 60+ partners (Mastercard, PayPal, Coinbase, Amex, Salesforce); represents each purchase as three signed **Mandates** — Intent, Cart, Payment — carried as **W3C Verifiable Credentials**; supports card, bank, and stablecoin rails ([Google Cloud blog](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/announcing-agents-to-payments-ap2-protocol); [ap2-protocol.org](https://ap2-protocol.org/)). **v0.2 released 2026-04-28 and donated to the FIDO Alliance** for community governance, adding Human-Not-Present payments and **Verifiable Intent** — "a tamper-proof log of user-authorized agent actions to ensure accountability," co-developed with Mastercard and also donated to FIDO ([Google blog](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/google-pay/agent-payments-protocol-fido-alliance/); [FIDO Alliance](https://fidoalliance.org/fido-alliance-to-develop-standards-for-trusted-ai-agent-interactions/); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/artificial-intelligence-2/2026/google-and-mastercard-contribute-agentic-commerce-standards-to-fido-alliance/)). FIDO simultaneously formed an **Agentic Authentication Technical Working Group** (2026-04-28; same FIDO source). **This is the single closest thing on Earth to a Causal Action Receipt — and it is scoped entirely to payments authorization.**

**x402** — Coinbase's revival of HTTP 402: server returns 402 with payment terms, agent pays in stablecoin, retries with a payment-receipt header ([Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com/developer-platform/discover/launches/x402); [x402.org](https://x402.org/)). x402 Foundation announced with Cloudflare 2025-09-23 ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/x402/)); formalized under the Linux Foundation 2026-04-02 with 22 members incl. AWS, Adyen, Amex, Circle, Google, Mastercard, Microsoft, Shopify, Solana Foundation, Stripe, Visa ([BlockEden](https://blockeden.xyz/blog/2026/03/05/x402-foundation-ai-payment-internet/) — secondary; UNVERIFIED against an LF press release). Volume: 100M+ cumulative transactions on Base through Q1 2026, **but** growth was substantially meme-coin/speculative (PING pay-to-mint), with volume moderating in Q1 2026 as speculation cooled; $1+ transactions grew from 49% to 95% of volume ([Chainalysis, 2026-06-03](https://www.chainalysis.com/blog/x402-agentic-payments-adoption/)). Coinbase reported ~69k active agents / 165M tx / ~$50M cumulative volume by late April 2026 (secondary via [Digital Applied](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/x402-payment-protocol-ai-agents-pay-coinbase-cloudflare); UNVERIFIED primary). Chainalysis explicitly notes the on-chain signature identifies *wallet-protocol interaction*, not whether an autonomous agent acted — payment receipts without actor accountability.

**ACP (Agentic Commerce Protocol, OpenAI+Stripe)** — launched Sept 2025 powering "Instant Checkout" in ChatGPT (Etsy live; "a million Shopify merchants coming soon") ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/buy-it-in-chatgpt/); [Stripe](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-openai-instant-checkout); [spec on GitHub](https://github.com/agentic-commerce-protocol/agentic-commerce-protocol)). **Reality check, 2026-03-06:** OpenAI deprioritized native Instant Checkout — "Instant Checkout is moving to Apps" — after adoption stalled; ACP survives as discovery/transaction infrastructure behind merchant apps (Target, DoorDash, Instacart, The Knot) ([Digital Commerce 360](https://www.digitalcommerce360.com/2026/03/06/openai-shifts-checkout-plans-agentic-commerce-strategy/); [Rye analysis](https://rye.com/blog/openai-chatgpt-checkout-agentic-commerce)). Lesson for the Commons: even OpenAI+Stripe couldn't force merchants through integration friction in six months; protocol victory ≠ adoption.

**Card networks** — Mastercard Agent Pay (2025-04-29) tokenizes and verifies *user intent*; Visa Intelligent Commerce (2025-04-30) + **Trusted Agent Protocol** (2025-10-14) verifies *the agent* via attestation headers; Visa took agentic payments live with 30 European issuers ([eco comparison](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15192003-mastercard-agent-pay-vs-visa-trusted-agent-2026-compared); [Stellagent on VIC/TAP](https://stellagent.ai/insights/visa-intelligent-commerce-vic-explained); [FIDO/Finextra](https://fidoalliance.org/finextra-deep-dive-mastercard-verifiable-intent-vs-visa-trusted-agent-protocol/); [Industry Spread](https://theindustryspread.com/visa-agentic-payments-live-30-european-issuers/); [Cloudflare partnership](https://blog.cloudflare.com/secure-agentic-commerce/)). Four incompatible trust models now compete in payments alone (tokenized agent identity / attestation headers / VC mandates / DIDs) ([eco](https://eco.com/support/en/articles/15192005-agent-identity-verification-how-ai-agents-authenticate-purchases-in-2026)).

### 2.4 Provenance & Evidence

**C2PA / Content Credentials** — spec family at v2.x; being fast-tracked as an ISO standard via ISO/IEC JTC 1 (secondary: [EyeSift](https://www.eyesift.com/faq/c2pa-content-credentials-2026-cryptographic-provenance-adoption/), [Internet Pros](https://internet-pros.com/blog/ai-content-provenance-watermarking-c2pa-2026/) — UNVERIFIED against ISO itself). OpenAI attaches C2PA metadata to generated media with a May 2026 layered approach adding SynthID-style watermarking + public verification; Google is rolling C2PA verification into Gemini/Search/Chrome; Samsung Galaxy S25 and Pixel 10 sign captures natively (same secondary sources). C2PA answers *content* provenance — "what produced this artifact" — not *action* accountability. Fragility: metadata is stripped by screenshots/platform transforms, so it is "a provenance signal rather than proof" ([EyeSift](https://www.eyesift.com/faq/c2pa-content-credentials-2026-cryptographic-provenance-adoption/)).

**IETF SCITT — the sleeping giant of this report.** The SCITT architecture was published as **RFC 9943 (June 2026)**: COSE-signed Statements about artifacts, registered with a **Transparency Service** running an append-only verifiable data structure under a registration policy, which then issues **Receipts** — signed inclusion proofs verifiable offline ([IETF datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-scitt-architecture/); [scitt.io](https://scitt.io/)). The Reference API (SCRAPI) draft continues ([draft-ietf-scitt-scrapi](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-scitt-scrapi/)). SCITT was built for software supply chains, but the primitive is domain-agnostic: **the IETF has already standardized "non-backdateable signed claim + independent witness + receipt."** A Causal Action Receipt does not need new cryptography — it needs a SCITT *profile* with agentic semantics.

**Academic convergence on agent-action receipts (2026):**
- **"Notarized Agents: Receiver-Attested Confidential Receipts for AI Agent Actions"** (Juan Figuera, [arXiv 2606.04193](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.04193), June 2026): the *receiver* of an agent's action signs what it observed, encrypts to the owner's key, posts to a transparency log — because "the entity producing the activity log is the same entity whose activity is being logged." Attests identity, authority-token binding, and observed outcome. Acknowledged gaps: receipt suppression, collusion, and no adoption incentive.
- **"Decision Evidence Maturity Model for Agentic AI"** (Solozobov, [arXiv 2605.04093](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.04093), April 2026): names "the container fallacy — the automatic equation of evidence-container presence with audit sufficiency"; ships an open Decision Event Schema + trace reconstructor.
- Governance analyses note that NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, and the EU AI Act contain essentially no direct treatment of "agents" ([CSA/NIST note](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/governance-nist-ai-agent-standards-agentic-governance-v1-csa-styled.pdf)); law firms are now writing about the "agentic liability gap" in contracts ([Clifford Chance](https://www.cliffordchance.com/insights/resources/blogs/talking-tech/en/articles/2026/02/agentic-ai-and-the-liability-gap-your-contracts-may-not-cover.html); [IBM](https://www.ibm.com/think/insights/accountability-gap-autonomous-ai)).

The receipt idea is *in the air* as of Q2 2026 — held by isolated single-author papers and one payments consortium, not by any open standard or movement.

## 4. Who Is Closest to Owning the Receipt Layer?

Ranked by proximity as of 2026-07-11:

1. **FIDO Alliance (AP2 + Mastercard Verifiable Intent + Agentic Authentication TWG)** — the only consortium already shipping a "tamper-proof log of user-authorized agent actions" branded as accountability ([FIDO](https://fidoalliance.org/fido-alliance-to-develop-standards-for-trusted-ai-agent-interactions/)). Backed by Google, Mastercard, and the passkey install-base. Constraint: payments DNA; nothing in its charter touches material burden, community authority, or non-commercial outcomes. If anyone accidentally owns the layer by 2028, it's FIDO extending Verifiable Intent from "purchases" to "actions."
2. **IETF SCITT (RFC 9943)** — owns the *envelope*: signed statement → transparency service → receipt, already a standards-track RFC ([datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-scitt-architecture/)). Constraint: supply-chain framing, no agentic semantics, no community/authority layer. The correct move is to *ride* it, not race it.
3. **Cloudflare** — sits at the network edge signing/verifying a growing share of agent traffic, co-founded the x402 Foundation, partners with both card networks, and drives the IETF WEBBOTAUTH WG. It could unilaterally make edge-witnessed action receipts a product. Constraint: single company, request-level view only.
4. **Hyperscaler enterprise audit** — MCP's 2026 roadmap "audit trails" + Microsoft Entra-style agent IDs will satisfy *enterprise-internal* audit demand, sucking oxygen from an open cross-boundary layer without ever serving the public-accountability need.
5. **Ethereum/ERC-8004 ecosystem** — has the decentralized-registry ideology, but the empirical study shows its trust layer is currently sybil-saturated theater ([arXiv 2606.26028](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.26028)). Post-critique redesigns will look for exactly the "grounded, verifiable interaction evidence" a receipt schema provides — making it a partner/adopter more than a threat.
6. **Solo academics (Figuera, Solozobov, Chan's governance lineage)** — have the concepts, no infrastructure, no consortium. Natural allies or co-authors.

**Nobody owns the semantics.** The named whitespace — receipts binding authority, burden, telos, evidence, challenge — has no foundation, no working group, no vendor category as of today.

## 5. Realistic Standardization Paths for a Small Player

Three routes, with live 2025–2026 precedents:

**A. De facto open source → foundation adoption (recommended spine).** Precedents: AGENTS.md went from an OpenAI markdown convention (Aug 2025) to a Linux Foundation AAIF founding project (Dec 2025) in four months ([LF](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-the-formation-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation)); x402 went from one company's HTTP trick (May 2025) to an LF foundation with Visa/Mastercard/AWS members (April 2026) ([Cloudflare](https://blog.cloudflare.com/x402/); [BlockEden](https://blockeden.xyz/blog/2026/03/05/x402-foundation-ai-payment-internet/)); MCP itself was a single-vendor spec for its entire first year. The 2025–2026 pattern is unambiguous: **running code + permissive license + one visible adopter beats committee drafts.** For a solo operator, the only credible move is: publish the receipt schema (JSON/CBOR profile over SCITT/COSE), a verifier CLI, and a corpus of *real receipts from dharma_swarm's own operation* — then let a foundation fight to host it.

**B. W3C/IETF institutional route (legitimacy wrapper, not the spine).** A **W3C Community Group costs nothing and requires no membership** — and two directly adjacent CGs (AI Agent Protocol; Agent Identity Registry Protocol, open since 2026-04-24) already exist to federate with ([W3C](https://www.w3.org/community/agent-identity/2026/04/24/call-for-participation-in-agent-identity-registry-protocol-community-group/)). At IETF, an individual Internet-Draft costs only effort, and SCITT + WEBBOTAUTH are the two WGs whose charters a "Causal Action Receipt profile" plugs into ([SCITT](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-ietf-scitt-scrapi/); [WEBBOTAUTH](https://datatracker.ietf.org/group/webbotauth/about/)). Realistic timeline to RFC: 3–5 years (Web Bot Auth: BoF at IETF 123 → RFC possible 2027 — and that's with Cloudflare's muscle). Use this route to anchor terminology and gain reviewers, not to gate shipping.
**C. EU route (market wedge, not authorship).** The GPAI Code of Practice was drafted by independent experts convened by the AI Office ([artificialintelligenceact.eu](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/introduction-to-code-of-practice/)) — that door is now closed (final since 2025-07-10; [Latham](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/eu-ai-act-gpai-model-obligations-in-force-and-final-gpai-code-of-practice-in-place)). CEN-CENELEC JTC21 harmonized standards are behind schedule (first publications now targeted Q4 2026 under exceptional acceleration) and run through national bodies/members — structurally hostile terrain for a solo outsider ([CEN-CENELEC](https://www.cencenelec.eu/news-events/news/2025/brief-news/2025-10-23-ai-standardization/); [artificialintelligenceact.eu](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/standard-setting-overview/)). The realistic EU play: **map receipt fields to AI Act articles and sell the mapping** (compliance evidence tooling), and file comments/join the AI Act stakeholder consultations — influence, not authorship.

**Verdict:** de facto open-source spine (A), SCITT-profiled so the cryptography is pre-standardized, wrapped in a free W3C CG for legitimacy (B), with the EU mapping as the commercial wedge (C). This mirrors the 7/10 upstream-sweep convergence on "sign the receipt spine" — with one correction below.

## 6. EU AI Act: What Actually Lands 2026-08-02 — Precisely

Verified against the Commission and law-firm sources:

- **GPAI obligations have applied since 2025-08-02**; what begins **2026-08-02 is the Commission's enforcement power** — information requests, model access demands, recalls, fines up to 3%/€15M for GPAI violations ([Commission AI Act page](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai); [Commission FAQ](https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/faq); [Latham & Watkins](https://www.lw.com/en/insights/eu-ai-act-gpai-model-obligations-in-force-and-final-gpai-code-of-practice-in-place)).
- **Article 50 transparency applies from 2026-08-02**: disclosure of AI interaction, and machine-readable marking of AI-generated/manipulated content — with a Digital Omnibus grace period for watermarking on existing systems to **2026-12-02** ([Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/)).
- **Correction to the forcing-function narrative:** the Digital Omnibus provisional agreement (2026-05-06, formal adoption expected before 2026-08-02) **postponed high-risk obligations — including Article 12 logging/record-keeping — to 2027-12-02 (Annex III) and 2028-08-02 (Annex I)** ([Gibson Dunn](https://www.gibsondunn.com/eu-ai-act-omnibus-agreement-postponed-high-risk-deadlines-and-other-key-changes/); [Inside Global Tech](https://www.insideglobaltech.com/2026/05/28/eu-ai-act-update-timeline-relief-targeted-simplification-and-new-prohibitions/)). The biggest receipt-shaped compliance wave is therefore late-2027, not next month. 2026-08-02 is real urgency for *GPAI providers and transparency marking*, and a 17-month runway for everyone else — arguably the ideal shape for a small player: immediate wedge, time to build.

**What a signed-receipt/evidence layer would satisfy** (GPAI Code of Practice, final version; [code-of-practice.ai](https://code-of-practice.ai/); [overview](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/introduction-to-code-of-practice/)):
1. **Transparency chapter:** Model Documentation Form covering training data, **compute and energy usage**, capabilities/limits — kept current, **retained ≥10 years**, provided to the AI Office and downstream providers on request. Non-backdateable signed receipts of training runs, evals, and releases are a direct implementation — and the compute/energy field is the *only* place in the whole 2026 stack where "material burden" already appears as a legal requirement. That is SIS's opening.
2. **Safety & Security chapter (systemic-risk models, ~5–15 companies):** serious-incident tracking/documenting/reporting on tight deadlines; **records of safety activities retained ≥10 years**; Safety and Security Model Reports. Tamper-evident, independently witnessed logs are strictly stronger than what the Code requires — "exceeds compliance" positioning.
3. **Article 50:** machine-readable content marking = C2PA-shaped provenance; a receipt layer that embeds/links Content Credentials covers it.
4. **Enforcement posture:** from 2026-08-02, providers must answer Commission information requests; cryptographically signed evidence trails convert that from archaeology into a query. Code signatories get lighter monitoring; non-signatories "must prove compliance through alternative means, typically requiring more detailed documentation" — i.e., *more* evidence, either way.
5. **The agent gap:** the AI Act barely addresses "agents" as such ([CSA](https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/governance-nist-ai-agent-standards-agentic-governance-v1-csa-styled.pdf)); when EU guidance inevitably extends to agentic systems (as legal commentary is already demanding: [Clifford Chance](https://www.cliffordchance.com/insights/resources/blogs/talking-tech/en/articles/2026/02/agentic-ai-and-the-liability-gap-your-contracts-may-not-cover.html)), whoever has the running open receipt schema becomes the reference.

## 7. What This Means for the Commons

**1. The thesis survives contact with the evidence — sharpened, not flattered.** After 25+ sources: identity, communication, payments, and content provenance each have owners, foundations, and momentum. The five-way binding (authority + material burden + telos + evidence + challenge/reversal) exists nowhere — not in FIDO, not in the LF foundations, not at IETF, not on-chain. The Causal Action Receipt is a real whitespace, not a framing vanity. And the strongest *empirical* result found (ERC-8004: 3–15% valid registrations, 59–91% sybil feedback) is a direct demonstration of the Commons' core claim: **identity + reputation without witnessed, grounded evidence produces theater.** Cite that study in everything.

**2. Do not invent cryptography or envelopes — invent semantics on SCITT.** RFC 9943 already standardizes signed statements, transparency services, and receipts. The Commons' contribution is the *ontology*: delegation-of-authority claims, compute/material-debit claims, declared-telos claims, independent-witness outcome claims, challenge/reversal claims — as a SCITT profile + JSON-LD/CBOR schema. This collapses years of standards risk into a document the operator can draft this quarter, and it makes every SCITT implementation a potential runtime.

**3. The window is 12–24 months and the encroachment vector is FIDO.** "Verifiable Intent — a tamper-proof log of user-authorized agent actions to ensure accountability" is the Commons' pitch in Mastercard's mouth, donated to FIDO in April 2026. Today it covers payments only. The generalization from "authorized purchase" to "authorized action" is obvious enough that a FIDO TWG will attempt it. A small player wins only by (a) publishing first with running receipts, (b) covering what FIDO structurally cannot: material burden, community/bioregional authority, non-commercial outcomes, and challenge rights held by affected third parties rather than cardholders.

**4. The x402/ACP data teaches distribution humility.** x402's 100M transactions were substantially meme-coin speculation; OpenAI+Stripe couldn't push merchants through checkout integration in six months. Protocols without a desperate first user stall. The Commons' first user is already in custody: **dharma_swarm itself.** The credible launch is not a whitepaper but a public transparency log of the estate's own receipts — every agent action, compute debit, and council decision, independently witnessable. "The first organism that audits itself in public" is a demonstration no consortium can fake — and it converts the operator's existing receipts-spine work from internal hygiene into the product.

**5. EU timing, stated honestly, is better than the slogan.** 2026-08-02 = GPAI enforcement + Article 50 transparency (immediate, narrow, sellable to a handful of model providers and their auditors); 2027-12-02 = Annex III logging/record-keeping (the mass market, 17 months out). The upstream sweep's "sign the receipt spine before 08-02" stands as strategy; as *marketing copy* it must say "enforcement begins" not "obligations land," or the first EU lawyer who reads it discredits the whole layer.

**6. Deepest identity, restated for the master plan.** A2A/MCP built the nervous system's axons; AP2/x402 built its metabolism's currency; C2PA/SCITT built fragments of memory. What no one built is the **conscience**: the layer where an action's authority, cost, purpose, evidence, and contestability travel together and accumulate into learning. That layer is small enough for one person to specify, already standard enough to implement, legally demanded soon enough to fund — and constitutionally, it is the only layer where "earth-tuned, federated, witnessed purpose" is a technical requirement rather than poetry. Whitespace confirmed. Move.
