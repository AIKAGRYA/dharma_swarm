## 0. The headline finding: GDPR appears ZERO times in ~600KB of design

Before the individual verdicts, one corpus-level fact that frames everything: **the word "GDPR" does not occur once** in the five architect essays, the five canon digests, or the eight field reports (verified by grep across all 18 files, 2026-07-11). The design proposes a *global identity + receipt layer* — human-rooted delegation chains, per-action declared intent, location, material burden, witness countersignatures, permanent public queryability — whose flagship legal strategy is anchoring into **EU law** (eIDAS qualified ledgers, AI Act compliance sales, PLD evidence packets), and it never once analyzes the EU's own data-protection regime. The privacy design consists of two sentences about SD-JWT/BBS+ selective disclosure (arch_protocol.md:110) and a "fail-open for humans" welfare clause that is about *entitlement denial*, not data protection.

This is not a gap in my adversarial imagination; it is a gap in the design. A system that (a) markets itself as court-grade evidence, (b) constitutionalizes non-erasure, and (c) roots every receipt in a human key has designed itself into the exact intersection where GDPR, AML/sanctions law, and hostile-state discovery all converge. The verdicts below work through the consequences.

## VERDICT 1 — eIDAS qualified-ledger anchoring = "statutory presumption of integrity across 27 states; admissible/court-grade evidence from day one"

**Claim as made**: arch_constitutional.md:51 ("the constitution's atomic unit is *admissible evidence* from day one"), arch_protocol.md:108 ("the sleeper — eIDAS 2.0 qualified electronic ledgers... statutory presumption"), arch_civilizational.md:88, arch_dharmic.md:141, arch_commons-economist.md:94. All five essays treat this as the master legal hook; field_regulatory-dpi.md:68 calls it "the single strongest legal hook found in this entire survey."

**Attack**:
1. **The presumption is not theirs to own.** Art. 45 presumptions attach only to records in a *qualified* electronic ledger operated by a **qualified trust service provider** under EU national supervision (their own field report says this: field_regulatory-dpi.md:66 — "Qualified ledgers must be run by qualified trust service providers"). The Commons cannot be a QTSP (QTSP status requires EU establishment, conformity assessment, national supervisory approval, and is *revocable*). "Anchor via a partner QTSP" (arch_protocol.md:108, arch_commons-economist.md:142) means the parallel lane's legal weight is a **rented grant from the incumbent legal order** — revocable by a supervisory body, subject to political pressure, and withdrawn precisely in the crisis scenarios the "load-bearing lane" is designed for. The design's deepest legal asset inverts its deepest premise: a lane meant to substitute when "traditional structures give way" derives its evidentiary force from those structures' most bureaucratic organ.
2. **"Day one" is false on the implementing-acts calendar.** The implementing act specifying reference standards for qualified ledgers (Art. 45l(3)) had a deadline of 16 December 2025, and industry comment on the eIDAS implementing-act package was still ongoing into 2026 ([Bitkom statement 2026](https://www.bitkom.org/EN/List-and-detailpages/Publications/eIDAS-Implementing-Acts); [eIDAS timeline](https://eidasreadiness.com/eidas-2-timeline)). Whether ANY QTSP currently offers a qualified-ledger service is UNVERIFIED — I could find no evidence of an operating qualified-ledger market as of mid-2026. The essays' own field report flagged the article numbering "UNVERIFIED against EUR-Lex directly" (field_regulatory-dpi.md:66) and the architects hardened that caveat into "court-grade from day one."
3. **Integrity ≠ veracity.** The presumption covers *sequential chronological ordering and integrity of the records* — i.e., the record wasn't altered or reordered. It does **not** presume the truth of the witnessed outcome, the independence of the witnesses, or the validity of the delegation chain. "Court-grade receipts" conflates tamper-evidence with probative force. A perfectly anchored lie is perfectly anchored.
4. **Self-nomination as regulatory subject.** Anchoring in eIDAS + selling to GPAI deployers + citing receipts in EU proceedings places the Commons squarely inside EU jurisdiction — GDPR (see Verdict 2), AMLR (Verdict 5), AI Act (Verdict 6). The honeypot does not need to be raided; it files itself with the authorities.

**Defense-advocate pass**: the architects do hedge ("anchors into an existing QTSP's qualified ledger; it does not become one" — arch_protocol.md:108), and using an optional anchor is not the same as depending on it. Correct — which is why this is WOUNDED, not REFUTED. But the rhetorical weight ("admissible from day one," "statutory presumption across 27 states" repeated in all five essays, Year-3/5 milestones "eIDAS anchoring live") makes it load-bearing in the pitch, and the pitch is where it fails.

**VERDICT: WOUNDED.**
**Nearby survivor**: multi-anchored non-backdateability without a gatekeeper — RFC 3161 timestamps + OpenTimestamps + cross-log countersigned checkpoints (already in arch_protocol.md:106-108) produce evidence courts can weigh under free assessment of evidence in essentially every jurisdiction, with no revocable EU grant in the chain. Sell "non-backdateable and independently verifiable," never "statutory presumption." Treat eIDAS anchoring as an optional EU-market *bonus* purchased late (Year 3+, if a QTSP market exists), and never let a Commons milestone depend on it.

## VERDICT 2 — "Compost, not Erasure" + append-only public transparency logs holding identity-bearing receipts is survivable under EU data-protection law

**Claim as made**: Ten Articles, Article 9 (arch_constitutional.md:77 — "Rejected, superseded, and failed artifacts remain queryable with reasons and revival paths"); Compost Archive as permanent law reports (arch_constitutional.md:108); "the membership roll IS the receipt graph" (arch_constitutional.md:25); "history is never deleted, only answered" (arch_protocol.md:129); public self-audit logs, published challenge adjudications with named parties, authority histories per key.

**Attack**:
1. **The receipts are personal data.** S1 roots every CAR in a human key (passkey or eIDAS PID — arch_protocol.md:115-116); DIDs, public keys, signatures, delegation chains, location (S6), declared intent (S2), and witness identities are pseudonymous identifiers relating to identifiable persons — *personal data* under GDPR Art. 4(1). Pseudonymization is not anonymization. The design's own citizenship mechanism guarantees identifiability: authority accrues to keys via longitudinal receipt history, so keys MUST be linkable to their history to function.
2. **GDPR applies.** Extraterritorially via Art. 3(2) (offering services to EU data subjects — the entire commercial wedge is EU-targeted), and directly the moment any log node, wrapper, or QTSP anchor sits in the EU (the design mandates multi-jurisdiction federation with EU anchoring). Every federated log operator replicating identity-bearing receipts is plausibly a joint controller (CJEU C-210/16 *Wirtschaftsakademie*, C-40/17 *Fashion ID* — background knowledge) and every cross-border replication is a Chapter V transfer.
3. **The constitutional text mandates what the law forbids.** GDPR Arts. 16 (rectification) and 17 (erasure) grant data subjects rights the Ten Articles constitutionally deny ("compost, not erasure"; "reversal never deletes"). The EDPB's blockchain guidelines — [Guidelines 02/2025, adopted 8 April 2025, final version published 2026](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2026-07/edpb_guidelines_202502_blockchain_v2_en.pdf); [adoption notice](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/news/news/2025/edpb-adopts-guidelines-processing-personal-data-through-blockchains-and-ready_en) — state that storing personal data on an immutable ledger should generally be avoided, that controllers must be able to give effect to erasure (via off-chain storage, key deletion/crypto-shredding, or anonymization), and that hashes/commitments of personal data generally remain personal data. "We are append-only by constitutional design" is precisely the argument the EDPB rejected.
4. **Their own legitimacy path enforces the collision.** DPG registration — a named milestone in all five essays — requires Indicator 7 ("Privacy & Applicable Laws") and the 2026 revisions tightened it toward *demonstrated* data-minimization, consent, and retention practice (field_regulatory-dpi.md:122). The design cannot both register as a DPG and constitutionalize non-erasure of person-linked records.
5. **Fine exposure**: up to 4% of worldwide turnover / €20M, per infringement, per supervisory authority — against an org whose Treasury is designed to be small.

**Defense-advocate pass**: Art. 17(3) contains real exceptions — freedom of expression (17(3)(a)), legal claims (17(3)(e)), archiving in the public interest (17(3)(d), via Art. 89 with a Union/Member-State law basis). A dispute-evidence archive can legitimately retain much adjudication data. And SCITT-style logs *can* be built to hold only digests. Both points are true — and both are redesigns, not defenses of the text as written: 17(3) is case-by-case balancing, not a blanket constitutional immunity a private foundation can self-declare; and nowhere do the essays specify that personal data stays off-log — the Compost Archive, published challenge case law, public Miss Registers, and "membership roll IS the receipt graph" all require person-linked public permanence as designed.

**VERDICT: REFUTED (as designed).** The design decision — constitutional non-erasure of identity-bearing artifacts on public permanent logs, with zero data-protection architecture — is facially incompatible with the law of the very jurisdiction the design anchors in.
**Nearby survivor** (strong, and cheap): the **commitment-only log**. On-log: salted commitments/digests + inclusion proofs only — never personal payloads. Off-log: encrypted receipt payloads under per-receipt keys held by the data's custodial node, erasable via crypto-shredding; compost becomes a tombstoned commitment ("a receipt existed here, was adjudicated thus, and its payload was lawfully erased") — which is arguably *more* dharmic than eternal retention: the confession stands, the person is released. Non-backdateability, challenge lifecycle, and the outcome graph (computed over redacted/aggregated receipts) all survive intact. This must be a Ring-0 compiled invariant (arch_protocol's ~7 invariants gain an 8th: *no personal data on-log, ever*), plus a DPIA before the first public log and an Art. 17(3)(e) retention policy for adjudication records. Even then, EDPB caution on residual identifiers applies — but this is a defensible position instead of an indefensible one.

## VERDICT 3 — Identity-layer privacy is adequate: "verifiable not public" defaults + SD-JWT/BBS+ selective disclosure, achieved with "zero new cryptography"

**Claim as made**: arch_protocol.md:103 ("achieved with zero new cryptography"), :110 ("Selective disclosure via SD-JWT VC / BBS+ where segments carry personal or commercially sensitive data... CARP defaults to *verifiable* not *public*").

**Attack**:
1. **The two halves of the sentence contradict each other.** Unlinkable selective disclosure (BBS) is exactly the cryptography that is NOT standardized: [draft-irtf-cfrg-bbs-signatures](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/draft-irtf-cfrg-bbs-signatures/) is an *informational IRTF Internet-Draft* (version -10, January 2026), not an RFC, "not endorsed by the IETF," requiring BLS12-381 pairings that neither passkeys (ES256), nor eIDAS certificates, nor the COSE mandatory-to-implement profile (Ed25519 + ES256, arch_protocol.md:110) support. So the privacy layer either uses non-standardized cryptography (falsifying "zero new cryptography, ride existing rails") or falls back to SD-JWT — which is **linkable**: the same issuer signature and holder key correlate every presentation. Pick one; the essay claims both.
2. **The constitutional layer is a linkability *maximizer* by design.** Citizenship = keyholder with receipt history (arch_constitutional.md:90); authority = decaying stock accumulated per key via countersigned receipts (arch_constitutional.md:115-124); "the membership roll IS the receipt graph" (:25). You cannot have authority-as-longitudinal-receipt-history AND unlinkable receipts. The design chooses total linkability and renames it citizenship. What accumulates per key: declared intent (S2 — a *purpose* field, the single most sensitive metadata class in surveillance law), location (S6), material consumption (S3), counterparties (S6), community affiliations (S4), and the human root (S1). That is a per-person activity dossier of a completeness no ad-tech or state system currently assembles in one schema — cryptographically authenticated by its own subject, marketed as court-grade, and (per Verdict 2) permanent.
3. **Subpoena/discovery magnetism.** The design *advertises* the honeypot: "juridically decisive receipts," "evidence packets," "cited in a real proceeding." Litigants and states go where the evidence is; a federated public log with legal-presumption anchoring is a one-stop discovery shop with no warrant friction for the public layer and ordinary compulsion for the off-log layer. The EUDI wallet ecosystem the design rides was itself heavily criticized for linkability weaknesses; CARP inherits and then amplifies them by chaining wallet-rooted credentials into public outcome graphs.

**Defense-advocate pass**: "verifiable not public" as a per-segment policy choice is a genuinely good default, and the ConstitutionDAO lesson (naked transparency = exploitable) shows the architects saw part of the problem. The honest reading is that privacy was sketched, not designed — one sentence against nine segments of correlatable structure.

**VERDICT: WOUNDED.**
**Nearby survivor**: the design already contains its own fix — **domain-scoped authority** (arch_constitutional.md:118: "closing loops in watershed restoration earns watershed authority; it confers nothing over schema amendment"). Carry that constitutional principle into key architecture: per-domain pairwise pseudonymous personas (unlinkable *across* domains by default), per-receipt ephemeral keys chained to the persona via off-log delegation, disclosure of cross-domain linkage only to arbiters under an active challenge, and purpose/intent fields disclosed as predicate proofs ("intent was within delegated scope") rather than plaintext. Authority stays longitudinal *within* a domain — which is all the design needs — while the global dossier never forms. This costs real cryptographic engineering (accept the BBS/ZK dependency openly, or scope claims down to what SD-JWT delivers) and should be named as such.

## VERDICT 4 — Publishing FPIC/community authority/witness countersignatures on a permanent public log protects the powerless (the anti-Kariba control)

**Claim as made**: FPIC/community veto as "first-class evidenced field" published to the log (arch_constitutional.md:107, arch_commons-economist.md:81 rule 8); "a bioregional veto is exercised against a funded project and honored, publicly" (arch_commons-economist.md:150); witness countersignatures with independence declarations as public receipt segments (arch_protocol.md S5/S7).

**Attack**:
1. **The same record that proves consent maps the resistance.** [Global Witness documented at least 146 land and environmental defenders killed or disappeared in 2024](https://globalwitness.org/en/press-releases/at-least-146-land-and-environmental-defenders-killed-or-disappeared-globally-in-2024/), with Indigenous people ~one-third of victims; Colombia, Guatemala, Mexico, Brazil lead — several of which are exactly the bioregional-node geographies the design courts. A geolocated, immutable, publicly queryable, cryptographically attributed registry of *which named community members vetoed which funded project* is a targeting directory. Kariba's sin was faking consent; this design's failure mode is *authenticating the dissenters to their enemies*. The threat is not hypothetical in the design's own home geography either: Auroville's residents were pressured individually through visa denial with annotations naming who "obstructed the Master Plan" (field_failure-forensics.md:43) — an exact preview of what a state does with a public record of who blocked what.
2. **The estate's own doctrine forbids this dataset.** Loomwork's hard-refuse categories include "state-actor target lists... ethnic/racial profiling... religious surveillance" (canon_loomwork-darshan.md:52). A public FPIC/veto/witness graph over Indigenous communities *is* a state-actor target list built as a side effect — the receipt schema constructs what the refusal architecture refuses.
3. **Witness identity is structurally exposed.** Independence verification requires knowing who the witness is and their funding edges (S5 `independence_decl`); rotation caps and lottery assignment require a public-ish roster. No essay contains any witness-protection design — no pseudonymous accountable witnessing, no deferred publication, no threat model for the human layer at all. Witnesses paid $50-150/event (arch_commons-economist.md:57) in villages where challenging a funded project gets people killed is not a priced risk; it is an unexamined one.

**Defense-advocate pass**: real FPIC regimes do require *some* public evidencing, and the alternative (secret consent) is the Kariba fraud vector. The tension is real and the architects are right that consent must be evidenced. But evidenced ≠ individually attributed on a permanent public log.

**VERDICT: WOUNDED.**
**Nearby survivor**: **institutional signatures, individual escrow**. The community authority signs as an entity — threshold/k-of-n signature under a council key, members unlisted on-log; individual signer identities are held in escrow by the local custodial node (Ring 2, whose whole point is local data custody — arch_protocol.md:137) and disclosed only to arbiters under an active, bonded challenge. Add publication-deferral windows for sensitive geographies and pseudonymous-but-accountable witnessing (persistent witness pseudonyms whose track records accrue, identity revealed only on adjudicated misconduct). The veto's evidential force and the independence audit survive; the targeting directory does not. This is also the strongest available answer to the Gaian-surveillance critique the field reports worry about (field_planetary-computation.md:139).

## VERDICT 5 — "No enrollment, no identity documents, citizen = keyholder" coexists with the bounty/bond/escrow economics

**Claim as made**: citizenship with "no identity documents, payment, or biometrics" (arch_constitutional.md:90); "no enrollment counts ever" (:25); simultaneously — paid challenge bounties (arch_constitutional.md:104), witness stipends, assurance bonds 5-15% of routed value (arch_commons-economist.md:60), and Shakti Ginko release-on-receipt escrow at 25-75 bps custody of "Just Climate-class" institutional capital (arch_commons-economist.md:66).

**Attack**:
1. **The money layer has an identity law of its own.** The EU's [AML Regulation 2024/1624](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1624/oj/eng) applies directly from **10 July 2027** — inside the design's Year-1/2 window — as a single rulebook: customer due diligence, beneficial-ownership identification, bans on anonymous accounts, travel-rule identity on transfers ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/global-anti-corruption-perspective/new-eu-anti-money-laundering-rules-what-to-know)). Ginko-as-escrow-trustee is plausibly a payment service / trust-company activity requiring authorization (PSD2/PSD3 in the EU; state money-transmitter licensing in the US); an entity that takes third-party funds, holds them against conditions, and disburses to third parties is the definitional case. None of the five essays mention AML, KYC, sanctions, or licensing — the words do not appear.
2. **Sanctions are strict liability.** Paying bounties/stipends to pseudonymous keyholders means paying parties you cannot screen against OFAC/EU/UN lists. The Tornado Cash arc is the wrong comfort: the Fifth Circuit ([Van Loon, Nov 2024](https://www.venable.com/insights/publications/2025/04/a-legal-whirlwind-settles-treasury-lifts-sanctions)) protected *immutable smart contracts* as non-property, and OFAC delisted in March 2025 — but Ginko is not immutable code; it is a human-operated fiduciary with discretion, exactly the kind of actor sanctions law reaches. A single bounty paid to a sanctioned wallet is an enforcement action against the Treasury organ.
3. **The collision is constitutional, not operational.** The identity floor ("anti-Aadhaar, anti-Worldcoin — no identity documents ever") is written as a Tier-0-grade principle; the revenue design requires flows that legally mandate identification. As written, the constitution promises what the treasury cannot legally do.

**Defense-advocate pass**: the design could claim bounties/stipends stay small, in-kind, or below thresholds; and that Ginko contracts only with KYC'd institutions. Plausible for the escrow leg (funders and restoration partners are identified organizations) — much weaker for bounties, whose whole design intent is that *anyone*, including pseudonymous challengers, is paid for proving error.

**VERDICT: WOUNDED.**
**Nearby survivor**: **pseudonymous standing, identified payout** — split the layers constitutionally. The civic verbs (ACT/WITNESS/CHALLENGE) and all standing/authority/reputation remain enrollment-free and pseudonymous; every fiat crossing runs through a KYC membrane operated by a regulated fiscal agent (fiscal sponsor now, licensed EMI/trust partner later). Challengers who refuse identification can elect settlement in non-transferable authority credit instead of cash (which the design already values above money). The anti-Aadhaar principle survives intact where it matters — no human is ever *denied standing or entitlements* for lack of ID — while the money boundary complies. Ginko should be scoped from birth as "regulated fiduciary partner + Commons-defined disbursement triggers," never as an unlicensed money transmitter.

## VERDICT 6 — "The first constituency is a population with no government at all: AI agents... dual power without a rival; no incumbent to displace"

**Claim as made**: arch_civilizational.md:16-20 (elevated thesis), :187 (boldest claim: "receipts as birth certificates, the challenge court as their first law"; "first jurisdiction for the stateless").

**Attack**:
1. **Category error: agents are not stateless — they are property.** Every CAR chains to a human or legal-person root by the design's own S1 (arch_protocol.md:39-42: delegation_chain root→leaf, human_root required). A "jurisdiction over agents" is therefore a jurisdiction over *the persons whose delegations root the receipts* — the same constituency every state already governs. There is no ungoverned population; there is ungoverned *conduct* of governed persons, which is a different thing, and states are actively closing it.
2. **The incumbent exists and the essays prove it themselves.** The entire commercial wedge sells into the incumbent's rules: EU AI Act Art. 50 + GPAI enforcement, PLD strict liability, California AB 316, Singapore IMDA's agentic framework (which already *requires* verifiable agent identity + authority audit trails — field_regulatory-dpi.md:46). Law that presupposes evidence about agents IS government of agents. "No incumbent to displace" and "liability regimes are already demanding this" cannot both be true; the second claim (which is documented) refutes the first (which is rhetoric).
3. **The framing is the named kill condition.** "Jurisdiction," "birth certificates," "challenge court as first law," "first durable parallel polity" is precisely the sovereignty vocabulary the same essay's Auxiliary Doctrine prohibits (arch_civilizational.md:97-98: "no sovereignty vocabulary, ever" — kill condition 7). The boldest claim ships the banned framing.
4. **Both horns of the court dilemma.** If Challenge Desk outcomes produce legal effects (bond forfeiture, authority slashing with economic consequences), then: (a) it is arbitration/ADR, living inside state law (NY Convention, national arbitration acts) — fine, but then it is not a parallel jurisdiction; and (b) any AI system substantively contributing to adjudication is **high-risk under AI Act Annex III point 8(a)** ("...or to be used in a similar way in alternative dispute resolution" — [Annex III](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/3/); [Kluwer Arbitration analysis](https://legalblogs.wolterskluwer.com/arbitration-blog/we-need-to-talk-about-the-eu-ai-act/)), with full high-risk obligations from Dec 2027 — an AI-native commons running AI-assisted challenge adjudication becomes itself the regulated artifact. If outcomes produce NO legal effects, it is not a jurisdiction at all — it is an evidence-and-reputation service. Either way, "first jurisdiction for the stateless" is false.

**Defense-advocate pass**: the steelman — "no agent-*specific* governance regime existed at the seed date; the coordination layer between agents of different principals is genuinely unowned" — is true and valuable. But that is the *evidence-layer* claim, which the essay separately makes and which survives; the *jurisdiction/polity* claim is the elevation, and the elevation is what fails.

**VERDICT: REFUTED (as framed).**
**Nearby survivor** (explicitly strong): the rest of arch_civilizational's own architecture — the **same-law, auxiliary, evidence-first posture** (Dáil courts applying existing law; M-Pesa attaching to existing flows; ARES/RACES chartered auxiliaries). Restated survivably: *the Commons is the first evidence and dispute-support layer for conduct no one yet governs, constituted inside existing law — its challenge desks are contractual expert-determination/arbitration, its receipts are evidence in anyone's court, and its authority is reputational.* Everything load-bearing survives; only the polity narrative composts. Their own kill-review should have caught this: it is kill-condition 7 fired by section 7.

## VERDICT 7 — Auroville-class host-state capture is answered by multi-homed wrappers + fork-with-history

**Claim as made**: Article 7, fork-right/exit-with-history + custody multi-homed across ≥3 wrappers/jurisdictions (arch_constitutional.md:75); "multi-home or die" (arch_civilizational.md:51); failure-forensics Law 1 (field_failure-forensics.md:192).

**Attack**:
1. **Fork-right protects bits; Auroville lost atoms.** The Auroville pressure instruments were physical custody, asset control, paramilitary presence, and **visas** — individually annotated visa denials naming residents who "obstructed the Master Plan" (field_failure-forensics.md:41-44). No amount of log federation forks a residence permit. The design's mitigations all live in the digital/corporate layer; the attack that killed the precedent lives in the human/territorial layer.
2. **The founder is the single point of territorial failure, and no essay models his host state.** The operator is a foreign national resident in Bali; arch_commons-economist.md:127 names "Bali as the natural first node" and :169 makes the estate Customer #0 there; arch_civilizational.md:60 flags the subak as design ancestry. Indonesia's Ormas law (2013, hardened by Perppu 2/2017) permits government dissolution of organizations deemed contrary to Pancasila **without prior court order**; foreign NGOs require Foreign Ministry permits with State Intelligence Agency participation; foreign founders face steep residency and deposit requirements; foreign-funded organizations are barred from "political activities" and activities disrupting "stability and oneness" ([ICNL Indonesia monitor](https://www.icnl.org/resources/civic-freedom-monitor/indonesia); [HRW](https://www.hrw.org/news/2013/07/17/indonesia-amend-law-mass-organizations); [IFEX](https://ifex.org/indonesias-ormas-law-a-ready-weapon-against-civil-society-and-free-speech/)). A foreign operator running a "governance layer" whose local "bioregional custodial node" publicly vetoes funded projects (arch_commons-economist.md:150) is close to a textbook trigger profile. Indonesia also suspended Worldcoin — the design's own forensics file records it (field_failure-forensics.md:120). The design that derives Ten Articles from Auroville's corpse re-creates Auroville's *setup*: spiritual-lineage foreign founder, earth-tuned polity, host state with discretionary dissolution powers and visa leverage — and answers it with... corporate structure in other countries.
3. **Multi-homing multiplies regulatory surface without proving resilience.** Each wrapper is fully seizable/compellable in its own jurisdiction; three wrappers = three states with levers (and, per Verdict 2, three GDPR establishments; per Verdict 5, three AML regimes). The historical record for multi-homing *defeating* a determined host state is thin; the record for single points of human failure (founder arrest, deportation, asset freeze) defeating distributed organizations is thick.

**Defense-advocate pass**: multi-homing + fork-right genuinely does defeat the *specific* Auroville mechanism (single charter capture), and the architects explicitly note "zero wrappers exist yet" as momentary safety (arch_constitutional.md:255). The failure is not the mechanism; it is the unmodeled residual: the founder's body, the first node's geography, and the treasury.

**VERDICT: WOUNDED.**
**Nearby survivor**: the **grammar-not-network posture taken literally**. An IETF internet-draft, a schema, test vectors, and a verifier CLI cannot be dissolved by three ministers — specs are not incorporable souls (the design's own Beckn/FIDE insight, arch_protocol.md:154). Concretely: (a) first custodial node NOT in the founder's jurisdiction of residence — run Bali as design ancestry and study site, not Node #1; (b) wrappers only in strong-rule-of-law jurisdictions, treasury minimal and fiscally sponsored; (c) founder treated as compromisable from day one — the succession/seat-externalization instruments the essays schedule for Year 2-7 are actually Month-1 work, because the founder's host-state exposure exists now; (d) never operate the veto-legitimizing registry from soil where the vetoed interests can reach the operator's visa.

## VERDICT 8 — The underlying demand thesis: 2026-2028 liability regimes create real demand for non-backdateable, third-party-verifiable evidence of agent actions

**Claim as made**: arch_protocol.md:221 (boldest claim), field_regulatory-dpi.md:57 ("liability law is converging on evidence as the battleground"), arch_constitutional.md:149.

**Attack attempted**: I tried to break this three ways. (1) *Regulatory-target inversion*: does the evidence layer itself become regulated out of existence? Mostly no — producing and notarizing evidence is not a licensed activity (unlike adjudicating disputes or moving money, Verdicts 5-6); PLD/AB 316/Art. 50 pressure on deployers is real and verified in the field reports; the omnibus deferral moved dates but not direction. (2) *Date fragility*: the essays themselves already police the 08-02 hype (arch_protocol.md:234, kill #10) — the discipline exists in-design. (3) *Attestation liability*: selling "$500-5,000 agent-audit attestations" as "audit-readiness" (arch_protocol.md:197) does carry real exposure — negligent-misstatement liability to relying third parties, and risk of implying accredited conformity-assessment status the operator does not hold; "court-grade" marketing invites the integrity-vs-veracity conflation of Verdict 1. But this is a language-discipline wound to one product SKU, not a break of the demand thesis: notarization-of-existence (registration facts, timestamps, inclusion proofs) is unambiguously safe to sell; compliance *opinions* are not, and should be left to lawyers.

**VERDICT: SURVIVES.** The demand-side thesis — evidence of agent conduct is becoming commercially and legally existential, and nothing in the current stack produces contestable, cross-boundary, non-backdateable records — held up against everything I threw at it. It is the solid ground under all five essays.
**Hardening note (not required, but cheap)**: scope the attestation product as "notarization + verification report of registered facts," never "audit," "readiness," or "compliance"; carry E&O insurance before the first paid attestation; and lead sales with PLD evidence-disclosure and AB 316 (which are about *records*), not Art. 50 (which is about *marking*).

## Cross-cutting salvage synthesis (what a survivable redesign looks like)

The five essays' deepest instincts — bind the founder first, sell the challenge layer, ride existing rails, never mint an asset — all survive this attack. What does not survive is the unexamined assumption that *evidence infrastructure is privacy-neutral and jurisdiction-neutral*. Seven redesign requirements, all compatible with the existing architecture:

1. **No personal data on-log, ever** — commitment-only transparency logs, payloads off-log under deletable keys (Ring-0 invariant #8). [Verdict 2]
2. **Domain-scoped pseudonymous personas** — authority longitudinal within domains, unlinkable across them; cross-domain linkage disclosed only to arbiters under challenge. [Verdict 3]
3. **Institutional signatures, individual escrow** for FPIC, community authority, and witnesses — threshold keys, deferred publication, pseudonymous accountable witnessing. [Verdict 4]
4. **Pseudonymous standing, identified payout** — a KYC membrane at every fiat boundary, run by a regulated fiscal agent; authority-credit settlement for those who refuse it. [Verdict 5]
5. **Compost the polity vocabulary** — jurisdiction/birth-certificate/first-law language dies before anything ships; the challenge desk incorporates as contractual expert-determination inside arbitration law; assess Annex III 8(a) before any AI touches adjudication. [Verdict 6]
6. **Found from safe soil** — first node and wrappers outside the founder's residence jurisdiction; succession and seat-externalization instruments in Month 1, not Year 2; eIDAS as optional bonus, not spine. [Verdicts 1, 7]
7. **DPIA + data-protection counsel before the first public log entry** — the cheapest possible insurance against the only verdict on this list that is a flat REFUTED. [Verdict 2]

None of these weaken the mission. Several strengthen it: a commons that can prove it *cannot* build a surveillance dossier — because the dossier is cryptographically impossible, not merely promised away — is a more credible constitutional layer than one that asks for trust. That is, after all, the design's own core doctrine: compiled invariants over cultural discipline.
