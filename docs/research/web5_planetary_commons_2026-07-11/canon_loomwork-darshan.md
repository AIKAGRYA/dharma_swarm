## 1. Core thesis of the cluster

**Loomwork is the organism's outward-facing immune sense — "Palantir reforged for Jagat Kalyan."** The one-sentence executive statement:

> "Loomwork is the public, telos-gated, multi-evaluator dot-connection substrate that Palantir would have been if its customer were civil society instead of intelligence agencies." (L100:13)

It ingests world-data atoms (deforestation alerts, methane plumes, leaked filings, court records, sanctions, dark-vessel tracks, refugee profiles, regulatory dockets, AI-harm reports), cross-pollinates them across heterogeneous sources with ≥3 decorrelated evaluators, and publishes **cited revelations engineered for outcome-conversion** — "named target × pre-existing legal infrastructure × audience-specific artifact × coordinated drop" (L100:13). Its civilizational role is explicitly bounded: **"immune-system + catalyst, NOT mirror, NOT substrate primary, NOT memorial, NEVER arbiter-of-truth"** (L100:117, echoing L100:29).

The theory-of-change inversion is the intellectual heart: **the bottleneck is not atom→revelation, it is revelation→outcome.** "Pandora Papers had MORE atoms than Panama Papers but produced 18× LESS outcome ($76.4M vs $1.36B in tax recoveries) — *more atoms produce less outcome unless the publication moment is engineered*" (L100:23). Hence the outcome-engineering layer: ≥3 artifact shapes per revelation across eight audiences — "journalist brief / NGO advocacy pack / regulator complaint draft / prosecutorial evidence pack / political brief / activist card / market-signal feed / citizen card" (L100:39). This is precisely the "casefiles for journalists/NGOs/regulators/citizens" function named in the mission brief.

**Darshan is the two-way membrane between the organism and the human noosphere.** The 2026-07-08 report designs "the articulation membrane: one organ in which writing an article for real readers *is* the act that crystallizes machine-readable direction for the organism — three braided roles (downward direction, outward build-in-public, returning reader-response as the only fitness), three firewalls, all on existing substrate" (DARS:14). Its single legitimate success metric is `external_readers_who_read_and_replied` (DARS:27) — a human on the outside acted. Its `is_not` list guards against drift: "[cms, paywall, daily_engine, autonomous_writer, consulting_funnel, spiritual_brand]" (DARS:27).
