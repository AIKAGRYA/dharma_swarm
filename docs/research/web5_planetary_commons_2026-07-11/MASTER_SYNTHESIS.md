**Web5, honest version:** the web becomes capable of coordinating and evolving under *witnessed purpose, material accountability, reciprocal obligation, and institutionalized correction* — where confessing and reversing an error is cheaper than defending it. This is THE ONE LAW (NORTH_STAR.md:57-59) generalized from one organism to civilization: authority is never granted, purchased, or declared — it is the decaying, domain-scoped residue of witnessed loops closed through reality.

**Three inversions that survived all attacks:**
