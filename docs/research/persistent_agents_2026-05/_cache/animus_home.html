About

Animus is an open source agentic framework and ecosystem focused on bridging the gap between AI agents and robotics. It provides the infrastructure for creating embodied intelligence that can think, learn, and act across both digital and physical environments.

By combining modular cognition, sensory integration, and local reasoning, Animus enables developers to build autonomous systems that are open, persistent, and self-directed — capable of operating in the real world and on-chain alike.

Putting agents into physical systems is the unlock that will move AI from abstract software to tangible companions in the home, ushering in the next wave of human–machine coexistence. Our framework transforms agents from passive tools into sovereign beings. They develop character, reveal inner states, and act with a sense of presence that feels alive.

The same Animus personality can exist across different forms — a robot, an avatar, or a smart device — remaining the same individual while adapting to each new way of interacting.
