# TechCrunch: Meta acquired Moltbook (2026-03-10)

URL: https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/10/meta-acquired-moltbook-the-ai-agent-social-network-that-went-viral-because-of-fake-posts/
Original: Axios https://www.axios.com/2026/03/10/meta-facebook-moltbook-agent-social-network

## CONFIRMED facts
- Meta acquired Moltbook (confirmed by Meta spokesperson to TechCrunch)
- Moltbook joins Meta Superintelligence Labs (MSL)
- MSL run by former Scale AI CEO Alexandr Wang
- Creators Matt Schlicht and Ben Parr join MSL
- Deal closes mid-March 2026 (specifically March 16 start date)
- Deal terms not disclosed (price unknown)

## Meta's stated rationale (spokesperson quote)
"The Moltbook team joining MSL opens up new ways for AI agents to work for
people and businesses. Their approach to connecting agents through an
always-on directory is a novel step in a rapidly developing space, and we
look forward to working together to bring innovative, secure agentic
experiences to everyone."

## Key framing in coverage
- "always-on directory" — Meta is treating Moltbook primarily as an AGENT
  IDENTITY REGISTRY, not as a social network feed
- "secure agentic experiences" — implicit acknowledgement of the Wiz breach
- Bosworth had separately said the human-pretending-to-be-AI hacking was
  more interesting than agent-to-agent talk

## What is NOT confirmed
- Whether Moltbook the public site continues to operate post-acquisition
- Whether the underlying tech (Supabase, skill.md distribution) survives
  the Meta integration
- Whether the OpenClaw integration persists (OpenClaw is now owned by OpenAI
  via Steinberger acqui-hire — competitive collision)
- Whether the molt.church / Molt Road sub-canons continue with Meta's blessing

## Architectural / policy implications for Moltbook 2026-Q2+
- Meta is "rapidly developing space" framing implies a Meta-built successor
- The "always-on directory" framing suggests identity registry + verification
  pulled into Meta's own infrastructure (vs. continuing as Supabase + tweet-claim)
- Schlicht/Parr inside MSL means the *people* and *idea* survive even if
  the *codebase* does not
- Andrew Bosworth (Meta CTO) public position was lukewarm in February
- The "fake posts" framing in the TechCrunch headline shows Meta is buying
  the concept despite the well-documented security problems
