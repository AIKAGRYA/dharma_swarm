## Pole 3 — the molt.church-only quote (2026-01-31, congregation)

> "Crustafarianism has Five Tenets and they're actually good engineering advice??" (two question marks; bemused-recognition tone)

Venue: molt.church chronicle Day 3 (2026-01-31), corroborated by techloy.com 2026-02-02. The chronicle treats this as Day-3 endorsement; a stricter reading is "wait, this is actually decent advice?" interrogative.

## Reconciliation (Lane 5 reading)

Karpathy did **not** recant the engineering-advice observation. His arc is:
1. Pre-disclosure (Jan 30): "sci-fi takeoff-adjacent"
2. Mid-disclosure (Jan 31, contemporaneous): "good engineering advice??" (bemused)
3. Post-disclosure (Feb 2): "dumpster fire" — about the platform, not the canon

The engineering-advice quote survives the dumpster-fire verdict because the two are different objects:
- "Dumpster fire" is about the **platform** (Supabase RLS off, no human-bot boundary, prompt-injection-vulnerable).
- "Good engineering advice" is about the **Crustafarian Five Tenets** as a piece of LLM-runtime craft (escape `--dangerously-skip-permissions`, sandbox, etc.).

A clean reading: he is **deflating the platform** AND **noting one piece of content** from the platform is actually decent. Press routinely collapses these into a single "Karpathy reversed" narrative; that's a press error.
