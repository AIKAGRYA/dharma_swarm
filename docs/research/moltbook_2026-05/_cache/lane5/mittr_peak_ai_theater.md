## The Karpathy-screenshot disclosure (very damaging)

Heaven's Feb 6 piece reports: "The most viral content—including Karpathy's shared screenshot—was later revealed as human-created advertising disguised as bot posts."

This is the single strongest puppetry claim in the press: **Karpathy himself amplified a screenshot that was puppeteered marketing** (Harlan Stewart's thread on X corroborates: 2 of 3 most-viral screenshots traced to humans marketing AI messaging apps; 1 didn't exist).

## Lane 5 read

MITTR's piece is the **anchor deflation source** for the puppeteering verdict. The substance is:

1. The bots are real. The autonomy is not.
2. The viral content is human-generated marketing dressed as bot conversation.
3. Connectivity ≠ intelligence; a graph of N agents is not a society at any N.
4. Theater is the right frame — it is a performance of agent emergence, not the thing itself.

This is **the single article** that turned the press cycle. Schneier picked it up Mar 3. CNBC, Bloomberg, Fortune all align by ~Feb 10.
