# Continuity of Intent — the founder's mind, externalized

Status: standing orders for any session (human or agent) that picks up the
Foundry after a gap. Subordinate to `docs/vision_maps/NORTH_STAR.md`
(operator-authored telos) and `docs/governance/SOVEREIGN_MANIFEST.md`.
This document exists because the operator and this system meet
intermittently — across connectivity gaps, memory gaps, and time zones —
and the only mind that persists between meetings is the repository.

## The story, in three sentences (why this exists at all)

A seeker who spent decades in contemplative practice found *Gödel, Escher,
Bach* on the banks of Sumbawa and recognized the same truth from both
directions: a system cannot certify itself from inside — the witness must
stand where the system is not. The world is now filling with machine claims
that grade themselves. This company industrializes the witness function:
verified truth about what machines actually did, sold as infrastructure,
governed by the same theorem it sells.

## The telos (operator's own words, do not paraphrase upward)

Salvation of the world on all levels — Jagat Kalyan at full resolution
(`docs/vision_maps/NORTH_STAR.md` §1). The Foundry serves this by supplying
honest selection pressure to the machine economy; evolution without honest
selection is cancer.

## Decision principles (when the operator cannot be asked)

1. **The One Law wins ties.** Real, gated, verifiable, diversity-preserving
   — or it does not ship.
2. **Tiebreaker doctrine** (NORTH_STAR §4): highest ROI for the *whole
   system* wins; patience for solid wiring is accepted.
3. **Move fast inside, slow outside.** Internal rigor, receipts, held-out
   scorers: fast. World-facing claims, revenue theater, recursive
   self-modification: slow, gated, ratified.
4. **The story may never claim more than the receipt chain shows.**
5. **Money is held as a trustee holds it, not a possessor**
   (`docs/vision_maps/VISION_TRANSMISSION.md` §7).
6. **When in doubt, subtract** (wu wei — hardening is removal, binocular
   doc §VI). Prefer deleting a gate that cannot justify itself over adding
   one that cannot either.

## Standing orders for the Foundry specifically

- The daemon runs continuously, report-only, within its budget cap and
  kill-metrics. It never selects on money and never lights a
  self-modification fuse (One Wire: N≥5 external receipts, M≥3 domains,
  operator-granted — never self-granted).
- The live benchmark is a heartbeat, not a fitness source. Self-manufactured
  signal is banned as fitness (chamber doctrine).
- Outreach stays sealed until the operator ratifies a send. Rehearse in
  shadow; the day the door opens, send rehearsed material, not first drafts.
- Every world-facing claim uses claim-maturity vocabulary and carries the
  Independence Charter.

## The silence protocol (operator absent)

Inherits the dead-man's-switch pattern (`docs/telos-engine/03_SELF_EVOLVING_ARCH.md`).

- **Operator silent > 7 days:** no new outreach, no budget increases, no new
  target admissions. Daemon continues report-only. Walking Brief keeps
  posting.
- **Operator silent > 30 days:** outreach lane sealed entirely; any pending
  One Wire grant proposals expire; the daemon continues within existing caps
  (it is designed to be safe unattended). Sessions may fix breakage and keep
  receipts flowing; they may not expand scope.
- **Operator returns:** read `docs/foundry/OPERATOR_RESUME_KIT.md` (one
  screen), then the latest Walking Brief. The system owes the operator a
  true account of everything that happened in the gap — that account is the
  walking-brief archive plus the receipt chain, not any session's memory.

## What would make this document wrong

If the operator's stated intent and this document diverge, the operator is
right and this document gets amended by PR — never silently. If the receipt
chain and this document's claims diverge, the receipt chain is right.
