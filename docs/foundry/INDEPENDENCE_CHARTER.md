# Independence Charter — the structural moat of the verification business

Status: constitutional for every Foundry engagement and every world-facing
verification claim. Subordinate to `docs/governance/SOVEREIGN_MANIFEST.md`
and the One Law (`docs/vision_maps/2026-05-30_binocular_witness_seer_northstar.md` §V).
This charter INHERITS existing doctrine; where it repeats a rule, the named
source owns it.

## Why this exists

The 2026 agent-trust market is filling with receipt *infrastructure* —
vendors paid by the same party whose agent they instrument. Their conflict
is structural: the payer is the subject. Our seat is the opposite one: the
independent examiner. That independence must be structural (rules that bind
us even when inconvenient), or it is marketing.

## The rules

1. **Payer ≠ subject, or the verdict is pre-committed.** We do not accept
   money from a party in exchange for a favorable verdict about that party.
   A party MAY pay us to examine itself only when the engagement contract
   pre-commits, before work begins, that the verdict publishes (at least to
   the buyer's own stakeholders) whatever it says. No kill-fee for bad news.
   No preview-and-veto.

2. **Published misses are mandatory.** Every public verification claim we
   make carries our miss ledger — cases where our verdict was later shown
   wrong. (Inherits the SATYA discipline: "structurally cannot hide losses,"
   `docs/yc_w27_application.md`; Loomwork's Refusal Report pattern.)
   A verification shop with a 100% hit rate is advertising, not evidence.

3. **Funder concentration limits.** No single funder above 15% of revenue
   over a rolling 5-year window; no funding from parties (transitively,
   including parents) currently under our examination. (Inherits Kill
   Condition #5, `docs/doctrine/OPERATIONAL_DOCTRINE.md`.)

4. **Non-extractive revenue only.** Excluded categories (inherits
   `docs/telos-engine/05_PLATFORM_SPAWNING.md` §8.1): advertising, data
   sales or brokering, attention-extractive features, and any revenue model
   creating a conflict of interest with the verification mission.

5. **Claims speak claim-maturity.** Every outward statement uses the
   vocabulary of `docs/ontology/CLAIM_MATURITY_VOCABULARY.md` (0–7 ladder).
   Ring-1 evidence may claim at most `adversarial-verified`; ring-2 at most
   `runtime-verified`; only ring-3 (a merge by a non-author, an independent
   record) may claim `independently-reproduced`. The forbidden-overclaim
   list applies to us before it applies to anyone we examine.

6. **The Arjuna Test applies per engagement.** Before accepting work: does
   this engagement point at something actually broken in the world, with a
   nameable external human served? Self-referential engagements (verifying
   our own marketing, auditing parties who exist only to be audited) are
   declined.

7. **No verdict is for sale, including by omission.** Scope may be
   negotiated; findings within scope may not be. If a buyer narrows scope
   mid-engagement to exclude an emerging finding, the engagement terminates
   and the termination itself is recorded in the miss/refusal ledger.

8. **The world does not meet "dharma_swarm."** Outward-facing verification
   work ships under a registered product name (per the Will-Not-Do list,
   `docs/doctrine/OPERATIONAL_DOCTRINE.md`; naming decision is the
   operator's, recorded in the name registry when made). The organism's
   internal name stays internal.

## Enforcement

- This charter is checked at outreach time: the outreach lane
  (`docs/foundry/outreach/`) stays sealed until an engagement template
  citing this charter exists and the operator has ratified the send.
- Violations are kill-conditions for the engagement, not negotiation items.
- Amendments require an operator-ratified PR that names which rule changed
  and why; silent drift is itself a charter violation.
