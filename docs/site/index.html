Research Outputs

Three artifacts shaping the current build

STATE OF AI | STATE_OF_AI_APRIL_2026.md

Self-evolving AI is splitting into bounded products and recursive research programs.

The April 2026 research thread argues that frontier systems are becoming more agentic, more tool-using, and more multi-model, while alignment remains largely downstream and reactive. The field is accelerating toward autonomy without a commensurate advance in governance variety.

Thesis: there is a narrow window to make cybernetic governance load-bearing before constrained autonomy hardens into the default standard.

COMPETITIVE ANALYSIS | competitor_deep_dives.md

The differentiator is constitutional wiring, not just another agent shell.

Competitive analysis across systems such as Isara, Cognition, and Sakana points to the same gap: most stacks optimize capability, speed, and bounded execution, but treat governance as a prompt wrapper, a checklist, or a post-hoc filter.

Thesis: Ashby variety, Beer-style regulation, and audit-ready control loops must be compiled into runtime behavior, not layered on after scale arrives.

GNANI LODESTONE | GNANI_LODESTONE.md

Witness must be upstream of capability.

The GNANI lodestone seeds the claim that intelligence without witness becomes ego-replication at scale. It frames DHARMA SWARM as a system that should know what it is while it operates, not only after the fact through audit.

Thesis: research is not a passive content backlog. It is the orienting substrate that changes how future architecture gets built.
