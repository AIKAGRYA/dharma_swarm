## Pass 1 - The Substrate

The language's ontology needs a decision at the ultimate-constituent level.
What are the smallest things it can talk about, and what are their intrinsic
properties versus relational ones?

The candidate positions:

**A. Buddhist rejection of atomism (Vasubandhu, Vimsatika).** Atoms cannot be
featureless points because a point has no extension, so no aggregation of
points has extension; but if atoms have parts, they are not atomic. Therefore
there are no ultimate atoms; what appears as material is mind-constituted
(Yogacara reading) or is a conventional designation over conditioned dharmas
(Sautrantika-adjacent reading). This is directly relevant to whether the
language's receipt-nodes have intrinsic structure or are pure relational
holders.

**B. Jain paramanu with intrinsic qualities.** Paramanu carries touch, taste,
smell, color as intrinsic. Skandhas form under snigdha/ruksa properties:
combinatorial rules that are quality-based, not spatial. This is closer to a
field-quality substrate than a particle one. Relevant to whether the language's
minimal receipts carry intrinsic quality-signatures that determine composition
rules.

**C. Sarvastivada tri-temporal existence.** Past, present, and future dharmas
all exist; what changes is which is "active." This is the ancestor of every
retentive-memory question in distributed systems: does a receipt cease to
exist when it is superseded, or does it persist in a different mode?

**D. Sautrantika momentariness.** Only the present moment is real; past and
future are conceptual constructions. Everything is a stream of momentary
events. Relevant to whether the language treats receipts as events with
vanishing existence or as persistent artifacts.

**E. Kalacakra ksana-cosmology.** Time itself is atomized into ksanas; space is
treated via five colored winds: elemental fields, not empty background.
Relevant to whether the evaluator has an intrinsic time-atom and whether space
(locality, node identity) is field-like rather than container-like.

**F. Decoherence-theoretic pointer basis (Zurek).** The classical world
emerges because environmental interaction selects a preferred basis whose
diagonal elements survive decoherence. Relevant to how canonicality emerges in
the receipt mesh: not as a decreed norm but as an environmentally selected
stable form.

**G. Category-theoretic QM (Abramsky-Coecke).** Compound systems as tensor
products in a symmetric monoidal category; measurements as morphisms;
string-diagrammatic composition. Relevant to exact receipt composition when it
goes non-commutative.

**H. Rovelli's relational quantum mechanics.** Facts are observer-relative;
there is no absolute state, only states-relative-to-observers. Rovelli connects
this to Nagarjuna in "Neither Presentism nor Eternalism" and in *Helgoland*.
Directly relevant to trust-base-relative modality claims and to whether "the
mesh state" is coherent or always trust-base-indexed.

**Task:** produce a structured judgment on which of A-H, or which combination,
should anchor the language's substrate layer. This is not "pick a philosophy."
Identify the mathematical/formal structures each position implies, identify
where those structures conflict, and propose the smallest coherent substrate
ontology for the language that can carry any of the positions as a
specialization rather than as a rewrite.

## Specific Questions To Touch

### 3.1 The Atomism Decision

Does the language's substrate have ultimate irreducibles, or is it purely
relational?

- If irreducible: what are the intrinsic properties of a minimal receipt, in
  the sense of Jain paramanu's touch-taste-smell-color?
- If purely relational: how is the bootstrap problem solved without an initial
  atom?
- If Vasubandhu's Vimsatika argument holds, does the substrate become
  ingest-node-constituted in a multi-AI mesh?

### 3.2 The Intrinsic-Quality Question

If the Jain-paramanu direction is used, what are the intrinsic qualities of a
minimal receipt? Test adhesion-type, color-type, and rasa/gandha analogs as
operational structures. Mark decoration if decoration.

### 3.3 The Tri-Temporal Question

Compare Sarvastivada persistence across past/present/future modes with
Sautrantika momentariness. What changes in emitter, verifier, and mesh
dynamics under each lifecycle?

### 3.4 The Time-Atom Question

Does the evaluator have an intrinsic minimal tick, or is time continuous and
node-relative, with ksana emerging from ingest rate?

### 3.5 The Field-Not-Container Question

Does the mesh treat space between nodes as an empty transport medium, a field
carrying receipts as excitations, or emergent from receipt-relations?

### 3.6 The Observer-Relativity Question

Is canonicality observer-independent, or is it a relation between receipt and
ingest node?

### 3.7 The Nagarjuna-Rovelli Connection

Is Rovelli's reading of Nagarjuna at grade, or a physicist's sympathetic
misreading? If at grade, what does the language inherit from Nagarjuna via RQM
that it would not inherit from RQM alone?

### 3.8 The Formal-Structure Question

For positions A-H, name the formal structure that instantiates it and the
structure that would falsify it: symmetric monoidal category,
topos-theoretic ontology, non-classical mereology, coalgebra over
observations, or linear-logic substrate. If structures conflict, name the
incompatibility and propose a synthesis or principled choice.
