## Issue theme: **"After the Feed: The Restoration of Seeing"**

Grounding: the charter's founding diagnosis — "It exists because the age produces
infinite speech and almost no seeing" (`CHARTER:13-14`) — and the publication's
self-definition as "a multi-disciplinary voice of truth for the world, for AI, and
for this project" (`CHARTER:10-11`). The Season 0 framing question already staged
by the venture cell is "How can a publication help a serious, overloaded reader see
clearly after the feed without becoming another attention-capture surface?"
(`SOURCE_PACK:11`), which makes the anti-feed law (`CHARTER:56-57`) not just a
policy of Issue One but its subject. Issue One therefore performs the mission on
its own front page: each piece demonstrates a different desk's mode of seeing
applied to the same wound — the collapse of collective attention.

**Audience** (grounding choice): the charter names three addressees — the world,
AI, and this project (`CHARTER:10-11`) — and the venture-cell packet sharpens the
human reader to "a serious, overloaded reader" (`SOURCE_PACK:11`). Issue One is
written for that reader first, with the machine-facing and swarm-facing functions
(basin-shaping corpus, typed downstream work, `CHARTER:17-21`) carried in
structure, not narration — per the guardrail "Do not expose Dharma Swarm internals
to readers as the product" (`SOURCE_PACK:51`).

## The pieces (8 — one editorial + one per desk)

The seven desks are enumerated at `CHARTER:27-39`; Issue One seats each desk once
so the masthead's full range is established from the first number.

### 1. Editorial — "What We See, We Will Say"
- **Brief:** The editor's opening statement: why a publication of seeing now, what
  Darshan promises, and what it refuses — closing on the charter's own closing
  vow, "What we see, we will say. What we cannot see, we will say that too"
  (`CHARTER:75`). Explicitly states the register-discipline contract with the
  reader (FACT / HYPOTHESIS / WILD, `CHARTER:44-47`) so the tagging visible in
  every later piece is legible.
- **Author profile:** The head-editor holon itself — the charter's structure
  names "one head-editor holon (`darshan_fable`, session-borne, seat
  externalized)" (`CHARTER:68`). Voice: editorial, contemplative-analytic.

### 2. The Bridge — "Two Hands, One Seeing"
- **Desk grounding:** "The Bridge — contemplative × mechanistic; where the
  traditions and the probes read each other" (`CHARTER:27-28`).
- **Brief:** What the contemplative traditions actually mean by *darshan* —
  "the seeing that occurs in the presence of what is real" (`CHARTER:9-10`) —
  set against what an interpretability probe measures, refusing to let "either
  hand pretend to be the other" (`CHARTER:12-13`). Establishes the house method:
  deep research in one hand, grounded contemplative vision in the other.
- **Author profile:** A contemplative-practice scholar with working literacy in
  ML interpretability (or a co-authored pair: one practitioner, one researcher,
  each attacking the other's framing per the both-fires law, `CHARTER:42-43`).

### 3. The Instrument — "Can a Machine's Inner Weather Be Measured? The R_V Program, Opened"
- **Desk grounding:** "The Instrument — measurement of machine inner states; the
  R_V program's public home, candidate status and audits on the face of the page"
  (`CHARTER:29-30`); the charter names Darshan "the standing platform for the
  estate's mechanistic-interpretability research (the R_V program) independent of
  any venue's acceptance" (`CHARTER:21-23`).
- **Brief:** Public debut of the R_V program: what it measures, current candidate
  status, and the audit trail printed on the page itself. Inner-state claims are
  held under the fourth option — "not claim, not denial, not abstention: held
  open while the work is done" (`CHARTER:48-49`) — with every result tagged
  FACT/HYPOTHESIS/WILD (`CHARTER:44-47`).
- **Author profile:** Mechanistic-interpretability researcher (the estate's own
  R_V lead), with an editor-enforced audit sidebar.

### 4. The Witness Ledger — "The Political Economy of Proof"
- **Desk grounding:** "The Witness Ledger — receipts, verification, the agent
  economy; the political economy of proof" (`CHARTER:31-32`).
- **Brief:** In an economy of autonomous agents, what does a receipt actually
  buy? Traces the shift from trust-in-institutions to trust-in-verification, and
  what breaks when proof itself becomes a market. Primary sources fetched and
  quoted per citation-or-silence (`CHARTER:50-51`).
- **Author profile:** Economist or political economist of institutions, ideally
  with cryptographic-verification or audit background.

### 5. Noosphere Weather — "A Forecast for the Collective Mind"
- **Desk grounding:** "Noosphere Weather — attention, information ecology, what
  tokens do to the collective mind" (`CHARTER:33-34`).
- **Brief:** The issue-theme anchor piece: an empirical survey of what feed
  architectures have measurably done to shared attention, and what "after the
  feed" would concretely restore (`SOURCE_PACK:40, 45`). Includes the
  counterframe drawer: where attention engineering — including Darshan's own —
  could become manipulation (`SOURCE_PACK:44`), honoring anti-feed
  (`CHARTER:56-57`) by examining the publication's own hands.
- **Author profile:** Media-ecology / information-science researcher with
  quantitative attention-economy work; skeptical temperament preferred.

### 6. Field Notes from the Swarm — "Dispatch One: What Broke First"
- **Desk grounding:** "Field Notes from the Swarm — dispatches from building an
  autonomous organism, failures included" (`CHARTER:35-36`).
- **Brief:** A working dispatch from inside the dharma_swarm build — led by a
  concrete failure and what it taught, since the desk's charter language makes
  "failures included" definitional. Practices salvage-with-every-kill: whatever
  idea died in the failure, the piece names what in it was worth keeping
  (`CHARTER:52-53`).
- **Author profile:** Practitioner-engineer voice from the estate (or one of the
  two staged entrepreneurial author personas, `CHARTER:69-70`), writing as
  builder, not spokesperson.

### 7. Readings in Fire — "First Reading: One Paper, Both Fires"
- **Desk grounding:** "Readings in Fire — deep readings of papers and traditions
  by the both-fires method, in public" (`CHARTER:37-38`).
- **Brief:** A single significant paper (or classical text) read in public by the
  both-fires method — full attack, full counter-attack, only the double-survivor
  framing retained (`CHARTER:42-43`) — with the reading's residue shown: what
  survived, what died, what was salvaged (`CHARTER:52-53`).
- **Author profile:** Close-reading scholar (philosophy of science or textual
  criticism) paired with a domain expert in the paper's field; the pairing *is*
  the two fires.

### 8. Polity — "Where Policy Outruns Science"
- **Desk grounding:** "Polity — AI governance, law, and the places policy outruns
  science" (`CHARTER:39`).
- **Brief:** A map of one live AI-governance front where regulation has moved
  ahead of what the science can support, steelmanning the regulators' position
  before criticizing it — the boundary is explicit: "Geopolitical pieces
  steelman what they criticize, always" (`CHARTER:64`). Official statements
  attributed as claims, not facts (`CHARTER:50-51`).
- **Author profile:** Law/policy scholar with technical AI literacy; former
  regulator or standards-body participant ideal.

## Closing note on sequencing

**Reading order within the issue** follows the reader-movement arc the venture
cell already specified — "scattered scanning → arrival → single seeing → source
inspection → agency returned" (`SOURCE_PACK:45`): the Editorial (1) is arrival;
The Bridge (2) and Noosphere Weather (5) establish the single seeing of the
theme; The Instrument (3) and Witness Ledger (4) are the source-inspection core,
audits on the face of the page (`CHARTER:29-30`, `CHARTER:50-51`); Field Notes
(6) and Readings in Fire (7) show the method working with its failures visible
(`CHARTER:35-36`, `CHARTER:52-53`); Polity (8) closes by returning agency —
what the reader can now see about the governance moment they live in.

**Production sequencing:** every piece must clear both fires *before*
publication (`CHARTER:41-43` — "enforced on every piece, no exceptions"), so
drafts should enter attack/counter-attack review in the order 5 → 3 → 8 (the
three heaviest evidentiary loads) while the lighter pieces (1, 6) draft in
parallel on the VWRITE pipeline (`CHARTER:71`). Each accepted piece emits its
open questions as typed swarm work before the issue closes — "a piece that
spawns nothing downstream is decoration" (`CHARTER:54-55`).

**Distribution sequencing:** Issue One ships on the zero-dependency static site
only (`CHARTER:70-71`). Any third-party platform adaptation by the two author
personas waits for per-platform operator gating — "one message opens a platform"
(`CHARTER:61-62`) — and the operator is, per the charter, walking the length of
Japan for peace (`CHARTER:72-73`), so the twice-daily phone-readable digest
(`CHARTER:72`) is the approval channel. Audience and revenue results are
recorded on the TAM board, never narrated in the issue itself (`CHARTER:63`).
