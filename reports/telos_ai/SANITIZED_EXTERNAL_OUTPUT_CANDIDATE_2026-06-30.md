## Shareable Output

TELOS Morning Refinery is a private-by-default workflow for turning a raw
morning reflection into a consent-gated, usable idea artifact.

The key design claim is simple:

1. A first pass protects depth, source truth, and user sovereignty.
2. A second pass only sees sanitized outputs and can turn them into possible
   essays, research questions, venture seeds, collaboration prompts, or next
   actions.
3. Nothing leaves private context unless the operator explicitly chooses a
   sanitized output and records why it is allowed to leave.

The current prototype is not a journaling app and not a public claim. It is a
small privacy-boundary test: can a system help one person preserve the source
of an idea, clarify the idea enough to act on it, and expose only the minimum
safe derivative artifact needed for another human to help?
