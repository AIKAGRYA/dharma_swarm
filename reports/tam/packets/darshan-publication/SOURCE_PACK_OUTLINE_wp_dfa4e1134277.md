## First Packet Shape

- Lead question: What is being restored after the feed?
- Source spine: venture-cell seed, attention-engineering seed, After the Feed container.
- Evidence drawer: reading/deep-attention anchors from the attention-engineering atom.
- Counterframe drawer: where attention engineering could become manipulation.
- Reader movement: scattered scanning -> arrival -> single seeing -> source inspection -> agency returned.
- Internal return: claim ledger, source-pack record, Attention Effect Ledger, Chetana/ontology ingest, follow-up task, decision delta.

## Guardrails

- Keep sacred-language framing internal until the public artifact earns plain trust.
- Do not expose Dharma Swarm internals to readers as the product.
- Keep source links beside the argument, not sprayed through the attentional line.
- Treat metrics as humble proxies until real reader evidence exists.
- Require an explicit "what would change this" section before any public draft is promoted.
