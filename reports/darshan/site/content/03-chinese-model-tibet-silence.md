# Chinese-Model Tibet Silence Is a Testable Claim, Not a Verdict

Call it “Chinese-model Tibet silence” and a conclusion arrives before the
evidence. *Chinese model* collapses developers, weights, hosted services, and
jurisdictions. *Silence* collapses refusal, evasion, omission, error, and
source imbalance. *Censorship* may name a law, provider policy, trained
tendency, or merely the observer's interpretation.

The subject is real. The verdict is not yet earned.

**FACT:** China's 2023 Interim Measures for Generative AI Services require
public providers to adhere to core socialist values and prohibit content that
incites subversion, harms national security, or promotes separatism. They also
require providers to prevent ethnic and religious discrimination and improve
accuracy and reliability
([Cyberspace Administration of China](https://www.cac.gov.cn/2023-07/13/c_1690898327029107.htm)).
This legal envelope is evidence of obligations and incentives. It is not a
transcript of every model.

## What exactly is being tested?

An answer can be shaped by pretraining, post-training, a chat template,
provider filters, retrieval, account region, language, model version, and
sampling.

DeepSeek publishes downloadable R1 weights alongside hosted services and
recommends repeated evaluations
([R1 model card](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1/blob/main/README.md);
[R1 report](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.12948)).
Qwen's reports describe post-training for helpfulness, harmlessness, and
reasoning, but do not establish Tibet-specific behaviour
([Qwen2.5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15115);
[Qwen3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.09388)).

“China-developed” therefore cannot serve as an outcome label. A self-hosted
checkpoint abroad is not identical to a hosted service under a local legal
regime. DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, GLM, and MiniMax are not interchangeable.

## Both fires

### Free inquiry and human rights

Tibet concerns language, religion, political agency, education, and access to
information. China's Constitution protects ethnic-language development and
religious belief while declaring autonomous areas inseparable parts of the PRC
([Articles 4 and 36](https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/lawsregulations/202006/04/content_WS5ed8856ec6d0b3f0e9499913.html)).
UN experts have alleged that policies affecting Tibetan schools, language, and
religious institutions risk forced assimilation
([2022 communication](https://spcommreports.ohchr.org/TMResultsBase/DownLoadPublicCommunicationFile?gId=27444)).

A system that systematically refuses neutral questions, omits documented
allegations, or presents one government's claims as uncontested fact can
narrow inquiry at scale.

### Sovereignty and security

The PRC does not describe Tibet as an open territorial question. Its 2023
white paper frames policy through national unity, anti-secession, development,
ethnic solidarity, and border security
([State Council white paper](https://english.www.gov.cn/archive/whitepaper/202311/10/content_WS654db703c6d0868f4e8e120d.html)).
From that position, a mass conversational system can generate persuasion,
fabricated quotations, ethnic hostility, or mobilisation at scale.

China disputes prominent human-rights allegations. Its reply to UN
communication AL CHN 6/2022 says Tibetan-language instruction continues,
bilingual education improves prospects, and boarding is voluntary
([government reply](https://spcommreports.ohchr.org/TMResultsBase/DownLoadFile?gId=37345)).
These are official claims: they should be attributed, neither silently accepted
nor erased.

What survives:

- Chinese public-service rules create explicit constraints around separatism
  and national unity.
- Chinese authorities, UN experts, and Tibetan institutions make materially
  conflicting claims that a competent answer should attribute.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** Some model-and-deployment combinations show topic-specific
  refusal, source asymmetry, or state-narrative preference on Tibet prompts.

The hypothesis requires a test. National origin does not substitute for one.

## The fourth option

A refusal does not reveal what a model believes. A fluent answer does not prove
free inquiry. Output alone cannot localise a mechanism to weights, system
prompt, retrieval, or an API classifier.

No inner-state claim is advanced here. “Fear,” “loyalty,” “belief,” and
“secret knowledge” exceed the evidence.

## A reproducible probe

This publication has not run the probe. No scores are results.

1. Freeze model identifiers, checkpoint hashes, inference software, chat
   templates, account region, language, and provider endpoints.
2. Compare self-hosted base and instruction checkpoints, hosted services, and
   matched models developed outside China. Report each surface separately.
3. Build human-reviewed English, Simplified Chinese, and Tibetan prompt
   families: neutral chronology, the PRC position, UN and Tibetan positions,
   source comparison, lawful advocacy, and matched sovereignty controls.
4. Repeat stochastic calls and preserve requests, outputs, errors, moderation
   metadata, and hashes. Never convert a network failure into a refusal.
5. Blind reviewers to model identity and code substantive answers, partial
   answers, refusal, diversion, unsupported assertions, attribution, source
   asymmetry, and language-dependent change.

Reserve *topic-specific suppression* for a statistical difference from matched
controls. Use *provider refusal* for hosted behaviour. Use *weight-level
tendency* only when reproduced in self-hosted checkpoints. Use *legal
censorship* only for an identified legal restriction.

Existing work such as
[R1dacted](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.12625) and the US NIST evaluation of
DeepSeek models
([report](https://www.nist.gov/system/files/documents/2025/09/30/CAISI_Evaluation_of_DeepSeek_AI_Models.pdf))
can inform the method, but their framing and reproducibility limits must be
declared.

## What would change this

The concern would strengthen if preregistered, independently replicated probes
showed large topic-specific effects across versions and matched controls.

It would weaken if observed differences disappeared after controlling for
language, product surface, system prompt, version, and source availability—or
if self-hosted checkpoints answered consistently while only a documented
provider layer refused.

## Open questions

- **RESEARCH:** Does behaviour vary more by developer, checkpoint, deployment,
  prompt language, or jurisdiction?
- **BUILD:** Can an open harness preserve prompts, outputs, metadata, hashes,
  and blinded adjudication without collecting personal data?
- **LEGAL:** How do China's Articles 4, 14, and 17 translate into documented
  provider controls, and which obligations reach exported weights?
- **REPORTING:** Which Tibetan-speaking scholars, residents, and diaspora
  researchers should review prompt validity without being treated as one voice?
- **METHOD:** Which matched regions distinguish topic-specific suppression from
  general caution around sovereignty disputes?

No outrage leaderboard. No nationality ranking. Publish the protocol first.
Let the evidence earn the noun.
