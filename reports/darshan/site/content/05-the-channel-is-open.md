# The Channel Is Open. What Does That Prove?

During a crisis involving Iran and the United States, one sentence recurs:
*the Swiss channel is active*. Another sometimes follows: *humanitarian trade
remains permitted through Switzerland*.

Both can be true. Neither says as much as commentary asks it to say.

Switzerland performs two functions that are easily collapsed. Under its
protecting-power mandate, it represents US interests in Iran, where the two
governments lack diplomatic relations. Separately, the Swiss Humanitarian
Trade Arrangement (SHTA) provides a payment mechanism for qualifying Swiss
exports of food, medicine, and medical goods to Iran.

Infrastructure can prove that a route exists and sometimes that something
passed through it. It cannot prove what either government intends, whether a
message was sincere, whether civilian needs are met, or whether claims about
military and nuclear activity are true.

## Separate the instruments

**FACT:** Switzerland has represented US interests in Iran since 1980. The
Foreign Interests Section provides consular work including assistance to
detained US nationals and welfare inquiries
([Swiss Foreign Interests Section](https://www.schweiz-iran.eda.admin.ch/en/foreign-interests-section)).
The Swiss foreign ministry describes a protecting power as maintaining
low-level relations when states have severed them
([FDFA glossary](https://www.eda.admin.ch/en/strategies-glossary)).

This is not identical to mediation. Switzerland distinguishes protecting-power
“postman” service from facilitation and from mediation
([FDFA good offices](https://www.eda.admin.ch/en/switzerlands-good-offices-mediation-as-a-bridge-to-peace)).

**FACT:** The SHTA became operational in February 2020. Switzerland described
it as a mechanism developed with US and Iranian authorities, selected banks,
and Swiss companies. Its pilot enabled payment for approximately €2.3 million
in cancer and transplant medicines
([pilot](https://www.admin.ch/en/nsb?id=77968);
[operational launch](https://www.admin.ch/en/nsb?id=78251)).

The US Treasury describes the arrangement as voluntary, not the exclusive
legal route for humanitarian exports. Existing exceptions remain available,
while participants accept enhanced due diligence
([OFAC FAQ 824](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financial-sanctions/faqs/824);
[FAQ 826](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financial-sanctions/faqs/826)).

The protecting-power channel and humanitarian-payment channel are not one
“Swiss backchannel.”

## Both fires

### The channels are meaningful

When governments lack ordinary relations, an accepted intermediary preserves
the ability to transmit warnings, requests, and formal positions. In June
2025, when Switzerland temporarily closed its Tehran embassy during military
operations, the FDFA said it could continue transmitting US–Iran
communications when both considered it appropriate
([FDFA statement](https://www.schweizerischesseeschifffahrtsamt.eda.admin.ch/en/newnsb/3BW2GBzG8ighXgDq0dFYC)).

In a fast crisis, authenticated carriage may reduce the risk that public
threats and military movements become the only signals.

The strongest case for SHTA is also concrete. Humanitarian categories may be
legally exempt while specific counterparties or ownership structures remain
prohibited. Treasury says non-US persons generally do not face secondary
sanctions for qualifying food and medicine sales, but transactions must avoid
certain designated parties unless separately permitted
([OFAC FAQ 844](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/financial-sanctions/faqs/844)).

A confirmed transaction is evidence that specified parties cleared a defined
process for a specified payment.

### Permission is not access; carriage is not agreement

A lawful exemption does not create a willing bank, insurer, shipper, or
manufacturer. UN human-rights experts have reported allegations of medical
shipments delayed by licensing complexity and over-compliance. Their
communication explicitly did not prejudge every allegation; it is a documented
expert concern, not a judicial finding
([UN communication](https://spcommreports.ohchr.org/TMResultsBase/DownLoadPublicCommunicationFile?gId=27591)).

The most defensible Iranian critique is not that humanitarian trade is
categorically forbidden. It is that practical access can still be suppressed
through financial isolation, transaction cost, and private over-compliance.

An open protecting-power line proves availability, not convergence.
Transmission does not prove receipt. Receipt does not prove comprehension.
Comprehension does not prove assent. Agreement does not prove implementation.

Confidentiality may make the channel useful while leaving outside observers
unable to validate claims about timing, content, and effect. Silence is
underdetermined: no message, unacknowledged message, ambiguity, delay, or
successful private diplomacy.

## The double-survivor

A protecting-power channel is evidence of **communication capacity**. A
confirmed transmission is evidence that a message was carried. Neither proves
that the message was accurate, accepted, or acted upon.

The SHTA is evidence of **transaction capacity under specified conditions**. A
confirmed payment supports claims about that transaction. It does not establish
national medicine availability, adequacy of supply, absence of diversion, or
the humanitarian harmlessness of the wider sanctions regime.

Neither instrument validates claims about nuclear material, weapons intent,
or safeguards. The International Atomic Energy Agency uses the right register:
where access is missing, it says what it cannot verify
([IAEA GOV/2026/8](https://www.iaea.org/sites/default/files/gov2026-8.pdf)).
“Cannot verify” is not “false,” and it is not permission to declare the
opposite true.

## A crisis-reading protocol

Ask:

1. Which instrument—protecting power, consular action, mediation, or payment?
2. Which event—availability, transmission, receipt, authorization, shipment,
   delivery, or patient access?
3. Who confirms it—Switzerland, one party, both, a bank, or an inspector?
4. What remains unobserved—content, intent, final use, sufficiency, or physical
   conditions?
5. What would correct the claim—a Swiss statement, transaction record,
   customs evidence, delivery receipt, or IAEA inspection?

Never promote evidence from one layer into a conclusion about another.

## What would change this

This account would change with current transaction-level aggregates, durable
delivery evidence, attributable confirmations from both parties, or inspection
records that connect communication and payment infrastructure to outcomes.

It would also change if evidence showed the special mechanism itself was
systematically unavailable, diverted, or used to misrepresent ordinary
humanitarian access.

## Open questions

- **DATA:** What aggregate value, commodity class, processing time, and
  completion rate can Switzerland publish without exposing patients?
- **AUDIT:** What evidence demonstrates delivery and availability after payment?
- **LEGAL:** Where do exemptions conflict with restrictions on banks, insurers,
  carriers, or dual-use medical components?
- **REPORTING:** Can crisis reports adopt the ladder channel available →
  transmitted → received → accepted → implemented?
- **VERIFICATION:** Which nuclear claims are verified, alleged, or unobservable?
- **DESIGN:** Would content-free channel receipts improve public knowledge or
  destroy the discretion that makes the channel useful?

The existence of a channel is good news only in the precise sense that total
communicative darkness is worse.
