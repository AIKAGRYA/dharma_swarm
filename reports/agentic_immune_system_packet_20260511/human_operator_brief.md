# Human Operator Brief

Date: 2026-05-11
Status: active human decision sheet

## Your Job As The Human

You do not need to hold the whole architecture manually.
You need to make the few judgments that the system cannot make ethically or commercially without you.

## Decisions Needed

### 1. Artifact Title

Default:

```text
Agentic Era Immune System
```

Human action:

- Approve this title, or give a replacement.

Decision pressure:

- Keep it if you want the big category frame.
- Rename it if it feels too grand for first public contact.

### 2. Public Boundary

Recommended default:

```text
semi-public Agora/SABP basin first, not broad public launch
```

Human action:

- Choose one:
  - internal only,
  - semi-public/witnessed basin,
  - public web publication.

Recommendation:

- Start semi-public. The artifact needs challenge before a polished public page.

### 3. Red Lines

Human action:

Name anything that must not be included in public/semi-public form:

- private spiritual biography,
- private repo paths,
- customer names,
- financial claims,
- raw conversations,
- sensitive family/personal material,
- unsupported AI consciousness claims,
- private contact lists,
- anything else.

Recommended default:

```text
No private people, no raw transcripts, no secrets, no private contact graph,
no unsupported consciousness claims, no customer/revenue claims without proof.
```

### 4. Founder Source Statement

Human action:

Optional but useful: write 300-700 messy words answering:

```text
Why must an agentic immune system exist now?
What failure in the world are you refusing to accept?
What vow constrains the company from becoming extractive or inflated?
```

Do not polish it. The system can polish. Your job is to provide the living signal.

### 5. First Witness / Challenger

Human action:

Pick who challenges the first artifact:

- you first,
- a trusted human,
- SABP process,
- an AI reviewer pass,
- or a named external skeptic later.

Recommended default:

```text
founder review first, then SABP challenge/correction path.
```

### 6. External Direction

Human action:

Choose the first reader archetype:

- AI-native founder,
- AI lab/research studio,
- agent governance buyer,
- spiritual/scientific collaborator,
- public intellectual/media reader,
- open-source agent builder.

Recommended default:

```text
AI-native founder or small AI lab with agent/runtime complexity.
```

Reason:

- They feel the pain fastest and can buy or collaborate without enterprise procurement.

## What You Should Not Do

- Do not redesign the whole system in this step.
- Do not choose all possible venture cells.
- Do not demand final naming now.
- Do not publish a grand claim before the claim register is tightened.
- Do not let the system turn this into another internal-only archive.
