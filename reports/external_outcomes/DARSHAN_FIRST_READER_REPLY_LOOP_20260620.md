## Why this exists

The current active-track portfolio is heavily weighted toward substrate-nativeness. That is valuable, but the organism needs one clean loop where value leaves the repo and reality answers.

Darshan is the safest first external-outcome wedge because the portfolio already marks it `ACTIVE_SEASON_0` and defines the primary metric as external readers who read and replied. This packet does not claim an outcome. It defines the smallest loop worth lighting.

## One-loop objective

Ship one Darshan artifact to one real external reader and capture a verifiable reply.

The reply can be positive, negative, confused, or indifferent. The point is not praise. The point is contact with reality.

## Acceptance criteria

- One bounded Darshan artifact exists with a title, intended reader, and source/evidence boundary.
- One real human reader receives it through an operator-approved channel.
- One response or explicit non-response is recorded as an external-outcome receipt.
- The receipt records date, channel class, reader class, artifact id/title, and whether the person read/replied/acted.
- No revenue, audience, publication, or product-market-fit claim is made unless directly evidenced.
