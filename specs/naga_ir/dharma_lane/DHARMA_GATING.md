## 0. The Telos

**Statement.** The system evolves toward a fixed point at which every receipt in the mesh is coherent with every other receipt under the join operation, no unresolved claims remain above budget, and the empirical modality of every claim equals its declared modality. This fixed point is the operational referent of *dharma* in this specification: not a metaphor for correctness but the specific attractor state the system's dynamics are engineered to approach.

**Mathematical structure.** A vector-valued Lyapunov function \( V: \Sigma \to \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}^k \) on the space \( \Sigma \) of global receipt states, with \( k \) components:

\[
V(\sigma) = \big( V_{\text{coh}}(\sigma),\ V_{\text{unres}}(\sigma),\ V_{\text{drift}}(\sigma),\ V_{\text{auth}}(\sigma) \big)
\]

- \( V_{\text{coh}} \) — total pairwise incoherence between receipts under join. Zero at the fixed point.
- \( V_{\text{unres}} \) — count of unresolved claims whose age exceeds their resolution budget. Zero at the fixed point.
- \( V_{\text{drift}} \) — total absolute distance between declared modality and empirically-verified modality across all live receipts. Zero at the fixed point.
- \( V_{\text{auth}} \) — count of receipts whose trust base has been superseded without a coercion receipt linking old to new. Zero at the fixed point.

The system is *well-oriented* iff every legal state transition weakly decreases \( V \) componentwise, and every gate is a projection that strictly decreases at least one component whenever it fires and passes. This is a direct application of Lyapunov's second method ([Khalil, *Nonlinear Systems*, Ch. 4](https://www.pearson.com/en-us/subject-catalog/p/nonlinear-systems/P200000003484)) and gives us the operational meaning of "the system is approaching the telos": we can compute \( V \) at any moment and verify it is not increasing.

**Computable predicate.** `telos_lyapunov(state) -> (V_coh, V_unres, V_drift, V_auth)`. Emitted as a receipt of class `state`, modality `Tested_by`, on every boundary run.

**Operational consequence.** A boundary run that observes \( V(\sigma_t) \not\leq V(\sigma_{t-1}) \) componentwise (with \( \leq \) the product order) emits a `dharma.divergence.v1` receipt and blocks the following commit until the increase is either (a) explained by a supersession receipt or (b) manually cleared by a quorum. This is the operational referent of "the system is drifting from dharma": Lyapunov increase without warrant.

**Why this is not decoration.** Delete the philosophy and the Lyapunov structure still forces every gate to be a projection that reduces a specific scalar. Delete the mathematics and the philosophy still names the direction of evolution. They meet in the middle at "the system has a computable notion of getting worse, and the notion is named."

**Citation anchor.** Lyapunov 1892; Khalil 2002; extended to hybrid systems in [Goebel, Sanfelice, Teel 2012](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691153896/hybrid-dynamical-systems).

## 1. Ontology

**Statement.** A receipt has no independent existence. It is constituted entirely by (a) its content hash, (b) the set of `prev_receipt_hash` links that induce its predecessors, and (c) the trust base under which it was emitted. This is *pratītyasamutpāda* — dependent origination — read as a claim about graph structure, not as metaphor.

**Mathematical structure.** The receipt space is not a set of objects but the free category \( \mathcal{R} \) generated by:

- Objects: content-addressed nodes (the receipts).
- Morphisms: `prev_receipt_hash` edges, typed by the fragment authority under which the successor was emitted.
- Composition: transitive predecessor closure, associative by construction.

A "receipt" is fully specified by its position in \( \mathcal{R} \). Two receipts with identical content but different predecessor sets are *different receipts*, because their meaning is fixed by their causal past. This is a strong claim and it is *forced* by the hashing discipline: the content-hash includes the sorted list of `prev_receipt_hash` values, so bit-identical content with different predecessors produces different hashes.

**Computable predicate.** `receipt_identity(r) == sha256_uri(jcs(r.content || sort(r.prev_receipt_hashes) || r.trust_base_id))`. Two receipts are equal iff their identities match. There is no other notion of receipt equality.

**Operational consequence.** You cannot copy a receipt across contexts. If agent A emits receipt \( r_A \) and agent B wants to make the same claim, B must emit a *new* receipt \( r_B \) with its own predecessor set and its own authority. \( r_B \) may cite \( r_A \) via `prev_receipt_hash`, but \( r_B \) is not \( r_A \). Cross-agent belief propagation is therefore always explicit, always audit-visible, and always leaves a graph trace. This is dependent origination as an anti-plagiarism law for claims.

**Why this is not decoration.** The alternative — treating receipts as free-standing atomic facts — is the ambient assumption of every logging system on Earth, and it is the assumption that makes provenance forensics impossibly hard. Dependent origination gives us the *structural* fix: identity is relational, and the relations are on-disk.

**Citation anchors.** Category theory: [Mac Lane, *Categories for the Working Mathematician*, 2nd ed.](https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-4757-4721-8). Content-addressed graph identity: [Merkle 1987](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/3-540-48184-2_32). Pratītyasamutpāda as formal structure: [Priest 2018, *The Fifth Corner of Four*](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/the-fifth-corner-of-four-9780198758716) — used here only for the correspondence with graph-relational identity; no metaphysical commitment beyond the formal move is required.

## 2. The Theorems as Conservation Laws

Each T-theorem in `core.md` is restated here with its physical / metaphysical analog. The two readings must not contradict; if they do, the mathematics is authoritative.

### T4: Trust-Base Non-Substitution as Noether Conservation

**Physical reading.** Trust is a conserved quantity under the symmetry of "receipt re-parenting." Just as Noether's theorem says that energy is conserved because the laws of physics are invariant under time translation ([Noether 1918](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0503066)), NĀGA-IR asserts: authority is conserved because the receipt structure is invariant under authorial re-labeling *only when* a coercion receipt is present.

**Mathematical structure.** Let \( T \) be the set of trust bases and \( \mathrm{Auth}: \mathcal{R} \to T \) the authority-assignment function. The claim: \( \mathrm{Auth} \) is *not* a functor under re-parenting morphisms. Attempting to re-parent \( r \) from \( t_1 \) to \( t_2 \) requires exhibiting a coercion receipt \( c: t_1 \to t_2 \) that itself carries authority under a trust base that dominates both. Without \( c \), the re-parenting is not a legal morphism.

**Computable predicate.** `is_authority_preserving(r_before, r_after) := has_coercion_link(r_after) or r_before.trust_base_id == r_after.trust_base_id`.

**Operational consequence.** The receipt verifier rejects any two-receipt sequence in which the trust base changes without a coercion receipt. This is enforced at ingest, not at emit — meaning the mesh will *reject* mis-parented receipts even if a producer emits them.

**Why not decoration.** Delete Noether and you still get non-substitution. Delete the code and you still have the conservation law as a design constraint. They meet at: authority is a conserved quantity and the conservation is enforced by ingest.

### T8: No Silent Strengthening as Second-Law Analog

**Physical reading.** Modality can be freely weakened; strengthening requires paying an entropy cost. `Proven_by` → `Tested_by` → `Attested_by` → `Assumed` is a downhill direction and is free. The reverse direction is uphill and requires a coercion receipt whose emission is itself work — someone must produce evidence, sign it, and expose it to challenge.

**Mathematical structure.** Let \( M = \{ \text{Proven\_by}, \text{Tested\_by}, \text{Attested\_by}, \text{Assumed} \} \) be totally ordered by strength. Define \( S: \mathcal{R} \to M \). The transition rule: \( S(r_{n+1}) \geq S(r_n) \) is legal iff there exists a coercion receipt \( c \) with \( S(c) \geq S(r_{n+1}) \), i.e. the coercion carries at least the strength it authorizes. This is a strict analog of the second law: entropy of the claim-system does not spontaneously decrease.

**Computable predicate.** `is_modality_legal(chain) := forall (r_i, r_{i+1}) in chain: modality_rank(r_{i+1}) <= modality_rank(r_i) or has_coercion_at_or_above(chain, r_{i+1}.modality)`.

**Operational consequence.** Verifier rejects any modality upgrade without a corresponding coercion receipt in the predecessor set. Same enforcement point as T4: at ingest, not at emit.

**Why not decoration.** Delete the second-law analog and you still have the rule. Delete the rule and you lose the enforcement. The physical framing gives you the *reason the rule cannot be relaxed*: relaxing it would be equivalent to a perpetual motion machine for claims — the ability to strengthen assertions without doing epistemic work.

**Citation anchors.** [Noether 1918](https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0503066); [Lieb & Yngvason 1999, *The Physics and Mathematics of the Second Law*](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/9708200).

### T5a / T5b: Hash Stability as Gauge Invariance / Symmetry Breaking

**Physical reading.** T5a (stability under key reordering) is *gauge invariance* — the physical content of a receipt is invariant under representational choices that carry no meaning (whitespace, key order). T5b (breaking under semantic mutation) is *symmetry breaking under measurement* — a genuine change in content produces an observably different hash.

**Mathematical structure.** JCS ([RFC 8785](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8785)) is the gauge-fixing procedure. The equivalence class \( [r]_{\text{JCS}} \) is the physical receipt; the representative is the gauge choice. Hash operates on the class, not the representative.

**Computable predicate.** `hash(jcs(r)) == hash(jcs(permute_keys(r)))` and `hash(jcs(r)) != hash(jcs(mutate_content(r)))`. Both checked in test suite.

**Operational consequence.** Producers may represent receipts in any legal JSON form; verifiers always canonicalize before hashing. No producer can gain advantage by choosing a specific representation.

## 3. The Four-Valued Truth Structure

**Statement.** Canonicalization returns a value in \( \{ \text{canonical}, \text{noncanonical}, \text{unknown}, \text{both} \} \). The fourth value — *both* — is not a bug; it is a first-class outcome corresponding to a receipt whose canonical form is undecidable within its resource budget *and* for which partial canonicalization has produced evidence of multiple normal forms. This maps to the tetralemma's fourth corner ([Nāgārjuna's Mūlamadhyamakakārikā ch. 18](https://global.oup.com/academic/product/the-fundamental-wisdom-of-the-middle-way-9780195093360) — used here only for the four-valued structure).

**Mathematical structure.** Belnap's four-valued logic ([Belnap 1977, *A Useful Four-Valued Logic*](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-94-010-1161-7_2)) is the exact formal instantiation:

| value | meaning | source cardinality |
|---|---|---|
| `canonical` | evidence for canonicality, no evidence against | 1 (for) |
| `noncanonical` | evidence against, no evidence for | 1 (against) |
| `unknown` | no evidence either direction (or budget exceeded before either found) | 0 |
| `both` | evidence for *and* evidence against (multiple normal forms found under partial canonicalization) | 2 (contradiction) |

The lattice: `unknown ≤ canonical ≤ both` and `unknown ≤ noncanonical ≤ both`. Join and meet are componentwise on the (evidence-for, evidence-against) pair. This is *not* classical logic and it is *not* three-valued logic; it is the specific lattice Belnap constructed to reason about databases with contradictory sources — which is exactly what the mesh will be.

**Computable predicate.** `canonicalize_belnap(r, budget) -> Belnap4`. Returns the four-valued result plus the evidence set.

**Operational consequence.** A receipt with `canonicalization: both` is not rejected. It is emitted as a valid receipt with `challenge_state.contradiction_witnesses: [hash1, hash2, ...]` listing the multiple normal forms found. The mesh treats `both`-valued receipts as objects requiring resolution and routes them to a `dharma.contradiction_reconciler.v1` process (spec: PR #7).

**Why not decoration.** Every real distributed system encounters contradictory canonicalizations under adversarial input. Three-valued logic forces you to collapse contradiction to `unknown`, which throws away information — you knew there were multiple normal forms, and now the receipt doesn't record that. Four-valued logic preserves the information. This is the tetralemma doing actual work: preserving distinctions that Aristotelian logic collapses.

**Citation anchors.** [Belnap 1977](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-94-010-1161-7_2); [Priest 2008, *An Introduction to Non-Classical Logic*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/an-introduction-to-nonclassical-logic/AB7BBBCB57B29DC38FBA23FC13FBBEB1) for the correspondence with tetralemma structure.

## 4. The Mesh as Decoherence

**Statement.** The witness mesh is not a passive database. It is a *decoherence process* that collapses distributed superpositions of belief into locally-consensual states, with the collapse dynamics determined by the receipt-ingest order at each node.

**Mathematical structure.** Let each node \( n \) in the mesh maintain a *belief operator* \( \rho_n \) — a density-matrix-analog over the space of receipt-consistent world states. When node \( n \) ingests receipt \( r \), \( \rho_n \) is updated by:

\[
\rho_n \mapsto \frac{P_r \rho_n P_r^\dagger}{\mathrm{Tr}(P_r \rho_n P_r^\dagger)}
\]

where \( P_r \) is the projector onto states consistent with \( r \). This is the projection postulate ([von Neumann 1932](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691178561/mathematical-foundations-of-quantum-mechanics)) applied to belief states.

The critical property: projectors for receipts \( r_A \) and \( r_B \) *do not in general commute*. Ingesting \( r_A \) then \( r_B \) yields \( \rho' = P_B P_A \rho P_A^\dagger P_B^\dagger / Z \). Ingesting \( r_B \) then \( r_A \) yields \( \rho'' = P_A P_B \rho P_B^\dagger P_A^\dagger / Z \). In general \( \rho' \neq \rho'' \). Order matters.

**This is the quantum-grade correction to the classical join-semilattice in `witness_mesh.md`.** The classical version says: any two receipts merge to the same result regardless of order (idempotence + commutativity). That is true only in the *decoherence limit* — after enough receipts have been ingested that the projectors have destroyed all cross-node phase information. Before that limit, order matters, and the belief state is genuinely a superposition.

**Computable predicate.** `belief_state(node, seq) -> DensityMatrix`. `commutator_norm(P_A, P_B) -> float`. When `commutator_norm > 0`, the two receipts do not commute and merge order is a real degree of freedom.

**Operational consequence.** The reconciler (PR #6) does not treat merge conflicts as errors. It emits a `dharma.noncommuting_merge.v1` receipt recording the two possible collapsed states and the commutator norm between the projectors. Downstream consumers can either (a) commit to one branch and record which, (b) hold both as a `both`-valued canonicalization (see §3), or (c) wait for a third receipt that dominates both projectors and resolves the commutator.

**Why not decoration.** CRDTs and join-semilattices are the safe engineering choice for distributed systems. They are also the *classical limit* — they assume all disagreements ultimately reconcile to a single state. Real multi-agent systems with adversarial members, hidden state, and Byzantine faults do not satisfy that assumption. The quantum-grade framing gives us a language for saying: two agents genuinely disagree, the disagreement is not a bug, and the system's next state depends on which measurements it performs in which order. This is the shape of every real belief-revision dynamic under partial information.

**Citation anchors.** [von Neumann 1932](https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691178561/mathematical-foundations-of-quantum-mechanics); [Nielsen & Chuang 2010, *Quantum Computation and Quantum Information*, §2.2](https://www.cambridge.org/highereducation/books/quantum-computation-and-quantum-information/01E10196D0A682A6AEFFEA52D53BE9AE) for the projection postulate and density matrices. The *analogy* between belief revision and quantum measurement is developed in [Aerts 2009, "Quantum Structure in Cognition"](https://arxiv.org/abs/0805.3850) — used here as an analogy, not a metaphysical claim about consciousness.

## 5. The Gates as Invariant Protectors

Each AB gate is restated here with its invariant-theoretic meaning. The Python-AST check is the *shadow* of the invariant; the invariant is what the gate is actually defending.

### AB-01: Frozen Record Classes → Substance Identity Under Time Translation

**Invariant.** An entity that presents itself as a substance (a record class) must have identity that is stable under all legal time evolutions of the system. If any field can mutate without the record ceasing to be "the same record," then the record's identity is not substance-identity — it is process-identity, and it must be represented as such.

**Physical analog.** Time-translation symmetry of the record's identity. Noether: this symmetry generates conservation of substance-identity.

**Cosmological analog.** *Anātman* (non-self) has a precise operational form here: things that appear substantial are actually processes, and the code should reflect which is which. AB-01 forces the choice: if you claim substance-hood (frozen record), you must actually be substance under time translation. If you cannot be, use a different representation.

**Computable predicate.** Existing AST check plus: `has_time_translation_symmetry(cls) := all(f.frozen for f in fields(cls))`.

**Threshold.** 100% of fields frozen for records declared as substance-representations. Any other rate is a violation.

### AB-02: No Silent Exception Swallow → Causal Traceability

**Invariant.** Every exception is a *causal signal* — it carries information about a state the system entered. Swallowing it silently is destruction of causal information, which violates the receipt-graph completeness property (§1).

**Physical analog.** Conservation of information under unitary evolution. Silent exception-swallow is a non-unitary operation on the causal graph — information is destroyed.

**Cosmological analog.** *Karma* has a precise operational form: every action leaves a trace, and the system's future is conditioned by its past. Silent exception-swallow is the operational form of karmic laundering — pretending an event did not happen.

**Computable predicate.** Existing AST check. Extension: `has_receipt_trail(exception_handler) := emits_receipt(handler) or reraises(handler) or is_documented_absorption(handler)`.

**Threshold.** 0 undocumented absorptions. Documented absorptions must carry a receipt explaining why the exception is safe to swallow.

### AB-03: Import Boundary Enforcement → Locality Under Interaction

**Invariant.** No module reaches across the TCB boundary. Interactions between TCB and non-TCB code happen only through named interfaces with declared authority.

**Physical analog.** Locality principle — no action at a distance. All interactions are mediated by named intermediaries.

**Cosmological analog.** *Pratītyasamutpāda* at module scope: no module has independent existence relative to any other; all inter-module dependencies are explicit and recorded.

**Computable predicate.** Existing AST + import-graph check. Extension: every cross-boundary edge carries a `mediator_receipt` documenting the authority under which the crossing is legal.

### AB-04 / AB-05: Zero-Tolerance Gates

**Invariant.** Certain properties admit no gradient — they are boolean, and any violation is total. `eval`, `exec`, dynamic imports in TCB code fall in this class.

**Physical analog.** Selection rules in quantum mechanics — some transitions have zero amplitude, not "small amplitude." No perturbation makes them legal.

**Cosmological analog.** The Vinaya's *pārājika* class: root offenses that admit no rehabilitation within the same status. Some violations require expulsion from the category, not mere penalty. The mathematical structure is the same: a boolean gate with no gradient, no partial credit, no graceful degradation.

**Computable predicate.** Existing AST check with hold-at-zero semantics.

**Threshold.** 0. Not ε. Not "small." Zero.

## 6. The Path

The PR arc is not incidental. Each PR unlocks a specific dynamic property of the system, and the order is dictated by the dependency structure of those properties.

**PR #2 (spec triple).** Establishes the algebra. Without the algebra, no property is well-defined.

**PR #3 (assurance_boundary receipt emission).** First producer. The system now has *observed states* — receipts are being emitted from a real gate. Before PR #3 the system is potential; after PR #3 it is actual. This is the initial condition of the dynamics.

**PR #4 (SAB shadow export).** First long-distance communication. Receipts leave the local process. The Lyapunov function \( V \) is now defined over more than one node.

**PR #5 (titanium-verify NĀGA metadata).** First feedback loop. Verifier reads receipts, updates its own state, emits new receipts. The system now has *dynamics*, not just states.

**PR #6 (reconciler).** First cross-node consensus mechanism. Non-commuting merges (§4) become resolvable. Belnap `both` values (§3) get routed to the contradiction reconciler.

**PR #7 (Molt Arena).** *Phase transition mechanism.* This is why it is last. A molt is a discontinuous change in the trust base — the system genuinely becomes a different system, with different invariants. Attempting this before PR #2..#6 is like trying to run a phase transition on a system that has no thermodynamic state defined. Molt Arena requires the substrate to be mature — coherent receipts, active dynamics, consensus mechanism — before phase transitions can be safe.

**Why this order is the only order.** Each PR's invariants depend on the invariants established by all prior PRs. Reversing any two adjacent PRs produces a system where the later PR's invariants reference undefined quantities. This is why the sequence is not a preference — it is forced by the dependency graph of the mathematical structures.
