## I. Garden Purpose

This garden maps **revenue engines** — potential revenue streams for dharma_swarm — ranked by two dimensions:

1. **Alignment Score** (0-1): How well the revenue stream serves Jagat Kalyan (world welfare)
2. **Viability Score** (0-100): Market size, competition, execution feasibility

An **aligned revenue engine** generates revenue while advancing the system's telos.
