## III. Welfare-ton MRV (Execution Wedge)

### What Welfare-ton Is

The Welfare-ton is a **verification unit** that measures ecological restoration outcomes across multiple dimensions:

```
W = C × E × A × B × V × P

Where:
- C = verified CO2 sequestration (tCO2e/year)
- E = employment factor (job density × job quality)
- A = community agency factor (FPIC score)
- B = biodiversity co-benefit
- V = verification confidence
- P = permanence factor
```

### What Welfare-ton Is NOT

Welfare-ton is NOT:
- The full JAGAT KALYAN vision
- The AI Reciprocity Ledger
- The Planetary Reciprocity Commons

Welfare-ton is a **verification methodology** that can become a **component** of the larger architecture.
