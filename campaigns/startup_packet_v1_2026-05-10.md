## I. Vision: Recognition-Native Ecological Verification

### The Problem

1. **Carbon Credit Integrity Crisis**: Nature study (April 2026) found widespread over-crediting in forest carbon projects
2. **Single-Dimension Verification**: Existing platforms verify carbon only, missing biodiversity and livelihoods
3. **Documentation Burden**: Restoration practitioners spend 30-50% of time on verification paperwork
4. **Quality Premium Gap**: High-integrity projects can't differentiate from low-quality credits

### The Solution

**Welfare-ton MRV**: Multi-dimensional verification that measures:
- **Carbon**: Core carbon accounting
- **Biodiversity**: Co-benefits quantification
- **Livelihoods**: Community impact assessment
- **Governance**: Rights-based verification
- **Resilience**: Long-term sustainability metrics

**Value Proposition**: "Carbon credits that actually deliver ecological welfare"

### The Differentiation

| Pachama | Forest carbon | Welfare framing, community integration |

## III. Product: Welfare-ton MRV Platform

### Core Features

1. **Multi-Dimensional Verification**
   - Carbon accounting (remote sensing + ground truth)
   - Biodiversity metrics (species, habitat, genetic diversity)
   - Livelihoods assessment (community impact, economic benefits)
   - Governance verification (rights-based, indigenous participation)

2. **API-First Architecture**
   - RESTful API for integration with existing platforms
   - Webhook notifications for verification events
   - SDK for custom implementations

3. **Open Methodology**
   - Transparent verification methodology
   - Community-contributed improvements
   - Standards body alignment (ICVCM, Verra, Gold Standard)
