## I. Garden Purpose

This garden maps **attractors** — potential directions the dharma_swarm could evolve toward — ranked by two dimensions:

1. **Freedom Score** (0-1): Alignment with the system's telos (Jagat Kalyan, recognition-mediated autopoiesis, ecological welfare)
2. **Execution Score** (0-100): Feasibility given current capabilities, market timing, and resource constraints

The **highest-free attractor** is the direction that maximizes both alignment and feasibility.
